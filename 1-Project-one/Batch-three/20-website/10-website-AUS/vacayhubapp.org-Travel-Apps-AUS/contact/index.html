Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about VacayHub and our services.

How do I download and install the VacayHub app?

VacayHub is available for download on both iOS and Android devices. Simply visit the App Store or Google Play Store, search for "VacayHub," and click install. The app is free to download with optional premium features available through in-app purchases.

What makes VacayHub different from other travel apps?

VacayHub is specifically designed for Australian travelers, with features tailored to domestic travel needs. Our app combines AI-powered trip planning with verified local recommendations, exclusive deals on Australian accommodations, and 24/7 local customer support.

Is my payment information secure with VacayHub?

Absolutely. We use bank-level encryption and never store your full payment details on our servers. All transactions are processed through secure payment gateways that comply with Australian financial regulations.

How can I cancel or modify a booking made through VacayHub?

You can manage your bookings directly in the app under "My Trips." Cancellation policies vary by provider - we clearly display these before you book. For complex changes, our support team is available 24/7 to assist you.

Do you offer corporate or group travel solutions?

Yes, we have special solutions for corporate clients and group travelers. Please contact our business team at corporate@vacayhubapp.org or call +61280807901 for tailored solutions.
