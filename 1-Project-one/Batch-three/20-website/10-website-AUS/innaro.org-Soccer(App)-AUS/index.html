About Innaro

Innaro is Australia's premier soccer app, designed by football fans for football fans. Our mission is to bring you closer to the game you love with real-time updates, comprehensive statistics, and engaging content.

Founded in 2023 in Sydney, we've quickly become the go-to platform for Australian soccer enthusiasts who want to stay connected to both local and international football.
