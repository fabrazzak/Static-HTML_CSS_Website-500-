Frequently Asked Questions

What leagues do you cover?

We cover all major leagues worldwide including A-League, W-League, Premier League, La Liga, Bundesliga, Serie A, Ligue 1, and more. We also provide extensive coverage of AFC Champions League, Australia Cup, and other competitions relevant to Australian football.

Can I try before I buy?

Absolutely! Our free tier gives you access to basic features so you can experience the app before committing to a subscription. We also offer a 7-day free trial of our Premium features when you sign up.

How often is data updated?

Live match data is updated in real-time, with our servers pushing updates faster than any other platform in Australia. Other statistics and data are updated at least daily, with many metrics updated continuously throughout matchdays.

Is there a family plan?

Yes! We offer a family plan that allows up to 5 family members to share a Premium subscription at a discounted rate. Contact our support team for more information about family plans.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time. If you cancel during a billing period, you'll continue to have access until the end of that period. We don't believe in locking customers into long-term contracts.
