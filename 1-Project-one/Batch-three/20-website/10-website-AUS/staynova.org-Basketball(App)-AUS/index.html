About StayNova Basketball

StayNova is Australia's premier basketball training app, designed by professional coaches to help players of all levels improve their game. Our mission is to make elite-level training accessible to everyone.

Founded in 2020 by former NBL players, we've helped over 50,000 players across Australia develop their skills and confidence on the court.
