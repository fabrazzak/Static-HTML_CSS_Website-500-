Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our coaching programs

What ages do you coach?

We offer programs for players aged 5 through adult. Our youth programs are divided into age-appropriate groups: Mini (5-8), Junior (9-12), Youth (13-17), and Senior (18+). Each program is tailored to the developmental needs of that age group.

How do I book a coaching session?

You can book sessions through our contact form, by calling our office, or by emailing bookings@offsidealert.org. We recommend booking at least 2 weeks in advance for private sessions, especially during peak seasons.

What should I bring to my first session?

Please bring football boots (cleats), shin guards, comfortable athletic clothing, a water bottle, and any necessary medical items (inhalers, etc.). We provide all training equipment including balls, cones, and training vests.

Do you offer group training or just individual sessions?

We offer both! Our programs include individual coaching, small group sessions (2-5 players), team training, and academy programs. The best option depends on your goals and budget - we're happy to advise.

What's your cancellation policy?

We require 24 hours notice for cancellations to avoid being charged. In case of bad weather, we'll either move indoors or reschedule at no additional cost. Medical cancellations with documentation are always accommodated.
