Frequently Asked Questions

How do I download the Hockey Hype App?

The Hockey Hype App is available for download on both the Apple App Store and Google Play Store. Simply search for "Hockey Hype" and click install. The app is free to download with optional premium features available through in-app purchase.

What devices are supported by the app?

Our app supports iOS devices running iOS 13 or later and Android devices running Android 8.0 or later. We also have a web version available for desktop users at app.hockeyhypeapp.org.

How can I report a bug or technical issue?

If you encounter any technical issues, please contact our support team through the in-app support feature or email us at support@hockeyhypeapp.org. Please include details about your device, operating system version, and a description of the issue.

Do you offer team or league subscriptions?

Yes! We offer special pricing for teams and leagues. Please contact our sales team at sales@hockeyhypeapp.org or call us at +612 8080 7901 to discuss bulk pricing options and team features.

How can I suggest new features for the app?

We welcome all feature suggestions! You can submit your ideas through the in-app feedback form or email them to feedback@hockeyhypeapp.org. Our product team reviews all suggestions and prioritizes them based on user demand.
