Frequently Asked Questions

What's included in the free version of Hockey Hype?

The free version includes basic game tracking, limited player statistics, and access to our community forums. You'll get live scores for major games but without the detailed analytics and advanced features available in our premium plans.

Can I switch between plans?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time through your account settings. Changes will take effect at the start of your next billing cycle, with prorated charges or credits applied as needed.

Do you offer discounts for teams or organizations?

Absolutely! We offer significant discounts for teams, clubs, and organizations with multiple users. Contact our sales team at team@hockeyhypeapp.org for custom pricing based on your needs.

Is my data secure with Hockey Hype?

We take data security very seriously. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and we comply with Australian privacy laws. You can read more in our Privacy Policy.

What devices support the Hockey Hype app?

Hockey Hype is available on iOS (iPhone/iPad), Android devices, and through any modern web browser. Your account syncs seamlessly across all platforms so you can access your data anywhere.
