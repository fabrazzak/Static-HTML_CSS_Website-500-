About Hockey Hype

Hockey Hype App was founded by a team of passionate hockey players and tech enthusiasts in Sydney, Australia. We noticed a gap in the market for a comprehensive hockey app that could serve both casual fans and serious players.

Our mission is to bring the global hockey community closer together through technology. Whether you're a player, coach, or fan, Hockey Hype provides the tools you need to stay connected to the game you love.

With over 50,000 active users across Australia and growing international presence, we're quickly becoming the go-to platform for hockey enthusiasts worldwide.
