Our Basketball Story

Our Story

Founded in January 2025, Baseline Brief emerged from a shared passion for basketball and a gap in comprehensive coverage that truly served fans worldwide. Our founders, a group of seasoned basketball journalists and former players, noticed that most platforms focused exclusively on the NBA or specific regions, leaving international basketball underserved.

We started small, with just five team members working from a shared office in Zwolle,Australia. Today, we've grown to a team of 25 basketball experts covering games, analyzing stats, and delivering insights across all major leagues and competitions.

What sets us apart is our commitment to depth and context. We don't just report scores; we explain what they mean for players, teams, and the sport. Our analytics team has developed proprietary metrics that provide unique insights into player performance and team dynamics.

Our Mission & Values

Basketball First

We're fans first, journalists second. Our love for the game drives everything we do, from coverage choices to how we analyze plays.

Global Perspective

Basketball is a worldwide game. We cover the NBA, WNBA, EuroLeague, and emerging leagues with equal passion and professionalism.

Data-Driven

While we love the artistry of basketball, we ground our analysis in advanced statistics and proprietary metrics for deeper insights.

Community Focused

We engage with our readers, valuing their perspectives and incorporating feedback to improve our coverage continuously.

Innovative

From interactive game visualizations to unique stat visualizations, we're always pushing to present basketball content in fresh ways.

Integrity

We maintain strict journalistic standards, ensuring our reporting is accurate, fair, and free from undue influence.
