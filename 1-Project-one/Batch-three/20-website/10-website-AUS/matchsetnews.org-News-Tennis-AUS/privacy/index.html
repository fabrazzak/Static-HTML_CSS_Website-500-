Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. Learn how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Effective Date: January 1, 2025

Introduction

MatchSet News ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website matchsetnews.org (the "Site").

Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the Site.

Information We Collect

Personal Data

We may collect personally identifiable information that you voluntarily provide to us when you:

  • Register on the Site
  • Subscribe to our newsletter
  • Submit a contact form
  • Participate in surveys or promotions
  • Make a purchase

This information may include:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Mailing address
  • Payment information

Derivative Data

Information our servers automatically collect when you access the Site, such as:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Operating system
  • Access times
  • Pages viewed

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We may use cookies, web beacons, and other tracking technologies to collect information about your interactions with our Site, including:

  • Session cookies to keep you logged in
  • Preferences cookies to remember your settings
  • Analytics cookies to understand how visitors use our Site

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and maintain our Site
  • Notify you about changes to our Site
  • Allow you to participate in interactive features
  • Provide customer support
  • Gather analysis to improve our Site
  • Monitor usage of our Site
  • Detect and prevent technical issues
  • Send newsletters and marketing communications
  • Process transactions

Disclosure of Your Information

We may share information we have collected about you in certain situations:

By Law or to Protect Rights

If we believe the release of information is necessary to comply with Australian law, investigate potential violations, or protect against legal liability.

Third-Party Service Providers

We may share your information with third parties that perform services for us, including:

  • Payment processing
  • Data analysis
  • Email delivery
  • Hosting services
  • Customer service

Business Transfers

In connection with any merger, sale of company assets, or acquisition of all or a portion of our business by another company.

Your Privacy Rights

Under Australian privacy laws, you have certain rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: You may request access to your personal data.
  • Correction: You may request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Deletion: You may request deletion of your personal data.
  • Opt-out: You may opt-out of marketing communications.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at privacy@matchsetnews.org.

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information. However, no internet transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Our Site is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn we have collected such information, we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the effective date.
