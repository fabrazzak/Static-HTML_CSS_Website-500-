About MatchSet News

MatchSet News is Australia's premier tennis news platform, dedicated to bringing you comprehensive coverage of all things tennis. From Grand Slam tournaments to local Australian competitions, we've got you covered.

Founded in 2023 by a team of tennis enthusiasts and professional journalists, our mission is to deliver accurate, timely, and engaging content to tennis fans worldwide.

Our team consists of former players, coaches, and seasoned sports journalists who bring unique perspectives and in-depth analysis to our coverage.
