Frequently Asked Questions

How can I submit a tennis news tip to MatchSet News?

We welcome news tips from our readers! You can email your tips to tips@matchsetnews.org or use the contact form on this page. Please include as much detail as possible, including any sources or evidence to support your tip. Our editorial team will review all submissions and may follow up for more information.

Do you cover local Australian tennis tournaments?

Yes, we have dedicated coverage of major local tournaments across Australia, including state championships and notable regional events. If you're organizing a tournament and would like coverage, please contact our sports desk at least 4 weeks in advance at sports@matchsetnews.org with details about the event.

How can I advertise on MatchSet News?

We offer various advertising opportunities for brands looking to reach tennis enthusiasts. Our advertising team can be reached at ads@matchsetnews.org or by calling +612 8080 7901. We offer display ads, sponsored content, newsletter placements, and custom partnership opportunities.

Can I republish MatchSet News content on my website?

All content on MatchSet News is protected by copyright. For republication requests, please contact our permissions team at permissions@matchsetnews.org with details about the content you wish to republish and how it will be used. We typically require attribution and may charge a licensing fee for commercial use.

How often is your tennis rankings data updated?

Our ATP and WTA rankings are updated weekly, typically within hours of the official rankings being released. During Grand Slam tournaments, we provide daily updates on player performance and projected ranking changes. You can find our complete rankings at /rankings/.
