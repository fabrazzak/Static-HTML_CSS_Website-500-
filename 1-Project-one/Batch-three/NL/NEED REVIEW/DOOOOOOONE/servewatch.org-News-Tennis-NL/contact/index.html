Frequently Asked Questions

How can I submit tennis news tips to ServeWatch?

You can email your news tips to tips@servewatch.org. Please include any relevant details, sources, or media to help us verify and potentially cover your submission. Our editorial team reviews all incoming tips daily.

Do you cover junior or amateur tennis tournaments?

Yes, we cover select junior and amateur tournaments, especially those featuring promising young players or significant regional events. If you have a tournament you'd like us to consider covering, please contact our editorial team with details at least two weeks before the event.

How often is your player ranking data updated?

Our ATP and WTA rankings are updated weekly, typically within hours of the official releases. Other specialized rankings (juniors, doubles, etc.) are updated at least monthly. Historical ranking data is continuously being added to our archives.

Can I advertise on ServeWatch?

Absolutely! We offer various advertising opportunities for brands looking to reach tennis enthusiasts. Please contact our sales team at advertising@servewatch.org for our media kit and available options tailored to your marketing goals.
