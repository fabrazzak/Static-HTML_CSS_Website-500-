Our Story

Bringing Tennis Fans Together

ServeWatch was founded in Zwolle, Netherlands by a group of passionate tennis enthusiasts who saw a need for comprehensive, real-time tennis coverage that caters to fans at all levels. What began as a small blog has grown into one of the most trusted sources for tennis news worldwide.

Our team of experienced journalists and former players work tirelessly to bring you accurate, insightful coverage of all major tournaments, from Grand Slams to ATP and WTA events. We pride ourselves on our in-depth analysis, player interviews, and behind-the-scenes access that you won't find anywhere else.

With correspondents at tournaments around the globe and partnerships with major tennis organizations, ServeWatch delivers content that goes beyond the scores to tell the stories that matter in the world of tennis.

Our Mission

At ServeWatch, we believe tennis is more than just a sport—it's a global community. Our mission is to connect fans with the game they love through:

  • Comprehensive coverage of all levels of professional tennis
  • In-depth analysis that helps fans understand the nuances of the game
  • Exclusive content that goes beyond what you see on TV
  • A platform that celebrates the diversity and global nature of tennis

We're committed to journalistic integrity, accuracy, and bringing you the stories that matter most to tennis fans around the world.
