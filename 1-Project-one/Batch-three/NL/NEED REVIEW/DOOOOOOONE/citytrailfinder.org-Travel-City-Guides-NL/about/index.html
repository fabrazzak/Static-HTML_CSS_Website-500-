Our Story

Discover how City Trail Finder began and our mission to transform how people experience cities

Helping Travelers Experience Cities Like Locals

We believe the best travel experiences happen when you step off the beaten path

City Trail Finder was founded in 2020 by a group of passionate travelers who were tired of generic travel guides that only covered tourist traps. After years of visiting cities and feeling like we only scratched the surface, we set out to create something different.

Our journey began in Melbourne, Australia, where we started documenting the city's hidden laneways, local cafes, and street art that most visitors never discovered. What started as a passion project quickly grew into a comprehensive platform with guides for cities worldwide.

Today, we work with a network of local experts in each destination to research, test, and curate authentic experiences that showcase the true character of each city.

Our Mission

To empower travelers to experience cities authentically by connecting them with local knowledge and hidden gems that transform ordinary trips into unforgettable adventures.

Our Local Experts

We work with residents who know their cities inside out

How We Select Our Experts

Each city guide is created by a local resident who has lived in the city for at least 5 years and has deep knowledge of its hidden gems. Our experts go through a rigorous selection process to ensure they share our values of authenticity and quality.

  • Must be current residents of the city they're writing about
  • Demonstrated expertise in local culture and history
  • Passion for sharing authentic experiences with visitors
  • Commitment to regular updates and accuracy
