Frequently Asked Questions

How can I submit a news tip to NetNewsZone?

We welcome news tips from our readers! You can submit a tip through our contact form above, selecting "News Tip" as the subject. Please provide as much detail as possible, including sources or evidence to support your tip. Our editorial team will review all submissions and follow up if we need more information.

What are your advertising rates and options?

NetNewsZone offers various advertising options including banner ads, sponsored content, and newsletter placements. Our rates vary based on placement, duration, and campaign scope. Please use our contact form and select "Advertising" as the subject, and our sales team will provide you with a detailed media kit and rate card.

How often is your content updated?

We update our content continuously throughout the day, with breaking news posted immediately. During major events like NBA playoffs or EuroLeague games, we provide real-time updates. Our team works around the clock to ensure you have the latest basketball news when you need it.

Can I republish your content on my website?

All content on NetNewsZone is protected by copyright. If you wish to republish any of our articles, you must request permission by contacting us with details about the specific content and your intended use. We may grant permission with proper attribution and under certain conditions.
