Team Spotlight

Donar Groningen

Donar is the most successful basketball team in the Netherlands, having won the Dutch Basketball League championship 8 times. Based in Groningen, the team plays its home games at the MartiniPlaza, which has a capacity of 4,350 spectators.

The club has a strong fan base known for creating an electric atmosphere during games. Donar has also participated in European competitions, representing Dutch basketball on the international stage.
