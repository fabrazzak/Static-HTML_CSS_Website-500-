About NetNewsZone

Discover the story behind the Netherlands' premier basketball news platform and the team dedicated to bringing you the best coverage.

Our Story

Founded in Zwolle, Netherlands, NetNewsZone was born out of a passion for basketball and a desire to provide comprehensive coverage for fans across Europe. What began as a small blog has grown into the Netherlands' most trusted source for basketball news and analysis.

Our founder, Michael van den Berg, a former professional basketball player in the Dutch Basketball League, recognized the need for a dedicated platform that could cover both international leagues like the NBA and EuroLeague, as well as local Dutch competitions with equal depth and professionalism.

Today, NetNewsZone boasts a team of experienced journalists, analysts, and former players who work around the clock to deliver accurate, timely, and insightful basketball coverage to our growing community of fans.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide basketball fans with the most comprehensive, accurate, and engaging coverage of the sport at all levels, from local Dutch competitions to the global stage of the NBA and international tournaments.

Our Vision

To become the leading basketball media platform in Europe, recognized for our journalistic integrity, in-depth analysis, and commitment to growing the sport's popularity in the Netherlands and beyond.
