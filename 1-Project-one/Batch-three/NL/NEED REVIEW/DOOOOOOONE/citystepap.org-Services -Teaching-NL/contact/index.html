Frequently Asked Questions

What teaching methods do you use at CityStepAP?

At CityStepAP, we employ a variety of evidence-based teaching methods tailored to each student's needs. Our approach includes personalized learning plans, interactive sessions, and a combination of traditional and modern teaching techniques. We focus on active learning, critical thinking development, and practical application of knowledge.

How do I schedule a consultation or trial lesson?

You can schedule a consultation or trial lesson by filling out our contact form, calling us at +31 572 388 188, or emailing info@citystepap.org. We typically respond within 24 hours to arrange a convenient time for your consultation or trial session.

What languages do you offer courses in?

We currently offer courses in Dutch (for non-native speakers), English, French, and Spanish. Our language courses cater to all proficiency levels from beginner to advanced. We also provide specialized courses for business language needs and exam preparation.

Do you offer online tutoring sessions?

Yes, we offer both in-person and online tutoring sessions. Our online sessions are conducted via secure video conferencing platforms with interactive digital whiteboards and shared learning materials. Many students find our online sessions just as effective as in-person instruction.
