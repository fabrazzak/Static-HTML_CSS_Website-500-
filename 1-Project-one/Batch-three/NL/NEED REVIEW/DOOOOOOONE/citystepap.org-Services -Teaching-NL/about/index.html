Our Story

Founded in 2020, CityStepAP began as a small tutoring center in Zwolle with a simple mission: to provide high-quality, personalized education to students of all ages and backgrounds. What started as a modest operation with just two instructors has grown into a respected educational institution serving hundreds of students annually.

Our founder, Dr. Anna Petrov, recognized the need for a more individualized approach to learning in the Zwolle area. After years of teaching in traditional school settings, she saw how many students struggled when their unique learning needs weren't being met. CityStepAP was born from her vision of creating a space where every student could receive the attention and resources they need to succeed.

Today, we're proud to have a team of 12 dedicated educators and a beautiful learning center in the heart of Zwolle, but our core values remain the same: student-centered learning, academic excellence, and a commitment to helping each individual reach their full potential.

Our Mission & Values

At CityStepAP, we believe that education should be transformative, engaging, and tailored to each learner's needs. Our mission is to empower students through personalized instruction that builds confidence, fosters curiosity, and develops critical thinking skills.

We're committed to creating an inclusive learning environment where students feel valued and supported. Our approach combines academic rigor with compassionate teaching to help students not only achieve their immediate educational goals but also develop lifelong learning skills.
