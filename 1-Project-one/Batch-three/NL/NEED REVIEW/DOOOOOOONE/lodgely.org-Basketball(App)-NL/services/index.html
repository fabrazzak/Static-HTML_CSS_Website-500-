Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services

What devices support the Lodgely training app?

Our training app is available for both iOS and Android devices. You can download it from the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. The app is optimized for smartphones and tablets running the latest operating systems.

How often are new training programs added?

We update our training library monthly with new programs and drills. Pro and Elite subscribers get immediate access to all new content, while Starter subscribers may have limited access to certain advanced programs.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes, all our subscriptions are month-to-month with no long-term contracts. You can cancel anytime through your account settings, and you'll continue to have access until the end of your current billing period.

What qualifications do your coaches have?

Our coaching team consists of former professional players, certified trainers, and experienced college coaches. All coaches undergo rigorous training in our methodology and are required to maintain current coaching certifications.

Do you offer team or group packages?

Yes, we offer special pricing for teams, clubs, and schools. Our team packages include shared access to training programs, group video analysis sessions, and discounted coaching rates. Contact us for custom team solutions.
