Our Story

How Lodgely Basketball became the go-to platform for players across Europe

From Local Passion to European Platform

Founded by former professional basketball player Lars van den Berg and tech entrepreneur Sophie de Vries, Lodgely Basketball began as a small training app for local players in Zwolle, Netherlands.

What started as a side project quickly gained traction when local teams saw significant improvements in their players' performance., we had expanded to serve players across the Netherlands, and by the end of the year, we were working with teams in Germany, Belgium, and France.

Today, Lodgely Basketball is used by over 50,000 players and 500 teams across Europe, with our innovative training methods and analytics platform helping players at all levels improve their game.

Our Mission & Values

What drives us to innovate in basketball training every day

Our Mission

To democratize access to professional-level basketball training by combining cutting-edge technology with proven coaching methodologies, making elite performance achievable for players at all levels.

We believe that every player deserves access to the same quality of training, regardless of their location, resources, or current skill level.

Our Values

  • Player-First Approach: Every feature we build is designed with the player's development in mind.
  • Continuous Innovation: We constantly evolve our platform based on the latest sports science research.
  • Community Building: Basketball is a team sport, and we foster connections between players.
  • Data-Driven Results: We measure everything to ensure our methods deliver real improvements.
  • Integrity: We're transparent about what works and what doesn't in basketball training.
