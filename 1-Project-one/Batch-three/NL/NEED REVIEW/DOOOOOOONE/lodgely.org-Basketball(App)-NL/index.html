About Lodgely Basketball

We're revolutionizing basketball training with technology-driven solutions for players and coaches.

Your Personal Basketball Coach

Founded in Zwolle, Netherlands, Lodgely Basketball was created by former professional players and tech enthusiasts who saw a gap in basketball training technology.

Our mission is to make professional-level training accessible to everyone, regardless of skill level or location. We combine cutting-edge technology with proven basketball training methodologies.

With over 50,000 active users across Europe, we're quickly becoming the go-to platform for serious basketball players looking to improve their game.
