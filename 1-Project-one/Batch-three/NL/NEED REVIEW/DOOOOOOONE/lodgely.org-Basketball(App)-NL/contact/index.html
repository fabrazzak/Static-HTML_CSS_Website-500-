Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our basketball training app and services.

How do I get started with the Lodgely training app?

Getting started is easy! Simply download our app from the App Store or Google Play, create an account, and complete your player profile. Our system will then recommend personalized training programs based on your skill level and goals.

What devices are compatible with the Lodgely app?

Our app is compatible with most iOS and Android devices. For iOS, you'll need version 14.0 or later. For Android, you'll need version 8.0 or later. The app is optimized for both smartphones and tablets.

Do you offer team or group subscriptions?

Yes, we offer special pricing for teams, schools, and basketball organizations. Our team plans include centralized billing, progress tracking for all players, and coach dashboards. Contact our sales team for more information.

How does the video analysis service work?

After subscribing to our video analysis service, you can upload your game or practice footage through our app or website. Our certified coaches will analyze your performance and provide detailed feedback within 48 hours, including annotated video clips and specific improvement recommendations.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, and iDEAL for customers in the Netherlands. All payments are processed securely through our payment partners.
