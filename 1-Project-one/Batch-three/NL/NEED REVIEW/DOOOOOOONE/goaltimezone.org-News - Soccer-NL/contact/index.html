Common Questions

Answers to some of our most frequently asked questions

How can I submit a news tip or story idea?

We welcome news tips and story ideas from our readers. Please email your tip to tips@goaltimezone.org with as much detail as possible. Our editorial team will review all submissions and may follow up for more information.

What's the best way to contact your editorial team?

For editorial inquiries, please use our contact form above or email editors@goaltimezone.org. Please include specific details about your request, including any relevant deadlines.

Do you offer advertising or sponsorship opportunities?

Yes, we offer various advertising and sponsorship packages. Please contact our sales team at advertising@goaltimezone.org for our media kit and current opportunities.

Can I write for Goaltimezone as a contributor?

We're always interested in hearing from qualified writers with expertise in football journalism. Please send your resume and writing samples to careers@goaltimezone.org with "Writer Application" in the subject line.
