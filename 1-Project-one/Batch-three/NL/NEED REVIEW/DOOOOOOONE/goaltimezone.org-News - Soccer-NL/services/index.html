Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services

What leagues do you cover?

We cover all major European leagues (Premier League, La Liga, Bundesliga, Serie A, Ligue 1), Champions League, Europa League, and select other competitions worldwide. Our coverage focuses primarily on top-tier leagues but we also provide updates for major international tournaments.

How often is your content updated?

We update our content continuously throughout the day. Match coverage is updated in real-time during games, news articles are published as stories break, and transfer rumors are verified and posted multiple times daily.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes, all our subscription plans are month-to-month with no long-term contracts. You can cancel anytime through your account settings, and you'll continue to have access until the end of your current billing period.

Do you offer discounts for annual payments?

Yes! We offer a 15% discount when you pay annually instead of monthly. This applies to all our subscription plans and can be selected during the checkout process.

How accurate are your transfer rumors?

We pride ourselves on only reporting transfer news from verified sources. Our team vets each rumor before publication and assigns a reliability rating so you can judge the likelihood of each potential transfer.
