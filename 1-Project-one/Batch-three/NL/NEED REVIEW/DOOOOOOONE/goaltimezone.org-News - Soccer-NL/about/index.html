Our Story

Founded in Zwolle, Netherlands, Goaltimezone began as a passion project by a group of soccer enthusiasts who believed football coverage could be more comprehensive, accurate, and engaging.

What started as a small blog covering local Dutch football has grown into an international platform with readers in over 120 countries. Our team now includes journalists, former players, and tactical analysts dedicated to bringing you the best soccer coverage.

We pride ourselves on our independent perspective, refusing to be swayed by club affiliations or sponsorship deals. Our only allegiance is to the truth and the beautiful game.

Our Mission & Values

The principles that guide everything we do at Goaltimezone

Mission

To provide soccer fans worldwide with the most accurate, timely, and insightful coverage of the game they love, while maintaining the highest standards of journalistic integrity.

Accuracy

We verify every fact before publication and correct errors promptly. Our readers trust us to get it right, and we take that responsibility seriously.

Insight

Beyond reporting what happened, we explain why it matters. Our analysis helps fans understand the deeper aspects of the game.

Global Perspective

While based in the Netherlands, we cover football as a global game, giving equal respect to all leagues and competitions worldwide.

Community

We believe football is nothing without its fans. We actively engage with our readers and value their perspectives.

Integrity

We maintain editorial independence and refuse to let commercial interests influence our coverage.

Our Journey

Key milestones in Goaltimezone's history

June 2020

Founded in Zwolle

Goaltimezone launched as a Dutch football blog with just three team members working from a small office.

January 2021

First International Readers

Our coverage expanded to include Premier League, attracting readers from the UK and beyond.

September 2022

Mobile App Launch

Released our first mobile application, making our content more accessible to fans worldwide.

March 2023

100,000 Subscribers

Reached the milestone of 100,000 newsletter subscribers across 85 countries.

November 2024

World Cup Coverage

Continuing to Grow
