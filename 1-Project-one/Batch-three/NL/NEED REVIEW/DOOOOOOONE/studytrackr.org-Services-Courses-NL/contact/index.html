Frequently Asked Questions

How do I enroll in a course?

You can enroll in any of our courses directly through our website. Visit the Services page, select the course you're interested in, and click the "Enroll Now" button. You'll be guided through the registration and payment process.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept various payment methods including credit/debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, and iDEAL for our Dutch students. All payments are processed securely through our payment gateway.

Can I get a refund if I'm not satisfied?

Yes, we offer a 14-day money-back guarantee for all our courses. If you're not satisfied with your purchase within the first 14 days of enrollment, you can request a full refund with no questions asked.

Do you offer certificates upon completion?

Yes, all our courses provide a certificate of completion once you finish all the required modules and assessments. Our certificates are recognized by employers and can be shared on LinkedIn or included in your resume.

How long do I have access to the course materials?

You'll have lifetime access to all course materials after enrollment. This includes any future updates we make to the course content, so you can always come back to review the material.
