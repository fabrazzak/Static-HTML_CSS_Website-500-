Our Story

StudyTrackr was founded in 2020 in Zwolle, Netherlands, with a simple vision: to make quality education accessible to everyone, regardless of their location or schedule constraints.

What began as a small platform offering a handful of courses has grown into a comprehensive learning ecosystem serving thousands of students across Europe. Our journey has been fueled by the success stories of our students and the dedication of our team.

From our humble beginnings in a small office in Zwolle to becoming one of the leading online education platforms in the Netherlands, we've remained committed to our core values of accessibility, quality, and student success.

Today, we partner with industry experts and academic professionals to deliver courses that are not only informative but also practical and career-oriented.
