About GameOnIce

Transforming hockey training through technology and community

Your Hockey Journey Starts Here

Founded in 2022, GameOnIce is a Dutch-based hockey technology company dedicated to helping players of all levels improve their skills and performance. Our app combines cutting-edge training tools with a vibrant community of hockey enthusiasts across the Netherlands.

Whether you're a beginner learning to skate or an experienced player preparing for competitive play, our platform provides personalized training plans, skill tracking, and access to professional coaching resources.
