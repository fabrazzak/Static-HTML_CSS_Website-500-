Our Story

From a local Dutch hockey passion to a leading hockey training platform

Founded on the Ice

GameOnIce was born in 2022 from a simple idea shared by three hockey enthusiasts in Zwolle, Netherlands. As former players and coaches, we recognized the challenges Dutch hockey players faced in accessing quality training resources and connecting with the right coaches.

What started as a small project to help local players track their progress has grown into a comprehensive platform serving thousands of hockey players across the Netherlands. Our Dutch roots remain at the heart of everything we do, with a deep understanding of the unique needs of the Netherlands hockey community.

Today, we're proud to partner with Dutch hockey clubs, schools, and organizations to make high-quality training accessible to players at every level.
