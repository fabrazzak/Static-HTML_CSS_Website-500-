Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our hockey training services

How do I get started with GameOnIce training app?

Getting started is easy! Simply visit our Services page and choose the subscription plan that fits your needs. After signing up, you'll receive immediate access to all training materials and can begin your hockey improvement journey right away.

Do you offer services for hockey teams in the Netherlands?

Yes! We have special team solutions designed specifically for Dutch hockey clubs. Our team packages include group training plans, performance tracking for all players, and coach communication tools. Contact us for customized solutions for your team.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), iDEAL for Dutch customers, PayPal, and bank transfers. All transactions are secure and encrypted.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Absolutely. There are no long-term contracts, and you can cancel your subscription at any time through your account settings. You'll continue to have access until the end of your current billing period.

Do you offer in-person training in Zwolle?

Yes, we organize periodic training camps and clinics at locations in Zwolle and throughout the Netherlands. Check our Events page or contact us for the current schedule of in-person training opportunities.
