Frequently Asked Questions

How do I download the Travora app?

Travora is currently in development and will be available for download on both iOS and Android devices in Q2 2025. You can sign up on our website to be notified when the app launches.

Which leagues does Travora cover?

We cover all major European leagues including Eredivisie, Premier League, La Liga, Bundesliga, and Serie A, as well as international competitions like the Champions League and World Cup qualifiers.

Is there a premium version of the app?

Yes, Travora offers a free version with basic features and a premium subscription that unlocks advanced analytics, ad-free experience, and exclusive content. Pricing will be announced at launch.

Can I use Travora for my amateur team?

Absolutely! Our team management features are designed for teams at all levels, from professional clubs to local amateur teams. You can track player stats, manage schedules, and analyze performance.

How accurate are your live scores?

We partner with leading sports data providers to deliver the most accurate and fastest live scores available. Our system updates every 10 seconds during matches to ensure you don't miss a moment.
