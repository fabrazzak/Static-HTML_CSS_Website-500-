About Travora

Travora was founded in 2023 by a team of soccer enthusiasts and tech experts in the Netherlands. We noticed a gap in the market for a comprehensive soccer app that combines live scores with advanced team management features.

Our mission is to provide fans, players, and coaches with the tools they need to stay connected to the game they love. Whether you're following your favorite team or managing your local club, Travora has you covered.
