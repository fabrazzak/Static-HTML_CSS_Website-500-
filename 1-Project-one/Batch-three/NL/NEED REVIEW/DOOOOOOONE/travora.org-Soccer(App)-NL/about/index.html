Our Story

Travora was born in 2023 from a shared passion for soccer and technology among a group of friends in Zwolle, Netherlands. What started as a small project to help local coaches manage their teams better has grown into a comprehensive soccer platform used by thousands across Europe.

Our founders, all former semi-professional players and tech enthusiasts, recognized the need for a unified solution that could bring together live match data, team management tools, and player analytics in one intuitive platform.

Today, Travora serves amateur and professional teams alike, with a special focus on the Dutch football scene while expanding our coverage to major European leagues.

Our Mission & Values

Empowering the Soccer Community

At Travora, we believe technology should enhance, not complicate, the beautiful game. Our mission is to provide innovative tools that help players, coaches, and fans connect with soccer in meaningful ways.

We're committed to:

  • Delivering accurate, real-time data
  • Creating intuitive user experiences
  • Supporting grassroots football development
  • Promoting fair play and sportsmanship
