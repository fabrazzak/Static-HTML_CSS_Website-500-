Frequently Asked Questions

Which leagues are covered in your live scores service?

We cover all major European leagues including Eredivisie, Premier League, La Liga, Bundesliga, Serie A, and Ligue 1, as well as international competitions like Champions League and World Cup qualifiers. Our Basic plan includes 5 leagues of your choice, while Pro and Enterprise plans offer comprehensive coverage.

Can I try the service before committing to a subscription?

Yes! We offer a 14-day free trial for all our plans with no credit card required. You'll get full access to all features during the trial period so you can evaluate if our service meets your needs.

How does the team management feature help amateur clubs?

Our team management tools are designed for clubs at all levels. You can manage player registrations, schedule training sessions and matches, track attendance, analyze performance, and communicate with your team - all from one platform. It's particularly valuable for youth teams and amateur clubs looking to professionalize their operations.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, and iDEAL for our Dutch customers. All payments are processed securely through our PCI-compliant payment processor.

Can I cancel my subscription at any time?

Absolutely. There are no long-term contracts, and you can cancel your subscription at any time through your account settings. If you cancel, you'll continue to have access until the end of your current billing period.
