About WandrGuide

Founded in 5 in the heart of the Netherlands, WandrGuide was born from a passion for travel and technology. Our team of globetrotters and tech enthusiasts came together with one mission: to make travel easier, more enjoyable, and more accessible for everyone.

We believe that technology should enhance your travel experience, not complicate it. That's why we've developed a suite of intuitive apps that help you navigate unfamiliar places, discover local favorites, and plan your perfect itinerary - all from your smartphone.
