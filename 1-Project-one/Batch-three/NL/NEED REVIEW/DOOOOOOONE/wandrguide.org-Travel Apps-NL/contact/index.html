Frequently Asked Questions

How do I download and install WandrGuide apps?

Our apps are available on both the Apple App Store and Google Play Store. Simply search for "WandrGuide" in your device's app store, select the app you want to download, and tap "Install." Once installed, open the app and follow the setup instructions.

Do your apps work offline?

Yes! All WandrGuide apps offer offline functionality. For navigation apps, you'll need to download maps for your destination before your trip. For recommendation apps, you can save locations and information for offline access.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, and for some regions, local payment methods. All transactions are processed securely through encrypted connections.

How can I cancel my subscription?

You can manage your subscription directly in the app settings or through your device's app store account. Cancellations take effect at the end of your current billing period, and you'll continue to have access until that date.

Do you offer discounts for students or groups?

Yes! We offer a 20% discount for students with valid .edu email addresses. For groups of 10 or more, please contact our sales team for special pricing options.
