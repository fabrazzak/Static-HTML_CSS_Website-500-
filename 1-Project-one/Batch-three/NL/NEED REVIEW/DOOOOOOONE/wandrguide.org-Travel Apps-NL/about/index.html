Our Story

Discover the passion and purpose behind WandrGuide, and meet the team dedicated to revolutionizing your travel experiences.

From Passion to Innovation

Founded in 2025 in Zwolle, Netherlands, WandrGuide began as a simple idea between two travel enthusiasts who were frustrated with the limitations of existing travel apps. Our co-founders, Lucas van Dijk and Sofia Müller, met while backpacking through Southeast Asia and bonded over their shared vision for better travel technology.

What started as sketches on napkins in hostels across the world has grown into a thriving company with a team of 25 passionate individuals. We've remained true to our roots, with every team member required to spend at least 4 weeks per year traveling to understand our users' needs firsthand.

Today, WandrGuide apps are used by over 500,000 travelers worldwide, helping them navigate unfamiliar places, discover hidden gems, and create unforgettable memories with ease.
