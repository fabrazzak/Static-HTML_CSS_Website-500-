Discover Australia's Cities on Foot

Founded in 2018, Smart City Walks began with a simple mission: to help people experience Australian cities in a more meaningful, intimate way. We believe the best way to understand a city is to walk its streets, meet its people, and discover its hidden stories.

What started as a small operation in Melbourne has grown into a national network of passionate local guides offering unique walking experiences in six major Australian cities. Our team comes from diverse backgrounds - historians, artists, urban planners, and lifelong locals - all united by a love for their cities and a desire to share that passion with others.

Explore Our Tours

Our Mission & Approach

At Smart City Walks, we're committed to sustainable, responsible tourism that benefits both visitors and local communities. We keep our groups small (never more than 15 people) to ensure quality experiences and minimize our impact on the neighborhoods we visit.

Unlike traditional sightseeing tours, we focus on authentic local experiences. Our guides don't just recite facts - they share personal stories, introduce you to local business owners, and help you see the city through a local's eyes.

We're proud to partner with local businesses, artists, and community organizations to create tours that are not just entertaining, but also support the cultural and economic vitality of the cities we love.

Our Core Values

What guides everything we do

Local Expertise

Our guides are true locals who know their cities inside out. We don't do scripted tours - every walk is shaped by personal knowledge and passion.

Community Focus

We're committed to supporting local businesses and communities. Many of our tours include stops at independent shops, cafes, and cultural spaces.

Sustainable Tourism

Walking is the most sustainable way to explore a city. We keep group sizes small to minimize our impact and maximize your experience.

Hidden Gems

We go beyond the tourist hotspots to show you the authentic character of each city - the places locals love and visitors rarely find on their own.

Passionate Storytelling

Our guides don't just share facts - they tell stories that bring each city's history, culture, and people to life in memorable ways.

Quality Commitment

From guide training to customer service, we maintain the highest standards to ensure every tour exceeds your expectations.
