Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our tours and services

How do I book a walking tour?

You can book directly through our website by selecting your preferred tour and date. Alternatively, you can call our office or email us to make a booking. We recommend booking at least 48 hours in advance for our regular tours, and further ahead for private or group tours.

What is your cancellation policy?

We offer free cancellation up to 24 hours before your scheduled tour for a full refund. For cancellations within 24 hours, we can reschedule your tour but cannot provide a refund. In case of extreme weather conditions, we'll either reschedule your tour or provide a full refund.

Are your tours suitable for children?

Most of our tours are family-friendly and suitable for children aged 8 and up. We do have some specialized tours that may be more appropriate for older children and adults. Please check the tour description or contact us for recommendations based on your children's ages and interests.

Do you offer private or custom tours?

Yes! We specialize in creating custom walking tours for private groups, corporate events, and special occasions. Our team will work with you to design an experience tailored to your group's interests, pace, and schedule. Contact us with your requirements for a personalized quote.

What should I bring on a walking tour?

We recommend comfortable walking shoes, weather-appropriate clothing, sunscreen, a water bottle, and your camera. Most tours last 2-3 hours with opportunities to stop for refreshments, but you may want to bring snacks if needed. We'll send specific recommendations when you book.
