About Smart City Walks

Founded in 2018, Smart City Walks has been helping locals and visitors alike discover the authentic heart of Australia's urban landscapes. Our team of passionate guides combines local knowledge with engaging storytelling to create unforgettable walking experiences.

We believe that the best way to experience a city is on foot, at street level, where you can truly connect with the local culture, architecture, and people. Our tours are designed to be informative, entertaining, and accessible to everyone.
