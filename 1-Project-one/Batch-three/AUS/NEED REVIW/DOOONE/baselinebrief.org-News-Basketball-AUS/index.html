About Hockey Hype

Hockey Hype App was founded in 2023 by a team of passionate hockey players and tech enthusiasts in Sydney. We noticed a gap in the market for a comprehensive hockey app tailored specifically to the Australian hockey community.

Our mission is to provide players of all levels with the tools they need to improve their game, connect with other players, and stay engaged with the hockey community nationwide.

Whether you're playing in a local Sydney competition or representing Australia internationally, Hockey Hype has features designed to support your hockey journey.
