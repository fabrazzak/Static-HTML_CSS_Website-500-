Frequently Asked Questions

What are your customer support hours for Australian students?

Our dedicated Australian support team is available Monday to Friday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM AEST, and Saturdays from 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM AEST. For urgent matters outside these hours, you can email support@studytrackr.org and we'll respond as soon as possible.

How can I enroll in one of your courses?

You can enroll directly through our website by visiting the Courses page, or contact our enrollment advisors at enroll@studytrackr.org. We also offer phone enrollment for Australian students at 1800 123 456.

Do you offer in-person consultations in Australia?

Yes! We have education consultants available in major Australian cities including Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, and Perth. Contact us to schedule an appointment at our nearest office or arrange a video consultation if you're in regional areas.

What payment methods do you accept for Australian students?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, and direct bank transfers through Australian payment gateways. We also offer payment plans for many of our courses.

How quickly can I expect a response to my inquiry?

We strive to respond to all inquiries within 24 hours during business days. For technical support issues, our average response time is under 4 hours during support hours.
