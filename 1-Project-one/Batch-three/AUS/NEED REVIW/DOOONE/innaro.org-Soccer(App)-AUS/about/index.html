Our Story

From a passion project to Australia's premier soccer app - discover the people and values behind Innaro.

How It All Began

Innaro was founded in 2023 by a group of football enthusiasts in Sydney who were frustrated with the lack of comprehensive soccer coverage tailored for Australian fans. What started as a side project quickly grew into Australia's most trusted football app.

Our founders, all former players and coaches, saw an opportunity to create a platform that would serve both casual fans and hardcore football analysts with real-time data, insightful analysis, and unparalleled A-League coverage.

Today, Innaro serves over 250,000 users across Australia, with our team growing from 3 to 30 dedicated professionals who share the same passion for the beautiful game.

Our Mission

At Innaro, we believe football is more than just a game - it's a universal language that brings people together. Our mission is to connect Australian fans to the football world through technology that's fast, reliable, and easy to use.

We're committed to providing the most comprehensive coverage of both local and international football, with a special focus on developing the Australian game and supporting our domestic leagues.
