Common Questions

How do I report a bug or technical issue?

Please use our contact form above and select "Technical Support" as the subject. Include as much detail as possible about the issue, including your device model, operating system version, and steps to reproduce the problem. Screenshots are also helpful.

What are your customer support hours?

Our customer support team is available Monday through Friday from 9am to 5pm AEST. We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24 hours during business days. For urgent issues outside these hours, please call our support line.

Can I suggest a new feature for the app?

Absolutely! We welcome all feature suggestions from our users. Please use the contact form and select "Feedback/Suggestions" as the subject. Our product team reviews all suggestions and the most popular ones often make it into future updates.

Do you offer partnerships for football clubs or organizations?

Yes, we work with football clubs, leagues, and organizations across Australia. Please select "Business/Partnerships" in the contact form and provide details about your organization and proposed partnership.
