Our Use of Cookies

This Cookie Policy explains how OffsideAlert ("we", "us", or "our") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies when you visit our website (offsidealert.org). By using our website, you consent to the use of cookies as described in this policy.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, smartphone, tablet) when you visit websites. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and to provide information to website owners.

Types of Cookies We Use

Category Purpose Examples
Essential Cookies Necessary for the website to function properly Session cookies, authentication cookies
Performance Cookies Help us understand how visitors use our site Google Analytics cookies
Functional Cookies Remember your preferences and settings Language preference cookies
Targeting/Advertising Cookies Used to deliver relevant ads and track ad performance Facebook Pixel, Google Ads cookies

Specific Cookies We Use

Below is a list of the main cookies we use on our website:

Cookie Name Provider Purpose Duration

Third-Party Cookies

We may use services from third parties that set cookies on our behalf to deliver their services. These include:

  • Google Analytics: For analyzing website traffic and usage patterns
  • Facebook Pixel: For measuring ad effectiveness and building audiences
  • YouTube: For embedded video content
  • Payment Processors: For secure payment transactions

We don't control these third-party cookies. Please refer to the respective privacy policies of these providers for more information.

How to Control Cookies

You can control and/or delete cookies as you wish:
