Our Story

Founded in 2018 by former professional footballer Mark Williams, OffsideAlert began as a small coaching clinic in Melbourne with a simple mission: to provide high-quality football education to players of all levels.

What started as a weekend training program has grown into one of Australia's most respected football coaching services, with programs now running in five major cities across the country.

Our growth has been driven by a commitment to excellence and a focus on long-term player development rather than short-term results. We measure our success not just by trophies won, but by the technical, tactical, and personal growth of every player who trains with us.

Our Mission & Values

The principles that guide everything we do

Player Development

We prioritize long-term development over short-term results, focusing on technical mastery, tactical understanding, and personal growth.

Inclusive Environment

Football is for everyone. We create welcoming spaces where players of all backgrounds and abilities can thrive.

Continuous Learning

Our coaches are lifelong learners who stay current with the latest coaching methodologies and sports science research.

Passion for the Game

We believe football should be fun. Our sessions foster a love for the game that lasts a lifetime.
