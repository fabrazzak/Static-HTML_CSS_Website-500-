Frequently Asked Questions

What age groups do you train?

We offer training programs for players of all ages, from 8 years old to adult. Our programs are tailored to different age groups and skill levels to ensure appropriate development.

How do I get started with StayNova?

Getting started is easy! You can download our app from the App Store or Google Play, or contact us to schedule an assessment session with one of our coaches.

Do you offer team training programs?

Yes, we provide specialized team training programs for school teams, club teams, and recreational teams. Our team packages include customized training plans and group sessions.

What makes StayNova different from other training apps?

Our app combines professional coaching with advanced performance tracking. Unlike other apps, we offer personalized feedback from real coaches and adapt your training plan based on your progress.

Can I try a session before committing?

Absolutely! We offer a free trial session for all new players. Contact us to schedule your introductory session with one of our coaches.
