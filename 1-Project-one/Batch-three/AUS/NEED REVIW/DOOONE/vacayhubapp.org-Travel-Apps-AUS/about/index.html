Our Story

Discover the passion and innovation behind Australia's favorite travel companion app

How VacayHub Began

VacayHub was founded in 2023 by a group of travel enthusiasts and tech experts who were frustrated with the complexity of planning Australian vacations. What started as a simple idea to streamline travel planning has grown into Australia's most trusted travel app.

Our journey began in a small Sydney office with just three team members. Today, we're proud to serve over 500,000 Australian travelers with a team of 50+ travel experts, developers, and customer service professionals.

We've been featured in major Australian travel publications and won multiple awards for our innovative approach to travel technology, including the 2024 Australian Travel Tech Innovation Award.
