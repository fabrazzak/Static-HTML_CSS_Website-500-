Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our travel services.

How does the price guarantee work?

If you find the same hotel or experience at a lower price within 24 hours of booking through VacayHub, we'll match the price and give you an additional 10% credit towards your next booking. Simply contact our customer service with proof of the lower price.

Can I modify my itinerary after booking?

Yes! Our Premium and Family plan members can make unlimited changes to their itineraries up to 48 hours before each booked activity or accommodation. Basic members can make up to 2 changes per booking for a small fee.

Do you offer services for international travel?

While we specialize in Australian travel, we do offer limited international trip planning services for our Premium and Family members. Our international services focus primarily on popular destinations for Australian travelers like New Zealand, Bali, and Southeast Asia.

How do I cancel a booking?

Cancellation policies vary by provider. You can view the specific cancellation policy for each booking in your account dashboard. Generally, most accommodations allow free cancellation up to 48-72 hours before check-in, while experiences may have stricter policies.

Is my payment information secure?

Absolutely. We use industry-standard encryption for all transactions and never store your full payment details on our servers. All payments are processed through secure payment gateways that comply with Australian financial regulations.
