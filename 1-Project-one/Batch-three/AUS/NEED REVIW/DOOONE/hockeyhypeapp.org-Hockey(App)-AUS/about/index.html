Our Hockey Journey

From a small idea in Sydney to Australia's premier hockey analytics platform, we're passionate about connecting the hockey community through technology.

Our Story

Founded by former professional hockey player James Wilson and tech entrepreneur Sarah Chen, Hockey Hype App began as a solution to a common problem - the lack of comprehensive, easy-to-use hockey analytics tools for Australian teams and fans.

What started as a side project quickly grew into a full-fledged platform after winning the Australian Sports Tech Accelerator program. Our team of hockey enthusiasts and data scientists worked tirelessly to create a product that would serve both casual fans and serious athletes.

Today, Hockey Hype is used by over 500 teams across Australia, from school competitions to the Australian Hockey League. Our mission remains the same: to make hockey more accessible, engaging, and data-driven for everyone involved in the sport.

Our Mission & Values

At Hockey Hype, we believe technology should enhance the hockey experience, not complicate it. Our platform is built on three core principles that guide everything we do.

Love for the Game

We're hockey fans first, tech company second. Every feature is designed with real hockey players and fans in mind.

Data-Driven Insights

We transform complex statistics into actionable insights that help players improve and fans engage deeper.

Community Focus

Hockey is about connection. We build tools that bring players, coaches, and fans together.
