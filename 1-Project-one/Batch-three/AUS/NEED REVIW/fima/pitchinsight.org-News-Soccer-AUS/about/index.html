Our Story in Soccer Journalism

Discover the passion and dedication behind Australia's most trusted soccer news platform

Our History

PitchInsight was founded in 2023 by a group of soccer enthusiasts and seasoned sports journalists who recognized a gap in quality soccer coverage with an Australian perspective. What began as a small blog covering local A-League matches has grown into Australia's premier soccer news destination.

Our breakthrough came during the 2024 Asian Cup, where our exclusive behind-the-scenes coverage and expert analysis set us apart from traditional sports media outlets. Since then, we've expanded our team to include former professional players, coaches, and data analysts who bring unique insights to our coverage.

Today, PitchInsight reaches over 500,000 soccer fans monthly across Australia and New Zealand, with our content regularly featured in mainstream sports media.

Our Mission & Values

Our Mission

To provide comprehensive, accurate, and engaging soccer coverage that informs and inspires Australian football fans while promoting the growth of the sport at all levels.

Our Values

Journalistic integrity, passion for soccer, commitment to Australian football development, and respect for the game's traditions while embracing innovation in sports media.

Our Vision

To be the definitive source for soccer news in Australia and a respected global voice in football journalism, while nurturing the next generation of Australian soccer talent.
