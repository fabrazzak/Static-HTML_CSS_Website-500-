Our Basketball Journey

From Sydney courts to digital platforms, discover how StayNova is revolutionizing basketball training in Australia and beyond.

Our Story

StayNova was founded in 2020 by former NBL players Michael Johnson and Sarah Williams, who saw a gap in accessible, high-quality basketball training resources for players at all levels.

What began as a small training camp in Sydney has grown into Australia's premier basketball training platform, serving over 50,000 players nationwide through our app and in-person programs.

Our name "StayNova" comes from our philosophy - helping players maintain (stay) their explosive (nova) potential throughout their basketball journey.

Our Approach

At StayNova, we believe basketball training should be accessible, personalized, and results-driven. Our methodology combines:

  • Professional coaching techniques adapted for all skill levels
  • Data-driven performance tracking
  • Position-specific skill development
  • Mental conditioning and game IQ training

Our app brings elite-level training to your local court, with programs that adapt to your progress and challenges.
