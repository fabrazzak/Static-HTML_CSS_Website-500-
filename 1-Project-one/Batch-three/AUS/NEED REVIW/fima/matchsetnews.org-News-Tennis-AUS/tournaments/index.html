Australian Tournament Highlights

Jan 20 - Feb 2, 2025

Australian Open 2025

Melbourne Park, Melbourne

The first Grand Slam of the year returns to Melbourne with record prize money and exciting new developments. This year features a new 15,000-seat stadium and extended night sessions to accommodate more fans.

Australian hopes rest on Alex de Minaur and Ajla Tomljanović, both coming off career-best seasons. The tournament will also see the return of several stars from injury, including Rafael Nadal and Naomi Osaka.

View Tournament Details
Jan 6 - Jan 12, 2025

United Cup

Sydney & Perth

The premier team event in tennis returns with 18 nations competing across two Australian cities. Australia's team, led by Alex de Minaur and Ajla Tomljanović, will aim to improve on last year's semifinal finish.

New for 2025: The group stage expands to include six more nations, and the final will be played in Sydney's Ken Rosewall Arena with enhanced fan experiences including player meet-and-greets and interactive tennis clinics.
