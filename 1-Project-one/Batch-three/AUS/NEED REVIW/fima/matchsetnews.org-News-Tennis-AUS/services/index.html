Frequently Asked Questions

How do I access the services after purchase?

After completing your purchase, you'll receive an email with login credentials to access our members area. All services are available through our website and mobile app (coming soon). You can access your content anytime, anywhere.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes, all our subscriptions are month-to-month with no long-term contracts. You can cancel anytime through your account settings, and you'll continue to have access until the end of your current billing period.

Do you offer discounts for annual subscriptions?

Currently we only offer monthly subscriptions, but we plan to introduce annual plans with significant discounts in the near future. Sign up for our newsletter to be notified when these become available.

Are your services suitable for beginners?

Absolutely! While some of our content is aimed at competitive players and coaches, we have plenty of resources perfect for beginners. Our training guides in particular start with fundamental techniques and progress at your pace.

Do you cover wheelchair tennis and other adaptations?

Yes, we're proud to cover all aspects of tennis including wheelchair tennis and other adaptive forms of the game. Our Australian Tennis Focus service includes regular coverage of Dylan Alcott and other stars in adaptive tennis.
