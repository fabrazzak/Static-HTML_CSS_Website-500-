Our Story in Tennis Journalism

MatchSet News was founded with a passion for tennis and a commitment to delivering the most comprehensive coverage of the sport we love, with a special focus on Australian tennis.

Get In Touch

Our History

MatchSet News began in 2023 when a group of tennis enthusiasts and professional journalists noticed a gap in quality tennis coverage focused on Australian players and tournaments. What started as a small blog covering local competitions has grown into Australia's premier tennis news platform.

Our first major breakthrough came when we secured exclusive interviews with several Australian Open players in 2024. This established our reputation for getting access that other outlets couldn't and asking the questions real fans wanted answered.

In 2025, we expanded our team to include former ATP/WTA players who provide unique insights into the game. Their analysis has become a hallmark of our coverage, setting us apart from other tennis media.

Today, we cover everything from Grand Slams to junior tournaments, always with an eye toward developing Australian talent and celebrating our country's rich tennis history.

Our Mission & Values

Our Mission

To provide comprehensive, accurate, and engaging tennis coverage that informs and inspires Australian tennis fans while promoting the growth of the sport at all levels.

Our Values

Journalistic integrity, passion for tennis, commitment to Australian tennis development, and respect for the game's traditions while embracing innovation.

Our Vision

To be the definitive source for tennis news in Australia and a respected global voice in tennis journalism, while nurturing the next generation of Australian tennis talent.
