Service Details

Advanced Performance Tracking

Our performance tracking system provides comprehensive analytics for every aspect of your hockey game. Designed specifically for Australian playing conditions and competitions, our system tracks:

  • Game statistics (goals, assists, shots, etc.)
  • Fitness metrics (speed, endurance, recovery)
  • Skill development over time
  • Comparison with similar players
  • Custom reports for coaches and selectors

Used by clubs across Australia including Sydney Ice Dogs and Melbourne Mustangs, our tracking system helps identify strengths and areas for improvement with precision.

Complete Team Management

Streamline your team administration with our all-in-one management platform, trusted by clubs across Australia from grassroots to elite levels.

  • Game scheduling with automatic notifications
  • Player availability tracking
  • Team communication hub
  • Document sharing (plays, strategies, videos)
  • Payment processing for fees and events
  • Integration with Hockey Australia systems

Our system reduces administrative workload by up to 60%, allowing coaches and managers to focus on what matters most - the game.

Custom Training Programs

Developed in collaboration with Hockey Australia certified coaches, our training programs are tailored to Australian players and conditions.

  • Position-specific training (forwards, defense, goalies)
  • Skill development (stick handling, shooting, skating)
  • Fitness programs (endurance, strength, agility)
  • Mental preparation and game strategy
  • Nutrition plans for Australian climate
  • Injury prevention and recovery

Our programs adapt to your progress and can be customized based on your team's playing style and competition requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Hockey Hype App suitable for both ice and field hockey?

Yes! We offer specialized features for both ice hockey and field hockey. During setup, you can select your preferred hockey type, and the app will customize the tracking metrics, training programs, and community features accordingly.

How does Hockey Hype handle data privacy and security?

We take data privacy very seriously. All data is stored securely on Australian servers compliant with Australian Privacy Principles. We never share your data with third parties without consent. You can read our detailed Privacy Policy for more information.

Can I use Hockey Hype App for my entire team?

Absolutely! Our Pro and Elite plans include team management features. The Elite plan is specifically designed for clubs and teams, offering bulk discounts for 10+ players. Many Australian clubs from the AIHL to local competitions use Hockey Hype for their entire team.

Do you offer discounts for schools and junior teams?

Yes, we offer special educational pricing for schools and junior teams across Australia. Contact our sales team at schools@hockeyhypeapp.org to learn about our 40% discount for educational institutions and our specialized junior hockey programs.

How does the free trial work?

Our 14-day free trial gives you full access to all features of the plan you choose. No credit card is required to start the trial. At the end of 14 days, you can choose to subscribe or your account will be paused. You can restart anytime.
