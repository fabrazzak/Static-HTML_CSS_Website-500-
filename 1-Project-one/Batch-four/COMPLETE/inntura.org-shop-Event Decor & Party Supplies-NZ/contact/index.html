Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions

How can I track my order?

Once your order has shipped, you'll receive a confirmation email with tracking information. You can also track your order by logging into your account on our website.

What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 30 days of delivery for unused items in their original condition. Please contact our customer service team to initiate a return. Custom or made-to-order items may not be eligible for return.

Do you offer international shipping?

Currently, we only ship within New Zealand. We're working on expanding our shipping options in the future.

How do I care for my handmade products?

Each product comes with specific care instructions. Generally, we recommend hand-washing ceramic and wood items, avoiding direct sunlight for textiles, and using mild cleaners. Check the product description or contact us for specific care advice.

How can I become a Nestivo artisan?

We're always excited to discover new artisans! Please email artisans@nestivo.org with photos of your work, your creative process, and information about your materials and techniques. Our team reviews submissions monthly.
