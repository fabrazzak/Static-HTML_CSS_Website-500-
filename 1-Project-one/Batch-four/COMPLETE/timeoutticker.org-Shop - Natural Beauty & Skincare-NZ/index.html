Frequently Asked Questions

Are your products suitable for sensitive skin?

Yes! All our products are formulated with sensitive skin in mind. We avoid common irritants like synthetic fragrances, parabens, and sulfates. However, we always recommend doing a patch test before full application, especially if you have known allergies.

How long does shipping take within New Zealand?

We typically process orders within 1-2 business days. Standard shipping takes 2-3 business days to reach urban areas, and 3-5 business days for rural locations. Express shipping options are also available at checkout.

Are your products vegan?

Most of our products are vegan, with the exception of those containing Manuka honey or beeswax. These ingredients are clearly labeled in the product description. All our products are cruelty-free.

Can I return products if they don't work for me?

Absolutely. We offer a 30-day satisfaction guarantee. If you're not completely happy with your purchase, you can return it for a full refund or exchange, even if the product has been opened and used.

How should I store my skincare products?

For best results, store products in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Some products may benefit from refrigeration, which will be noted on the packaging. Always keep lids tightly closed to preserve product integrity.
