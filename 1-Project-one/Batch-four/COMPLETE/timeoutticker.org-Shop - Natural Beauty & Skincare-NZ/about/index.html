Our Story

Founded in 2020 in the pristine landscapes of New Zealand, Timeout Ticker was born from a passion for natural beauty and a commitment to sustainable skincare. Our founder, Sarah Wilson, a botanist and skincare enthusiast, noticed a gap in the market for truly natural products that harnessed the power of New Zealand's unique flora.

What began as a small kitchen operation creating handmade balms for friends and family has grown into a beloved skincare brand trusted by thousands across New Zealand and beyond. We remain true to our roots, crafting small batches of each product to ensure quality and freshness.

At Timeout Ticker, we believe that skincare should be a moment of pause in your busy day - a timeout that nourishes both your skin and your spirit. Our products are designed to bring you back to nature, even in the midst of modern life.

Our Values

The principles that guide everything we do

Purity

We use only the purest natural ingredients, free from harsh chemicals, synthetic fragrances, and unnecessary additives.

Sustainability

From sourcing to packaging, we prioritize eco-friendly practices that protect New Zealand's precious environment.

Transparency

We believe you have the right to know exactly what you're putting on your skin. Full ingredient disclosure is our standard.

Efficacy

Natural doesn't mean ineffective. Our products are formulated to deliver visible results you can see and feel.

Cruelty-Free

We never test on animals and are proud to be certified by the Leaping Bunny program.

Community

We support local growers and producers, and give back to environmental causes that protect New Zealand's natural beauty.

Our Signature Ingredients

Harnessing the power of New Zealand's native plants

Manuka Honey

Renowned for its antibacterial and healing properties, our UMF 15+ Manuka honey soothes and repairs skin while providing natural hydration.

Kawakawa

This traditional Māori medicinal plant has anti-inflammatory properties that help calm irritated skin and reduce redness.

Harakeke Flax

The gel from New Zealand flax leaves forms a protective barrier on the skin, locking in moisture and supporting natural repair processes.

Kanuka Oil

Similar to tea tree oil but gentler, Kanuka oil has antimicrobial properties that help combat acne without drying out the skin.
