Our Story

From humble beginnings to becoming New Zealand's premier music gear destination - this is the Gadget Roar story.

Who We Are

Founded in 2020 by music enthusiasts, Gadget Roar began as a small passion project in Nelson, New Zealand. What started as a modest online store selling guitar accessories has grown into the country's most trusted source for premium music equipment.

Our founders, all professional musicians themselves, recognized a gap in the New Zealand market for a music store that combined expert knowledge with exceptional customer service. They set out to create a business that would serve musicians at every level - from beginners to touring professionals.

Today, with our headquarters in Wakefield and a growing team of music experts, we're proud to serve thousands of customers nationwide with the same passion and dedication that started it all.
