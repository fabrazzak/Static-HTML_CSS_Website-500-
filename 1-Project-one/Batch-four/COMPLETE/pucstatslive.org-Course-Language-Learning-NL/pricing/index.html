Pricing FAQs

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, and bank transfers. All payments are processed securely through our payment gateway.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. When you change plans, the new pricing will apply from your next billing cycle. Downgrades may result in loss of certain features.

Is there a free trial available?

We offer a 7-day free trial for our Standard and Premium plans. This gives you full access to all features so you can experience our platform before committing. No credit card is required for the trial.

What's your refund policy?

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee for all plans. If you're not satisfied with our service within the first 30 days, we'll give you a full refund, no questions asked.

Are there any discounts for students or groups?

Yes! We offer a 15% discount for students with valid ID. For groups of 5 or more, we provide special enterprise pricing. Contact us for more information about group discounts.
