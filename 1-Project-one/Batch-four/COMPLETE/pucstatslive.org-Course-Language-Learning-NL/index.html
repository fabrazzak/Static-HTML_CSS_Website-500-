Frequently Asked Questions

How much time should I dedicate to learning each week?

We recommend dedicating at least 3-5 hours per week to see consistent progress. This can be broken into shorter daily sessions for better retention. Our courses are designed to be flexible to fit your schedule.

Do I need any prior language learning experience?

No prior experience is needed for our beginner courses. We offer courses at all levels from complete beginner to advanced, with clear placement guidance to help you choose the right starting point.

What makes your courses different from other language apps?

Unlike many apps that focus only on vocabulary, our courses provide comprehensive language training including grammar, pronunciation, conversation practice, and cultural context, all guided by expert instructors.

Can I switch courses if I change my mind?

Yes, you can switch to a different language course within the first 14 days of your subscription at no additional cost. After that period, changes may be subject to certain conditions.

What technology do I need to participate?

You'll need a computer, tablet, or smartphone with internet access. For live sessions, a microphone is recommended, and a webcam is optional but helpful for interactive activities.
