Common Questions

How quickly can I expect a response to my inquiry?

We typically respond to all inquiries within 24-48 hours during our business hours. If you contact us on weekends or holidays, we'll get back to you on the next business day.

Do you offer in-person language classes?

Currently, all our language courses are offered online through our platform. This allows us to provide flexible learning options to students worldwide with access to our expert instructors.

Can I schedule a call with an instructor before enrolling?

Yes! We offer free 15-minute consultation calls with our instructors to help you choose the right course. Please mention this in your message, and we'll arrange a convenient time.

What payment methods do you accept for courses?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, and bank transfers. All payments are processed securely through our payment gateway.

Do you offer discounts for group enrollments?

Yes, we offer special group rates for organizations or groups of 5 or more students. Contact us with details about your group, and we'll provide customized pricing options.
