Who We Are

Founded in 2020, PucStatsLive began with a simple mission: to make language learning accessible, effective, and enjoyable for everyone. What started as a small team of language enthusiasts has grown into a thriving online learning platform serving thousands of students worldwide.

Our founders, experienced language educators themselves, recognized the limitations of traditional language learning methods and set out to create a better solution. Combining the latest in educational technology with proven teaching methodologies, we've developed a platform that delivers real results.

Today, we're proud to have helped over 10,000 students achieve their language learning goals, whether for travel, business, or personal enrichment.
