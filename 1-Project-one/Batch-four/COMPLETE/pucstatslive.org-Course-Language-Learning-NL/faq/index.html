Frequently Asked Questions

How much time should I dedicate to learning each week?

We recommend dedicating at least 3-5 hours per week to see consistent progress. This can be broken into shorter daily sessions for better retention. Our courses are designed to be flexible to fit your schedule.

For optimal results:

  • 30-45 minutes daily is ideal for language retention
  • Include a mix of activities (listening, speaking, reading, writing)
  • Participate in at least one live session per week
  • Review previous material regularly
Do I need any prior language learning experience?

No prior experience is needed for our beginner courses. We offer courses at all levels from complete beginner to advanced, with clear placement guidance to help you choose the right starting point.

Our beginner courses:

  • Start with the absolute basics
  • Assume no prior knowledge
  • Provide all necessary learning materials
  • Include pronunciation guides from day one

If you have some experience, we offer free placement tests to determine which course level is right for you.

What makes your courses different from other language apps?

Unlike many apps that focus only on vocabulary, our courses provide comprehensive language training including grammar, pronunciation, conversation practice, and cultural context, all guided by expert instructors.

Key differences:

  • Live instruction: Regular sessions with qualified teachers
  • Structured curriculum: Carefully sequenced learning path
  • Cultural immersion: Learn the culture behind the language
  • Personalized feedback: Get corrections from native speakers
  • Community: Practice with fellow learners
  • Comprehensive skills: Balanced focus on reading, writing, listening, and speaking
Can I switch courses if I change my mind?

Yes, you can switch to a different language course within the first 14 days of your subscription at no additional cost. After that period, changes may be subject to certain conditions.

Our flexible policy:

  • 14-day free course switch for all plans
  • After 14 days, switches allowed between terms
  • Premium plan subscribers get unlimited switches
  • Progress is saved if you return to a previous course

Contact our support team for assistance with course changes.

What technology do I need to participate?

You'll need a computer, tablet, or smartphone with internet access. For live sessions, a microphone is recommended, and a webcam is optional but helpful for interactive activities.

Technical requirements:

  • Device: Computer (Windows/Mac), tablet, or smartphone
  • Browser: Latest version of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge
  • Internet: Minimum 5Mbps download speed for video sessions
  • Audio: Microphone (built-in or external)
  • Optional: Webcam for interactive sessions

Our platform works on most modern devices without requiring additional software installation.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and in some countries, bank transfers. All payments are processed securely through our payment partners.

Payment options:

  • Visa, Mastercard, American Express
  • PayPal
  • Apple Pay (on supported devices)
  • Google Pay (on supported devices)
  • Bank transfer (available in select countries)

We don't store your payment information on our servers for added security.

How are the courses structured?

Our courses follow a structured weekly format with a mix of self-paced lessons and live sessions, designed to build your skills progressively.

Typical course structure:

  • Weekly modules: Each focuses on specific themes and grammar points
  • Video lessons: Pre-recorded explanations and demonstrations
  • Interactive exercises: Practice what you've learned
  • Live sessions: 2-3 times per week with instructors
  • Cultural insights: Learn about the culture behind the language
  • Progress checks: Regular assessments to track your improvement

Most courses run for 10-12 weeks but can be completed at your own pace.

What's the best way to practice outside of lessons?

Regular practice outside of lessons will significantly accelerate your progress. Here are our top recommendations for supplemental practice:

  • Language exchange: Partner with a native speaker through our community
  • Media immersion: Watch shows, listen to music, or read in your target language
  • Daily journaling: Write a few sentences each day
  • Flashcards: Use our built-in spaced repetition system
  • Speak aloud: Practice pronunciation daily
  • Teach others: Explain concepts to friends or family

Our platform includes additional practice resources and recommendations tailored to your level.

Can I access the courses on multiple devices?

Yes, you can access your courses on any device with internet access. Your progress syncs automatically across all devices.

Device access details:

  • Simultaneous login on up to 3 devices
  • Progress syncs in real-time
  • Download lessons for offline viewing (mobile app only)
  • Customize settings per device

For the best experience, we recommend using our mobile app for on-the-go learning and a computer for live sessions and writing exercises.

Is there a free trial available?

Yes, we offer a 7-day free trial for all new students. The trial gives you full access to one beginner course of your choice, including live sessions and all learning materials.

Trial includes:

  • Full access to one beginner course
  • Participation in live sessions
  • All learning materials and exercises
  • Community access
  • Progress tracking

No credit card is required to start the trial. You can upgrade to a paid plan at any time during or after the trial period.
