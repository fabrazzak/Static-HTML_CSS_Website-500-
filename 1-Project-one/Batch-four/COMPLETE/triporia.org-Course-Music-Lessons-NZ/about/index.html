How It All Began

Triporia Music was founded in 2020 by a group of professional musicians who shared a common vision: to make high-quality music education accessible to everyone in New Zealand. What started as a small studio in Nelson has grown into a thriving music school with both physical and online presence across the country.

Our founders noticed a gap in the market for music education that combined professional instruction with flexible learning options. They wanted to create a space where students of all ages and skill levels could learn in a supportive environment tailored to their individual needs.

Today, we're proud to have helped hundreds of students discover the joy of music, whether they're complete beginners taking their first lesson or advanced musicians refining their craft.
