Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our music school

What types of music lessons do you offer?

We offer a variety of music lessons including piano, guitar, vocals, music theory, and more. Our lessons are available for all skill levels from complete beginners to advanced musicians. You can choose between private one-on-one lessons or group classes.

Do you offer online lessons?

Yes! We offer both in-person and online lessons via Zoom or other video conferencing platforms. Our online lessons are just as effective as in-person instruction, with the added convenience of learning from home.

What's the best age to start music lessons?

We offer lessons for students of all ages! For young children (ages 4-6), we recommend our introductory music classes that focus on basic concepts through fun activities. Older children and adults can start with our beginner courses tailored to their specific instrument.

How do I choose the right instructor?

All our instructors are highly qualified professionals. We'll help match you with an instructor based on your musical goals, preferred learning style, and schedule availability. You can also read instructor bios on our website or request a trial lesson.
