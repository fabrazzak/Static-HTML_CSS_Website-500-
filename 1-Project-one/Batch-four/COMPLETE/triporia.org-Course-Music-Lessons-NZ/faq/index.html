What types of music lessons do you offer?

We offer a variety of music lessons including:

  • Piano (classical, jazz, pop)
  • Guitar (acoustic, electric, bass)
  • Vocals (classical, contemporary, musical theater)
  • Music theory and composition
  • Songwriting

Lessons are available for all skill levels from complete beginners to advanced musicians.

What's the best age to start music lessons?

We offer lessons for students of all ages:

  • 4-6 years: Introductory music classes focusing on basic concepts through fun activities
  • 7-12 years: Beginner courses tailored to younger students
  • Teens & Adults: Lessons adapted to your musical interests and goals

It's never too late to start learning music!

How do I enroll in lessons?

Enrolling is easy:

  1. Browse our course offerings
  2. Contact us to schedule a free consultation or trial lesson
  3. Complete the enrollment form
  4. Make your first payment
  5. Begin your musical journey!

You can also call us at +64 27 285 1651 or email info@triporia.org for assistance.

Do I need my own instrument?

Requirements vary by instrument:

  • Piano: We have pianos available for in-studio lessons. For home practice, we recommend having at least a keyboard with weighted keys.
  • Guitar: You'll need your own guitar for both lessons and practice.
  • Vocals: No equipment needed for beginners, though we may recommend a microphone for advanced students.

Our instructors can provide guidance on purchasing or renting instruments.

What are your lesson rates?

Our current rates (as of January 2025):

  • 30-minute private lesson: $49
  • 45-minute private lesson: $69
  • 60-minute private lesson: $89
  • Group classes (per session): $35

We offer discounted packages for multiple lessons purchased in advance. See our pricing page for complete details.

What is your refund policy?

Our refund policy is as follows:

  • Before first lesson: Full refund available
  • After first lesson: 50% refund if requested within 14 days of purchase
  • After second lesson: No refunds available

All refund requests must be made in writing to support@triporia.org.

Do you offer online lessons?

Yes! We offer both in-person and online lessons via Zoom, Skype, or other video conferencing platforms. Our online lessons include:

  • Real-time video instruction
  • Digital sheet music and resources
  • Recording of each lesson for review
  • Interactive learning tools

You'll need a stable internet connection, a device with camera/microphone, and your instrument.

Are online lessons as effective as in-person?

While in-person lessons allow for more immediate physical feedback, online lessons can be just as effective for many students because:

  • Our teachers are trained in online instruction techniques
  • High-quality audio/video ensures clear communication
  • Screen sharing allows for real-time notation viewing
  • Recorded lessons enable students to review material

Many students appreciate the convenience of learning from home without sacrificing quality.
