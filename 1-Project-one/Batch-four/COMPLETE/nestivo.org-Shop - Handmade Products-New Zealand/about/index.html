From Humble Beginnings

Nestivo was founded in 2020 by Sarah Thompson, a passionate advocate for traditional craftsmanship and sustainable living. What began as a small online marketplace featuring just five local artisans has grown into a thriving platform supporting over 50 talented makers across New Zealand.

Our journey started in a cozy Wellington studio, where Sarah first connected with ceramicist James Wilson. Recognizing the challenges artisans faced in reaching wider audiences while maintaining fair compensation, she envisioned a platform that would bridge this gap with integrity and care.

Today, we're proud to be the trusted destination for those seeking authentic, high-quality handmade products that tell a story and stand the test of time.

Our Mission

At Nestivo, we're on a mission to preserve traditional craftsmanship while supporting sustainable livelihoods for artisans. We believe that beautiful, well-made objects enrich our daily lives and connect us to the human stories behind them.

We carefully curate each piece in our collection, ensuring it meets our high standards for quality, sustainability, and ethical production. Every product you purchase directly supports the artisans who created it, helping to keep valuable skills alive for future generations.

By choosing Nestivo, you're not just buying a product - you're becoming part of a movement that values quality over quantity, people over profits, and tradition alongside innovation.

Our Values

These principles guide everything we do at Nestivo

Artisan First

We prioritize fair compensation and transparent relationships with our makers, ensuring they receive proper recognition and reward for their skills.

Sustainable Practices

From materials to packaging, we champion environmentally responsible choices that minimize our ecological footprint.

Quality Craftsmanship

We celebrate time-honored techniques and exceptional attention to detail that results in heirloom-quality pieces.

Authentic Connection

We build genuine relationships between makers and customers, sharing the stories behind each creation.

Local Focus

We're committed to supporting New Zealand's creative community and preserving our unique cultural heritage.

Timeless Design

We favor enduring beauty over fleeting trends, offering pieces that will be cherished for years to come.
