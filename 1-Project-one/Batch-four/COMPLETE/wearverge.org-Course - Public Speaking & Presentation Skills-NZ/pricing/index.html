Pricing FAQs

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), bKash/Nagad, PayPal, and bank transfers. All payments are processed securely through our gateway.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. New pricing applies from the next billing cycle. Some features may be removed if you downgrade.

Is there a free trial available?

Yes! We offer a 7-day free trial for the Pro Speaker and Elite Coach plans. Experience all features without a credit card.

What's your refund policy?

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee. If you're not satisfied, we’ll refund you—no questions asked.

Do you offer discounts for students or teams?

Yes! Students with valid ID get 15% off. Teams of 5+ can request a custom discount. Contact us to learn more.
