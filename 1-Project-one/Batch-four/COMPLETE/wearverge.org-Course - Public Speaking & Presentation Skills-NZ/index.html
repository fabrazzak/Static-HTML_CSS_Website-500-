Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to have prior public speaking experience?

No prior experience is required. Our courses start from the fundamentals and gradually build your confidence and skills through practice and expert guidance.

How long does it take to see improvement?

Most students begin to notice improvements in confidence and delivery within 3-4 weeks, especially with regular practice and participation in live sessions.

What kind of presentations will I learn to deliver?

You’ll learn to deliver professional speeches, business presentations, academic talks, and even impromptu speeches. The course adapts to your goals and industry needs.

Is feedback provided on my presentations?

Yes, our instructors provide personalized feedback on your recorded or live presentations to help you improve structure, tone, clarity, and body language.

Can I access the course materials anytime?

Absolutely. You’ll have 24/7 access to video lessons, practice exercises, and downloadable resources, so you can learn at your own pace.
