About Roomovo

Roomovo is a premier online platform dedicated to fashion education and personal style development. Founded in 2020, we've helped thousands of students worldwide discover their unique fashion voice and build confidence through their wardrobe choices.

Our mission is to make high-quality fashion education accessible to everyone, regardless of their background or location. We believe that style is a powerful form of self-expression and everyone deserves to feel confident in their appearance.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I access the courses after purchasing?

After completing your purchase, you'll receive an email with login credentials to our learning platform. All your courses will be available immediately in your dashboard.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time through your account settings. Your access will continue until the end of your current billing period.

Are there any prerequisites for the courses?

No prerequisites are required for our beginner courses. For advanced courses, we recommend completing the foundational courses first or having equivalent experience.

How long do I have access to the course materials?

You'll have access to all purchased course materials for as long as you maintain an active subscription. If you cancel, you'll retain access to any courses you've completed.

Do you offer certificates upon completion?

Yes, all courses include a certificate of completion that you can download once you've finished all modules and passed the final assessment.
