Our Story

Roomovo was founded in 2020 by a team of fashion educators and industry professionals who saw a need for accessible, high-quality fashion education outside of traditional fashion schools.

What began as a small workshop series in Nelson, New Zealand has grown into an international online platform serving thousands of students worldwide. Our unique approach combines practical styling techniques with foundational fashion theory, creating a comprehensive learning experience.

We believe that fashion is a powerful form of self-expression that should be available to everyone, regardless of their background or budget. Our courses are designed to be inclusive, practical, and transformative.

Our Mission & Values

At Roomovo, we're committed to transforming the way people think about fashion education. Our core values guide everything we do.

Accessible Education

We believe quality fashion education should be available to everyone, regardless of location or financial means.

Personal Expression

Style is personal. We teach techniques to help students develop their unique voice, not follow trends blindly.

Sustainable Practices

We promote responsible fashion choices that reduce environmental impact without sacrificing style.

Inclusive Community

Our community welcomes all ages, sizes, genders, and backgrounds. Diversity makes fashion richer.
