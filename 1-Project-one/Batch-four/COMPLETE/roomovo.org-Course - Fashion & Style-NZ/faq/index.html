Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services and offerings

General Questions

What is Roomovo Style?

Roomovo Style is a fashion education platform dedicated to helping individuals develop their personal style through expert-led courses, styling services, and educational content. We combine practical fashion knowledge with personalized guidance to help you build confidence in your wardrobe choices.

Who are your courses for?

Our courses are designed for anyone interested in fashion and personal style, from complete beginners to those with some fashion knowledge looking to refine their skills. Whether you're looking to revamp your wardrobe, start a career in styling, or simply feel more confident in your clothing choices, we have resources to help you.

Do I need any prior experience?

No prior experience is necessary for most of our courses. We offer beginner-friendly content that builds from fundamental concepts. For more advanced courses, we clearly indicate any prerequisites in the course descriptions.

Where are you based?

Our headquarters are in Wakefield, New Zealand, but we serve clients worldwide through our online platform. All our courses and services are available digitally, allowing you to access them from anywhere with an internet connection.

Courses & Services

How do your online courses work?

Our online courses are self-paced and include video lessons, downloadable resources, practical assignments, and community discussion forums. Once enrolled, you'll have lifetime access to the course materials, allowing you to learn at your own convenience and revisit content whenever you need.

What's included in a personal styling session?

Our personal styling sessions include a comprehensive style assessment, body type analysis, color consultation, and customized wardrobe recommendations. Sessions can be conducted virtually or in-person (in select locations) and include follow-up support to help implement your new style direction.

Can I get certified through your courses?

Yes, we offer certification for our professional-level courses upon successful completion of all coursework and assessments. Our certificates are recognized in the fashion industry and can help establish your credentials as a personal stylist or fashion consultant.

Do you offer corporate styling services?

Yes, we provide corporate styling services including team workshops, executive image consulting, and uniform design consultation. These services help businesses present a cohesive, professional image while ensuring employee comfort and confidence.

Payments & Memberships

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, and bank transfers. For in-person services in New Zealand, we also accept EFTPOS payments.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes, we offer flexible payment plans for most of our courses and services. Payment plans typically split the total cost into 3-6 monthly installments with no additional fees. You'll find payment plan options at checkout.

What's your refund policy?

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee for all online courses if you're not satisfied with your purchase. For styling services, cancellations made at least 48 hours in advance receive a full refund. Please see our full Terms of Service for detailed policy information.

What are the benefits of membership?

Our premium membership includes unlimited access to all courses, exclusive monthly workshops, personalized style feedback, discounts on services, and entry to our private community. Membership is ideal for those serious about transforming their style or pursuing a career in fashion.

Technical Support

How do I access my purchased courses?

After purchase, you'll receive an email with login instructions for our learning platform. All your courses will be available in your account dashboard. You can access them anytime by logging in to our website from any device.

What if I have technical issues?

Our support team is available to help with any technical issues. Please email support@roomovo.org with details of your problem, including screenshots if possible. Most issues are resolved within 24 hours.

Can I download course materials?

Most course materials (worksheets, guides, etc.) are available for download. Video lessons are streamed but can be accessed offline through our mobile app. We're constantly working to improve our offline access options.

Do you have a mobile app?

Yes, our mobile app (available for iOS and Android) allows you to take courses on the go, download lessons for offline viewing, and participate in community discussions. The app includes all the features of our web platform optimized for mobile devices.

Still have questions?

We're here to help! If you can't find the answer you're looking for, our support team is ready to assist you.
