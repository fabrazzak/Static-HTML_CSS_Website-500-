Frequently Asked Questions

How do I enroll in a course?

You can enroll in our courses by filling out the contact form on this page or visiting our Courses section. Select the course you're interested in and click "Enroll Now." Our team will contact you with all the details and next steps.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, and bank transfers for our courses. Payment plans are available for some of our premium courses.

Can I get a refund if I'm not satisfied with a course?

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee for all our courses. If you're not satisfied with your purchase, contact our support team within 14 days of enrollment for a full refund.

Do you offer in-person styling consultations?

Yes, we offer limited in-person consultations at our Wakefield location. Please contact us to check availability and schedule an appointment. Virtual consultations are also available.
