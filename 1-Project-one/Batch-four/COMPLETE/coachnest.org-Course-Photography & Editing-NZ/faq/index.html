Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our photography and editing courses

General Questions

What is CoachNest and what do you offer?

CoachNest is an online education platform specializing in photography and photo editing courses. We offer structured learning programs taught by industry professionals, covering everything from basic camera operation to advanced editing techniques. Our courses are designed to help photographers of all levels improve their skills.

Who are your courses designed for?

Our courses cater to a wide range of learners:

  • Beginners: Those just starting with photography or editing
  • Enthusiasts: Hobbyists looking to improve their skills
  • Aspiring Professionals: Individuals considering photography as a career
  • Professionals: Working photographers wanting to refine specific skills

Each course clearly indicates its target skill level in the description.

How is CoachNest different from free tutorials on YouTube?

While free tutorials can be helpful, CoachNest offers several advantages:

  • Structured Learning: Our courses follow a carefully designed curriculum that builds skills progressively
  • Expert Instruction: Learn from professional photographers with years of experience
  • Personalized Feedback: Get critiques on your work (depending on your subscription level)
  • Community: Connect with other students for support and inspiration
  • Comprehensive Content: Our courses cover topics in depth with downloadable resources

Enrollment & Access

How do I enroll in a course?

Enrolling is simple:

  1. Browse our courses page to find the right program for you
  2. Select your preferred pricing plan (Basic, Standard, or Premium)
  3. Complete the enrollment form with your details
  4. You'll receive immediate access to the course materials after payment confirmation
Do I need any special software to access the courses?

You'll need:

  • A modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge)
  • Internet connection (minimum 5Mbps recommended for video)
  • For editing courses: Adobe Lightroom or Photoshop (some courses may recommend alternatives)
  • Our platform works on both desktop and mobile devices
How long do I have access to the courses?

Access duration depends on your subscription plan:

  • Basic: Access to enrolled courses for the subscription duration
  • Standard: Access to enrolled courses for the subscription duration + 3 months after cancellation
  • Premium: Lifetime access to all enrolled courses, even after cancellation

You can download course materials for offline reference where permitted.

Can I download the course videos?

Most course videos are available for streaming only to protect our instructors' intellectual property. However, many courses include downloadable resources like:

  • Course notes and cheat sheets
  • Practice images and project files
  • Presets and templates
  • Assignment briefs

Premium members get access to select downloadable video content.

Technical Requirements

What equipment do I need for the photography courses?

Requirements vary by course:

  • DSLR/Mirrorless Courses: Any interchangeable lens camera with manual controls
  • Mobile Photography: A smartphone with a decent camera (most modern phones will work)
  • Lighting Courses: Any light source (natural light or basic artificial light)
  • General: A computer or tablet for viewing lessons (smartphones work but larger screens are better)

Each course lists specific requirements in its description.

What software is required for the editing courses?

Our editing courses primarily use Adobe software:

  • Lightroom Courses: Adobe Lightroom Classic or Lightroom CC
  • Photoshop Courses: Adobe Photoshop (latest version recommended)
  • Mobile Editing: Free or paid apps like Lightroom Mobile, Snapseed, or VSCO

Some courses cover alternative software like Capture One or Affinity Photo. Check individual course requirements for details.

What if I don't have Adobe Creative Cloud?

While we recommend Adobe products for the best learning experience, we offer several alternatives:

  • Adobe offers free 7-day trials of their software
  • Some lessons include workarounds for free alternatives like GIMP or Darktable
  • Our mobile photography courses use free or low-cost apps
  • We occasionally offer courses on non-Adobe software like Capture One

You can still learn the fundamental concepts without Adobe software, but hands-on practice will be limited.

Pricing & Payments

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major payment methods:

  • Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express)
  • PayPal
  • Apple Pay
  • Google Pay
  • Bank Transfers (for annual plans)

All payments are processed securely through our payment partners.

Can I get a refund if I'm not satisfied?

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee for all our courses. If you're not satisfied with your purchase:

  1. Contact our support team within 30 days of enrollment
  2. Let us know what didn't meet your expectations
  3. We'll process your refund within 5 business days

Refunds may take 7-10 days to appear in your account depending on your payment method.

Do you offer any discounts or scholarships?

We occasionally offer promotions and discounts:

  • Seasonal Sales: Black Friday, New Year, etc.
  • Bundle Discounts: When purchasing multiple courses
  • Annual Subscription: Save 20% compared to monthly
  • Student Discount: 15% off with valid student ID

We also offer a limited number of need-based scholarships each quarter. Contact our support team for details.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my subscription plan?

Yes, you can change your plan at any time:

  • Upgrading: You'll get immediate access to the higher tier benefits. The price difference will be prorated.
  • Downgrading: Changes take effect at your next billing cycle. You'll maintain current benefits until then.

To change your plan, go to your account settings or contact our support team.

Learning Experience

Are the courses self-paced or scheduled?

All our courses are self-paced, allowing you to learn at your convenience. However, we offer several structured elements:

  • Recommended Schedules: Suggested timelines for completing courses
  • Live Sessions: Regular Q&A and coaching calls (depending on your plan)
  • Community Challenges: Optional group activities with deadlines
  • Office Hours: Scheduled times when instructors are available for questions

You can participate in as much or as little of the scheduled content as you like.

How much time should I dedicate to each course?

Time commitment varies by course complexity:

  • Beginner Courses: 3-5 hours per week for 4-6 weeks
  • Intermediate Courses: 5-8 hours per week for 6-8 weeks
  • Advanced Courses: 8-12 hours per week for 8-12 weeks
  • Workshops: Intensive 2-4 week programs requiring 15-20 hours/week

Each course lists estimated time requirements in its description. Remember, more practice leads to better results!

Can I get feedback on my work?

Feedback options depend on your subscription level:

  • Basic: Community feedback from fellow students
  • Standard: Monthly instructor critiques on selected work
  • Premium: Weekly personalized feedback from instructors

All students can participate in our community forums where many members voluntarily share constructive feedback.

Do you offer certificates upon completion?

Yes! All our courses offer certificates of completion when you:

  1. Finish all course modules
  2. Complete the required assignments (if any)
  3. Pass the final assessment (for some courses)

Certificates are digital and can be shared on LinkedIn or your portfolio. While not accredited, they demonstrate your commitment to learning.
