Frequently Asked Questions

How do I enroll in a photography course?

You can enroll directly through our website by visiting the Courses page and selecting your desired course. If you need assistance, feel free to contact us using the form above or call us during business hours.

What equipment do I need for the courses?

Most of our beginner courses only require a smartphone or basic camera. For intermediate and advanced courses, we recommend a DSLR or mirrorless camera, but we provide specific requirements for each course on its description page.

Can I get a refund if I'm not satisfied with a course?

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee for all our courses. If you're not satisfied with your purchase, contact our support team within 14 days of enrollment for a full refund.

Do you offer one-on-one coaching sessions?

Yes, we offer private coaching sessions with our instructors. Please contact us to discuss availability and pricing for personalized instruction.

How can I become an instructor at CoachNest?

We're always looking for talented photographers to join our team. Please send us your portfolio and teaching experience through our contact form, and we'll get back to you about potential opportunities.
