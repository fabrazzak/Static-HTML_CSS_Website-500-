Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need expensive equipment to take these courses?

Not at all! While having professional gear can be beneficial, our courses are designed to help you get the most out of whatever equipment you have. Many of our students start with just their smartphone or an entry-level DSLR.

How much time should I dedicate to the courses each week?

We recommend setting aside 3-5 hours per week to go through the material and complete the assignments. However, our courses are self-paced, so you can adjust this based on your schedule and learning style.

What software will I need for the editing courses?

For our Lightroom course, you'll need Adobe Lightroom Classic or Lightroom CC. For Photoshop courses, Adobe Photoshop is required. We provide free alternatives and workarounds for students who don't have access to these programs.

Can I access the courses on mobile devices?

Yes! Our platform is fully responsive and works on smartphones and tablets. However, some editing exercises may be easier to complete on a computer with a larger screen.

What if I need help during the course?

All students have access to our community forum where you can ask questions and get feedback from both instructors and fellow students. Higher-tier plans include direct access to instructors for personalized help.
