Our Story & Mission

Discover the passion behind SkillFoundry and meet the team dedicated to helping photographers across Australia unlock their creative potential.

Who We Are

Founded in 2020 by professional photographers, SkillFoundry has grown from a small passion project into Australia's leading online photography education platform. What began as a series of local workshops has transformed into a comprehensive digital learning experience accessible to photographers nationwide.

Our team of instructors brings together decades of combined experience across commercial, portrait, landscape, and editorial photography. We're united by a shared belief that great photography education should be accessible, practical, and inspiring.

Our Journey

How SkillFoundry grew from an idea to a leading education platform

2020

Founded in Perth

David and Sophia launched SkillFoundry as a series of in-person photography workshops in Western Australia.

2021

Went Digital

Transitioned to online courses during the pandemic, reaching students across Australia with our first three digital courses.

2022

Expanded Course Catalog

Grew our team of instructors and expanded to offer 10+ specialized photography courses.

2023

Community Milestone

Reached 3,000 active students and launched our community features including live Q&A sessions.

2024

Premium Offerings

Introduced premium mentorship programs and 1-on-1 portfolio reviews for advanced students.
