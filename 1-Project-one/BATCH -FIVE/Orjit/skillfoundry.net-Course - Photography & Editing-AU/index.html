Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our courses

Do I need any prior photography experience?

No prior experience is necessary for our beginner courses. We start with the fundamentals and gradually build up to more advanced techniques. Our courses are designed to accommodate all skill levels, with clear explanations and practical exercises to help you progress at your own pace.

What equipment do I need for the courses?

For photography courses, you'll need a DSLR or mirrorless camera that allows manual control. For editing courses, you'll need a computer capable of running Adobe Lightroom or Photoshop (depending on the course). Specific requirements are listed with each course description.

How long do I have access to the courses?

With our monthly subscription plans, you'll have access to all course materials as long as your subscription is active. If you cancel, you'll lose access but can resubscribe at any time to regain access to all your progress and materials.

Can I download the course materials?

Yes, most of our courses include downloadable resources like cheat sheets, presets, and practice files that you can keep forever. Video lessons are streamed but can be accessed anytime during your subscription period.

Is there a money-back guarantee?

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee on all subscriptions. If you're not satisfied with your courses within the first two weeks, simply contact us for a full refund, no questions asked.
