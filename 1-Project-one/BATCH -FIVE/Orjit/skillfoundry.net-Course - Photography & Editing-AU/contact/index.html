Common Questions

Quick answers to our most frequently asked questions

How quickly can I expect a response to my inquiry?

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24 hours during our business hours (Monday-Friday, 9am-5pm AWST). For technical support issues, we typically respond even faster, usually within 4-6 hours during business days.

Do you offer in-person consultations?

Yes, we offer limited in-person consultations at our Fremantle office by appointment. Please contact us to schedule a time that works for you. For students outside Western Australia, we're happy to arrange video calls.

What's the best way to get technical support?

For fastest technical support, please email support@skillfoundry.net with details of your issue, including screenshots if possible. You can also call our support line during business hours for immediate assistance with urgent issues.

Can I visit your office without an appointment?

While we welcome visitors, we recommend scheduling an appointment to ensure the right team member is available to assist you. Our office is primarily a workspace for our team, not a public reception area.
