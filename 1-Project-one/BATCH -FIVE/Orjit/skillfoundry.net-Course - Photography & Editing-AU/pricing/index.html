Pricing FAQs

Answers to common questions about our pricing and plans

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, and bank transfers for Australian customers. All payments are processed securely through our payment processor.

Can I cancel or change my plan anytime?

Yes, you can cancel or change your plan at any time through your account settings. If you cancel, you'll retain access until the end of your current billing period. Downgrading your plan may affect access to certain features.

Do you offer discounts for students or groups?

We offer a 20% discount for students with valid student ID. For groups of 5 or more, we provide special pricing - please contact our sales team for group rates.

Is there an annual payment option?

Yes! We offer annual billing with a 15% discount off the monthly price. For example, the Standard plan is $59/month or $600/year (equivalent to $50/month).

What's included in the free trial?

Our 7-day free trial gives you full access to all Basic plan features, including 3 beginner courses and community access. No credit card is required to start your trial.
