Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our public speaking courses

Who are these courses suitable for?

Our courses are designed for anyone looking to improve their public speaking skills, from complete beginners to experienced speakers wanting to refine their technique. We have programs tailored for students, professionals, executives, and anyone preparing for important presentations or speeches.

How long does each course last?

Most of our core courses run for 6-8 weeks, with weekly 2-hour sessions. We also offer intensive weekend workshops and ongoing coaching programs. The duration depends on the specific course and your chosen learning pace.

What if I miss a session?

All sessions are recorded and available for students to review. For private coaching sessions, we offer make-up sessions when scheduled in advance. Group sessions can't be rescheduled but you'll have access to all materials.

Do you offer in-person and online options?

Yes! We offer both in-person classes at our Fremantle training center and live online sessions via Zoom. Some courses are hybrid, allowing you to choose your preferred format for each session.

What's your cancellation policy?

You can cancel your enrollment up to 7 days before the course starts for a full refund. After that, we offer partial refunds or credit toward future courses. Private coaching sessions require 24-hour notice for cancellation without charge.
