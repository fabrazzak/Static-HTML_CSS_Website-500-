Frequently Asked Questions

Who are these courses designed for?

Our courses are designed for professionals at all levels who want to improve their public speaking and presentation skills. Whether you're a beginner who gets nervous speaking in front of groups or an experienced speaker looking to refine your skills, we have courses tailored to your needs.

How long do I have access to the course materials?

Access duration depends on your subscription plan. Basic plan members have access while their subscription is active. Standard and Premium plan members get extended access even if they pause their subscription. All plans include downloadable resources you can keep forever.

What's the difference between the live and self-paced options?

Our self-paced option gives you access to all course materials to complete on your own schedule. The live option includes scheduled sessions with instructors and cohort-based learning. Both options include feedback opportunities, but live sessions provide more immediate, personalized guidance.

Can I get a refund if I'm not satisfied?

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee for all our courses. If you're not completely satisfied with your experience within the first two weeks, we'll refund your payment in full, no questions asked.
