Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions

Who are Hostari's courses designed for?

Our courses are designed for professionals at all levels who want to improve their public speaking and presentation skills. Whether you're:

  • A beginner who gets nervous speaking in front of groups
  • An experienced speaker looking to refine your skills
  • A business professional needing to present more effectively
  • An executive preparing for high-stakes presentations
  • An entrepreneur who needs to pitch ideas confidently

We have courses tailored to your specific needs and experience level.

What's the difference between your live and self-paced courses?

We offer both formats to accommodate different learning preferences and schedules:

Live Courses:

  • Scheduled sessions with instructors and cohort-based learning
  • Real-time feedback and Q&A opportunities
  • More personalized, immediate guidance
  • Structured schedule with deadlines

Self-Paced Courses:

  • Complete course materials on your own schedule
  • Access to all video lessons and resources
  • Feedback provided within 48 hours
  • More flexibility for busy professionals

Both options include access to our online community and opportunities for feedback, but live courses offer more direct interaction with instructors.

How do I enroll in a course?

Enrolling in a Hostari course is simple:

  1. Browse our course offerings and select the one that fits your needs
  2. Click the "Enroll Now" button on the course page
  3. Create an account or log in if you already have one
  4. Complete the payment process
  5. You'll receive immediate access to the course materials (for self-paced) or details about your first session (for live courses)

If you need help choosing the right course, our advisors can guide you based on your goals and experience level.

What payment options do you accept?

We accept all major payment methods to make enrollment convenient:

  • Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express)
  • PayPal
  • Bank Transfers (for corporate enrollments)
  • Afterpay (for installment payments)

For corporate training programs or group enrollments, we also accept purchase orders. Please contact us for corporate payment arrangements.

What is your refund policy?

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee for all our courses. If you're not completely satisfied with your experience within the first two weeks, we'll refund your payment in full, no questions asked.

After the 14-day period, we offer partial refunds on a case-by-case basis. To request a refund:

  1. Email support@hostari.org with your request
  2. Include your enrollment details and reason for requesting a refund
  3. Our team will review your request and respond within 2 business days

Please note: Refunds may take 5-10 business days to process depending on your payment method.

What are the technical requirements for online courses?

To participate in our online courses, you'll need:

  • Device: Computer, tablet, or smartphone with internet access
  • Browser: Latest version of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge
  • Internet Speed: Minimum 5Mbps download/1Mbps upload (10Mbps recommended for HD video)
  • Software: Zoom (for live sessions), PDF reader for course materials
  • Optional: Webcam and microphone for interactive exercises (most built-in ones work fine)

Our platform is mobile-friendly, but we recommend using a computer for the best experience, especially when submitting assignments or receiving feedback.

How long do I have access to course materials?

Access duration depends on your subscription plan:

  • Basic Plan: Access while your subscription is active
  • Standard Plan: 6 months access after course completion
  • Premium Plan: 12 months access after course completion

All plans include downloadable resources (worksheets, templates, etc.) that you can keep forever. Live course recordings are available for 3 months after the course ends.

You can extend your access at any time by upgrading your plan or purchasing an access extension.

Can I request a specific instructor?

Yes! While we can't guarantee instructor availability for all courses, we do our best to accommodate instructor preferences. Here's how it works:

  1. Check our instructor profiles to identify who you'd like to work with
  2. When enrolling, note your instructor preference in the comments field
  3. For private coaching, you can directly select your preferred instructor

If your preferred instructor isn't available for your desired course date, we can:

  • Notify you when they have availability
  • Recommend similar instructors who match your needs
  • Arrange a one-time session with your preferred instructor

Do you offer corporate training programs?

Absolutely! We work with organizations of all sizes to develop customized communication training programs. Our corporate solutions include:

  • Team Workshops: On-site or virtual training for groups
  • Executive Coaching: One-on-one sessions for leadership teams
  • Company-Wide Programs: Scalable training for entire organizations
  • Custom Courses: Tailored content for your industry and needs

Corporate programs include:

  • Needs assessment and goal setting
  • Customized curriculum development
  • Progress tracking and reporting
  • Ongoing support and reinforcement

Contact our corporate team to discuss your organization's needs and receive a customized proposal.

How should I prepare for my first session?

Here's how to get ready for your Hostari experience:

Before Your First Session:

  • Complete any pre-course materials sent to you
  • Test your technology (microphone, camera, internet connection)
  • Identify a quiet space where you can participate without interruptions
  • Have a notebook or digital document ready for notes
  • Think about your specific goals for the course

For Your First Session:

  • Arrive 5 minutes early to get settled
  • Have water nearby (speaking exercises can be thirsty work!)
  • Wear comfortable clothing that makes you feel confident
  • Come with an open mind and willingness to participate

Remember, everyone in your course is there to improve, so there's no need to be nervous. Our instructors create a supportive, non-judgmental environment.
