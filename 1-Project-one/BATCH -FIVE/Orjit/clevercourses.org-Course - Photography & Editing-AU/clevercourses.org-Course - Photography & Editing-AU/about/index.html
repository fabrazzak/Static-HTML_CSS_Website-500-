Our Story

Founded in 2020 in Fremantle, Western Australia, CleverCourses began with a simple mission: to make professional photography education accessible to everyone. What started as a small workshop series has grown into Australia's premier online platform for photography and photo editing education.

Our founders, a group of professional photographers and educators, recognized that traditional photography education was often expensive, geographically limited, and didn't always focus on practical, real-world skills. They set out to create an alternative that would be affordable, flexible, and results-driven.

Today, CleverCourses serves thousands of students across Australia and beyond, with a growing library of courses taught by some of the country's most respected photography professionals.

Our Approach

At CleverCourses, we believe that great photography education should be:

  • Practical: Focused on skills you can use immediately in your photography
  • Accessible: Available to anyone with an internet connection, regardless of location
  • Flexible: Learn at your own pace, on your schedule
  • Community-driven: Connect with instructors and fellow students

We're proud to have helped thousands of students improve their photography skills, whether they're complete beginners looking to understand their cameras or experienced photographers wanting to refine their craft.

Our Core Values

Quality Education
