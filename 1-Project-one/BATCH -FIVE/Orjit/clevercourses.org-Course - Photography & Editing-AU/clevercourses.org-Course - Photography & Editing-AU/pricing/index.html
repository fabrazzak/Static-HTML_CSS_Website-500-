Pricing FAQs

Can I switch plans later?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. When you upgrade, you'll immediately gain access to the additional features. Downgrades will take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

Is there a free trial available?

We offer a 7-day free trial for our Basic plan. This gives you access to one beginner course so you can experience our teaching style and platform before committing.

Do you offer annual billing discounts?

Yes! If you choose annual billing, you'll save 15% compared to monthly payments. This applies to all three plans.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, and for Australian customers, we also accept direct bank transfers.

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. There are no long-term contracts, and you can cancel your subscription at any time. Your access will continue until the end of your current billing period.
