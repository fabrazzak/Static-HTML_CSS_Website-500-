Common Questions

What's the best way to contact CleverCourses?

For general inquiries, emailing info@clevercourses.org is usually the fastest way to get a response. For urgent matters during business hours, calling our office phone is recommended.

How long does it take to get a response?

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24 hours during business days. During peak times, it may take up to 48 hours. Technical support requests are prioritized and typically answered within 12 hours.

Do you offer in-person consultations?

Yes, we offer in-person consultations at our Fremantle office by appointment only. Please contact us to schedule a meeting with one of our course advisors.

Can I visit your office without an appointment?

While we welcome visitors, we strongly recommend scheduling an appointment to ensure the appropriate staff member is available to assist you with your specific needs.
