Frequently Asked Questions

What areas do you service?

We primarily service the Fremantle area and surrounding suburbs within a 50km radius. Delivery fees vary based on distance from our warehouse. Contact us with your specific location for an accurate quote.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking at least 4-6 weeks in advance for standard events, and 3-6 months for weddings during peak season (September to April). Last-minute bookings March be available depending on our inventory - please call to check availability.

Can I customize the decor packages?

Absolutely! All our packages can be customized to match your specific needs and preferences. Our design team can work with you to create a unique look for your event. Customization options March affect pricing and lead time.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancellations made more than 14 days before the event will receive a full refund. Cancellations between 7-14 days before the event will receive a 50% refund. Cancellations less than 7 days before the event are non-refundable but March be eligible for rescheduling based on availability.

Do you offer setup services?

Yes, all our packages include basic setup services. For more complex installations or full-service setup, we offer additional setup packages. Our team will ensure everything is perfectly arranged before your event begins.

Need Custom Decor Solutions?

Our design team can create bespoke decor packages tailored to your specific event needs and style preferences. Contact us today to discuss your vision.
