Our Story

Voynest was born in 2018 from a deep passion for holistic wellness and a desire to create skincare products that work in harmony with nature. Our founder, Sophia Chen, struggled for years with sensitive skin and frustration over the prevalence of harmful chemicals in mainstream beauty products.

After years of research and experimentation with natural ingredients in her home kitchen, Sophia developed a line of products that transformed her skin without compromising on her values. What began as a small batch operation in her apartment has now grown into Voynest – a brand trusted by thousands for its effective, pure, and sustainable approach to skincare.

Today, we continue to handcraft all our products in small batches in our studio in Portland, Oregon, ensuring the highest quality and freshness for our customers.

Our Process

1

Research & Sourcing

We meticulously research and select the highest quality ingredients from organic farmers and ethical suppliers around the world. Each ingredient is chosen for its specific skin benefits and compatibility with our formulations.

2

Small-Batch Formulation

Our products are handcrafted in small batches to ensure freshness and potency. This approach allows us to maintain strict quality control and minimize waste in our production process.

3

Rigorous Testing

Every formulation undergoes extensive stability and efficacy testing. We never test on animals, instead relying on ethical human testing methods and scientific research to validate our product performance.

4

Sustainable Packaging

We package our products in recyclable glass, biodegradable materials, and FSC-certified paper to minimize environmental impact. Our shipping materials are plastic-free and made from recycled content.

5

Direct to You

We ship directly from our studio to your doorstep, ensuring the freshest products and eliminating unnecessary middlemen. This allows us to offer premium quality at fair prices while maintaining our ethical standards.
