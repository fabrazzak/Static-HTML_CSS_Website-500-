Frequently Asked Questions

What are your shipping options and delivery times?

We offer standard shipping (3-5 business days) and express shipping (1-2 business days) within the United States. International shipping typically takes 7-14 business days depending on the destination. All orders are processed within 1-2 business days.

What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 30 days of purchase for unopened and unused products. Simply contact our customer service team to initiate a return. Please note that shipping costs are non-refundable, and return shipping is the responsibility of the customer.

Are your products cruelty-free and vegan?

Yes! All Voynest products are 100% cruelty-free and never tested on animals. Most of our products are vegan, with the exception of a few that contain responsibly sourced beeswax or honey. These are clearly marked on the product pages.

Do you offer wholesale or bulk purchasing?

Yes, we work with select retailers and spas. Please email us at wholesale@voynest.com with your business details, and our wholesale team will get back to you with pricing and terms.

How can I track my order?

Once your order ships, you'll receive a confirmation email with tracking information. You can also track your order by logging into your account on our website. If you have any issues, our customer service team is happy to help.
