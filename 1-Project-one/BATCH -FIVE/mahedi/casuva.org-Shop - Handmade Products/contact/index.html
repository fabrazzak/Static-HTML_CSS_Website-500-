Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping usually take?

For domestic orders within Germany, shipping typically takes 2-3 business days. For European Union countries, please allow 5-7 business days. For international orders, shipping can take 10-15 business days depending on customs processing times.

Do you offer custom orders?

Yes! We love creating custom pieces. Please contact us with your ideas and requirements, and our artisans will work with you to create something unique. Custom orders typically require 3-4 weeks for completion.

What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 14 days of delivery. Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and in the same condition you received them. Please note that custom orders are non-returnable unless there's a manufacturing defect.

Do you offer wholesale pricing?

Yes, we work with select retailers who share our values of quality and sustainability. For wholesale inquiries, please contact us at wholesale@casuva.com with details about your business, and our team will get back to you with our wholesale catalog and terms.

How do I care for my Casuva products?

Each Casuva product comes with specific care instructions. Generally, our wooden items should be hand washed and dried immediately, while ceramics are usually dishwasher safe. Our textiles should be washed on a gentle cycle with mild detergent. For specific care questions, please contact us.
