Who We Are

Crafting Beauty Since 2015

Casuva began as a small passion project between three friends who shared a love for traditional craftsmanship. What started as weekend workshops in a backyard studio has grown into a thriving community of artisans and craft lovers.

Today, we collaborate with over 50 skilled makers across 12 countries, bringing you unique handmade pieces that blend tradition with contemporary design. Each product in our collection tells a story of cultural heritage and meticulous craftsmanship.

We're committed to preserving artisanal techniques while ensuring fair wages and sustainable practices for all our makers. When you choose Casuva, you're not just buying a product - you're supporting a movement that values quality over quantity and people over profit.

Our Values

Ethical Sourcing

We build direct relationships with artisans, ensuring fair compensation and safe working conditions for all our makers.

Sustainable Materials

All our products use natural, renewable, or recycled materials with minimal environmental impact.

Quality Craftsmanship
