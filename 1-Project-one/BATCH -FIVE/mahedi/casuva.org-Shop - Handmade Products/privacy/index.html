Our Commitment to Your Privacy

At Casuva, we are committed to protecting your personal information and being transparent about what data we collect and how we use it. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding your personal data.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information when you use our website or make a purchase:

  • Contact information (name, email address, phone number)
  • Shipping and billing address
  • Payment details (processed securely through our payment processor)
  • Purchase history and preferences
  • Website usage data through cookies

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Process and fulfill your orders
  • Communicate with you about your orders
  • Improve our products and services
  • Send marketing communications (only with your consent)
  • Prevent fraud and enhance security

Data Protection

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information. All payment transactions are encrypted using SSL technology.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt-out of marketing communications

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@casuva.com.

Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services (payment processors, shipping carriers) that have access to your information only to perform specific tasks on our behalf.
