Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping take?

Shipping within Germany typically takes 2-4 business days. For EU countries, please allow 5-7 business days. International shipping may take 10-14 business days depending on your location. All orders include tracking information.

What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and in the same condition you received them. Please note that custom orders are non-returnable. Contact us at returns@hospora.org to initiate a return.

Do you offer wholesale pricing?

Yes, we do offer wholesale pricing for qualified retailers. Please contact us at wholesale@hospora.org with your business information, and our team will get back to you with our wholesale catalog and pricing structure.

Are all your products handmade?

Yes, all Hospora products are handcrafted by skilled artisans using traditional techniques and high-quality materials. Each piece is unique and may have slight variations, which is the beautiful nature of handmade items.
