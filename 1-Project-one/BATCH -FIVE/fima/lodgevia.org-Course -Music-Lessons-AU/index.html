Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our music courses.

Do I need any prior musical experience?

No prior experience is needed for our beginner courses! We start from the very basics and gradually build your skills. Our courses are designed to accommodate complete beginners as well as more advanced students looking to refine their technique.

What equipment do I need for online lessons?

For most courses, you'll need the instrument you're learning (piano, guitar, violin, etc.), a computer or tablet with internet access, and headphones (optional but recommended). Some courses may have additional requirements which will be listed in the course details.

How does the subscription work?

Your subscription gives you unlimited access to all course materials for your chosen plan during the billing period. You can cancel anytime, and you'll retain access until the end of your current billing cycle. We offer monthly and annual subscription options.

Can I switch courses after signing up?

Absolutely! Depending on your subscription level, you can switch between courses at any time. Standard and Premium plans allow access to multiple courses simultaneously, while Basic plan subscribers can switch to a different course once per month.

What if I need help with the course material?

We offer multiple support channels based on your subscription level. All students can post questions in our community forums. Standard and Premium subscribers get direct access to instructors during live Q&A sessions, and Premium members receive personalized feedback on their progress.
