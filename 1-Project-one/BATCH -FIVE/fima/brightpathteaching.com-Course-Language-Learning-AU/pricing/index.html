Frequently Asked Questions

Can I switch plans later if my needs change?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. When you upgrade, you'll immediately gain access to the additional features. If you downgrade, the changes will take effect at your next billing cycle.

Are there any long-term contracts?

No, all our plans are month-to-month with no long-term commitment. You can cancel anytime, though we'd love to hear how we could improve your experience.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, and bank transfers for Australian residents.

Is there a discount for students or seniors?

Yes, we offer a 15% discount for full-time students and seniors (65+). Please contact our support team with proof of eligibility to receive your discount code.

Can I get a refund if I'm not satisfied?

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee for all new students. If you're not satisfied with your course, you can request a full refund within the first two weeks.
