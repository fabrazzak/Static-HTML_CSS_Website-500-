Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our products and services.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, and Afterpay for online orders. In-store, we also accept cash and EFTPOS.

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping within Australia typically takes 3-5 business days. Express shipping is available for next business day delivery in most metropolitan areas. International shipping times vary by destination.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day return policy for unworn items with original tags attached. Sale items are final sale unless faulty. Please see our full Returns & Exchanges policy for more details.

Do you offer international shipping?

Yes, we ship worldwide! Shipping costs and delivery times vary by country. You'll see the exact shipping costs at checkout based on your location.

How can I track my order?

Once your order ships, you'll receive a confirmation email with tracking information. You can also track your order by logging into your account on our website.
