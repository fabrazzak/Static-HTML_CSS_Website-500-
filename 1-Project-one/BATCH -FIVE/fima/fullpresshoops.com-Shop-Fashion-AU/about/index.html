Our Story

Founded in 2025 in the vibrant coastal city of Fremantle, Western Australia, FullPressHoops began as a passion project between two friends with a shared love for urban fashion and streetwear culture. What started as a small boutique with carefully curated pieces has grown into a premier destination for those who appreciate quality, style, and individuality.

Our name, FullPressHoops, reflects our commitment to going all-in - just like in basketball when you give full press defense. We apply that same intensity to sourcing the best fashion pieces and providing exceptional service to our customers.

Today, we're proud to serve fashion-forward individuals across Australia with our unique blend of streetwear, urban fashion, and premium sportswear.

Our Mission

At FullPressHoops, our mission is to empower individuals to express their unique style through premium fashion that doesn't compromise on quality or ethics. We believe that what you wear should make you feel confident, comfortable, and authentically you.

We're committed to:

  • Curating only the highest quality pieces from both established and emerging designers
  • Promoting sustainable fashion practices and ethical production
  • Providing exceptional customer service that goes above and beyond
  • Building a community around streetwear culture and urban fashion

Every piece in our collection is hand-selected by our team to ensure it meets our high standards for style, quality, and wearability.

Our Core Values

We never compromise on quality. Every piece in our collection is carefully vetted for materials, construction, and durability.

Sustainable Practices

Customer Love

Our customers are at the heart of everything we do. We strive to exceed expectations at every touchpoint.

Innovation
