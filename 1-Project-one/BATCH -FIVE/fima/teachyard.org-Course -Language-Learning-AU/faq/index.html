Frequently Asked Questions

General Questions

What languages do you offer courses for?

We currently offer courses in English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, and Japanese. We're continuously expanding our language offerings based on student demand.

How do I know which course level is right for me?

We provide free placement tests for each language to assess your current proficiency level. After completing the test, you'll receive personalized course recommendations. You can also schedule a consultation with one of our language advisors.

Do you offer certificates upon course completion?

Yes, students enrolled in our Standard and Premium plans receive a certificate upon successfully completing a course, which includes passing all assessments. These certificates can be shared on professional networks like LinkedIn.

Can I learn at my own pace?

Absolutely! While we provide recommended timelines for each course, you can progress at your own speed. Course materials are available 24/7, and you can review lessons as many times as needed.

Enrollment & Pricing

What's included in each pricing plan?

Our Basic plan gives you access to 1 course with weekly Q&A sessions. The Standard plan (most popular) includes 3 courses with bi-weekly tutoring. The Premium plan offers unlimited courses with weekly private tutoring and personalized learning plans.

Can I switch or cancel my plan?

You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Cancellations are effective at the end of your current billing period. We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee if you're not satisfied with your course.

Do you offer discounts for students or groups?

Yes, we offer a 15% student discount with valid ID. For groups of 5 or more, we provide special pricing - please contact our team for group enrollment options.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, and bank transfers for annual plans. All payments are processed securely through our encrypted payment system.

Technical Requirements

What equipment do I need for the courses?

You'll need a computer or tablet with a stable internet connection, webcam, and microphone for interactive sessions. Our platform works on all major browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge). While mobile access is available, some exercises are best experienced on larger screens.

How do I access my course materials?

After enrollment, you'll receive login credentials for our learning platform. All materials are organized in your personal dashboard, accessible anytime from any device. You can download some resources for offline use.

What if I experience technical issues?

Our technical support team is available 24/7 for Premium plan users and during business hours for other plans. We have an extensive help center with troubleshooting guides, and most issues can be resolved quickly.

Are the live sessions recorded?

Yes, all live sessions are recorded and available in your dashboard within 24 hours. You can review them anytime during your enrollment period.

Learning Experience

How much time should I dedicate weekly?

We recommend 3-5 hours per week for optimal progress, but this varies by course intensity and your personal goals. Each lesson indicates the estimated time commitment, and you can adjust your pace as needed.

What teaching methods do you use?

Our approach combines communicative language teaching with task-based learning. You'll engage in interactive video lessons, practical exercises, real-world scenarios, and cultural immersion activities.

Can I interact with other students?

Yes! Each course includes access to our student community where you can practice with peers, join study groups, and participate in language exchange activities. We also host regular conversation practice sessions.

What if I fall behind in my course?

Our courses are designed to be flexible. You can revisit materials anytime, and our instructors can provide guidance if you need help catching up. Premium plan students receive personalized support to stay on track.

Still Have Questions?

Can't find what you're looking for? Our support team is ready to help you with any additional questions about our language courses and learning platform.
