Frequently Asked Questions

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, and bank transfers for Australian residents. All payments are processed securely through our payment gateway.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. When you upgrade, you'll only pay the difference for the remaining period. Downgrades will take effect at your next billing cycle.

Is there a free trial available?

We offer a 7-day free trial for our Standard plan. You'll get full access to all Standard features during this period with no obligation to continue. No credit card is required for the trial.

What's your cancellation policy?

You can cancel your subscription at any time. For monthly plans, cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period. Annual plans cancelled within 30 days receive a full refund.

Do you offer discounts for students?

Yes, we offer a 15% student discount on all plans. Simply verify your student status with a valid student ID or .edu email address when signing up.

How do I access my courses after purchasing?

Immediately after purchase, you'll receive an email with login details to our learning platform. All your enrolled courses will be available in your dashboard, accessible from any device.
