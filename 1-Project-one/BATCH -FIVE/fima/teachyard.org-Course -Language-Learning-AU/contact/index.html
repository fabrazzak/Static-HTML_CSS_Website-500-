Common Questions

How soon can I expect a response to my inquiry?

We typically respond to all inquiries within 24 hours during business days (Monday to Friday). For inquiries sent over the weekend, you can expect a response by the following Monday afternoon at the latest.

What's the best way to contact TeachYard for course information?

The contact form on this page is the most efficient way to get information about specific courses. If you prefer to speak with someone directly, you can call our office during business hours. For general questions, our FAQ page may have the answers you need.

Do you offer in-person consultations at your office?

Yes, we offer in-person consultations by appointment. Please contact us to schedule a time that works for you. Virtual consultations via Zoom or Google Meet are also available if you can't visit our Fremantle office.

Can I visit your location to learn more about the courses?

Absolutely! We welcome visitors to our Fremantle office during business hours. We recommend scheduling an appointment to ensure someone is available to give you a proper introduction to our programs and answer all your questions.
