Frequently Asked Questions

How do I choose the right course for my level?

We offer free placement tests for each language to help determine your current proficiency level. You can take these tests before enrolling to ensure you select the most appropriate course. Our advisors are also available to guide you through this process.

What's the time commitment for each course?

Most courses recommend 3-5 hours of study per week, including live sessions and independent practice. However, our platform is designed to be flexible, allowing you to progress at your own pace. Each lesson module clearly indicates the estimated time required.

Can I switch courses if I change my mind?

Yes, you can switch to a different course within the first 14 days of your enrollment at no additional cost. After this period, course changes may be subject to availability and potential price differences.

What technology do I need to participate?

You'll need a computer or tablet with a stable internet connection, webcam, and microphone for interactive sessions. Our platform works on all major browsers. Mobile access is available for most features, though some interactive exercises are best experienced on larger screens.

Do you offer certificates upon completion?

Yes, all Standard and Premium plan students receive a certificate upon successful course completion, which includes passing all assessments. Our certificates are widely recognized and can be shared on professional networks like LinkedIn.
