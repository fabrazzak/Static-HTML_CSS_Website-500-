Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our courses and services.

How do I enroll in a course?

You can enroll in any of our courses by filling out the contact form on this page or visiting our Courses page and clicking the "Enroll Now" button on your preferred course. Our team will contact you with enrollment details and payment options.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, and direct bank transfers for Australian residents. All payments are processed securely through our payment gateway.

Can I get a refund if I'm not satisfied with a course?

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee for all our courses. If you're not satisfied with your purchase, simply contact us within 14 days of enrollment for a full refund, no questions asked.

Do you offer in-person classes or consultations?

Currently, all our courses are offered online through our learning platform. However, we do offer limited in-person styling consultations in our Fremantle office by appointment. Contact us to schedule a consultation.

How long do I have access to course materials?

With individual course purchases, you get lifetime access to the course materials. For subscription plans, you maintain access as long as your subscription is active. All courses include downloadable resources you can keep forever.
