About TennisScoop

Your premier source for comprehensive tennis coverage, delivering news, analysis, and insights to fans worldwide.

Our Story

From passionate fans to trusted tennis journalists

TennisScoop was founded in 2010 by a group of tennis enthusiasts who wanted to create a platform that combined in-depth analysis with the latest news from all corners of the tennis world. What started as a small blog has grown into one of the most trusted sources for tennis coverage worldwide.

Our team of dedicated journalists and former players brings you comprehensive tournament coverage, exclusive interviews, and insightful commentary that goes beyond the scores. We're committed to delivering content that's as engaging for casual fans as it is valuable for die-hard tennis aficionados.

With correspondents at every Grand Slam and major tournament, we provide on-the-ground reporting you won't find anywhere else. Our mission is to connect tennis fans with the sport they love through accurate, timely, and compelling storytelling.

Our Values

Excellence

We strive for the highest standards in journalism, delivering accurate, well-researched content that stands out in sports media.

Passion

Our love for tennis drives everything we do. We're fans first, and that enthusiasm shines through in our coverage.

Integrity

We maintain strict editorial independence and always put journalistic ethics above all else in our reporting.

Community

We believe tennis is for everyone and work to foster an inclusive, welcoming environment for all fans.

Our Milestones

2010

TennisScoop Founded
