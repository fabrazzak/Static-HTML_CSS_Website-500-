Privacy Policy

Last Updated: Jan 15, 2025

At StickTracker, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or use our services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you:

  • Register for an account
  • Use our services
  • Subscribe to our newsletter
  • Contact us with inquiries
  • Participate in surveys or promotions

The types of personal information we may collect include:

  • Name and contact details (email, phone number)
  • Demographic information (age, gender, location)
  • Payment information for purchases
  • Performance data you choose to track
  • Other information you choose to provide

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and maintain our services
  • Process your transactions
  • Improve our products and services
  • Communicate with you about your account
  • Send you marketing communications (where permitted)
  • Respond to your inquiries and requests
  • Analyze usage trends and preferences
  • Ensure the security of our services
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service providers who assist with our operations
  • Coaches or team administrators if you're part of a team account
  • Legal authorities when required by law
  • Business partners in connection with a merger or acquisition

We require all third parties to respect your privacy and to treat your information in accordance with the law.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data, including:

  • Encryption of sensitive data
  • Regular security assessments
  • Access controls to personal information
  • Secure data storage solutions

While we strive to protect your information, no electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Data Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data
  • Restriction: Limit how we use your information
  • Objection: Object to certain processing activities
  • Portability: Request transfer of your data to another service

To exercise these rights, please contact us at privacy@sticktracker.org.

6. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to:

  • Remember your preferences
  • Analyze website traffic
  • Improve user experience
  • Deliver targeted advertisements

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect website functionality.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete such information.

For users aged 13-18, we recommend obtaining parental consent before using our services.

8. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and maintained on computers located outside of your country where data protection laws may differ. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy on our website and updating the "Last Updated" date.
