Maximize Your Individual Performance

Our Player Analytics service provides hockey players with detailed insights into every aspect of their game. Track over 50 performance metrics including skating speed, shot accuracy, faceoff percentage, and more.

  • Real-time tracking during games and practices
  • Personalized training plans based on your data
  • Video analysis integration to review key moments
  • Progress reports showing your improvement over time
  • Injury prevention alerts based on workload metrics
