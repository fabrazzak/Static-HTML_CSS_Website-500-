Our Hockey Analytics Journey

Pioneering the future of hockey performance technology since 2015

Our Story

From rink-side notebooks to cutting-edge analytics

2015

Founded in Stockholm

Started as a university research project by former hockey players and data scientists looking to bring advanced analytics to amateur teams.

2017

First Pro Team Client

Signed our first professional hockey team, proving the value of our analytics platform at the highest levels of competition.

2019

Series A Funding

Raised $8M to expand our engineering team and develop real-time tracking capabilities.

2021

NCAA Partnership

Became the official analytics provider for 50+ NCAA hockey programs across North America.

2025

Global Expansion

Now serving teams in 12 countries with localized support and multi-language platforms.
