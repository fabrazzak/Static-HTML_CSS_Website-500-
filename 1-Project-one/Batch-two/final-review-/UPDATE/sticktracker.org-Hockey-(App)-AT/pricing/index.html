Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our pricing and plans

Is there a free trial available?

Yes! All plans come with a 14-day free trial. No credit card is required to start your trial, and you can cancel anytime during the trial period with no obligation.

Can I switch plans later?

Absolutely. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from your account settings. Prorated credits will be applied when upgrading mid-billing cycle.

Do you offer discounts for non-profits or schools?

We offer special pricing for educational institutions and registered non-profit organizations. Please contact our sales team at sales@sticktracker.org with details about your organization to learn more.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, and bank transfers for annual plans. All payments are processed securely through our PCI-compliant payment processor.

How does the annual billing discount work?

When you choose annual billing, you get 20% off the equivalent monthly price. This discount is applied automatically when you select annual billing during signup or plan changes.

What's your cancellation policy?

You can cancel your subscription at any time. If you cancel mid-billing period, you'll retain access until the end of that period. We don't charge cancellation fees or require long-term contracts.

Still have questions? Contact our team for more information.
