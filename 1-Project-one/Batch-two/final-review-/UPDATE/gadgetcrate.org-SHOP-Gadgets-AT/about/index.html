Our Story

Founded in 2018 by tech enthusiasts Alex Morgan and Jamie Chen, GadgetCrate began as a small online store with a simple mission: to make cutting-edge technology accessible to everyone. What started as a passion project in a Berlin apartment has grown into one of Europe's most trusted tech retailers.

We're proud to have served over 50,000 customers across Europe, delivering premium gadgets with exceptional customer service. Our team of tech experts carefully tests and selects every product in our inventory to ensure it meets our high standards for quality and innovation.

At GadgetCrate, we believe technology should enhance your life without breaking the bank. That's why we work directly with manufacturers to bring you the best prices without compromising on quality.

Our Mission

Our mission is to bridge the gap between cutting-edge technology and everyday consumers. We carefully curate our product selection to bring you gadgets that are innovative, reliable, and truly useful in your daily life.

We're committed to exceptional customer service, transparent pricing, and making technology accessible to everyone. Whether you're a tech enthusiast or just looking for tools to simplify your life, we've got you covered.
