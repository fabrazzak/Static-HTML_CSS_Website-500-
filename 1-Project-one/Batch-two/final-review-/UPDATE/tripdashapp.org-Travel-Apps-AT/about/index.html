Our Journey

From a simple idea to a trusted travel companion for thousands of travelers worldwide.

2018

Founded in Vienna

TripDash was founded by a group of travel enthusiasts who were frustrated with the complexity of planning multi-destination trips. Our first office was a small co-working space in Vienna's historic center.

2019

First Mobile App Launch

We launched our first mobile app with basic itinerary planning features, quickly gaining 10,000 users in the first three months. This validated our vision of creating a unified travel planning platform.

2020

Expanded to 50 Countries

Despite global travel challenges, we expanded our coverage to 50 countries and introduced our AI-powered itinerary builder, which became our most popular feature.

2021

1 Million Users

TripDash reached the milestone of 1 million registered users and introduced premium subscription plans to support our growing service.

2022

Integrated Booking System

We partnered with leading travel providers to offer seamless booking directly within the TripDash platform, completing our vision of an all-in-one travel solution.

2023

Global Recognition

TripDash was named "Most Innovative Travel App" by the European Travel Awards and featured in major tech and travel publications worldwide.
