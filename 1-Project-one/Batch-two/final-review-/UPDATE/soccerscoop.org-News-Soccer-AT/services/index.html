Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our services

How often are subscription services updated?

Our Basic plan is updated daily with news and weekly with match previews. Premium and Professional plans receive multiple updates daily, including breaking news, tactical analyses, and exclusive content.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes, all subscriptions are month-to-month with no long-term commitment. You can cancel anytime through your account settings, with no cancellation fees.

How current are the scouting reports?

Our scouts provide updates at least monthly for each player we track, with major updates after significant performances or developments. Emergency updates are provided for major events like injuries or transfer rumors.

Do you offer custom analysis packages?

Yes, our Professional plan includes some customization, and we offer fully bespoke packages for clubs and media organizations. Contact our sales team to discuss your specific needs.
