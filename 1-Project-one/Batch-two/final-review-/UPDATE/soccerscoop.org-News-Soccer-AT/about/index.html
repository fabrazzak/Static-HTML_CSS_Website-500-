Our Story & Mission

Dedicated to providing unparalleled football analysis and insights

Who We Are

Soccer Scoop was founded in 2015 by a group of football enthusiasts, analysts, and former players who wanted to create a platform for intelligent, in-depth football discussion beyond the typical headlines.

What started as a small blog has grown into a respected source of football analysis, trusted by fans, journalists, and even professionals in the game.

We pride ourselves on going deeper than surface-level commentary, combining tactical understanding, data analysis, and football history to provide unique perspectives on the beautiful game.

Our Mission

Educate

We aim to help football fans understand the tactical and strategic nuances that make the game so complex and beautiful.

Analyze

Going beyond results, we examine why things happen on the pitch through data, video analysis, and expert insight.

Connect

We bring together a community of thoughtful football fans who appreciate deep discussion about the game.
