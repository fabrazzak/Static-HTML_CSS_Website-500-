Frequently Asked Questions

How can I submit news tips or story ideas?

We welcome news tips and story ideas from our readers. Please email your suggestions to tips@hoopreport.org with as much detail as possible. Our editorial team reviews all submissions and will follow up if we decide to pursue your tip.

Do you accept guest posts or contributor articles?

Yes, we occasionally publish guest contributions from qualified basketball writers and analysts. Please send your pitch along with writing samples to editors@hoopreport.org. Include "Guest Post Submission" in your subject line.

How can I advertise on HoopReport?

For advertising inquiries, please contact our sales team at ads@hoopreport.org. Include information about your brand, campaign goals, and target audience. We offer various advertising options including display ads, sponsored content, and newsletter placements.

Can I republish HoopReport content on my site?

Republishing our content requires written permission. Some content may be available for syndication. Please contact permissions@hoopreport.org with details about the content you're interested in and how you plan to use it.

How do I report an error in your content?

We strive for accuracy and appreciate when readers bring errors to our attention. Please email corrections@hoopreport.org with the article URL, specific error details, and any supporting information. Our editorial team will review all correction requests.

Do you offer internship opportunities?

Yes, we offer internships in writing, editing, analytics, and digital media. Internship opportunities are typically posted on our Careers page. You can also send your resume and cover letter to careers@hoopreport.org to be considered for future openings.
