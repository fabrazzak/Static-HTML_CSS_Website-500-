About HoopReport

Your trusted source for comprehensive basketball coverage since 2010.

Our Story

Founded in 2010 by a group of passionate basketball enthusiasts and former players, HoopReport has grown from a small blog to one of the most trusted sources for NBA news, analysis, and statistics. What began as a hobby project among friends has transformed into a comprehensive basketball platform with a global audience.

Our journey started with a simple goal: to provide basketball fans with insightful, accurate, and engaging content that goes beyond the surface-level coverage found elsewhere. Over the years, we've built a reputation for our in-depth analysis, timely reporting, and commitment to the basketball community.

Our Mission

At HoopReport, we're driven by a singular mission: to elevate basketball coverage through insightful analysis, accurate reporting, and engaging storytelling. We believe basketball is more than just a game—it's a culture, a community, and for many, a way of life.

Our team is committed to:

  • Providing the most accurate and timely basketball coverage
  • Offering in-depth analysis that goes beyond box scores
  • Supporting and growing the basketball community
  • Maintaining the highest journalistic standards
  • Innovating in basketball media and analytics

Our Core Values

Accuracy

We prioritize factual reporting and verify all information before publication. Our reputation is built on trust.

Insight

We go beyond surface-level coverage to provide meaningful analysis that enhances understanding of the game.

Community

Basketball is about connection. We foster a positive, inclusive community for all fans.

Innovation

We continuously explore new ways to analyze and present basketball content to our audience.

Passion

Our love for the game drives everything we do. We're fans first, just like you.

Integrity

We maintain editorial independence and are transparent about our processes and sources.
