Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services

How do I know which service is right for my child?

We offer free initial assessments to evaluate your child's needs and recommend the most appropriate service. Our educational consultants will discuss your child's goals, learning style, and schedule to create a personalized learning plan.

What qualifications do your tutors have?

All our tutors hold at least a bachelor's degree in their subject area, with many having advanced degrees. They undergo rigorous screening, including background checks, and receive ongoing training in our teaching methodologies.

Can we switch between in-person and online sessions?

Yes, our hybrid learning model allows you to seamlessly switch between in-person and online sessions as needed. Many families use a combination of both depending on their schedule and preferences.

What is your cancellation policy?

We require 24 hours notice for session cancellations to avoid being charged. In case of illness or emergency, please notify us as soon as possible and we'll do our best to accommodate you.

Do you offer discounts for siblings or multiple subjects?

Yes, we offer a 10% discount for siblings and for students taking multiple subjects. Additionally, our package deals and monthly plans provide significant savings compared to individual sessions.
