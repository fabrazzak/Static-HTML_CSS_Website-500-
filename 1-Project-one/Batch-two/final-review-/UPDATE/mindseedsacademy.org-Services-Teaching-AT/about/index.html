Who We Are

MindSeeds Academy was founded in 2015 with a simple yet powerful vision: to create an educational environment where every student can thrive. What began as a small tutoring center in Vienna has grown into a respected academy serving students across Austria and beyond.

We believe that education should be personalized, engaging, and focused on developing critical thinking skills that last a lifetime. Our approach combines proven teaching methods with innovative techniques tailored to each learner's needs.

Our Mission & Values

At MindSeeds Academy, we're driven by a commitment to educational excellence and student empowerment. Our mission is to:

  • Plant the seeds of knowledge that grow into lifelong learning
  • Ignite curiosity and critical thinking in every student
  • Provide personalized support that addresses individual learning needs
  • Prepare students for success in an increasingly globalized world
  • Foster a love of learning that extends beyond the classroom
