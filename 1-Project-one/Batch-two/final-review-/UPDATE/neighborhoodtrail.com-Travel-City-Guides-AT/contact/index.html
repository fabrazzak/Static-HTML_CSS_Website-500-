Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our tours and services

How do I book a tour?

You can book tours directly through our website by visiting the Tours page and selecting your desired experience. For private or custom tours, please contact us using the form above or give us a call.

What is your cancellation policy?

We offer free cancellation up to 48 hours before your scheduled tour for a full refund. Cancellations within 48 hours are non-refundable but may be rescheduled based on availability.

Are your tours suitable for children?

Most of our walking tours are family-friendly, though some may be more engaging for older children. Our food tours typically accommodate children aged 8 and up. Private tours can be customized for families with younger children.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking at least 1-2 weeks in advance, especially during peak travel seasons (May-September). Some popular tours sell out weeks in advance.

Do you offer tours in languages other than English?

Many of our guides speak multiple languages. While most tours are conducted in English, we can often arrange tours in German, Spanish, or French with advance notice. Please contact us for availability.
