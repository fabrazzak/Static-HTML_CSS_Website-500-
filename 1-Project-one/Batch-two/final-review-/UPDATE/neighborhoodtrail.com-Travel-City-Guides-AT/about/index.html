Our Story

How a passion for authentic travel experiences grew into Neighborhood Trail

From Local Passion to Global Vision

Neighborhood Trail began in 2015 when our founder, Markus Weber, grew frustrated watching visitors to his hometown of Vienna miss its true character. Despite the city's wealth of guidebooks and tours, most travelers never discovered the places that made locals proud.

What started as Markus offering personal recommendations to tourists he met in coffee shops evolved into informal walking tours. Word spread, and soon other Vienna residents joined him in sharing their neighborhoods.

By 2018, we had formalized our approach and expanded to Budapest and Prague. Today, we operate in 12 European cities with a network of over 50 local guides.

Our Mission

We believe travel should create meaningful connections between visitors and local communities. Our mission is to:

  • • Show travelers the authentic character of cities beyond tourist attractions
  • • Support local businesses and cultural preservation
  • • Create economic opportunities for residents to share their expertise
  • • Promote sustainable tourism practices that benefit neighborhoods

Every Neighborhood Trail experience is designed with these principles in mind, from our walking tours to our local business partnerships.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide everything we do

Local First

We only work with guides who live in the neighborhoods they showcase and have deep local knowledge.

Authenticity

We avoid tourist traps and commercialized experiences in favor of genuine local interactions.

Sustainability

Our walking-focused tours have minimal environmental impact and support local economies.

Passion

Our guides share their cities with the enthusiasm of showing friends around their hometown.

Quality

Every experience undergoes rigorous testing and continuous improvement based on feedback.

Community

We build relationships between travelers and residents that last beyond the tour.
