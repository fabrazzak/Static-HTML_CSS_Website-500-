We're City Dwellers First, Guides Second

Born from a passion for authentic urban exploration

Neighborhood Trail began when our founder, a lifelong Vienna resident, grew tired of seeing visitors miss the true character of his city. What started as personal recommendations to friends evolved into curated local experiences across Europe.

We don't just show you landmarks - we introduce you to the shop owners, artists, and residents who make each neighborhood special. Every guide is created by someone who calls the area home.
