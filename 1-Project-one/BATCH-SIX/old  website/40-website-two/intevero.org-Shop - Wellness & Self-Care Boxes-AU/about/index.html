Our Story of Wellness & Care

Discover the passion and purpose behind Intevero's journey to bring holistic wellness to your doorstep.

Our Journey

Intevero was born from a simple idea: that everyone deserves access to high-quality self-care, no matter how busy life gets. Our founder, Sarah, was working long hours in a high-stress corporate job when she realized how difficult it was to prioritize wellness amidst daily demands.

Frustrated by the lack of convenient, curated wellness solutions, Sarah began putting together personalized self-care packages for friends and family. The overwhelming positive response led to the creation of Intevero in 2020.

Today, we're proud to have helped over 10,000 customers across Australia and beyond discover the transformative power of consistent self-care. Each of our boxes is still hand-curated with the same care and attention as those first packages Sarah created.
