Our Story

Discover the passion behind strenlo and our commitment to bringing art into your life

How It All Began

Founded in 2025, strenlo began as a small passion project between friends who shared a love for art and design. What started as a weekend endeavor to showcase local artists quickly grew into a thriving online gallery with a mission to make beautiful art accessible to everyone.

Our founders, Anna and Michael, noticed that many people wanted to decorate their homes with quality art but found the process intimidating or expensive. They set out to create a platform that would connect art lovers with talented artists while removing the barriers of traditional art purchasing.

Today, strenlo works with over 50 independent artists from around the world, offering carefully curated prints that transform spaces and inspire lives. We're proud to support emerging artists while making their work available to a global audience.

Our Mission & Values

Art for Everyone

We believe art should be accessible to all, not just collectors. Our prints make it possible to own beautiful artwork at affordable prices without compromising on quality.

Artist Support

We're committed to fair compensation for artists. Each sale provides royalties to the creators, helping them continue their important work.

Sustainability

From eco-friendly inks to responsibly sourced materials, we prioritize sustainability in every aspect of our production and shipping processes.

Customer Happiness

Your satisfaction is our top priority. We stand behind every print with a 100% satisfaction guarantee and exceptional customer service.

Global Community

We celebrate diversity by showcasing artists from different cultures and backgrounds, creating a global artistic dialogue.

Continuous Innovation

We're always exploring new techniques, materials, and ways to enhance your art experience while staying true to our core values.
