Frequently Asked Questions

What's your shipping policy?

We offer standard shipping within United Kingdom for €4.99, with free shipping on orders over €50. International shipping rates vary by destination. Most orders are processed within 1-2 business days and delivered within 3-5 business days domestically. You'll receive a tracking number once your order ships.

Are your essential oils pure and organic?

Yes, all our essential oils are 100% pure, therapeutic-grade, and free from additives or fillers. Many are certified organic (clearly labeled on product pages), and all are sourced from ethical growers who use sustainable practices. We provide GC/MS test reports for every batch to verify purity and composition.

Do you offer wholesale pricing?

We do offer wholesale pricing for practitioners, retailers, and businesses purchasing in quantity. Minimum order quantities apply. Please contact us through the wholesale inquiry option on our contact form, and our wholesale team will send you our current price list and terms.

How should I store my essential oils?

To preserve their therapeutic properties, store essential oils in their original dark glass bottles in a cool, dark place away from direct sunlight and heat sources. Keep lids tightly closed to prevent oxidation. Most oils have a shelf life of 1-3 years when stored properly, with citrus oils lasting about 1 year and resins like frankincense lasting up to 3 years.

Can I return products if I'm not satisfied?

We offer a 30-day satisfaction guarantee on all unopened products in their original packaging. For opened products, we can accept returns within 14 days if you're unsatisfied (shipping not included). Please contact our customer service team to initiate a return. Note that due to hygiene reasons, we cannot accept returns on diffusers or other accessories that have been used.
