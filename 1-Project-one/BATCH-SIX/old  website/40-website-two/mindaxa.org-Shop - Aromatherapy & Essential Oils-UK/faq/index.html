Frequently Asked Questions

Product Information

What makes Mindaxa essential oils different from others?

Mindaxa essential oils stand out because:

  • We source only the highest quality, organic botanicals
  • Our oils are 100% pure with no additives or fillers
  • We use low-temperature distillation to preserve therapeutic compounds
  • Each batch is rigorously tested for purity and potency
  • We work directly with ethical growers worldwide
Are your essential oils safe to use undiluted?

Most of our essential oils should be diluted before topical use. Exceptions include:

  • Lavender - Can be used neat (undiluted) for minor burns or insect bites
  • Tea Tree - Can be used undiluted for acne spot treatment

For general use, we recommend diluting essential oils in a carrier oil (like jojoba or coconut oil) at 1-2% dilution (5-10 drops per ounce of carrier oil).

How should I store my essential oils?

To maximize shelf life and maintain quality:

  • Store in dark glass bottles (ours come in amber glass)
  • Keep in a cool, dark place away from sunlight
  • Ensure lids are tightly closed after each use
  • Avoid storing in plastic containers
  • Keep away from heat sources and open flames

Properly stored, most essential oils will last 3-5 years. Citrus oils have a shorter shelf life of 1-2 years.

Can I use your essential oils in my diffuser?

Yes! All Mindaxa essential oils are perfect for diffusing. We recommend:

  • Using 3-5 drops per 100ml of water in ultrasonic diffusers
  • Diffusing for 30-60 minutes at a time with breaks in between
  • Choosing oils based on your needs (lavender for relaxation, peppermint for energy, etc.)
  • Cleaning your diffuser regularly with white vinegar

Safety & Usage

Are your essential oils safe for children?

Some essential oils can be used with children when properly diluted:

  • For babies under 2: We recommend consulting a pediatrician first
  • Ages 2-6: Use 0.25% dilution (1 drop per 4 teaspoons carrier oil)
  • Ages 6-12: Use 1% dilution (1 drop per teaspoon carrier oil)
  • Always do a patch test first
  • Avoid using near eyes, nose, or mouth

Some oils (like peppermint and eucalyptus) should be avoided for young children.

Can I use essential oils if I'm pregnant?

Some essential oils are safe during pregnancy when used properly, but we recommend:

  • Always consult your healthcare provider first
  • Avoid using essential oils during the first trimester
  • Use only gentle oils like lavender and chamomile in low dilutions (0.5-1%)
  • Avoid known risky oils like rosemary, clary sage, and juniper
  • Never ingest essential oils during pregnancy
What should I do if I have an allergic reaction?

If you experience an allergic reaction:

  1. Immediately stop using the product
  2. Wash the area with mild soap and cool water
  3. Apply a carrier oil (like coconut or olive oil) to dilute any remaining essential oil
  4. For severe reactions (difficulty breathing, swelling), seek medical attention immediately
  5. Contact us with details of your reaction

We recommend doing a patch test before using any new essential oil: apply a diluted drop to your inner arm and wait 24 hours to check for reactions.
