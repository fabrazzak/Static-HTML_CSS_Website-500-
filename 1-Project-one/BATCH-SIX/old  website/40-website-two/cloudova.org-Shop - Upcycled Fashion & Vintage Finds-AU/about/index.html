Our Story

Discover the passion behind Cloudova and our commitment to sustainable fashion

From Passion to Purpose

Cloudova began in 2022 as a small passion project between friends Emma and Liam, who shared a love for vintage fashion and a concern for the environmental impact of the clothing industry. What started as weekend trips to flea markets and thrift stores quickly evolved into a mission to redefine sustainable fashion.

We noticed that many beautiful vintage pieces were being overlooked because they needed minor repairs or updates. At the same time, we saw firsthand the staggering amount of textile waste generated by fast fashion. Cloudova was born from the idea that we could bridge these two problems through creative upcycling.

Today, our small team of designers and vintage enthusiasts works tirelessly to give pre-loved clothing new life. Each piece in our collection is carefully selected for its quality and potential, then transformed with thoughtful updates that honor its history while making it relevant for today's wardrobe.

Our Mission

At Cloudova, we believe fashion should tell a story without costing the earth. Our mission is to reduce textile waste by creating beautiful, unique clothing through upcycling and responsible sourcing, while educating our community about sustainable fashion choices.
