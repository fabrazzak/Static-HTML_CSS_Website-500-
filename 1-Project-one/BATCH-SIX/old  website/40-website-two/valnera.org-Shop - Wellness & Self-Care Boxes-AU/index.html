At Valnera, we believe that self-care isn't selfish—it's essential. Founded in 2025, our mission is to make wellness accessible and enjoyable through our carefully curated subscription boxes.

Each month, we partner with ethical brands and wellness experts to bring you products that nourish your body, mind, and soul. From organic skincare to mindfulness tools, our boxes are designed to help you create meaningful self-care rituals.
