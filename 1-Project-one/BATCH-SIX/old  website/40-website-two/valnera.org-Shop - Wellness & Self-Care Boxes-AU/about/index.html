Our Story

From Passion to Purpose

Valnera was founded in 2025 by wellness enthusiasts Sarah and Michael, who saw a need for accessible, high-quality self-care solutions in our fast-paced world. What began as a small project to help friends prioritize their well-being has grown into a thriving community of like-minded individuals committed to living balanced, intentional lives.

Our name, Valnera, combines "valor" (meaning strength of mind or spirit) and "era" (signifying a new age). Together, they represent our mission to empower a new era of mindful self-care and personal growth.

Each of our wellness boxes is carefully curated with this philosophy in mind, bringing together premium products that nourish both body and soul. We partner with ethical suppliers and small businesses who share our values of sustainability, quality, and conscious living.

Our Core Values

Quality

We meticulously select only the finest products for our boxes, partnering with trusted brands that share our commitment to excellence. Every item is tested and approved by our team before being included in our collections.

Authenticity

We believe in being transparent about our sourcing, ingredients, and business practices. What you see is what you get—no hidden compromises or misleading claims.

Sustainability

From eco-friendly packaging to supporting small ethical producers, we're committed to reducing our environmental footprint while delivering exceptional products.

Innovation

We're constantly exploring new ways to enhance your self-care experience, whether through unique product combinations, helpful guides, or innovative wellness tools.

Community

Valnera is more than a business—it's a movement. We foster connections among our customers through events, online forums, and shared wellness journeys.

Joy

At the heart of everything we do is the belief that self-care should be delightful. We design experiences that bring moments of happiness to your everyday routine.
