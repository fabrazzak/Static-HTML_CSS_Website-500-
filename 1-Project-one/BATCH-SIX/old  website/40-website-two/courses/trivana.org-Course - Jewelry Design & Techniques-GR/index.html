Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need any prior experience to join these courses?

No prior experience is necessary for our beginner courses. We start with the fundamentals and gradually progress to more advanced techniques. Our instructors are skilled at adapting their teaching to students of all levels.

What tools and materials will I need?

For most beginner courses, you'll need basic tools like pliers, cutters, and mandrels, which we can recommend affordable options for. As you progress, we'll guide you in building your professional toolkit. Some courses include starter kits available for purchase.

How are the courses delivered?

Our courses are delivered through a combination of pre-recorded video lessons, live virtual sessions, downloadable resources, and community support. You can learn at your own pace while having access to instructor guidance.

Can I access the courses on mobile devices?

Yes! Our learning platform is fully responsive and works on smartphones, tablets, and computers. However, some detailed techniques may be easier to follow on a larger screen.

What if I need help during the course?

We offer multiple support channels including Q&A sessions, community forums, and direct messaging with instructors (depending on your plan). Our team is committed to helping you succeed in your jewelry making journey.
