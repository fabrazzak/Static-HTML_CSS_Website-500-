Pricing FAQs

Answers to common questions about our pricing and payment options

What's the difference between membership and individual course pricing?

Our membership plans give you unlimited access to all courses for a monthly fee, while individual course pricing lets you purchase specific courses one at a time. Memberships are ideal if you plan to take multiple courses, while individual pricing works best if you only want specific skills.

Can I switch from individual courses to a membership later?

Yes! If you've purchased individual courses and later decide to get a membership, we'll credit the amount you've already paid toward your membership fee. Just contact our support team to arrange this.

Do you offer discounts for students or groups?

We offer a 15% discount for full-time students with valid ID. Groups of 5 or more also receive discounted rates. Please contact us with details to receive your discount code.

What is your refund policy?

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee for all courses and memberships. If you're not satisfied with your purchase, simply contact us within 14 days of enrollment for a full refund.

Are there any additional costs beyond the course fees?

Some courses may require specific tools or materials that aren't included in the course fee. We always provide a list of required materials before you enroll, with recommendations for affordable options. Our memberships include all digital materials and resources.
