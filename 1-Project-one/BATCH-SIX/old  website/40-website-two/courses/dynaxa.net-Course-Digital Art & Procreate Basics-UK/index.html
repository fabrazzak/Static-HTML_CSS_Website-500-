Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our courses

Do I need any prior experience to take these courses?

No prior experience is needed! Our Procreate Basics course is designed for complete beginners. We start with the fundamentals and gradually build your skills through structured lessons and projects.

What equipment do I need for the courses?

You'll need an iPad (6th generation or newer) and Apple Pencil (1st or 2nd generation). The Procreate app (a one-time purchase from the App Store) is also required. That's all you need to get started!

How are the courses delivered?

All courses are delivered through our online platform with video lessons you can watch at your own pace. Each lesson includes project files, exercises, and additional resources to support your learning.

Can I access the courses on mobile?

Yes! Our learning platform is fully responsive and works on mobile devices, though we recommend using a tablet or computer for the best learning experience when watching the video lessons.

What if I need help during the course?

All plans include access to our student community where you can ask questions and get feedback. Standard and Premium plans include direct feedback from instructors through Q&A sessions or 1-on-1 mentoring.
