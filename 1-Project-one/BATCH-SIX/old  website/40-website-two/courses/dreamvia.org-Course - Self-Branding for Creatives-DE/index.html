Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our Self-Branding courses.

Who is this course for?

Our Self-Branding for Creatives course is designed for any creative professional looking to establish or enhance their personal brand. This includes designers, photographers, writers, artists, musicians, and other creatives who want to stand out in their field and attract better opportunities.

How long does the course take to complete?

The core curriculum can be completed in 4-6 weeks if you dedicate 3-5 hours per week. However, you'll have lifetime access to all course materials, so you can work at your own pace and revisit the content whenever you need a refresh.

What's included in the course?

Each course includes video lessons, downloadable workbooks, templates, case studies, and access to our private community. Higher-tier plans include additional resources like 1:1 coaching sessions and personalized feedback.

Do you offer any guarantees?

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee. If you complete the first two modules and don't find value in the course, we'll refund your payment in full.

Can I upgrade my plan later?

Absolutely! You can upgrade your plan at any time, and we'll apply your previous payment toward the new plan.
