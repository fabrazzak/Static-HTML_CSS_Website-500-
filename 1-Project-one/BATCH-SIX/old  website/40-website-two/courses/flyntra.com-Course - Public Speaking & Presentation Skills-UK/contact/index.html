Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can I expect a response to my inquiry?

We typically respond to all inquiries within 24-48 hours during our business hours (Monday-Friday, 9am-6pm CET). For urgent matters, please call us directly at +49 160 469 7373.

Do you offer corporate training or group discounts?

Yes, we offer customized corporate training programs and group discounts for teams of 5 or more. Please select "Corporate Training" as your subject in the contact form, and we'll provide you with tailored options based on your organization's needs.

What payment methods do you accept for courses?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, and bank transfers. Payment options will be provided when you enroll in any of our courses.

Can I schedule a consultation before enrolling in a course?

Absolutely! We offer free 15-minute consultations to discuss your goals and recommend the best course for your needs. Simply select "Course Inquiry" as your subject in the contact form and mention you'd like a consultation in your message.
