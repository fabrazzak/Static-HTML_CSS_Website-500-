Our Story

Discover the passion and purpose behind Kindara's commitment to pure, therapeutic-grade essential oils.

How It All Began

Kindara was founded in 2015 by Sarah and Michael Thompson, a husband-and-wife team with a shared passion for natural wellness. After experiencing the transformative power of essential oils during a difficult period in their lives, they set out to create a company that would make high-quality, pure essential oils accessible to everyone.

What started as a small operation in their home kitchen has grown into a trusted brand with customers around the world. Despite our growth, we've never lost sight of our original mission: to provide oils of uncompromising quality that truly make a difference in people's lives.

Today, Kindara works directly with ethical growers and distillers across the globe to source the finest botanicals. Each batch of oil is rigorously tested to ensure purity and potency before it reaches you.

Our Values

Purity

We never compromise on quality. Every essential oil is 100% pure, with no additives, fillers, or synthetic ingredients.

Sustainability

We work with growers who use sustainable farming practices and ensure fair wages for all workers in our supply chain.

Science

We combine ancient wisdom with modern science, rigorously testing each batch to verify its therapeutic properties.

Community

We believe in the power of plants to bring people together and foster connections with nature and each other.

Education

We're committed to helping our customers use essential oils safely and effectively through workshops and resources.

Integrity

We're transparent about our sourcing and production processes, and we stand behind every product we sell.
