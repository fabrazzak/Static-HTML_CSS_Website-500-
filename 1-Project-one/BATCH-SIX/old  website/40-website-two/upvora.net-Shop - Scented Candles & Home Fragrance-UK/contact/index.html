Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our products and services

What are UPVORA candles made of?

Our candles are handcrafted using premium soy wax, which is a natural, renewable resource. We use pure essential oils and phthalate-free fragrance oils for scent, and lead-free cotton wicks for a clean burn. All our ingredients are carefully selected for quality and safety.

How long do your candles typically burn?

Our standard 8oz candles have a burn time of approximately 50 hours when properly cared for. Larger candles will burn for longer. To maximize burn time, always trim the wick to 1/4 inch before lighting and allow the wax to melt completely across the surface on the first burn.

Do you offer international shipping?

Yes, we ship internationally to most countries. Shipping costs and delivery times vary depending on the destination. Please note that customers are responsible for any customs fees or import taxes that may apply to their order.

What is your return policy?

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If you're not happy with your candle for any reason, you may return it within 30 days of delivery for a full refund or exchange. The product must be unused and in its original packaging. Shipping costs are non-refundable.

Can I customize candles for special events?

Absolutely! We offer customization options for weddings, corporate events, and other special occasions. This includes custom scents, labels, and packaging. Please contact us with your requirements at least 4 weeks before your event date to discuss possibilities.
