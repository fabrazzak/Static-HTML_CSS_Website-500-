Our Journey to Natural Beauty

Founded in 2018 by skincare enthusiast Emma Brunner, Bruniva began as a small kitchen experiment with natural ingredients. Frustrated by the lack of truly natural skincare options that were both effective and affordable, Emma set out to create her own formulas.

What started as homemade remedies for friends and family quickly grew into a passion project, and then a full-fledged business. Today, Bruniva is proud to offer a complete line of natural skincare products that are loved by customers around the world.

Our name "Bruniva" combines Emma's surname "Brunner" with the Latin word "viva" meaning "alive" - representing our commitment to bringing your skin to life with nature's finest ingredients.

Our Mission & Values

Pure Ingredients

We use only the highest quality natural and organic ingredients, carefully selected for their effectiveness and safety.

Our Core Values

Natural First

We believe in the power of nature. All our products are formulated with at least 95% naturally derived ingredients, and we're constantly working to increase this percentage.

Transparency

We list every ingredient we use and explain its purpose. No hidden chemicals, no confusing terminology - just honest, clean beauty.

Sustainability

From biodegradable formulas to recyclable packaging, we're committed to reducing our environmental footprint at every step.

Community

We listen to our customers and create products that address real skincare needs. Your feedback directly shapes our product development.
