Our Story

Discover the passion and purpose behind Mindaxa's commitment to pure, therapeutic-grade essential oils

How It All Began

Mindaxa was founded in 2015 by Sophia Müller, a certified aromatherapist with a vision to bring the highest quality essential oils to wellness enthusiasts across Europe. What began as a small home-based business selling handcrafted oil blends at local markets has grown into a trusted source for premium aromatherapy products.

Sophia's journey with essential oils started during her years working as a massage therapist, where she witnessed firsthand the transformative power of aromatherapy. Frustrated by the lack of pure, unadulterated oils available to practitioners, she set out to create a company that prioritized quality, transparency, and sustainability above all else.

Today, Mindaxa works directly with ethical growers and distillers around the world to source the finest botanicals, while maintaining our commitment to fair trade practices and environmental stewardship.

Our Mission & Values

At Mindaxa, our mission is to enhance wellbeing through the therapeutic power of nature's purest essences. We believe in the profound connection between plants and people, and we're committed to preserving this relationship through ethical sourcing and sustainable practices.

Every bottle of Mindaxa oil represents our dedication to quality, purity, and environmental responsibility. We go beyond organic certification to ensure our products meet the highest standards of therapeutic efficacy while supporting the communities that grow and harvest these precious botanicals.

Our Process

1

Sourcing

We personally visit partner farms to verify sustainable growing practices and optimal harvest times. Our lavender comes from Provence, peppermint from the Pacific Northwest, and frankincense from sustainable harvests in Oman.

2

Harvesting

Plants are harvested at peak potency, often by hand at specific times of day when essential oil content is highest. We work with small-scale farmers who use traditional methods passed down through generations.

3

Distillation

Using low-pressure, low-temperature steam distillation, we gently extract the oils to preserve their full therapeutic profile. Each batch is distilled separately to maintain purity and traceability.

4

Testing

Every batch undergoes rigorous third-party GC/MS testing to verify purity, composition, and absence of contaminants. Certificates of Analysis are available for all our oils.

5

Bottling

Oils are packaged in dark glass bottles with tamper-evident seals to protect against light degradation and ensure freshness. We use recyclable materials for all packaging.
