Our Story

Discover the passion behind our handcrafted scented candles

Crafting Fragrance Experiences Since 2020

At UPVORA, we believe that scent has the power to transform spaces and elevate moods. Our journey began with a simple passion for creating beautiful, aromatic candles that bring warmth and comfort to any environment.

Each of our candles is carefully hand-poured using the finest natural ingredients, including premium soy wax, pure essential oils, and cotton wicks. We take pride in our meticulous attention to detail and commitment to quality.
