Our Story

Crafting luxury scented candles with passion and purpose

From Passion to Purpose

How UPVORA began its journey to create the finest scented candles

Crafting Fragrance Experiences Since 2020

UPVORA was born from a simple love for the way scent can transform a space and elevate a mood. Our founder, Emma Schneider, began making candles in her small kitchen as a way to bring warmth and comfort to her home. What started as a personal passion quickly grew into something more as friends and family fell in love with her creations.

Today, UPVORA remains committed to the same principles that guided those first candles: quality ingredients, meticulous craftsmanship, and scents that tell a story. Each of our candles is still hand-poured in small batches, ensuring the attention to detail that has become our signature.

Our Values
