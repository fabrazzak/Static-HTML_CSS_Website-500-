Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our products and services

How long does customization take?

Most customizations are completed within 1-2 business days after we receive your order. More complex customizations may take 3-5 business days. You'll receive an estimated completion date when you place your order.

What is your shipping policy?

We ship worldwide with delivery times varying by destination. For United Kingdom, most orders arrive within 3-5 business days after processing. International shipments typically take 7-14 business days. All orders are processed within 1-2 business days (excluding weekends and holidays).

Can I request a custom design?

Absolutely! We love creating unique custom gifts. Please contact us with your ideas and we'll work with you to bring your vision to life. Custom design requests may require additional time and could involve extra fees depending on complexity.

What is your return policy?

Due to the personalized nature of our products, we generally don't accept returns unless the item is defective or there was an error on our part. If you receive a damaged or incorrect item, please contact us within 7 days of delivery and we'll make it right.

How do I track my order?

Once your order ships, you'll receive a confirmation email with tracking information. You can also track your order by logging into your account on our website. If you have any issues tracking your package, don't hesitate to contact us.
