Introduction

At Voynest Natural Beauty ("we," "us," or "our"), we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website voynest.com (the "Site") or make a purchase from us.

We collect several types of information from and about users of our Site, including:

Personal Information

When you make a purchase or attempt to make a purchase through the Site, we collect certain information from you, including:

  • Your name
  • Billing and shipping addresses
  • Payment information (credit card numbers, etc.)
  • Email address
  • Phone number

Automatically Collected Information

When you visit the Site, we automatically collect certain information about your device and interaction with our Site, including:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Pages you view on our Site
  • Time spent on pages
  • Referring website addresses

We use the information we collect in the following ways:

  • To process and fulfill your orders

  • To comply with legal obligations

Your Rights and Choices

You may opt out of receiving promotional emails from us by following the unsubscribe instructions in those emails. Even if you opt out, we may still send you transactional or relationship messages.

If you have an account with us, you can review and change your personal information by logging into your account. You may also contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information.
