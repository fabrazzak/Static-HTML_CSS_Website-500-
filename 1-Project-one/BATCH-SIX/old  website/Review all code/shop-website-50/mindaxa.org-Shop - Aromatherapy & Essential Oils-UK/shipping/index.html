Shipping Policy

Last Updated: April 21, 2025

At Mindaxa, we strive to deliver your premium aromatherapy and essential oil products as quickly and safely as possible. This Shipping Policy outlines our delivery processes, timelines, and associated costs.

1. Processing Time

All orders are processed within 1-2 business days (Monday-Friday, excluding holidays) after receiving your order. During peak seasons or sales events, processing may take slightly longer.

You will receive an order confirmation email once your order has been processed and prepared for shipment.

2. Shipping Options & Delivery Times

Domestic Shipping (United Kingdom)

Shipping Method Delivery Time Cost
Standard Shipping 2-4 business days €4.90
Express Shipping 1-2 business days €9.90
Free Shipping 3-5 business days Free on orders over €50

International Shipping

Region Delivery Time Cost (Starting From)
European Union 5-10 business days €12.90
North America 7-14 business days €16.90
Rest of World 10-21 business days €22.90

International delivery times are estimates and may vary due to customs processing.

3. Shipping Restrictions

Due to the nature of our products, we have some shipping restrictions:

  • We cannot ship essential oils to PO Box addresses
  • Some countries restrict the import of essential oils - we'll notify you if we cannot ship to your location
  • During extreme weather conditions (hot or cold), we may delay shipping to protect product quality

4. Order Tracking

Once your order has shipped, you will receive a shipping confirmation email with tracking information. You can track your package using the provided tracking number on the carrier's website.

If you haven't received tracking information within 3 business days of placing your order, please contact us at shipping@mindaxa.org.

5. Shipping Address

Please ensure your shipping address is complete and accurate. We are not responsible for orders shipped to incorrect addresses provided by the customer.

If you need to change your shipping address after placing an order, please contact us immediately. We can only modify the address if the order hasn't been processed for shipping.

6. Customs & Import Fees

For international orders:

  • Customers are responsible for any customs duties, taxes, or fees imposed by their country
  • These charges are not included in the product or shipping costs
  • We cannot predict what these fees will be as they vary by country
  • For more information, please contact your local customs office

7. Damaged or Lost Packages

Damaged Packages

If your package arrives damaged:

  • Please take photos of the damaged package and products
  • Contact us within 7 days of delivery at support@mindaxa.org
  • We will replace damaged items at no additional cost

Lost Packages

If your tracking shows delivered but you haven't received your package:

  • First check with neighbors or your building management
  • Contact your local post office with the tracking number
  • If still unresolved, contact us and we'll investigate with the carrier

8. Special Handling

Our essential oils require special care during shipping:

  • All bottles are securely sealed and packed in leak-proof bags
  • Fragile items are cushioned with eco-friendly packing materials
  • During hot weather, we include temperature-stable packaging
  • Orders containing glass bottles are marked as "Fragile"

9. Holiday Shipping Schedule

During holiday periods, please allow extra processing and delivery time. We will announce specific holiday shipping deadlines on our website and in our newsletter.

10. Contact Us
