Our Story

About Hoterra

Founded in the vibrant coastal city of Fremantle, Hoterra began with a simple mission: to transform ordinary events into extraordinary experiences through stunning decor and high-quality party supplies.

What started as a small boutique serving local weddings has grown into Western Australia's premier event decor provider, serving clients across Perth and beyond. Our journey has been fueled by our passion for design, attention to detail, and commitment to customer satisfaction.

Today, we're proud to have decorated over 500 events, from intimate birthday celebrations to large corporate galas, each one as unique as our clients themselves.
