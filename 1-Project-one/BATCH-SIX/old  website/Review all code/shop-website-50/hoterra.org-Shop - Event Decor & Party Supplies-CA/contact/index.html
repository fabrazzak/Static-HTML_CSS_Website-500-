Contact FAQs

How quickly can I expect a response to my inquiry?

We typically respond to all inquiries within 24-48 hours during our business hours (Monday-Friday, 9am-5pm). For urgent inquiries, please call us directly at +18772552327.

Do you offer emergency event decor services?

Yes, we understand that last-minute needs can arise. We offer emergency services for an additional fee, depending on availability. Please call us directly for immediate assistance.

Can I visit your showroom to see decor options?

Absolutely! Our showroom is open by appointment Monday-Friday 9am-5pm and Saturday 10am-3pm. Please contact us to schedule your visit so we can ensure a team member is available to assist you.

What information should I include in my event decor inquiry?
To help us provide the most accurate quote, please include:
  • Event type (wedding, birthday, corporate, etc.)
  • Event date and location
  • Approximate guest count
  • Preferred color scheme or theme
  • Any specific decor items you're interested in
  • Your budget range
