Returns & Exchanges

Our hassle-free return policy ensures your complete satisfaction

Return Policy

We want you to be completely satisfied with your strenlo art prints. If for any reason you're not happy with your purchase, you may return it within 14 days of delivery for a full refund.

Important Notice

Custom orders and framed prints are final sale and cannot be returned. Sale items may only be returned for store credit. Original shipping fees are non-refundable.

Conditions for Returns:

  • The item must be in its original condition - unused, undamaged, and in the original packaging
  • All certificates of authenticity and documentation must be included
  • Proof of purchase (order number or receipt) is required
  • Returns must be initiated within 14 days of delivery

Exchange Policy

If you'd like to exchange an item for a different size or print, we're happy to help. Exchanges are subject to product availability.

How Exchanges Work:

  • Contact us within 14 days of receiving your order to initiate an exchange
  • You'll be responsible for return shipping costs unless the exchange is due to our error
  • The new item will be shipped once we receive and process your return
  • Price differences will be charged or refunded as applicable

Damaged or Defective Items

We take great care in packaging your art prints, but if your item arrives damaged or defective, please contact us immediately at info@strenlo.org with photos of the damage.

Our Process for Damaged Items:

  • We'll arrange for a replacement to be shipped at no additional cost
  • In some cases, we may ask you to return the damaged item (we'll provide a prepaid label)
  • If a replacement isn't available, we'll issue a full refund

How to Return an Item

1

Contact Us

Email info@strenlo.org with your order number and reason for return. We'll provide return instructions.

2

Package Your Item

Securely pack the item in its original packaging with all certificates and documentation included.

3

Ship It Back

Ship the package to our return center. We recommend using a trackable shipping service.

4

Receive Refund

Once received and inspected, we'll process your refund within 5 business days.

Refund Information

Refunds will be issued to the original payment method. Please allow 5-10 business days for the refund to appear in your account after we process it.

What to Expect:

  • Refunds typically process within 5 business days of receiving your return
  • Original shipping fees are non-refundable
  • Return shipping costs are the customer's responsibility unless the return is due to our error
  • Sale items will be refunded as store credit

Need Help With a Return?

Our customer service team is happy to assist with any questions about returns or exchanges.
