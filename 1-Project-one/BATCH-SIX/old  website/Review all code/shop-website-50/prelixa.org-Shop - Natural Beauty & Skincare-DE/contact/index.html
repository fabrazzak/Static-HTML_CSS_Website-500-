Frequently Asked Questions

What are your shipping options and costs?

We offer several shipping options within Germany and internationally. Standard shipping within Germany costs €4.90 and takes 2-3 business days. Express shipping is available for €9.90 with next-day delivery. International shipping costs vary by destination. All orders over €50 ship free within Germany.

Are your products really 100% natural?

Yes! All Prelixa products are formulated with at least 99% naturally derived ingredients. The remaining 1% (when present) are nature-identical preservatives required for product safety. We never use synthetic fragrances, parabens, sulfates, phthalates, or other harmful chemicals. Every ingredient is carefully selected for its efficacy and safety.

How do I know which products are right for my skin type?

Our products are clearly labeled for different skin types (dry, oily, combination, sensitive). If you're unsure, our customer service team can help you create a personalized routine. We also offer sample sizes of most products so you can try before committing to full sizes.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day satisfaction guarantee. If you're not completely happy with your purchase, you can return it within 30 days of delivery for a full refund or exchange. Products must be at least 50% full to qualify. Please contact our customer service team to initiate a return.

Do you offer wholesale for retailers?

Yes! We partner with select retailers who share our commitment to natural beauty and sustainability. Wholesale accounts receive discounted pricing, marketing support, and exclusive product offerings. Please contact our wholesale team at wholesale@prelixa.org for more information and to request a wholesale application.
