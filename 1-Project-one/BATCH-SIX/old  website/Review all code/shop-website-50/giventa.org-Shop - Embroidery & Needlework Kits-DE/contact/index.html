Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping take?

We typically process and ship orders within 1-2 business days. Delivery times vary by location: 3-5 business days within Germany, 5-10 business days for other European countries, and 10-15 business days for international orders.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, and bank transfers for European customers. All payments are processed securely through our encrypted payment system.

Do your kits include everything I need?

Yes! Each Giventa kit comes with all the materials you need to complete your project: pre-printed fabric, embroidery threads, needle, instructions, and any additional materials specific to that design. The only thing you'll need to provide is scissors.

Can I return or exchange a kit?

We accept returns of unopened kits within 30 days of delivery. For exchanges or returns, please contact our customer service team at support@giventa.org with your order number. We'll provide instructions for returning your item and processing your refund or exchange.

Do you offer wholesale or bulk orders?

Yes, we work with many retailers and craft groups. Please contact us at wholesale@giventa.org with details about your order and we'll provide pricing and shipping options for bulk purchases.
