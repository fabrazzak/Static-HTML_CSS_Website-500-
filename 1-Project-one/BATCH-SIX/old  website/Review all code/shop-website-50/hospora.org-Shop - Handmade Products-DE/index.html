Our Story

Hospora was born from a passion for preserving traditional craftsmanship in a modern world. Founded in Bad Brambach, Germany, we connect talented artisans with discerning customers who appreciate the beauty of handmade goods.

Each product in our collection is crafted with care, using time-honored techniques passed down through generations. We believe in fair trade practices and sustainable materials.
