Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book your services?

We recommend booking at least 3-6 months in advance for weddings and large events, and at least 1-2 months for smaller events. However, we sometimes have last-minute availability, so it's always worth checking with us.

Do you provide decor items only without setup?

Yes, we offer a rental-only option where you can rent our decor items and set them up yourself. This is available for most items except for complex installations that require professional setup.

Can you work with my budget?

Absolutely! We pride ourselves on being able to create beautiful decor for various budgets. During our consultation, we'll discuss your budget and suggest options that will give you the best value while achieving your desired look.

Do you offer custom decor items?

Yes, we specialize in custom decor pieces. Whether you need branded elements for a corporate event or unique centerpieces for a wedding, our team can design and create custom items to match your vision.

What areas do you serve?

We primarily serve the Bad Brambach area, but we're available for events throughout Germany. Travel fees may apply for locations more than 50km from our base. Contact us to discuss your specific location.
