Our Story

Home / About Us

From Passion to Profession

Frozira was born in 2020 from a shared love of beautiful stationery and the transformative power of putting pen to paper. What began as a small online shop selling curated notebooks has grown into a destination for premium journaling supplies and writing instruments.

Our founders, Anna and Marco, met at a stationery convention in and bonded over their appreciation for quality paper and thoughtful design. Frustrated by the lack of accessible, well-crafted stationery options, they decided to create a brand that would celebrate the art of writing in the digital age.
