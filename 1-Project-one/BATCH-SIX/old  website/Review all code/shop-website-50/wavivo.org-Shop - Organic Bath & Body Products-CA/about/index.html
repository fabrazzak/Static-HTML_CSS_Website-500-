Our Story

Discover the passion and purpose behind wavivo's organic bath and body products

From Passion to Purpose

Founded in 2020 by Sarah Williamson, wavivo began as a small kitchen experiment with natural ingredients. Frustrated by the lack of truly organic options in the market, Sarah set out to create products that were as good for your skin as they were for the environment.

What started as handmade gifts for friends and family quickly grew into a thriving business as word spread about the quality and effectiveness of our products. Today, wavivo remains committed to small-batch production, ensuring each product receives the same care and attention as those first handmade batches.

Our name, wavivo, comes from the Latin words "vita" (life) and "vivo" (to live), representing our belief in living in harmony with nature and bringing life to your skincare routine through pure, organic ingredients.

Our Values

Purity

We use only the purest organic ingredients, free from harmful chemicals, synthetic fragrances, and artificial preservatives.

Sustainability

From sourcing to packaging, we prioritize eco-friendly practices to minimize our environmental footprint.

Transparency

We believe in complete ingredient transparency - you'll always know exactly what you're putting on your skin.

Ethical Sourcing

We partner with ethical suppliers who share our commitment to fair trade and sustainable agriculture.

Cruelty-Free

All our products are vegan and never tested on animals. We're proud to be Leaping Bunny certified.

Community

We support local communities through partnerships with small farms and women-led cooperatives.
