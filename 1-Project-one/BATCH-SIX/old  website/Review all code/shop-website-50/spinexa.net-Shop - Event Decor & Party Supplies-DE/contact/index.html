Frequently Asked Questions

What are your delivery options and timelines?

We offer standard and express delivery options. Standard delivery typically takes 3-5 business days within Germany, while express delivery arrives in 1-2 business days. International shipping times vary by destination.

Do you offer custom decoration services?

Yes! Our design team specializes in creating custom decor solutions tailored to your specific event needs. Contact us with your ideas and we'll work with you to bring your vision to life.

What is your return policy?

Unopened and unused items in their original packaging can be returned within 14 days of delivery for a full refund. Custom items and perishable goods (like fresh flowers) are not eligible for return.

How can I track my order?

Once your order ships, you'll receive a confirmation email with tracking information. You can also log in to your account on our website to view order status and tracking details.

Do you offer event planning services?

While we specialize in decor and party supplies, we partner with excellent event planners across Germany. Let us know your needs and we'd be happy to recommend trusted professionals in your area.
