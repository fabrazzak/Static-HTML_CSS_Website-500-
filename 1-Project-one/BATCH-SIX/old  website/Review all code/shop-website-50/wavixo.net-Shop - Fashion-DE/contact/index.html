Frequently Asked Questions

How can I track my order?

Once your order has shipped, you'll receive a confirmation email with a tracking number. You can use this number on our website's tracking page or the carrier's website to follow your package's journey.

What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 30 days of purchase. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and with original tags attached. Please contact our customer service team to initiate a return, and we'll guide you through the process.

Do you offer international shipping?

Yes, we ship to most countries worldwide. Shipping costs and delivery times vary depending on your location. You'll see all available shipping options and costs at checkout.

How do I change or cancel my order?

If you need to change or cancel your order, please contact us immediately at support@wavixo.net or call us at +49 160 4697373. We process orders quickly, so the sooner you reach out, the better chance we have of accommodating your request.

Customer Service Hours
