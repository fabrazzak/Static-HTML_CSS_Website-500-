Our Story

Founded in 2022, Cloudova began as a small passion project with a big vision: to redefine fashion by giving pre-loved and vintage items a new life through creative upcycling.

We believe that sustainable fashion shouldn't compromise on style. Our team of designers carefully selects each vintage piece and transforms it into something truly special, blending the charm of the past with contemporary aesthetics.

Every item in our collection is unique, just like you. By choosing Cloudova, you're not just wearing fashion - you're wearing a story and making a positive impact on our planet.
