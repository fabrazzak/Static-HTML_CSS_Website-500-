Frequently Asked Questions

What are your shipping options and delivery times?

We offer standard shipping (3-5 business days) and express shipping (1-2 business days) within Germany. International shipping typically takes 7-14 business days depending on the destination. All orders are processed within 1-2 business days.

Are your products really 100% natural?

Yes! All Bruniva products contain at least 95% naturally derived ingredients, with most formulations reaching 98-100%. We're transparent about every ingredient we use and why we use it. Any synthetic ingredients we include are carefully chosen for safety and effectiveness when a natural alternative isn't available.

How do I know which products are right for my skin type?

Our website includes detailed product descriptions with recommended skin types. You can also take our quick Skin Quiz on the Shop page, or contact our skincare specialists who can provide personalized recommendations based on your specific concerns and skin type.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day satisfaction guarantee. If you're not completely happy with your purchase, you can return it within 30 days of delivery for a full refund or exchange. Products must be at least 50% full to qualify for a refund. Please contact our customer service team to initiate a return.
