Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

What materials do you use in your products?

All our products are made with natural, pet-safe materials including organic cotton, natural rubber, hemp, and sustainable wood. We never use toxic dyes, glues, or finishes. Each material is carefully selected for durability, safety, and environmental friendliness.

How long does shipping take?

We process orders within 1-2 business days. Shipping times vary by location:

  • Canada : 3-5 business days
  • International: 7-14 business days

You'll receive a tracking number once your order ships. During peak seasons, processing may take slightly longer.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day return policy for unused items in their original packaging. Due to hygiene reasons, we cannot accept returns on used pet toys. To initiate a return:

  1. Contact us at returns@floraxa.org with your order number
  2. We'll provide return instructions
  3. Once received, we'll process your refund within 5 business days

Customers are responsible for return shipping costs unless the item arrived damaged or incorrect.

Do you offer wholesale pricing?

Yes! We work with pet boutiques, groomers, and other retailers. Wholesale customers receive:

  • Discounts starting at 30% for orders over $500
  • Custom branding options
  • Dropshipping available

Contact our wholesale team at wholesale@floraxa.org for more information and to request a catalog.

How do I clean your products?

Cleaning instructions vary by product:

  • Fabric items: Machine wash cold with mild detergent, air dry
  • Rubber toys: Wash with warm soapy water, rinse thoroughly
  • Wooden items: Wipe with damp cloth, do not submerge
  • Rope toys: Can be washed in a mesh laundry bag

Always check the care label on your specific product. Avoid bleach or harsh chemicals.
