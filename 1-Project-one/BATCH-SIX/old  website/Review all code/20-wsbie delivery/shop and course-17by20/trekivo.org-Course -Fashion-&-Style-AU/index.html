Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our fashion courses and learning platform.

How do I access the courses after purchasing?

After completing your purchase, you'll receive an email with login details to our learning platform. All your purchased courses will be available in your account dashboard immediately.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time through your account settings. Your access will continue until the end of your current billing period.

Are there any prerequisites for the courses?

Most of our courses are designed for beginners with no prior fashion knowledge. Some advanced courses may recommend completing foundational courses first, but this will be clearly stated in the course description.

How long do I have access to each course?

With a subscription plan, you have access to all courses for as long as your subscription is active. If you purchase individual courses separately, you'll have lifetime access to those specific courses.

Do you offer certificates upon completion?

Yes, all our courses include a certificate of completion that you can download and share once you've finished all course materials and assignments.
