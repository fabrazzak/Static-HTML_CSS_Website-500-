Our Fashion Journey

Discover the story behind Trekivo Fashion and our passion for style education

Our Mission

Trekivo Fashion was founded in 2018 with a simple goal: to make high-quality fashion education accessible to everyone in Australia and beyond. We believe that personal style is a powerful form of self-expression and that everyone deserves to feel confident in their clothing choices.

Our team of industry professionals has created comprehensive courses that combine practical knowledge with creative inspiration. Whether you're looking to refine your personal style, understand fashion trends, or build a versatile wardrobe, we have courses designed to meet your needs.

Based in Fremantle, Western Australia, we're proud to serve students across the country with our online platform while maintaining strong connections to the local fashion community.

Our Core Values

These principles guide everything we do at Trekivo Fashion

Quality Education

We're committed to providing comprehensive, well-structured courses taught by industry professionals with real-world experience.

Inclusivity

Fashion is for everyone. Our courses are designed to be accessible and relevant to people of all ages, sizes, and backgrounds.

Our Story

How Trekivo Fashion became Australia's premier online fashion school

2018

Founded in Fremantle

Trekivo Fashion was launched by Sophia Williams with a single course on personal styling. Our first students were local fashion enthusiasts looking to improve their style knowledge.

2019

Expanded Course Offerings

We added three new courses and brought on James Wilson as our first instructor. Our student base grew to over 1,000 across Australia.

2020

Pandemic Pivot

With in-person fashion events cancelled, we saw increased demand for online education. We revamped our platform and added live virtual workshops.

2021

Sustainability Focus

Olivia Martinez joined to lead our new sustainable fashion curriculum as awareness grew about fashion's environmental impact.

2022

Subscription Launch

We introduced subscription plans giving students access to all courses, resulting in 300% growth in enrollments.

2023

10,000 Students

We celebrated helping over 10,000 students improve their fashion knowledge and personal style through our courses.
