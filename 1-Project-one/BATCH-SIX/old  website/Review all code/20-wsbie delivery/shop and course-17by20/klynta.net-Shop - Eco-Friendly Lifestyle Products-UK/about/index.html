Our Story

Discover the passion behind our eco-friendly mission

How It All Began

Klynta was founded in 2025 by environmental enthusiasts who were frustrated by the lack of accessible, stylish, and truly sustainable alternatives to everyday products. What started as a small market stall selling handmade bamboo toothbrushes has grown into a trusted source for eco-conscious consumers worldwide.

Our journey began when our founder, Sarah Green, returned from a backpacking trip through Southeast Asia shocked by the amount of plastic pollution she witnessed. Determined to make a difference, she started creating simple, sustainable alternatives to common household items, focusing first on products that would have the biggest environmental impact when swapped.

Today, we carefully curate products from ethical suppliers who share our commitment to sustainability. Every item in our store meets strict environmental standards, from sourcing to production to packaging.
