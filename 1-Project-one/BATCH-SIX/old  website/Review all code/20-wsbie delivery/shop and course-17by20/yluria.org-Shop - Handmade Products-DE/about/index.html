Our Story

Discover the passion, craftsmanship, and values behind yluria's unique handmade products

Who We Are

Founded in 2018, yluria began as a small workshop in the heart of Germany with a simple mission: to create beautiful, meaningful handmade products that connect people to the art of craftsmanship.

What started as a passion project between two friends has grown into a community of skilled artisans creating unique pieces that tell a story. Each product in our collection reflects our commitment to quality, sustainability, and the preservation of traditional crafting techniques.

Today, we work with over 50 artisans across Europe, supporting their craft and helping to keep these traditions alive for future generations.

Our Core Values

These principles guide everything we do at yluria, from product creation to customer service

Artisan First

We believe in fair compensation and ethical working conditions for all artisans who create our products.

Sustainable Practices

From material sourcing to packaging, we prioritize eco-friendly solutions that minimize our environmental impact.

Uncompromising Quality

Every product must meet our high standards of craftsmanship before it reaches your home.

Authentic Storytelling

We share the stories behind each product and the artisans who make them, creating meaningful connections.

Innovative Design

We blend traditional techniques with contemporary design to create timeless yet modern pieces.

Community Focus

We're committed to building a community that values craftsmanship and mindful consumption.
