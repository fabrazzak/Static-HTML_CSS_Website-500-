Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions below

How long does shipping take?

We typically process and ship orders within 1-2 business days. Shipping times vary depending on your location: 3-5 business days within Germany, 5-10 business days for other European countries, and 7-14 business days for international orders. You'll receive a tracking number once your order ships.

Do you accept custom orders?

Yes! We love creating custom pieces. Please contact us with your ideas, and we'll work with you to bring your vision to life. Custom orders typically take 2-4 weeks to complete, depending on complexity. We'll provide a quote and timeline after discussing your requirements.

What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 14 days of delivery for unused items in their original condition. Due to the handmade nature of our products, custom orders and personalized items cannot be returned. To initiate a return, please contact us with your order number. Return shipping costs are the customer's responsibility unless the item arrived damaged or defective.

How do I care for my handmade products?

Care instructions vary by product. Each item comes with specific care instructions, but generally: ceramic pieces should be hand washed (unless marked dishwasher safe), wooden items should be treated with food-safe oil periodically, and textile products should be washed gently in cold water. Avoid prolonged exposure to direct sunlight or extreme temperatures for all products.

Do you offer wholesale for retailers?

Yes, we work with select retailers. Please contact our wholesale department at wholesale@yluria.org with information about your store, including location, website, and the types of products you're interested in. We'll send you our wholesale catalog and terms. Minimum order quantities apply.
