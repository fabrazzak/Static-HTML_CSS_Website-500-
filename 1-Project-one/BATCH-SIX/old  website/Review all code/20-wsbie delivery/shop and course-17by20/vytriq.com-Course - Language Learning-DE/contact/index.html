Common Questions

How quickly can I expect a response to my inquiry?

We typically respond to all inquiries within 24 hours during our business hours. For urgent matters, please call our support line for immediate assistance.

Do you offer in-person consultations?

Yes, we welcome visitors to our headquarters in Bad Brambach by appointment. Please contact us to schedule a consultation with one of our language learning advisors.

What information should I include in my message?

Please include your name, contact information, and as many details as possible about your question or concern. If you're inquiring about a specific course, include the course name and your current language level if known.
