Our Story & Mission

How trayxa Began

trayxa was founded in 2020 by a group of meditation practitioners who saw the growing need for accessible, high-quality mindfulness education. What started as a small local meditation group has blossomed into an international platform serving thousands of students worldwide.

Our founders, coming from diverse backgrounds in psychology, neuroscience, and traditional meditation practices, recognized that while interest in mindfulness was growing, many people struggled to find authentic, science-backed instruction that fit into modern lifestyles.

We set out to create a bridge between ancient wisdom and contemporary life, making meditation practices practical and relevant for today's challenges. Over the years, we've refined our approach based on student feedback and the latest research in contemplative neuroscience.

Today, trayxa remains committed to our original vision: making transformative mindfulness practices available to everyone, regardless of experience level or background.

Our Core Mission

What drives us to share mindfulness with the world

Compassion

We believe mindfulness should cultivate kindness—toward oneself and others. Our teachings emphasize compassion as the foundation of all practice.

Education

We're committed to making meditation accessible through clear, structured learning paths that meet students where they are.

Science

We integrate traditional wisdom with contemporary neuroscience to create evidence-based programs with measurable benefits.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide everything we do

Authenticity

We teach only what we've practiced deeply ourselves. Our instructors maintain personal meditation practices and continually deepen their understanding.

Inclusivity

Mindfulness is for everyone. We create welcoming spaces that honor diverse backgrounds and make practices accessible across cultures and identities.

Integrity

We're committed to ethical teaching, transparent about the benefits and limitations of practice, and careful not to overpromise results.

Innovation

While rooted in tradition, we continually evolve our methods based on student feedback and scientific research to maximize effectiveness.

Community

We believe transformation happens in relationship. Our programs foster supportive connections among practitioners.
