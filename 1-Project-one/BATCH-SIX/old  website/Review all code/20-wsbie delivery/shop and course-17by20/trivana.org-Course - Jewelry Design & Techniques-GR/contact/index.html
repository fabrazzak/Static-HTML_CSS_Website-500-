Frequently Asked Questions

How soon can I expect a response to my inquiry?

We typically respond to all inquiries within 24-48 hours during our business hours (Monday-Friday, 9AM-6PM GMT+2). If you don't hear back within this timeframe, please check your spam folder or contact us by phone.

Do you offer in-person consultations?

Yes! We're happy to schedule in-person consultations at our Velestino studio by appointment. Please contact us to arrange a convenient time. Virtual consultations via Zoom are also available for international students.

What's the best way to reach you for urgent matters?

For urgent matters related to ongoing courses or immediate enrollment questions, please call us at +30 6980122386 during business hours. For after-hours emergencies related to active courses, your instructor will provide contact information.
