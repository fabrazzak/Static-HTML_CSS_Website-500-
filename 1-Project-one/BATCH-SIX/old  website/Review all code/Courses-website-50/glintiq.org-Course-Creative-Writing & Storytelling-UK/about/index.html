Our Story

Discover the passion and purpose behind Glintiq's creative writing and storytelling courses.

Empowering Writers Since 2020

Glintiq was founded with a simple mission: to make high-quality creative writing education accessible to everyone. What began as a small workshop series has grown into a comprehensive online platform serving thousands of writers worldwide.

Our founder, Dr. Eleanor Bennett, recognized the need for structured yet flexible writing education that combines craft fundamentals with practical application. After teaching creative writing at the university level for 15 years, she saw how many aspiring writers were excluded from traditional programs due to geography, finances, or life circumstances.

Today, Glintiq brings together a team of award-winning authors, experienced educators, and passionate storytellers committed to helping writers at all levels develop their craft and find their unique voice.

Our Mission & Values

These core principles guide everything we do at Glintiq.

Craft Excellence

We believe in teaching the fundamentals of strong writing while encouraging creative experimentation and individual voice.

Inclusive Community

Writing can be solitary, but growth happens in community. We foster supportive spaces where all voices are valued.

Lifelong Learning

Great writers are always learning. We provide resources for writers at every stage of their journey.

Accessibility

Quality writing education should be available to everyone, regardless of background or financial means.
