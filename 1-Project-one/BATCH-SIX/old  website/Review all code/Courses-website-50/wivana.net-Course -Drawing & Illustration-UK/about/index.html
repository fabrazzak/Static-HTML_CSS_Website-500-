How It All Began

Founded in 2025 by a group of passionate artists and educators, wivana began as a small studio offering local drawing classes in Bad Brambach, United Kingdom. What started as a way to share our love for art quickly grew into something much bigger.

We noticed that many aspiring artists struggled to find quality, affordable art education - either classes were too expensive, too rigid, or didn't fit into busy schedules. We knew there had to be a better way.

In 2025, we launched our first online course, bringing our teaching methods to students worldwide. The response was overwhelming, and we've been growing and evolving ever since.

Today, wivana serves thousands of students across 50+ countries, but we've never lost sight of our original mission: to make high-quality art education accessible to everyone, everywhere.

Our Core Values

These principles guide everything we do at wivana

Accessible Education

We believe everyone deserves access to quality art instruction, regardless of background or budget. Our flexible pricing and free resources make learning possible for all.

Creative Freedom

Art should be about expression, not rigid rules. We teach foundational skills while encouraging each student to develop their unique artistic voice.

Community Support

Learning is better together. Our vibrant community provides feedback, inspiration, and encouragement at every step of the artistic journey.
