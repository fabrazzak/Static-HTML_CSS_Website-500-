Our Story

Flyntra was founded in 2015 with a simple mission: to help people find their voice and communicate with confidence. What began as a small workshop series has grown into a leading provider of public speaking and presentation skills training, serving thousands of students worldwide.

Our founder, Sarah Williams, recognized that the fear of public speaking was holding back talented professionals from reaching their full potential. She assembled a team of expert communicators to develop a proven methodology that combines psychological techniques with practical speaking skills.

Today, Flyntra offers a comprehensive curriculum of courses, from beginner fundamentals to advanced executive communication techniques. Our approach has helped professionals at companies like Google, Amazon, and McKinsey improve their presentation skills and advance their careers.
