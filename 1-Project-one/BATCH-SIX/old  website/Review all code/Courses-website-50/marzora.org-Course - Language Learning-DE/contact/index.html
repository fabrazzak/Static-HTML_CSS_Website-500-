Common Questions

Quick answers to frequently asked questions

How long does it take to get a response?

Our team typically responds to inquiries within 24 hours during business days. For urgent matters, please call our support line during operating hours.

Do you offer phone support in multiple languages?

Yes! Our support team can assist you in English, German, French, and Spanish. Let us know your preferred language when you call.

Where can I find my course access information?

After signing up, you should receive an email with your login credentials and access instructions. If you didn't receive it, please check your spam folder or contact us for assistance.

Can I schedule a call with a language advisor?

Absolutely! Our language advisors are available for scheduled calls to help you choose the right course or discuss your learning goals. Please include your availability when you contact us.
