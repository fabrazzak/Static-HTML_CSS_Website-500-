Frequently Asked Questions

How long do I have access to the courses?

You'll have access to all purchased courses for as long as you maintain your subscription. If you cancel, you'll retain access until the end of your current billing period. We also offer lifetime access options for certain course bundles.

Are the courses suitable for complete beginners?

Absolutely! Our courses are designed to meet you where you are. Whether you're just starting your organization journey or looking to refine your systems, our step-by-step approach ensures success at any level.

How much time should I dedicate to the courses?

Most students find 2-3 hours per week sufficient to make steady progress. Each course is self-paced, allowing you to move faster or slower as needed. The key is consistency rather than intensity.

What if I need personalized help?

Our Standard and Premium plans include various levels of personalized support, from group coaching calls to 1:1 sessions. We also offer add-on consulting packages for specific challenges.

Can I gift a course subscription?

Yes! We offer gift subscriptions that can be emailed directly to the recipient. These make wonderful presents for friends or family members who could benefit from a more organized home.
