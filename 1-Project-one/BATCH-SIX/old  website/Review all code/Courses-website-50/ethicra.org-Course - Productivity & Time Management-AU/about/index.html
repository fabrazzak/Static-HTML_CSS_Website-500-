About Ethicra

Helping professionals and organizations achieve peak productivity through science-backed time management and workflow optimization strategies.

Our Story

From Frustration to Transformation

Ethicra was founded in 2020 by productivity expert David Chen after he witnessed firsthand how poor time management was limiting professionals across industries. While working as a management consultant, David noticed that even the most talented individuals often struggled with workflow inefficiencies, leading to burnout and missed opportunities.

What began as informal coaching sessions with colleagues grew into a comprehensive methodology that combines behavioral science, cognitive psychology, and practical business experience. Today, Ethicra has helped over 10,000 professionals transform their productivity through our courses, coaching, and corporate training programs.

Our name, Ethicra, reflects our philosophy: Ethical Productivity. We believe true productivity isn't about working more hours, but about working smarter to create space for what matters most in both professional and personal life.

Our Mission

Empowering Purposeful Productivity

At Ethicra, our mission is to help individuals and organizations achieve more meaningful results in less time through evidence-based productivity strategies. We reject the toxic "hustle culture" that glorifies busyness over effectiveness, instead focusing on sustainable systems that align with human psychology and physiology.

We believe that when people master their time and attention, they don't just become more productive—they become more present, more creative, and more fulfilled in all areas of life.

Our Core Values

Evidence-Based

We ground our methods in scientific research from psychology, neuroscience, and behavioral economics, not just trendy hacks.

Human-Centered

Our approach respects natural human limitations and works with your biology, not against it.

Holistic

We address productivity as part of a complete lifestyle that includes health, relationships, and personal growth.

Practical

Every strategy we teach is immediately applicable and adaptable to real-world professional environments.

Community

We foster supportive networks where members learn from and motivate each other toward shared goals.

Sustainability

Our methods are designed for long-term success, not short-term gains that lead to burnout.
