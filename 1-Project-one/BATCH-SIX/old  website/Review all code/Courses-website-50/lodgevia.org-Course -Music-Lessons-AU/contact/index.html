Common Questions

Find quick answers to frequent inquiries

How do I book my first lesson?

You can book your first lesson by filling out our contact form, calling us at +61 8 6256 5662, or emailing info@lodgevia.org. We'll help you choose the right instructor and schedule your trial lesson at a convenient time.

What should I bring to my first lesson?

For your first lesson, just bring yourself and any materials you already have (like an instrument if you own one). We provide all necessary materials for trial lessons. Your instructor will advise you on what to bring for subsequent lessons.

Do you offer gift certificates?

Yes! We offer gift certificates for any amount that can be applied toward lessons. They make perfect gifts for birthdays, holidays, or any special occasion. Contact us to purchase a gift certificate.

What is your cancellation policy?

We require 24 hours notice for lesson cancellations. Lessons canceled with less than 24 hours notice will be charged in full. In case of illness, please notify us as soon as possible and we'll try to reschedule.
