Common Questions

What's the best way to contact Bright Path Teaching?

For general inquiries, the contact form on this page is the most efficient way to reach us. Our team typically responds within 24 hours on weekdays. For urgent matters, please call us directly at +61 8 6256 5662 during our business hours.

Do you offer in-person consultations?

Yes, we welcome prospective students to visit our Fremantle location for in-person consultations. We recommend scheduling an appointment in advance to ensure one of our advisors is available to assist you. Walk-ins are also welcome during our business hours.

How soon can I expect a response to my inquiry?

We strive to respond to all inquiries within one business day. Responses may take slightly longer on weekends or during peak enrollment periods. If you haven't heard back within 48 hours, please check your spam folder or contact us again.

What information should I include in my message?

To help us respond effectively, please include: your name, contact information, the language course you're interested in, your current proficiency level (if known), and any specific questions or requirements you have. The more details you provide, the better we can assist you.

Do you offer virtual consultations?

Yes, we offer Zoom or phone consultations for those who can't visit in person. Please indicate your preference for a virtual meeting when contacting us, and we'll arrange a convenient time. Virtual consultations typically last 20-30 minutes.
