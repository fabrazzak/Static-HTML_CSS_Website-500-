How Tornivo Began

Tornivo was founded in 2020 by a group of professional digital artists who saw a gap in quality, accessible digital art education. What started as a small passion project among friends has grown into a thriving platform helping thousands of students worldwide.

Our founders, all working professionals in the digital art industry, were frustrated by the lack of comprehensive courses that combined technical skills with artistic development. They envisioned a learning platform that would empower creatives at all levels to develop their skills and find their unique artistic voice.

Today, Tornivo brings together some of the most talented digital artists and educators to create courses that are both technically rigorous and creatively inspiring. We're proud to have built a global community of artists who support and learn from each other.
