Discover the passion and purpose behind Hostari's commitment to transforming communication skills worldwide.

How It All Began

Hostari was founded in 2018 by a group of communication experts who shared a common frustration: despite the critical importance of public speaking skills in professional success, most training programs focused on theory rather than practical, actionable techniques.

After years of coaching executives, entrepreneurs, and professionals from all industries, our founders developed a unique methodology that combines neuroscience-based techniques with performance psychology to create rapid, lasting improvement in speaking abilities.

What started as a small workshop series in Perth has grown into a nationally recognized training organization with thousands of satisfied clients across Australia and beyond.
