Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our courses, enrollment process, payments, and more. If you can't find what you're looking for, feel free to contact us.

General Questions

What is Trevola and what do you offer?

Trevola is an online education platform specializing in professional blogging and content writing courses. We offer comprehensive training programs designed to help individuals develop their writing skills, build successful blogs, and create compelling content for various platforms. Our courses range from beginner to advanced levels and cover topics like SEO writing, freelance writing, content strategy, and more.

Who are your courses designed for?

Our courses are designed for:

  • Aspiring bloggers who want to start their own blog
  • Content writers looking to improve their skills
  • Freelancers wanting to build their writing business
  • Business owners who need to create better content
  • Marketing professionals seeking to enhance their content strategy
  • Anyone interested in developing professional writing skills

We offer courses for all skill levels, from complete beginners to experienced writers.

How are your courses different from other writing courses?

Trevola courses stand out because:

  • Practical Focus: We emphasize actionable skills you can apply immediately
  • Industry Experts: Our instructors are active professionals with proven success
  • Comprehensive Curriculum: Courses cover both creative and technical aspects
  • Community Support: Access to student communities and instructor feedback
  • Up-to-Date Content: Regular updates to reflect current industry trends
  • Flexible Learning: Self-paced courses with lifetime access to materials

Course Questions

Do I need any prior experience to join your courses?

No prior experience is required for most of our courses. We offer beginner-friendly content with step-by-step guidance. Each course listing indicates the recommended experience level:

  • Beginner: No experience needed
  • Intermediate: Some basic knowledge helpful
  • Advanced: For experienced writers looking to specialize

Our courses are designed to take you from complete beginner to proficient writer, regardless of your starting point.

What format do the courses take?

Our courses feature a mix of:

  • Video Lessons: High-quality instructional videos
  • Written Materials: Downloadable guides and resources
  • Practical Exercises: Hands-on assignments to apply what you learn
  • Quizzes: To test your understanding
  • Community Access: Discussion forums with other students

All content is available on-demand so you can learn at your own pace. Some courses also include live Q&A sessions with instructors.

How much time should I dedicate to a course each week?

We recommend dedicating 4-6 hours per week to go through the materials and complete assignments for most courses. However, you can progress at your own pace - all content is available on-demand once released.

Here's a typical weekly breakdown:

  • 2-3 hours watching video lessons
  • 1-2 hours completing exercises
  • 1 hour participating in discussions or Q&A

Some intensive courses may require more time, while shorter workshops may require less. Each course listing includes estimated time commitments.

Can I get personalized feedback on my writing?

Yes! The level of feedback depends on your enrollment option:

  • Basic Access: Peer feedback in community forums
  • Standard Membership: Monthly group feedback sessions with instructors
  • Premium Membership: Personalized 1-on-1 feedback on your work

Many courses also include automated feedback on quizzes and some assignments. For detailed writing feedback, we recommend our Standard or Premium memberships.

Enrollment & Access

How do I enroll in a course?

Enrolling is simple:

  1. Browse our course catalog and select the course you're interested in
  2. Click the "Enroll Now" button
  3. Choose your payment option (one-time purchase or membership)
  4. Create an account or log in if you already have one
  5. Complete the payment process
  6. You'll receive immediate access to the course materials

If you need help, our support team is available to guide you through the process.

How long do I have access to the course materials?

Access duration depends on your enrollment type:

  • Individual Course Purchase: Lifetime access to that specific course
  • Monthly Membership: Access to all courses while your membership is active
  • Annual Membership: Access to all courses for one year

Even if you cancel a membership, you'll retain access to any individually purchased courses forever. We also provide downloadable resources you can keep regardless of your membership status.

Can I access courses on mobile devices?

Yes! Our platform is fully responsive and works on:

  • Smartphones (iOS and Android)
  • Tablets
  • Laptops and desktop computers

You can access course videos, materials, and even complete assignments from any device with an internet connection. Our mobile app (available on iOS and Android) provides an optimized learning experience on the go.

Some features like certain quizzes or interactive exercises may work better on larger screens, but all core content is mobile-friendly.

What if I'm not satisfied with a course?

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee for all course purchases. If you're not completely satisfied with your purchase, simply contact us within the first two weeks for a full refund, no questions asked.

For memberships, you can cancel at any time and your access will continue until the end of your current billing period. Membership fees are non-refundable after the 14-day trial period.

We're committed to your satisfaction and will work with you to address any concerns about course content or your learning experience.

Payment & Pricing

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major payment methods:

  • Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover)
  • PayPal
  • Apple Pay
  • Google Pay
  • Bank Transfers (for annual memberships)

All payments are processed securely through our encrypted payment gateway. We don't store your full payment details on our servers.

For enterprise or group enrollments, we also accept purchase orders and can arrange custom payment terms.

Do you offer discounts or scholarships?

Yes, we offer several ways to save:

  • Seasonal Sales: Discounts during major holidays and events
  • Bundle Deals: Discounts when purchasing multiple courses together
  • Annual Membership: 15% cheaper than monthly payments

  • Scholarships: Limited need-based scholarships available

To inquire about current discounts or scholarship opportunities, please contact our team. We also occasionally offer free workshops and mini-courses that you can try before committing to a full program.

Can I get an invoice or receipt for my purchase?

Absolutely. After completing your purchase:

  • You'll receive an email confirmation with your receipt
  • You can download invoices from your account dashboard
  • For custom invoices (like adding VAT or company details), contact support

All our receipts and invoices include the necessary details for tax or reimbursement purposes. If you need a specific format for your accounting system, let us know and we'll do our best to accommodate.

Technical Support

What are the technical requirements for taking courses?

Our platform works with most modern devices and browsers. Here's what you'll need:

  • Device: Computer, tablet, or smartphone
  • Internet Connection: Minimum 5Mbps for video streaming
  • Browser: Latest version of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge
  • Software: PDF reader for downloadable materials
  • Storage: Some courses have downloadable files (100MB-1GB)

For the best experience, we recommend:

  • Using a computer for writing assignments
  • Headphones for clear audio
  • Updated browser with JavaScript enabled

Most courses don't require special software, but some advanced courses may recommend specific tools (like SEO plugins or writing apps). These are always listed in the course requirements.

What should I do if I'm having trouble accessing a course?

If you're experiencing access issues, try these steps:

  1. Refresh the page and try again
  2. Clear your browser cache and cookies
  3. Try a different browser (Chrome usually works best)
  4. Check your internet connection
  5. Log out and back in to your account

If problems persist:

  • Contact our support team with details of the issue
  • Include any error messages you're seeing
  • Let us know what device and browser you're using

We typically respond to technical support requests within 24 hours (often much sooner). For urgent issues during business hours, use the live chat feature on our website.

Do you offer certificates upon course completion?

Yes! All our courses offer certificates of completion when you:

  • Complete all video lessons
  • Finish required assignments
  • Pass any quizzes (if applicable)

Certificates include:

  • Your name
  • Course title
  • Completion date
  • Instructor signature
  • Unique verification code

You can download your certificate as a PDF or share it directly on LinkedIn. While our certificates demonstrate your completion of training, please note they're not accredited academic credentials.
