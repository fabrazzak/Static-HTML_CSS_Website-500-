Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need any prior experience to join these courses?

No prior experience is required for most of our courses. We offer beginner-friendly content with step-by-step guidance. Our courses are designed to take you from complete beginner to proficient writer, regardless of your starting point.

How long do I have access to the course materials?

You'll have access to all course materials for as long as your subscription is active. If you cancel, you'll retain access until the end of your current billing period. We also provide downloadable resources you can keep forever.

Can I get personalized feedback on my writing?

Yes! Our Standard and Premium plans include feedback opportunities. Premium members receive monthly detailed reviews of their work, while Standard members can participate in group feedback sessions.

What if I'm not satisfied with the course?

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee. If you're not completely satisfied with your purchase, simply contact us within the first two weeks for a full refund, no questions asked.

How much time should I dedicate to the courses each week?

We recommend dedicating 4-6 hours per week to go through the materials and complete assignments. However, you can progress at your own pace - all content is available on-demand once released.
