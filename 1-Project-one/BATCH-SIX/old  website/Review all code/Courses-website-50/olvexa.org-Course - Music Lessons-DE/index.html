Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need any prior experience to join the courses?

No prior experience is needed for our beginner courses. We have courses designed for absolute beginners as well as more advanced players looking to improve their skills.

What equipment do I need for online music lessons?

You'll need your instrument (piano, guitar, violin, etc.), a computer or tablet with internet access, and preferably headphones. A webcam is helpful but not required for most courses.

How do the live lessons work?

Live lessons are conducted via Zoom at scheduled times. You'll receive a link to join the session, where you can interact directly with the instructor and other students in your class.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time. You'll retain access to the course materials until the end of your current billing period.

Is there a money-back guarantee?

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee if you're not satisfied with your course. Just contact our support team within the first two weeks for a full refund.
