Our Story & Mission

Discover the passion and expertise behind Plorix Photography. We're dedicated to helping photographers of all levels achieve their creative potential.

Who We Are

Founded in 2015, Plorix began as a small group of professional photographers who shared a common goal: to make high-quality photography education accessible to everyone. What started as in-person workshops has grown into a comprehensive online learning platform serving thousands of students worldwide.

Our team brings together decades of combined experience across all aspects of photography - from commercial and portrait work to photojournalism and fine art. We've worked with major brands, publications, and galleries, and now we're channeling that expertise into creating the best online photography courses available.

At Plorix, we believe that great photography isn't about having the most expensive gear - it's about developing your creative eye, mastering technical skills, and finding your unique visual voice.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide everything we do

Quality Education

We're committed to providing the highest quality photography education, with courses designed by working professionals who are also skilled educators.

Community Focus

Photography is better together. We foster a supportive community where students can share work, get feedback, and grow together.

Creative Freedom

We don't teach a "right" way to take photos. Instead, we give you the tools to develop your own unique style and creative vision.

Accessibility

Great photography education shouldn't be limited by location or budget. Our courses are designed to be affordable and accessible worldwide.

Practical Skills

Our courses focus on real-world skills you can use immediately, whether you're shooting for fun or building a professional career.

Passion for Photography

Above all, we share a deep love for photography and are dedicated to helping others discover the joy of creating beautiful images.
