Common Questions

Answers to frequently asked questions

How quickly can I expect a response to my inquiry?

We typically respond to all inquiries within 24 hours during business days (Monday-Friday). For urgent matters, please call us during business hours for immediate assistance.

What information should I include in my support request?

Please include your full name, the email address associated with your account (if applicable), details about the course or issue you're contacting us about, and any relevant screenshots or error messages you're receiving.

Do you offer personalized branding consultations?

Yes! Our Premium plan includes monthly 1:1 coaching sessions. You can also purchase individual consultations. Contact us for availability and pricing.

Can I schedule a call with an instructor?

Our Standard and Premium plan members can schedule calls with instructors through our member portal. If you're not yet a member, you can book an introductory call through our contact form.
