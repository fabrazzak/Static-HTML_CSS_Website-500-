Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our courses

Do I need any experience to start?

No prior experience is necessary for our beginner courses. We welcome complete newcomers and provide all the foundational knowledge you'll need to start your mindfulness journey. Our instructors are skilled at meeting students where they are in their practice.

How much time should I dedicate daily?

We recommend starting with just 10-15 minutes per day for beginners. As you progress, you may naturally want to extend your practice. Our courses provide guidance on building a sustainable routine that fits your lifestyle. Even short, consistent practices yield significant benefits.

What equipment do I need?

All you need is a quiet space where you can sit comfortably. A meditation cushion or chair can be helpful but isn't required. We provide all course materials digitally, including guided meditations and worksheets. A smartphone, tablet, or computer with internet access is needed to view course content.

Can I access courses on mobile devices?

Yes! Our platform is fully responsive and works on smartphones, tablets, and computers. You can stream or download guided meditations to practice anywhere. Many students find mobile access convenient for maintaining their practice while traveling or during breaks at work.

What if I miss a live session?

All live sessions are recorded and available for replay within 24 hours. You'll have access to course materials for one year after enrollment, allowing you to learn at your own pace. Our community forum also helps you stay connected between sessions.

Is there any certification?

Our Mindfulness Teacher Training course provides certification upon completion. For other courses, we offer certificates of completion that document your training hours. These can be valuable for professional development or personal achievement records.
