Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need any prior experience to start these courses? +

Not at all! Our courses are designed to accommodate complete beginners. We start with the very basics and gradually build up your skills. Many of our most successful students began with no prior calligraphy or lettering experience.

What supplies do I need to get started? +

For most courses, you'll need basic supplies like calligraphy pens, nibs, ink, and paper. However, each course has a detailed supply list in the introduction, and we provide recommendations for budget-friendly starter kits. The Brush Lettering course only requires brush pens, which we can recommend based on your budget.

How much time should I dedicate to practice? +

We recommend practicing for at least 15-30 minutes daily for best results. However, even practicing a few times a week will show progress. The courses are self-paced, so you can adjust based on your schedule. Consistency is more important than the length of each practice session.

Can I access the courses on mobile devices? +

Yes! Our platform is fully responsive and works on smartphones, tablets, and computers. You can watch lessons and download practice sheets from any device. Some students even practice while watching lessons on their phones or tablets.

What if I'm not satisfied with the courses? +

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee with no questions asked. If you're not completely satisfied with your learning experience, simply contact our support team for a full refund within 30 days of purchase.
