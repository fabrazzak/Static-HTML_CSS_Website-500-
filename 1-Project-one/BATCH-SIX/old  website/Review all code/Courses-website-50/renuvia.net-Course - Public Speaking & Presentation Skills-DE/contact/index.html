Common Questions

Quick answers to some of our most frequently asked questions

How soon can I expect a response to my inquiry?

We strive to respond to all inquiries within 24 hours during our business hours (Monday-Friday, 9am-6pm CET). For more complex questions that require consultation with our training team, we may need up to 48 hours to provide a thorough response.

Do you offer customized corporate training programs?

Yes! We specialize in creating tailored communication training programs for organizations of all sizes. Our corporate programs can be customized to address your specific industry challenges, company culture, and learning objectives. Contact us to discuss your organization's needs.

What payment methods do you accept for courses?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, and bank transfers. For corporate clients, we also offer invoice payment options with net 30 terms.

Can I schedule a consultation call before enrolling?

Absolutely. We offer free 30-minute consultation calls to help you determine which of our courses or services would be the best fit for your needs. Use our contact form to request a consultation, and we'll schedule a time that works for you.
