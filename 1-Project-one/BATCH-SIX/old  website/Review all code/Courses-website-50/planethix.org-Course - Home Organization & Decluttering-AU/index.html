Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between the Basic and Standard plans?
The Basic plan gives you access to our 3 core courses (Decluttering Basics, Space Optimization, and Sustainable Organization) along with email support. The Standard plan includes all our courses (including future releases), live Q&A sessions, exclusive worksheets, priority email support, and monthly challenges. Most students find the Standard plan offers the best value.
How much time should I dedicate to the courses each week?
We recommend dedicating 2-3 hours per week to watch the lessons and complete the exercises. However, our courses are self-paced, so you can move faster or slower as needed. The key is consistency - even 30 minutes a day can lead to significant progress in organizing your home.
Do I need any special tools or supplies to take the courses?
No special tools are required! We'll guide you through using what you already have at home. As you progress, you might want to invest in some basic organization products (like storage bins or drawer dividers), but we'll provide budget-friendly recommendations and DIY alternatives.
Can I cancel my subscription at any time?
Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time through your account settings. There are no cancellation fees, and you'll continue to have access until the end of your current billing period. We'd appreciate your feedback on how we can improve if you do decide to cancel.
Is there a money-back guarantee?
Absolutely! We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee with no questions asked. If you're not completely satisfied with your courses within the first month, simply contact our support team for a full refund.
