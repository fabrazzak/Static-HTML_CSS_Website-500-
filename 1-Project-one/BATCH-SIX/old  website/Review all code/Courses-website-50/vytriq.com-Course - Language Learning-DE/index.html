Frequently Asked Questions

How do I choose the right language course for me?

We offer free placement tests for each language to help determine your current level. You can also schedule a consultation with one of our advisors who will assess your goals and recommend the most suitable course.

What's the time commitment for each course?

Most courses require 4-6 hours per week, including live sessions, self-study, and assignments. However, you can progress at your own pace and have access to all materials for the duration of your subscription.

Do you offer certificates upon completion?

Yes, all Premium plan students receive a certificate upon successful completion of a course. Our certificates are recognized by many employers and educational institutions.

Can I switch courses or get a refund?

You can switch to a different course within the first 7 days at no additional cost. We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee if you're not satisfied with your learning experience.

What technology do I need to participate?

You'll need a computer or tablet with internet access. Most features work on modern browsers, though we recommend Chrome or Firefox for the best experience. A microphone is required for speaking practice.
