Common Questions

How quickly can I expect a response to my inquiry?

We typically respond to all inquiries within 24 hours during our business hours (Monday-Friday, 9am-5pm ACST). For urgent matters, please call our support line or use the live chat feature when available.

Do you offer in-person workshops or only online courses?

Currently, all our courses are offered online for maximum accessibility. However, we occasionally host in-person workshops in major cities. Join our mailing list to be notified about upcoming events in your area.

Can I get a refund if I'm not satisfied with a course?

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee for all our courses. If you're not completely satisfied with your purchase, simply contact our support team within 30 days of enrollment for a full refund.

Do you offer group discounts or corporate training?

Yes! We offer special pricing for groups of 5 or more students, as well as customized corporate training packages. Contact our team at groups@aidello.org for more information and pricing.
