Our Humble Beginnings

Founded in 2015 by master goldsmith Sophia Laurent, Trivana began as a small workshop in Athens, Greece, with a simple mission: to preserve traditional jewelry making techniques while embracing modern design innovation.

What started as a passion project teaching local artisans has grown into an internationally recognized jewelry design school with students from over 45 countries. Our commitment to excellence and hands-on learning remains unchanged.

Today, Trivana offers comprehensive online and in-person courses that blend centuries-old craftsmanship with contemporary design principles, taught by some of the most respected names in the industry.

Our Mission & Values

At Trivana, we believe jewelry making is both an art and a craft. Our mission is to empower students with the technical skills, creative vision, and professional knowledge needed to excel in jewelry design, whether as a passionate hobby or a fulfilling career.

Craftsmanship

We honor traditional techniques while encouraging innovation, ensuring each student develops a strong foundation in quality craftsmanship.

Mentorship

Our instructors are practicing professionals who provide personalized guidance and real-world insights.

Creativity

We foster an environment where students can explore their unique artistic voice and develop distinctive design styles.

Sustainability

We teach responsible sourcing and environmentally conscious practices to create jewelry with integrity.
