Our Journey in Transforming Communication

Nolvex began in 2015 when our founder, Dr. James Wilson, noticed a critical gap in professional development. While technical skills were emphasized, communication skills—essential for career advancement—were often overlooked.

What started as a small workshop series in Berlin has grown into a global platform helping thousands of professionals from over 50 countries communicate with clarity, confidence, and impact.

2015

Founded in Berlin

Launched our first public speaking workshop with 12 participants

2017

Online Platform Launch

Expanded our reach globally with digital courses and live online coaching

2020
