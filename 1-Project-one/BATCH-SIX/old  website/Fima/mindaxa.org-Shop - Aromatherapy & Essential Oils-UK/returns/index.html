Returns & Refunds Policy

Last Updated: April 21, 2025

At Mindaxa, we want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase of our premium aromatherapy and essential oil products. If you're not satisfied, we're happy to offer returns and refunds under the following conditions.

1. Return Eligibility

General Returns

We accept returns of unopened, unused products in their original packaging within 30 days of delivery. To be eligible for a return:

  • The product must be in its original condition
  • The product must be in its original packaging with all seals intact
  • You must provide proof of purchase (order number or receipt)

Important: For health and safety reasons, we cannot accept returns of opened essential oil products unless they are defective or damaged.

Exceptions

The following items are not eligible for return:

  • Opened or used essential oils and aromatherapy products
  • Products purchased during sales or special promotions marked "final sale"
  • Gift cards
  • Personalized or custom-blended products

2. How to Initiate a Return

To initiate a return, please follow these steps:

  1. Contact our customer service team at returns@mindaxa.org within 30 days of receiving your order
  2. Provide your order number and the reason for return
  3. We will email you a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number and return instructions
  4. Package the items securely and include the RMA number on the package
  5. Ship the package to our return address (provided with your RMA)

We recommend using a trackable shipping service and purchasing shipping insurance for your return. We are not responsible for return packages lost in transit.

3. Refund Process

Timing

Once we receive and inspect your return, we will process your refund within 5-7 business days. You will receive an email confirmation when your refund has been processed.

Refund Method

Refunds will be issued to the original payment method used for purchase. Please note:

  • Credit card refunds may take 5-10 business days to appear on your statement
  • We cannot refund to a different payment method than the original

Deductions

The following may be deducted from your refund:

  • Original shipping costs (unless the return is due to our error)
  • Return shipping costs (unless the product is defective or incorrect)
  • Any discounts or promotions applied to the original order

4. Damaged or Defective Products

If you receive a damaged or defective product:

  • Contact us within 7 days of delivery at support@mindaxa.org
  • Include photos of the damaged/defective product and packaging
  • We will arrange for a replacement or full refund at no additional cost
  • In some cases, we may ask you to return the damaged item for quality inspection

Note: Essential oils are natural products and may vary slightly in color or consistency. Minor variations do not constitute a defective product.

5. Wrong Item Received

If you received the wrong item:

  • Contact us within 14 days of delivery
  • We will provide a prepaid return label for the incorrect item
  • We will ship the correct item as soon as possible at no additional cost
  • Alternatively, you may request a full refund

6. International Returns

For international customers:

  • You are responsible for return shipping costs and any customs fees
  • We recommend marking the package as "Returned Goods" to avoid additional customs charges
  • Refunds will be issued in the original currency and may be subject to exchange rate fluctuations

7. Exchanges

We currently do not offer direct exchanges. To exchange an item:

  • Return the original item following our return process
  • Place a new order for the desired item

This ensures you receive your new item as quickly as possible.

8. Store Credit

In some cases, we may offer store credit instead of a refund:

  • For returns initiated after 30 days but within 60 days of purchase
  • For opened products returned due to allergic reactions (with doctor's note)
  • Store credit never expires and can be used for future purchases

9. Return Shipping

Customers are responsible for return shipping costs unless:

  • The return is due to our error (wrong item shipped)
  • The product is defective or damaged

In these cases, we will provide a prepaid return label.
