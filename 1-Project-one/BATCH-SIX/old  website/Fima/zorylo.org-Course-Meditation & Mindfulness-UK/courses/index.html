Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our courses

Do I need any experience to start these courses?

No prior experience is needed! Our courses are designed for all levels, from complete beginners to experienced practitioners. We offer beginner-friendly explanations and gradually build up to more advanced techniques.

How much time do I need to dedicate to the courses?

Most of our courses recommend 10-20 minutes of practice per day, but even 5 minutes can be beneficial. The courses are designed to be flexible to fit into your schedule. You'll find recommendations for daily practice in each course, but you can always go at your own pace.

What if I don't have time to complete a course?

All our courses are self-paced, so you can take as long as you need. You'll retain access to the course materials even after the suggested completion time. Plus, with our subscription plans, you can pause and return to courses whenever you're ready.

Can I access the courses on my mobile device?

Yes! Our platform is fully responsive and works on smartphones, tablets, and computers. Many students find it convenient to practice with their mobile devices, especially for guided meditations.

What's your cancellation policy?

You can cancel your subscription at any time with no cancellation fees. When you cancel, you'll retain access to the courses until the end of your current billing period. We don't believe in locking people into long-term commitments.
