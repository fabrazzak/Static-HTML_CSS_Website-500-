Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Nylixa products eco-friendly?

All our products are carefully selected based on their environmental impact. We prioritize items made from sustainable, renewable, or recycled materials, with minimal packaging and ethical production practices. Each product in our collection meets strict sustainability criteria before we offer it to our customers.

How long does shipping take?

We process orders within 1-2 business days. Standard shipping within United Kingdom takes 2-3 business days, while international shipping typically takes 5-10 business days depending on the destination. All our shipping is carbon-neutral through our partnership with environmental organizations.

Do you offer wholesale pricing?

Yes! We love working with retailers and businesses who share our sustainability values. Please select "Wholesale Inquiry" in the contact form above, or email us directly at wholesale@nylixa.net for more information about our wholesale program and pricing.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day return policy for unused products in their original packaging. Due to the nature of our eco-friendly products, some items may have specific return requirements. Please contact us before returning any items, and we'll guide you through the process to ensure minimal environmental impact.

How can I make my lifestyle more sustainable?

Start small with simple swaps like reusable shopping bags, water bottles, and food containers. Check out our blog for practical tips on reducing waste in different areas of your life. Remember, sustainability is a journey - every small change makes a difference!
