Common Questions

Quick answers to frequently asked questions

How do I enroll in a course?

You can enroll directly through our website by visiting the Courses page and selecting your desired course. Choose your preferred pricing plan and complete the enrollment process. You'll receive immediate access to the course materials after payment.

What's your refund policy?

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee for all courses. If you're not satisfied with your purchase, simply contact our support team within 14 days of enrollment for a full refund, no questions asked.

Do you offer certificates of completion?

Yes! Our Premium plan includes certificates of completion for all finished courses. These certificates can be downloaded as PDFs and shared on LinkedIn or included in your portfolio.

Can I get personalized feedback on my work?

Absolutely. Our Standard and Premium plans include opportunities for personalized feedback. Premium members receive more extensive critiques and one-on-one mentoring sessions with our instructors.
