Enrollment Questions

How do I enroll in a course?

Enrolling is easy! Simply visit our Courses page, select the course you're interested in, and click the "Enroll Now" button. You'll be guided through the payment process, and once completed, you'll get immediate access to the course materials.

Do I need any prior experience to join?

Not at all! We offer courses for all skill levels, from complete beginners to advanced artists. Each course description indicates the recommended experience level, and many of our courses are specifically designed for those just starting their artistic journey.

What materials or software do I need?

Requirements vary by course:

  • Traditional drawing courses: Basic supplies like pencils, paper, and erasers
  • Digital courses: A computer and drawing tablet (specific software requirements listed in each course)

We always recommend affordable options and provide free alternatives when possible.

Course Questions

How are the courses structured?

Our courses are divided into modules and lessons, typically including:

  • Video tutorials (5-15 minutes each)
  • Downloadable worksheets and resources
  • Practice assignments
  • Community discussion boards
  • Optional critiques (depending on your plan)

You can progress at your own pace and revisit materials anytime.

Can I download course materials?

Yes! All course materials (worksheets, reference images, and in most cases, video lessons) are available for download. This allows you to learn offline and revisit materials whenever you need them.

How long do I have access to a course?

Access duration depends on your subscription plan:

  • Monthly plans: Access as long as your subscription is active
  • Annual plans: Full year of access from enrollment date
  • Premium plans: Lifetime access to completed courses

You can always download materials to keep forever.

Pricing & Plans

What's included in each pricing plan?

We offer three main plans:

  • Basic: Access to beginner courses, community forum, email support
  • Standard: All courses, priority support, monthly workshops, personalized feedback
  • Premium: Everything in Standard plus one-on-one mentoring, portfolio reviews, and certificates

See our Pricing page for full details.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes! We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee. If you're not satisfied with your purchase, contact our support team within 14 days of enrollment for a full refund, no questions asked.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?

Absolutely. You can change your plan at any time:

  • Upgrades take effect immediately (you'll pay the difference)
  • Downgrades take effect at your next billing cycle

Contact our support team or manage your subscription in your account settings.

Technical Support

What devices can I use to access courses?

Our platform works on:

  • Desktop computers (Windows/Mac)
  • Laptops
  • Tablets (iPad, Android)
  • Smartphones (though we recommend larger screens for best experience)

You'll need a modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge) and a stable internet connection for streaming videos.

How do I reset my password?

To reset your password:

  1. Go to the login page
  2. Click "Forgot password?"
  3. Enter your email address
  4. Check your email for a password reset link
  5. Follow the instructions to create a new password

If you don't receive the email, check your spam folder or contact our support team.

Why can't I access a course I purchased?

If you're having trouble accessing a course, try these steps:

  1. Log out and log back in to refresh your account status
  2. Clear your browser cache and cookies
  3. Try a different browser or device
  4. Check that your subscription is still active

If you still can't access your course, please contact our support team with details about the issue.
