Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our products and services

How Can We Help You?

We've compiled a list of frequently asked questions to help you find the information you need quickly. If you don't find what you're looking for, please don't hesitate to contact us.

Ordering Questions

How do I place an order?

Placing an order is simple! Browse our collection, select the art prints you love, add them to your cart, and proceed to checkout. You'll need to provide your shipping information and payment details to complete your order.

If you need any assistance during the process, our customer service team is available to help via email or phone.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, and bank transfers. All payments are processed securely through our encrypted payment gateway.

For custom orders or large purchases, we may also accept wire transfers - please contact us for arrangements.

Can I modify or cancel my order after placing it?

We process orders quickly to ensure fast delivery, but if you need to modify or cancel your order, please contact us immediately at info@strenlo.org or call +44 1697742985 . We'll do our best to accommodate your request if your order hasn't already been processed for shipping.

Orders that have already been shipped cannot be canceled but may be returned under our return policy.

Shipping Questions

How long does shipping take?

We typically process and ship orders within 1-2 business days. Shipping times vary depending on your location:

  • • United Kingdom: 2-3 business days after shipping
  • • Europe: 3-5 business days after shipping
  • • International: 5-10 business days after shipping

During peak seasons or for custom orders, processing times may be slightly longer. You'll receive tracking information once your order ships.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we ship worldwide! International shipping rates are calculated at checkout based on your location and the size/weight of your order. Please note that customers are responsible for any customs fees or import duties that may apply in their country.

For international orders, we recommend checking your local customs regulations before placing an order.

How are art prints packaged for shipping?

We take great care in packaging your art prints to ensure they arrive in perfect condition. All prints are carefully rolled in acid-free tissue paper and shipped in sturdy cardboard tubes for unframed prints, or flat in reinforced packaging for framed pieces.

Larger or more fragile items may include additional protective materials. If your artwork arrives damaged, please contact us immediately with photos of the damage and packaging.

Returns & Exchanges

What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 14 days of delivery for a full refund. The item must be in its original condition and packaging. To initiate a return, please contact us at info@strenlo.org with your order number and reason for return.

Please note: Custom orders and framed prints are final sale and cannot be returned. Sale items may only be returned for store credit.

How do I exchange an item?

If you'd like to exchange an item for a different size or print, please contact us within 14 days of receiving your order. We'll guide you through the exchange process, which typically involves returning the original item and placing a new order.

You'll be responsible for return shipping costs unless the exchange is due to an error on our part. The new item will be shipped once we receive and process your return.

What if my item arrives damaged?

We're sorry if your item arrived damaged! Please contact us immediately at info@strenlo.org with photos of the damaged item and packaging. We'll arrange for a replacement or refund as quickly as possible.

For damaged items, we may ask you to return the product (at our expense) or provide additional documentation to process your claim.

Product Questions

What materials are used for your art prints?

We use only premium materials for our art prints:

  • • Paper: 240gsm fine art paper with matte finish
  • • Inks: Archival pigment inks that resist fading for 100+ years
  • • Frames: Sustainably sourced wood with museum-quality acrylic

Each print is carefully inspected for quality before shipping. For specific material questions about a particular print, please contact us.

How should I care for my art print?

To ensure your art print lasts for years:

  • • Keep out of direct sunlight to prevent fading
  • • Avoid hanging in high humidity areas like bathrooms
  • • Dust gently with a soft, dry cloth
  • • For framed pieces, clean glass/acrylic with a microfiber cloth

Never use cleaning products or water directly on the print surface.

Are your prints limited editions?

Some of our prints are offered as limited editions, while others are open editions. Limited edition prints will be clearly marked with the edition size (e.g., "50/100" meaning print number 50 out of 100 total).

Each limited edition print comes with a certificate of authenticity signed by the artist. Open edition prints may be reproduced as needed, but we maintain strict quality control to ensure consistency.

Custom Orders

Do you offer custom sizes for art prints?

Yes, we offer custom sizing for many of our art prints. Please contact us with your desired dimensions and the artwork you're interested in, and we'll provide you with a quote and timeframe for your custom order.

Please note that extreme size modifications may affect image quality, and some artworks may not be available in all sizes due to artist restrictions.

Can I commission a custom artwork?

We do work with artists who accept commissions! Please contact us with details about your project including:

  • • Desired style/subject matter
  • • Size requirements
  • • Budget range
  • • Timeline

We'll connect you with an appropriate artist and provide a quote. A 50% deposit is typically required to begin custom work.

Can I request artwork from a specific artist?

Absolutely! We work with many talented artists and can often arrange special requests. If there's a particular artist or style you're interested in, please contact us with details, and we'll do our best to accommodate your request.

Some artists may have waiting lists for commissions or limited availability, so we recommend reaching out well in advance for time-sensitive projects.

Still Have Questions?

We're here to help! Contact our customer service team for personalized assistance.
