Frequently Asked Questions

Product Questions

What are UPVORA candles made of?

Our candles are handcrafted using premium soy wax, which is a natural, renewable resource. We use:

  • 100% natural soy wax (no paraffin or blends)
  • Pure essential oils and phthalate-free fragrance oils
  • Lead-free cotton wicks for a clean burn
  • Recyclable and reusable glass containers

All our ingredients are carefully selected for quality, safety, and environmental sustainability.

Are your candles vegan and cruelty-free?

Yes! All UPVORA candles are 100% vegan and cruelty-free. We never use any animal-derived ingredients (like beeswax) and we don't test on animals.

Our candles are also free from parabens, sulfates, and other harsh chemicals commonly found in home fragrance products.

How long do your candles typically burn?

Burn times vary by candle size:

  • 6 oz candles: 40-45 hours
  • 8 oz candles: 50-55 hours
  • 12 oz candles: 70-80 hours
  • 16 oz candles: 90-100 hours

To maximize burn time, always trim the wick to 1/4 inch before lighting and allow the wax to melt completely across the surface on the first burn (this prevents "tunneling").

How strong are the scents in your candles?

Our candles are designed to provide a balanced fragrance experience - noticeable but not overwhelming. The scent throw (how far the fragrance travels) depends on several factors:

  • Room size: Larger rooms may require multiple candles
  • Ventilation: Well-ventilated areas disperse scent more
  • Burn time: Fragrance develops over 30-60 minutes

We categorize our scents as Light, Medium, or Strong to help you choose. Most customers find our Medium scents perfect for living spaces, while Strong scents work well for larger areas.

Candle Care

How should I care for my candle to make it last longer?

Follow these tips to maximize your candle's lifespan and performance:

  1. First burn: Allow the wax to melt completely across the surface (this prevents tunneling)
  2. Wick trimming: Always trim wick to 1/4" before lighting (use a wick trimmer or scissors)
  3. Burn time: Don't burn for more than 4 hours at a time
  4. Placement: Keep away from drafts and vents to prevent uneven burning
  5. Storage: Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight

With proper care, your candle should burn cleanly and evenly until the very end.

Why does my candle have black smoke or soot?

Sooting (black smoke) can occur when:

  • The wick is too long (always trim to 1/4")
  • The candle is in a drafty area
  • The wax pool becomes too deep (extinguish if flame gets too large)

To prevent sooting:

  1. Trim wick before each use
  2. Burn in a draft-free location
  3. Don't burn for more than 4 hours at a time
  4. Keep wax pool free of debris (matches, wick trimmings, etc.)

If you notice sooting, extinguish the candle, let it cool, trim the wick, and relight.

What should I do with the leftover wax in my candle jar?

Our jars are designed to be reused! Here are some ideas:

  • Clean and reuse: Once only 1/2" of wax remains, place the jar in hot water to melt remaining wax, then wipe clean with paper towels
  • Make a new candle: Save wax remnants to make a custom blend candle
  • Storage: Use for cotton balls, q-tips, or small items
  • Plant holder: Small succulents or air plants look great in our jars

Never pour hot wax down the drain as it can cause clogs.
