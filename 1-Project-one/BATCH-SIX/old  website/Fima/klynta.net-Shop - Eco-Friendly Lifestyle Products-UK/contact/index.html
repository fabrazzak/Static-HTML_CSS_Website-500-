Frequently Asked Questions

What makes your products eco-friendly?

All our products are carefully selected based on strict sustainability criteria. We prioritize items made from renewable, biodegradable, or recycled materials with minimal environmental impact throughout their lifecycle. Our suppliers must demonstrate ethical production practices and transparent supply chains.

How do you offset your carbon footprint?

We calculate the carbon emissions from our operations and shipping, then invest in verified carbon offset projects including reforestation initiatives and renewable energy development. Additionally, we've implemented measures to reduce our emissions at the source, such as using eco-friendly packaging and optimizing our shipping logistics.

What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 30 days of purchase for unused products in their original packaging. Due to the nature of our sustainable products, we cannot accept returns on certain hygiene items. Return shipping is the customer's responsibility unless the item arrived damaged or incorrect. We'll provide a full refund or exchange once we receive the returned item.

Do you offer international shipping?

Yes, we ship to most countries worldwide. Shipping costs and delivery times vary by destination. We consolidate orders to minimize packaging waste and carbon emissions from transportation. Some products may have shipping restrictions based on local regulations.

How can I recycle your products at end of life?

Most of our products are designed to be compostable, recyclable, or biodegradable. Each product page includes specific end-of-life instructions. For items that require special recycling, we provide guidance on our website and can help locate appropriate recycling facilities in your area.
