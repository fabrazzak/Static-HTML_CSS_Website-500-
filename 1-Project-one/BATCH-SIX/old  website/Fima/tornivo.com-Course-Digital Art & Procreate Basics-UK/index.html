Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our courses

Do I need prior art experience to take these courses?

No prior experience is necessary for our beginner courses. We start with the fundamentals and gradually build up your skills. Our Procreate Basics course is specifically designed for complete beginners, while our intermediate and advanced courses do require some foundational knowledge.

What equipment do I need for the courses?

You'll need an iPad (6th generation or newer) and an Apple Pencil (1st or 2nd generation). All our courses use Procreate, which you'll need to purchase from the App Store (it's a one-time purchase). We recommend at least 64GB storage on your iPad for working with multiple art files.

How long do I have access to the course materials?

Access duration depends on your subscription plan. With the Basic plan, you maintain access to course materials as long as your subscription is active. Standard and Premium plans include lifetime access to completed courses even if you cancel your subscription.

Can I get feedback on my artwork?

Yes! Our Standard and Premium plans include regular feedback opportunities. Premium subscribers receive weekly 1-on-1 feedback sessions with instructors, while Standard plan members get bi-weekly group feedback sessions. Basic plan members can participate in community feedback forums.
