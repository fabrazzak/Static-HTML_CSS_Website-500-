Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our courses and services

How do I enroll in a course?

To enroll in any of our meditation courses, simply fill out the contact form on this page or visit our Courses section. You can choose your preferred pricing plan and we'll send you all the details to get started. No prior experience is required for our beginner courses.

What's included in the course membership?

Our course memberships include access to all course materials, guided meditation recordings, weekly live Q&A sessions with instructors, community forum access, and additional resources like worksheets and reading recommendations. Premium members also receive one-on-one coaching sessions.

Can I access courses on my mobile device?

Yes! All our course materials are fully responsive and can be accessed on smartphones, tablets, and computers. We also have a mobile-friendly platform where you can download guided meditations for offline use.

Do you offer refunds?

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee for all our courses. If you're not satisfied with your experience within the first two weeks, simply contact our support team for a full refund, no questions asked.

How often are new courses added?

We release new courses quarterly, with additional seasonal content and workshops throughout the year. All members receive notifications when new content is available, and premium members get early access to new courses.
