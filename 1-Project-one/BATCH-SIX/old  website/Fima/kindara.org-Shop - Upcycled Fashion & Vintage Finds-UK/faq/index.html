Product Questions

Are Kindara essential oils 100% pure?

Yes, all Kindara essential oils are 100% pure, therapeutic-grade, and free from additives, fillers, or synthetic ingredients. We source our oils from ethical suppliers and rigorously test each batch to ensure purity and potency.

How should I store my essential oils?

To preserve the quality of your essential oils:

  • Store in dark glass bottles (our bottles are designed for optimal storage)
  • Keep in a cool, dark place away from direct sunlight
  • Ensure lids are tightly closed after each use
  • Avoid storing near heat sources or in humid environments
  • Keep out of reach of children and pets

Properly stored, most essential oils will maintain their potency for 1-3 years.

Can essential oils be used directly on skin?

Most essential oils should be diluted with a carrier oil (like jojoba, coconut, or almond oil) before applying to skin. We recommend:

  • For adults: 2-3 drops of essential oil per teaspoon of carrier oil
  • For children: 1 drop per tablespoon of carrier oil
  • For sensitive skin: Perform a patch test first (apply a small amount to inner arm and wait 24 hours)

Some oils like lavender and tea tree can sometimes be used neat (undiluted) for small areas, but we generally recommend dilution for safety.

Are your products vegan and cruelty-free?

Yes, all Kindara products are 100% vegan and cruelty-free. We never test on animals, and none of our products contain animal-derived ingredients. We're proud to be certified by Leaping Bunny and Vegan Society.

What's the difference between essential oils and fragrance oils?

Essential oils are natural extracts from plants with therapeutic properties, while fragrance oils are synthetic creations designed to mimic scents. Key differences:

  • Essential Oils: Extracted from plants, contain therapeutic compounds, natural scent variations, can be used for aromatherapy
  • Fragrance Oils: Lab-created, consistent scent, no therapeutic benefits, often contain synthetic chemicals

Kindara uses only pure essential oils - we never use synthetic fragrance oils in our products.

Usage & Safety

Are essential oils safe for children?

Many essential oils can be safely used with children when properly diluted, but there are important precautions:

  • Under 3 months: Avoid all essential oils
  • 3-24 months: Only use very mild oils like lavender and chamomile, heavily diluted (0.25% concentration)
  • 2-6 years: Safe to use more oils at 0.5-1% concentration
  • 6+ years: Can use most oils at 1-2% concentration

Always avoid oils like eucalyptus, peppermint, and rosemary for young children. Consult with a healthcare provider before using essential oils with children.

Can I use essential oils if I'm pregnant?

Some essential oils can be used safely during pregnancy, but with extra precautions:

  • First trimester: Avoid all essential oils unless under professional guidance
  • Second & third trimesters: Can use mild oils like lavender, chamomile, and ylang-ylang at half the normal dilution
  • Always avoid oils known to stimulate menstruation or potentially affect hormones (like clary sage, rosemary, basil)
  • Never ingest essential oils during pregnancy
  • Consult with your healthcare provider before use

How do I use essential oils in a diffuser?

To use essential oils in a diffuser:

  1. Fill your diffuser with water to the indicated line
  2. Add 3-10 drops of essential oil (fewer drops for small rooms, more for large spaces)
  3. Turn on the diffuser and enjoy

Tips for best results:

  • Diffuse for 30-60 minutes at a time, then take a break
  • Clean your diffuser regularly according to manufacturer instructions
  • Use blends or single oils depending on your needs
  • Keep diffuser out of direct sunlight

Can essential oils be ingested?

Kindara does not recommend ingesting essential oils unless under the guidance of a qualified healthcare professional. Essential oils are highly concentrated and can be harmful if taken internally without proper knowledge and dosage.

For culinary use, we recommend using specifically labeled food-grade essential oils or traditional herbs and spices.

What should I do if I have an adverse reaction to an essential oil?

If you experience skin irritation, redness, or discomfort:

  1. Immediately remove the oil by washing the area with mild soap and cool water
  2. Apply a carrier oil (like coconut or olive oil) to help dilute any remaining essential oil
  3. If irritation persists, apply a cool compress
  4. Discontinue use of that essential oil
  5. For severe reactions (difficulty breathing, swelling), seek medical attention immediately

If you experience nausea, dizziness, or headache from aromatherapy:

  • Move to fresh air immediately
  • Drink plenty of water
  • Rest until symptoms subside

Please report any adverse reactions to our customer service team.

Returns & Refunds

What is your return policy?

We accept returns of unopened, unused items in their original packaging within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. To initiate a return:

  1. Contact us at returns@kindara.org to request a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number
  2. Package the item securely with the RMA number visible
  3. Ship the return to our warehouse

Once we receive and inspect your return, we'll process your refund to the original payment method. Shipping costs are non-refundable.

Some items cannot be returned for hygiene reasons (opened essential oils, used diffusers, etc.).

How long do refunds take to process?

Refund processing times vary by payment method:
