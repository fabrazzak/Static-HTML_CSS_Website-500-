Frequently Asked Questions

How long does custom order processing take?

Most custom orders are processed within 3-5 business days after approval of your design. Production time varies depending on the complexity of the personalization and current order volume. During peak seasons (like holidays), processing times may extend to 7-10 business days.

We'll provide a specific timeline when you place your order and keep you updated throughout the process.

What is your return policy for personalized items?

Because personalized items are made specifically for you, we generally don't accept returns unless the product is defective or there was an error on our part. If you receive a damaged or incorrect item, please contact us within 7 days of delivery with photos of the issue.

We'll work with you to resolve the problem, which may include remaking the item or issuing a refund at our discretion.

Can I make changes to my order after it's placed?

If your order hasn't entered production yet, we can often accommodate changes. Please contact us as soon as possible at info@volira.org with your order number and requested changes.

Once production has begun on personalized items, changes may not be possible as materials may have already been cut or engraved.

Do you offer international shipping?

Yes, we ship worldwide! International shipping costs and delivery times vary by destination. You'll see the exact shipping cost during checkout before you complete your purchase.

Please note that customers are responsible for any customs fees, import duties, or taxes that may be charged by their country.

How can I track my order?

Once your order ships, you'll receive a confirmation email with tracking information. You can click the tracking number in the email to see real-time updates on your package's journey.

If you haven't received tracking information within the estimated processing time, please check your spam folder first, then contact us if you still can't find it.
