Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our public speaking courses

Who are these courses designed for?

Our courses are designed for professionals at all levels who want to improve their public speaking and presentation skills. Whether you're a beginner who gets nervous speaking in front of groups or an experienced speaker looking to refine your technique, we have courses that will help you achieve your goals.

How are the courses delivered?

All courses are delivered online through our learning platform. They include video lessons, downloadable resources, practical exercises, and for Standard and Premium plans, live coaching sessions. You can access the courses anytime from any device.

What if I'm not satisfied with the course?

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee for all our courses. If you're not completely satisfied with your purchase, simply contact us within 30 days of enrollment for a full refund, no questions asked.

How much time should I dedicate to the courses?

Most students spend 3-5 hours per week on course materials and practice. However, you can progress at your own pace. The courses are designed to be flexible for busy professionals.

Do you offer corporate training?

Yes, we offer customized corporate training programs for teams and organizations. Our corporate solutions include group workshops, team coaching, and enterprise licensing options. Contact us to discuss your organization's specific needs.
