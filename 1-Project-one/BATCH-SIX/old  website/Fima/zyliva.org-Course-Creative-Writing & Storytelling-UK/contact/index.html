Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our courses and enrollment process

How do I enroll in a creative writing course?

You can enroll in any of our courses by filling out the contact form on this page or visiting the course page and clicking the "Enroll Now" button. Our team will contact you with further instructions and payment details within 24 hours.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, and bank transfers. Payment plans are available for some of our longer courses - please inquire for details.

Do you offer refunds if I'm not satisfied with a course?

Yes, we offer a 14-day money-back guarantee for all our courses. If you're not completely satisfied within the first two weeks of your course, simply contact us for a full refund, no questions asked.

Can I access course materials after completing a course?

Absolutely! You'll have lifetime access to all course materials, including videos, worksheets, and resources. This allows you to revisit lessons and continue learning at your own pace even after completing the course.
