Frequently Asked Questions

What materials do you use in your jewelry?

We use high-quality materials including 14k gold fill, sterling silver, and recycled brass. All our materials are nickel-free and hypoallergenic, making them suitable for sensitive skin.

How do I care for my nextvia jewelry?

To maintain the beauty of your jewelry, we recommend removing pieces before swimming, showering, or applying lotions. Store them in a dry place and clean with a soft jewelry cloth. Each order comes with detailed care instructions.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day return policy for unused items in their original packaging. Custom orders and sale items are final sale. Please contact us within 7 days of delivery for any damaged or incorrect items.

Do you offer international shipping?

Yes, we ship worldwide! Shipping costs and delivery times vary by destination. You'll see the exact shipping cost at checkout. All international orders may be subject to customs fees.

Can I customize a piece of jewelry?

Absolutely! We love creating custom pieces. Please contact us with your ideas and we'll work with you to bring your vision to life. Custom orders typically take 2-3 weeks to complete.
