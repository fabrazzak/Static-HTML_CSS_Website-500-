Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our products and services.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day return policy for most items. Products must be in their original condition with all packaging intact. Please contact our customer service team to initiate a return and receive a return authorization number. Shipping costs for returns are the responsibility of the customer unless the return is due to our error.

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping typically takes 3-5 business days within Canada . For international orders, delivery times vary by destination but generally take 7-14 business days. During peak seasons, there may be slight delays. You'll receive a tracking number once your order ships so you can monitor its progress.

Do you offer international shipping?

Yes, we ship to most countries worldwide. Shipping costs and delivery times vary depending on your location. During checkout, you'll see the available shipping options and costs for your specific address. Please note that international customers may be responsible for any customs duties or taxes imposed by their country.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, and Apple Pay. All transactions are securely processed through our encrypted payment gateway. We do not store your payment information on our servers.

Can I modify or cancel my order after placing it?

We process orders quickly to ensure fast delivery, but if you need to modify or cancel your order, please contact us immediately at support@puretica.org with your order number. We'll do our best to accommodate your request if your order hasn't already been processed for shipping.

Still have questions? Send us a message and we'll be happy to help.
