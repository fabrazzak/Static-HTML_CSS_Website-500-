Frequently Asked Questions

How can I track my order?

Once your order has shipped, you'll receive a confirmation email with a tracking number. You can use this number to track your package through our shipping partner's website. If you haven't received your tracking information within 3 business days, please contact our customer service team.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day return policy for unworn items in their original condition. Please contact us within 30 days of receiving your order to initiate a return. Custom or personalized items are final sale and cannot be returned. For full details, please visit our Returns & Exchanges page.

Do you offer international shipping?

Yes, we ship worldwide! Shipping costs and delivery times vary depending on your location. At checkout, you'll see the available shipping options and estimated delivery dates for your order. Please note that international orders may be subject to customs fees and import taxes.

How do I care for my jewelry?

To keep your jewelry looking its best, we recommend removing it before swimming, showering, or applying lotions and perfumes. Store each piece separately in a soft pouch or jewelry box to prevent scratching. Clean with a soft, lint-free cloth. For more detailed care instructions, check our Jewelry Care Guide.

Can I customize a piece of jewelry?

We offer customization options for select pieces in our collection. Please contact us with your customization request, and our team will let you know what's possible along with any additional costs. Typical customizations include engraving, chain length adjustments, and metal color choices.
