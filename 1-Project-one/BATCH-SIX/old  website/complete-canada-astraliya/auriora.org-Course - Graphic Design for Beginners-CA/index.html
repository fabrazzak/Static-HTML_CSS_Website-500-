Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our courses

Do I need any prior experience to start?

No prior experience is needed! Our beginner courses start from the very basics and gradually build up your skills. We've helped thousands of complete beginners become confident designers.

What software do I need for the courses?

For most courses, you'll need Adobe Photoshop and Illustrator. We offer free alternatives in our resources section if you're not ready to invest in Adobe products yet. Some courses focus on design principles that don't require specific software.

How much time should I dedicate each week?

We recommend 4-6 hours per week to complete a course in about a month. However, you can go at your own pace—all course materials remain accessible as long as your subscription is active.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes, you can cancel anytime with no penalty. You'll retain access until the end of your current billing period. We don't believe in locking people into long contracts.

Will I receive a certificate after completing courses?

Yes! Upon completing any course with all assignments, you'll receive a digital certificate that you can add to your LinkedIn profile or portfolio.
