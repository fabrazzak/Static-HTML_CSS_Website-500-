Our Story & Passion for Crafting

Discover the journey behind aidello and our commitment to preserving the art of candle and soap making

How It All Began

aidello was founded in 2020 by master artisan Elena Rodriguez, who began her journey with candle making as a therapeutic hobby during a difficult period in her life. What started as a personal passion quickly grew into a mission to share the joy and satisfaction of creating beautiful, handcrafted products with others.

After teaching small workshops in her local community and seeing the transformative power of these crafts, Elena envisioned a platform that could reach aspiring artisans worldwide. Together with a team of passionate crafters, aidello was born - a place where tradition meets innovation in candle and soap making.

Today, we've grown into a global community of over 50,000 students, but we've never lost sight of our core values: quality craftsmanship, sustainable practices, and the belief that everyone has an inner artist waiting to be discovered.

Our Mission & Approach

Empowerment

We believe in empowering individuals with skills that can become both fulfilling hobbies and viable businesses. Our comprehensive courses are designed to build confidence and competence at every level.

Sustainability

From sourcing eco-friendly materials to teaching waste-reducing techniques, we're committed to preserving our planet while preserving traditional crafting methods.

Community
