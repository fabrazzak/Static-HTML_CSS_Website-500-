Frequently Asked Questions

What are your shipping options and delivery times?

We offer standard and express shipping options. Standard shipping typically takes 3-5 business days within Canada , while express shipping delivers in 1-2 business days. International shipping times vary by destination but generally take 7-14 business days. You'll receive a tracking number once your order ships.

Are your products cruelty-free and vegan?

Yes! All wavivo products are 100% cruelty-free and never tested on animals. Most of our products are vegan, with the exception of a few that contain beeswax or honey (clearly labeled). We're proud to be Leaping Bunny certified.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day satisfaction guarantee. If you're not completely happy with your purchase, you can return it within 30 days for a full refund or exchange. Products must be in original, unused condition. Please contact our customer service team to initiate a return.

Where do you source your ingredients?

We source organic ingredients from ethical suppliers around the world, with priority given to Canada n producers whenever possible. All ingredients are carefully vetted for quality, sustainability, and ethical production practices. Many of our botanicals come from small family farms that practice regenerative agriculture.

Do you offer wholesale or bulk purchasing?

Yes, we work with retailers, spas, and wellness centers. Our wholesale program offers discounted pricing for qualifying orders. Please email wholesale@wavivo.org for our wholesale catalog and application. Minimum order quantities apply.

Still Have Questions?

Our customer care team is available to assist you with any inquiries about our products or your order.
