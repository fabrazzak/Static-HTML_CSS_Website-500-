Common Questions

Quick answers to frequently asked questions

How do I know which course is right for me?

Our beginner courses are designed for those with no prior experience. If you're completely new to design, we recommend starting with "Design Fundamentals." You can also take our quick course recommendation quiz or contact our advisors for personalized guidance.

What's your refund policy?

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee for all courses. If you're not satisfied with your purchase, simply contact our support team within 14 days of enrollment for a full refund, no questions asked.

Do you offer discounts for students?

Yes! We offer a 20% discount for students with valid educational email addresses or student ID. Contact our support team with proof of enrollment to receive your discount code.
