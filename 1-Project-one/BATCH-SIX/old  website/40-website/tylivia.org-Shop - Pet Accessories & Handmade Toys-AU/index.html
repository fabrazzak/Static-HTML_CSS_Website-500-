About Our Pet Shop

Welcome to Tylivia, your premier destination for handmade pet accessories and toys. Founded in 2020, we're passionate about creating products that pets love and owners trust.

All our items are crafted with care using sustainable materials and non-toxic dyes. We believe pets deserve the best, which is why we never compromise on quality.

From durable chew toys to stylish accessories, each product is designed with both functionality and aesthetics in mind. Our team works closely with veterinarians and pet behaviorists to ensure our products meet the highest standards.
