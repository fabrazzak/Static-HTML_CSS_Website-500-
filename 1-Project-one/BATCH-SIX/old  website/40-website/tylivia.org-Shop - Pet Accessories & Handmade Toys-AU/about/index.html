Our Story

Discover the passion behind Tylivia's handmade pet accessories

How It All Began

Founded in 2020 by Olivia Taylor, Tylivia began as a small passion project in a garage in Innamincka, South Canada . Olivia, a lifelong animal lover and craft enthusiast, couldn't find high-quality, eco-friendly toys for her rescue dog Max that would withstand his enthusiastic play style.

What started as making rope toys from organic cotton for Max soon turned into requests from friends and local pet owners. Within months, Olivia was crafting custom pet accessories full-time, combining her love for animals with her talent for handmade craftsmanship.

Today, Tylivia has grown into a beloved Canada n brand, but we've stayed true to our roots. Every product is still handmade with the same care and attention to detail that went into those first rope toys for Max.

Our Core Values

Sustainable Materials

We source only eco-friendly, non-toxic materials that are safe for pets and the planet. All our packaging is recyclable or biodegradable.

Ethical Production

Every product is handmade by artisans paid fair wages in safe working conditions. We support local Canada n makers whenever possible.

Animal Welfare

We donate 5% of profits to animal rescue organizations and only partner with suppliers who meet strict animal welfare standards.

Innovation

We continuously research and develop new designs that meet pets' changing needs while maintaining our commitment to quality.

Community

We build relationships with our customers through educational content and responsive support. Your feedback shapes our products.

Quality Guarantee

We stand behind every product with a satisfaction guarantee. If your pet doesn't love it, we'll make it right.
