About Lumeroa

Founded in 2020 by event planning veteran Sophia Laurent, Lumeroa was born from a simple idea: every event deserves beautiful, high-quality decor that reflects the host's unique vision.

What began as a small boutique offering custom decor pieces has grown into a leading provider of event decorations and party supplies, serving clients across Europe and beyond. Our curated collections combine timeless elegance with contemporary trends to help you create unforgettable moments.

At Lumeroa, we believe that great decor does more than just look beautiful - it creates atmosphere, tells a story, and turns ordinary gatherings into extraordinary experiences.

Our Journey

Jan 2020

Founding of Lumeroa

Sophia Laurent launches Lumeroa from her home studio, offering custom decor pieces for local weddings and events.

Jan 2021

First Retail Space

We open our first physical location in Bad Brambach, allowing clients to see and feel our products in person.

March 2022

Online Store Launch

Our e-commerce platform goes live, making Lumeroa decor accessible to clients across Europe.

September 2023

Expanded Product Lines

We introduce five new decor collections and begin offering complete event styling services.

Present

Continuing Our Mission

Today, we serve thousands of clients annually while maintaining our commitment to quality, creativity, and exceptional service.
