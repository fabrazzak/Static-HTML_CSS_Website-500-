Welcome to Virtuno

Founded in 2020, Virtuno began as a small passion project with a simple mission: to create high-quality custom products that help people express their individuality and businesses promote their brands.

What started as a home-based operation printing custom t-shirts has grown into a thriving business offering a wide range of personalized products including mugs, tote bags, and more. Our commitment to quality, sustainability, and customer satisfaction has remained unchanged.

Today, we're proud to serve customers across Australia and beyond, helping them create meaningful gifts, unique personal items, and effective promotional products.

Our Journey

2020

Humble Beginnings

Virtuno was founded in a small garage in Perth with just one screen printing machine and a big dream. Our first products were custom t-shirts for local bands and businesses.

2021

Expanding Our Range

We added custom mugs and tote bags to our product line after receiving numerous requests from customers. Our team grew to 5 dedicated members.

2022

Going Nationwide

With the launch of our e-commerce website, we began shipping Australia-wide. We moved to a larger facility and invested in state-of-the-art printing equipment.

2023

Sustainability Commitment

We transitioned to 100% organic cotton for our apparel and introduced eco-friendly packaging. Received the Green Business Certification.

2024

10,000 Happy Customers

Celebrated serving our 10,000th customer while maintaining a 98% satisfaction rate. Expanded our team to 15 talented individuals.
