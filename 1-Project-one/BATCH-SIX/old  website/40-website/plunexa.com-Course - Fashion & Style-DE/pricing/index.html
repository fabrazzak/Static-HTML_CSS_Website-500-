Pricing FAQs

Answers to common questions about our plans

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, and bank transfers. All payments are processed securely through our payment processor.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. When you upgrade, you'll immediately gain access to the additional features, and we'll prorate the difference in cost. Downgrades will take effect at your next billing cycle.

Is there a free trial available?

We offer a 7-day free trial for our Basic plan so you can experience our platform before committing. After the trial, you can choose to continue with any plan that suits your needs.

What's your refund policy?

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee for all plans. If you're not satisfied with your purchase, simply contact our support team within 14 days of enrollment for a full refund, no questions asked.

Do you offer discounts for students or teams?

Yes! We offer a 15% discount for students with valid student ID. For teams of 3 or more, we offer group discounts up to 25%. Contact our sales team for more information about group pricing.
