The Art of Layering Minimalist Necklaces

Layering delicate necklaces is an art form that can elevate any outfit from simple to sophisticated. When done correctly, layered necklaces create visual interest while maintaining the clean aesthetic of minimalist style.

Start with Lengths

The key to successful layering is varying the lengths of your necklaces. We recommend starting with three lengths: 16 inches (choker length), 18 inches (just below the collarbone), and 20 inches (at the base of the neckline). Our layering sets are designed with these perfect proportions in mind.

Mix Textures, Keep Metals Consistent

While mixing metals is trendy, for beginners we suggest sticking to one metal tone when layering. Instead, vary the textures - pair a simple chain with a delicate pendant and perhaps a small charm necklace.

Consider the Neckline

The neckline of your outfit should guide your layering choices. Crew necks work well with shorter layers, while V-necks and button-downs provide more space for longer pieces. Scoop necks are versatile and can accommodate various lengths.

Our Favorite Combinations

1. The Classic Trio: Simple chain + small pendant + tiny charm
2. The Asymmetrical Pair: One delicate chain with an off-center pendant + a simple choker
3. The Mixed Width: A thin chain + a slightly thicker chain with small details

Remember, the beauty of minimalist layering is in its subtlety. Start with two necklaces if you're new to layering, and gradually build your collection. Our stylists are always available for virtual consultations to help you create your perfect layered look.
