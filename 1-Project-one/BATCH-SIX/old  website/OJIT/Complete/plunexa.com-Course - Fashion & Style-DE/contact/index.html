Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How quickly can I expect a response to my inquiry?

We typically respond to all inquiries within 24-48 hours during our business hours (Monday-Friday, 9am-6pm CET). For urgent matters, please call our support line.

Do you offer in-person consultations?

Yes, we offer limited in-person consultations at our Bad Brambach location by appointment only. Please contact us to schedule a consultation.

Can I get advice on which course is right for me?

Absolutely! Our education advisors are happy to help you choose the perfect course based on your goals and experience level. Contact us with details about what you're hoping to achieve.

What payment methods do you accept for courses?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, and bank transfers. All payments are processed securely through our payment processor.
