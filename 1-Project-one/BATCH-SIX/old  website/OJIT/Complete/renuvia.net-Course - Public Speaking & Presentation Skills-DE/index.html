Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our courses

Who are these courses designed for?

Our courses are designed for professionals at all levels who want to improve their public speaking and presentation skills. Whether you're a beginner who gets nervous speaking in front of groups or an experienced speaker looking to refine your technique, we have courses that will meet your needs.

How are the courses delivered?

All courses are delivered online through our learning platform. They include video lessons, interactive exercises, downloadable resources, and live coaching sessions (depending on your plan). You can access the materials anytime, anywhere, and learn at your own pace.

What if I'm not satisfied with the course?

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee. If you're not completely satisfied with your course within the first two weeks, simply contact us for a full refund, no questions asked.

How much time should I dedicate to the courses?

We recommend dedicating 3-5 hours per week to get the most out of your course. This includes watching lessons, completing exercises, and practicing your skills. However, you can move faster or slower depending on your schedule and learning style.

Will I receive a certificate upon completion?

Yes, students who complete Premium plan courses and meet all requirements will receive a Certificate of Completion that can be shared on LinkedIn and included in professional portfolios.
