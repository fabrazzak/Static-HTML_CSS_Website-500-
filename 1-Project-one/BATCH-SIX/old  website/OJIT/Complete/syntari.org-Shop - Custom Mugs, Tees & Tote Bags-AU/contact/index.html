Frequently Asked Questions

How long does custom printing take?

Our standard production time is 3-5 business days after design approval. Rush services are available for an additional fee, which can reduce production time to 1-2 business days.

What file formats do you accept for custom designs?

We prefer vector files (.ai, .eps, .pdf) for the best quality, but we also accept high-resolution raster files (.png, .jpg, .psd) at 300dpi or higher. Our design team can help optimize your artwork if needed.

What is your return policy?

Due to the custom nature of our products, we generally don't accept returns unless there was an error on our part. If you receive a defective or incorrect item, please contact us within 7 days of delivery and we'll make it right.

Do you offer wholesale or bulk discounts?

Yes! We offer discounted pricing for bulk orders. The discount increases with order quantity. Please contact our sales team at sales@syntari.org for a custom quote.

How can I track my order after it ships?

You'll receive a shipping confirmation email with tracking information once your order leaves our facility. You can use this tracking number on the carrier's website to monitor your package's progress.
