Frequently Asked Questions

What are your shipping options?

We offer standard (3-5 business days) and express (1-2 business days) shipping within Germany. International shipping available with varying delivery times.

Can I return or exchange an item?

Yes, we offer a 30-day return policy for most items. Items must be in original condition with all packaging. Some exclusions apply.

Do you offer instrument repairs?

Yes, our in-house technicians can repair most instruments. Contact us to schedule an evaluation and estimate.

How can I track my order?

Once shipped, you'll receive a tracking number via email. You can track your package using the link provided or through our website.
