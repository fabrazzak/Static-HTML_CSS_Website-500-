Our Passion for Music Drives Everything We Do

For over a decade, Tylixy has been providing musicians with the highest quality instruments and gear to help bring their musical visions to life.

Our Story

Founded in 2012 by a group of passionate musicians, Tylixy began as a small boutique shop in Bad Brambach, Germany. What started as a humble storefront has grown into one of Europe's most trusted destinations for musical instruments and audio equipment.

Our founders recognized a need for a music store that combined expert knowledge with exceptional customer service. Too often, musicians were faced with either big-box retailers lacking expertise or specialty shops with intimidating atmospheres.

At Tylixy, we've created a welcoming environment where musicians of all levels can find the perfect gear to match their needs and budget. Our team consists of working musicians who understand the challenges and joys of making music.
