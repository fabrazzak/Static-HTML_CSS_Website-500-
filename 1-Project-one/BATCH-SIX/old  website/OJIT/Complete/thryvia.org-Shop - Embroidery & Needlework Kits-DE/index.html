About Thryvia

Our passion for needlework shines through in every kit we create

Founded in 2020, Thryvia has been dedicated to providing high-quality embroidery and needlework kits to crafters around the world. We believe that everyone should have access to beautiful, well-designed kits that make stitching enjoyable and rewarding.

Our kits include everything you need to create stunning pieces, from premium fabrics and threads to detailed instructions. Whether you're a beginner or an experienced stitcher, we have something for you.
