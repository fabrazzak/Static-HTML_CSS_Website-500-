Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our services

How quickly can I expect a response to my inquiry?

We typically respond to all inquiries within 24 hours during our business hours. For urgent requests, please call us directly at +49 160 469 7373.

Do you offer emergency event decor services?

Yes! We understand that last-minute events happen. We offer emergency decor services with as little as 24 hours notice, depending on availability. Additional fees may apply for last-minute requests.

Can I visit your showroom to see decor samples?

Absolutely! We welcome visitors by appointment at our Bad Brambach location. Please contact us to schedule a visit so we can ensure someone is available to assist you.

What information should I include in my event decor inquiry?

For the most accurate quote, please include: event date, venue location, approximate guest count, preferred color scheme or theme, and any specific decor items you're interested in. Photos or inspiration images are always helpful!
