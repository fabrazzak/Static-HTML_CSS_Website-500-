Frequently Asked Questions

How long does custom printing take?

Our standard production time is 3-5 business days after order confirmation. Rush services are available for an additional fee (contact us for details). Shipping times vary based on your location.

What file formats do you accept for custom designs?

We prefer vector files (.ai, .eps, .pdf) for the best quality, but we also accept high-resolution (300dpi) raster files (.png, .jpg, .psd). Our design team can help optimize your files if needed.

Do you offer bulk discounts for large orders?

Yes! We offer volume discounts for orders of 50+ items. Contact our sales team with your quantity and product requirements for a customized quote.

What's your return policy?

Due to the custom nature of our products, we generally don't accept returns unless there's a defect or error on our part. If you receive a damaged or incorrect item, contact us within 7 days of delivery for a replacement.

Can I visit your facility to see samples?

Absolutely! We welcome visitors by appointment Monday-Friday 9am-4pm. Contact us to schedule a visit and we'll prepare sample products for you to see.

Have More Questions?

Our friendly customer service team is ready to help with any inquiries about our products, customization options, or your order.
