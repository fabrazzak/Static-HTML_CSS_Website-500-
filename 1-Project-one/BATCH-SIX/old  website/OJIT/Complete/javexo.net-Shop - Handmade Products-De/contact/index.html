Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping take?

We typically process and ship orders within 1-3 business days. Domestic shipping within Germany takes 2-5 business days. International shipping times vary by destination but generally take 7-14 business days. You'll receive tracking information once your order ships.

Do you offer wholesale pricing?

Yes! We love working with retailers and offer special wholesale pricing for qualified businesses. Please contact us at wholesale@javexo.net with details about your business and we'll send you our wholesale catalog and pricing information.

What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 30 days of delivery for unused items in their original packaging. Due to the handmade nature of our products, we cannot accept returns on customized or personalized items. Please contact us at returns@javexo.net to initiate a return.

How do I care for my handmade products?

Each product comes with specific care instructions. Generally, our ceramic items are dishwasher safe but handwashing is recommended to preserve the finish. Wooden items should be hand washed and periodically treated with food-safe mineral oil. Textile products should be spot cleaned or hand washed in cold water.

Can I visit your workshop?

Our workshop is open to visitors by appointment only. We offer guided tours on Fridays where you can see our artisans at work and learn about our crafting process. Please contact us at least 48 hours in advance to schedule your visit.
