Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our pricing and plans

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, and bank transfers. All payments are processed securely through our payment processor.

Can I cancel or change my plan anytime?

Yes! You can cancel or change your plan at any time. If you cancel, you'll continue to have access until the end of your current billing period. For annual plans, we offer prorated refunds for unused months.

Is there a free trial available?

We offer a 7-day free trial for our Standard plan. This gives you full access to all courses and features so you can experience everything Drayzo Music has to offer. No credit card is required to start your trial.

Do you offer discounts for students?

Yes, we offer a 20% student discount on all plans. Just verify your student status with us after signing up, and we'll apply the discount to your subscription.

Can I gift a subscription to someone else?

Absolutely! We offer gift subscriptions that you can purchase for friends or family members. The recipient will receive an email with instructions to activate their subscription.
