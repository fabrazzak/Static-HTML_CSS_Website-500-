Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can I expect a response to my inquiry?

We typically respond to all inquiries within 24 hours during our business hours (Monday-Friday, 9AM-5PM AWST). For course-related questions, you may receive an automated response with helpful resources immediately while you wait for our personal reply.

Do you offer one-on-one organizing consultations?

Yes! We offer limited private consultations for more personalized organizing advice. These sessions are available to students enrolled in our premium courses or as standalone services when availability permits. Please mention your interest in the contact form and we'll provide more details.

Can I get help choosing the right course for my needs?

Absolutely. Our team is happy to guide you to the course that best matches your organizing goals. When contacting us, please describe your current challenges and what you hope to achieve, and we'll recommend the most suitable options from our curriculum.

Do you offer corporate or group training?

We do provide customized organizational training for businesses, teams, and groups. Our corporate programs focus on workspace organization, digital decluttering, and productivity systems. Contact us with details about your organization and needs for a tailored proposal.
