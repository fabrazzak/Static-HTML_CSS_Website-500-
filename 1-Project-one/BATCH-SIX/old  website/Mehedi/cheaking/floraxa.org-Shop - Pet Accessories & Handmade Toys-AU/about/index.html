Our Story

Crafting premium pet accessories with love and natural materials since 2018

How It All Began

Floraxa was founded in 2018 by Emma Richardson, a passionate pet lover and craft enthusiast. What started as a small hobby making bandanas for her golden retriever, Max, quickly grew into a full-fledged business when friends and family began requesting custom pieces for their pets.

Emma's vision was simple: create beautiful, high-quality pet accessories that were as safe and durable as they were stylish. She was frustrated by the lack of natural, non-toxic options in the pet market and set out to change that.

Today, Floraxa has grown from a one-woman operation to a team of 12 dedicated artisans, but we've never lost sight of our roots. Every product is still handmade with the same care and attention to detail that went into Max's very first bandana.

Our Mission & Values

What guides everything we do at Floraxa

Pet Safety First

We use only non-toxic, pet-safe materials in all our products. Every item is tested for durability and safety before reaching your pet.

Sustainable Materials

From organic cotton to natural rubber, we source eco-friendly materials that are gentle on pets and the planet.

Handmade Quality

Each product is carefully crafted by skilled artisans who take pride in their work. No mass production, just thoughtful craftsmanship.

Pet Happiness

Our ultimate goal is creating products that pets love and that make their lives (and yours) more joyful.
