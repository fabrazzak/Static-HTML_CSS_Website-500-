Who We Are

Founded in 2020, auriora began as a small team of design educators frustrated by the high barriers to entry in the design industry. We noticed that many aspiring designers were held back by expensive software, inaccessible courses, and gatekeeping in the field.

Our solution was to create affordable, beginner-friendly courses that focus on fundamental principles rather than specific tools. While we teach industry-standard software, we emphasize design thinking that can be applied with any toolset.

Today, we've helped over 25,000 students worldwide develop their design skills, whether for professional growth, personal projects, or career transitions.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide everything we do

Accessible Education

We believe design skills should be available to everyone, not just those who can afford expensive art schools or software.

Practical Skills

Our courses focus on real-world applications, not just theory. You'll create portfolio-ready work from day one.

Community Focus

Learning happens best in community. We foster supportive spaces for feedback and collaboration.

Creative Confidence

We help students overcome "I'm not creative" mental blocks through structured, confidence-building exercises.

Inclusive Design

Accessibility and inclusivity are baked into our curriculum, not treated as afterthoughts.

Lifelong Learning

We model continuous growth and curiosity, showing that even experts are always learning.
