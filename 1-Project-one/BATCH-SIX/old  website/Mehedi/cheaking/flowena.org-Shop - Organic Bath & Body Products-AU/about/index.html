Our Story

Discover the passion and purpose behind Flowena's organic bath and body products

How It All Began

Flowena was born in 2020 from a simple idea: to create organic bath and body products that are as good for the earth as they are for your skin. Our founder, Sarah Williamson, started making small batches of soap in her kitchen after struggling to find natural products that were truly free from harsh chemicals.

What began as a personal mission soon grew into a community of like-minded individuals who shared Sarah's passion for clean, sustainable beauty. Today, we're proud to be a certified organic and cruelty-free brand, but our commitment to small-batch craftsmanship remains unchanged.

Every Flowena product is still carefully handcrafted with the same attention to detail and love that went into those first batches. We believe that what you put on your body matters just as much as what you put in it, and we're committed to making products you can trust.

Our Core Values

Our Sustainability Commitment

At Flowena, we believe beauty shouldn't cost the earth. That's why sustainability is at the heart of everything we do.

We source ingredients from certified organic farms that practice regenerative agriculture, supporting soil health and biodiversity. Our packaging is either recyclable, biodegradable, or reusable, and we've eliminated all single-use plastics from our supply chain.

In 2024, we became a carbon-neutral company by offsetting our emissions through verified reforestation projects. But we're not stopping there - our goal is to be carbon negative by 2026.
