Our Story

Founded in 2020, Flowena began with a simple mission: to create organic bath and body products that are as good for the earth as they are for your skin. Every product is carefully crafted using sustainably sourced ingredients.

We believe in transparency, quality, and the power of nature to heal and nourish. Our products are free from harsh chemicals, parabens, and synthetic fragrances.
