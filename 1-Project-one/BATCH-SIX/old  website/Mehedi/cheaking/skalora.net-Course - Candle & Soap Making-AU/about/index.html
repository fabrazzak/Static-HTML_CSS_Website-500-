Our Story

Discover the passion and craftsmanship behind Skalora's handmade candles and soaps

From Humble Beginnings

Skalora began in 2020 as a small home-based workshop founded by Jessica Williams, a passionate candle maker with a vision to create natural, high-quality products that bring warmth and comfort to people's homes.

What started as a hobby quickly grew into a thriving business as friends and family fell in love with Jessica's hand-poured soy candles. The addition of natural soap products came soon after, inspired by a desire to create a complete line of artisanal home goods.

Today, Skalora has evolved into a respected brand and educational platform, helping thousands of students discover the joy of candle and soap making while maintaining our commitment to quality, sustainability, and craftsmanship.
