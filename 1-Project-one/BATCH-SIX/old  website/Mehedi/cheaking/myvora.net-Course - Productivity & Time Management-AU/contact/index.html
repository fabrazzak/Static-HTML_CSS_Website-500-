Common Questions

How quickly can I expect a response to my inquiry?

We typically respond to all inquiries within 24 hours during our business hours (Monday-Friday, 9AM-5PM). If you contact us over the weekend, we'll get back to you first thing Monday morning.

Do you offer in-person training or consultations?

Yes, we offer in-person training sessions and consultations for businesses and organizations. Please contact us with details about your needs, and we'll provide you with available options and pricing.

Can I schedule a call with one of your productivity coaches?

Absolutely! We offer free 15-minute discovery calls with our coaches. Use our contact form to request a call, and we'll arrange a time that works for you.

Do you have a physical office I can visit?

Our headquarters are located at the address above, but we primarily operate by appointment only. Please contact us before planning a visit so we can ensure someone is available to assist you.
