Our Story

Discover how EnvironIQ is revolutionizing children's education through innovative toys and STEM kits that make learning an exciting adventure.

Who We Are

Passionate educators and innovators dedicated to children's learning

Founded in 2025, EnvironIQ began with a simple idea: learning should be as exciting as playtime. Our team of educators, engineers, and child development experts came together to create educational toys that spark curiosity and inspire young minds.

What started as a small workshop creating science kits for local schools has grown into an international provider of high-quality STEM educational materials. Today, we serve thousands of families and schools worldwide, all united by the belief that hands-on learning creates lasting understanding.

We carefully design each product to align with educational standards while maintaining the fun, engaging elements that children love. Every EnvironIQ toy is tested by children and approved by educators before reaching your home.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide everything we do

1

Educational Excellence

Every product must deliver real learning outcomes. We work with educators to ensure our toys align with curriculum standards and effectively teach key concepts.

2

Safety First

All materials are non-toxic and rigorously tested. We exceed international safety standards to ensure children can explore and learn without risk.

3

Sustainable Practices

We're committed to minimizing our environmental impact through eco-friendly materials, responsible manufacturing, and recyclable packaging.

4

Inclusive Design

Our products are designed to engage all children, regardless of gender, ability, or learning style. We celebrate diversity in how children learn and play.

5

Continuous Innovation

We constantly research and develop new ways to make learning exciting. Our team stays at the forefront of educational trends and technologies.

6

Community Impact

We donate a portion of profits to educational initiatives and partner with schools to bring STEM learning to underserved communities.
