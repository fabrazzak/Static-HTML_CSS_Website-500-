Pricing FAQ

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, and bank transfers for annual plans.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Your billing will be prorated based on your current subscription.

Is there a free trial available?

We offer a 7-day free trial for our Basic plan so you can explore our platform before committing.

Do you offer discounts for students?

Yes! Students can get 15% off any plan with a valid student ID. Contact our support team for verification.

What's your refund policy?

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee if you're not satisfied with your purchase.

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. There are no long-term contracts, and you can cancel your subscription anytime with just a few clicks.
