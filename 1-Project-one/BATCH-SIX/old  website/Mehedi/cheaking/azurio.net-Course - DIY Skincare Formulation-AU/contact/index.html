Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our courses and services

How do I enroll in a course?

You can enroll in any of our courses by visiting the Courses page and selecting the course you're interested in. Click the "Enroll Now" button and complete the registration process. You'll receive immediate access to the course materials upon payment.

Do I need any special equipment for the courses?

Our beginner courses require only basic kitchen equipment (bowls, measuring spoons, etc.). For advanced courses, we provide a detailed equipment list before the course begins. Most specialty equipment can be purchased online or at local stores.

Can I get personalized formulation advice?

Yes! Our Premium course tier includes one-on-one formulation reviews with our instructors. You can also post questions in our community forums where our team and other students can offer suggestions.

What if I have problems accessing my course?

If you're experiencing technical difficulties, please email support@azurio.net with a description of the issue. Our team typically responds to support requests within 4 hours during business days.
