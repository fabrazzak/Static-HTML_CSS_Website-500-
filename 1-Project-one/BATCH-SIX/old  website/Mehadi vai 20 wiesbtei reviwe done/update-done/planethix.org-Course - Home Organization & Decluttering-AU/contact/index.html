Common Questions

How quickly can I expect a response to my inquiry?
We strive to respond to all inquiries within 24-48 hours during our business hours (Monday to Friday, 9am-5pm AEST). For urgent matters, please call our support line for immediate assistance.
Do you offer in-person consultations?
Yes, we offer limited in-person consultations in the Perth metropolitan area for Premium plan members. For those outside this area, we offer comprehensive virtual consultations via video call.
Can I get help choosing the right course for my needs?
Absolutely! Our team would be happy to guide you to the course that best matches your goals and current situation. Just let us know what challenges you're facing with your space and we'll make personalized recommendations.
Do you offer corporate or group training?
Yes, we provide specialized corporate training programs focused on workplace organization and productivity. We also offer group rates for organizations looking to train multiple team members in home organization techniques.
