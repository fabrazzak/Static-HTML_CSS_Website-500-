About zorylo

Discover our mission to make mindfulness and meditation accessible to everyone, and meet the team behind our transformative courses.

Our Story

Founded in 2025, zorylo began with a simple vision: to help people find peace and clarity in an increasingly busy world. Our founder, Elena Rodriguez, recognized the growing need for accessible, science-based mindfulness practices that could fit into modern lifestyles.

What started as a small meditation group in Bad Brambach, United Kingdom, has grown into an international platform serving thousands of students worldwide. We remain committed to our original principles of authenticity, accessibility, and evidence-based practice.

At zorylo, we believe mindfulness isn't just a practice—it's a way of living. Our approach combines ancient wisdom with modern psychology to create programs that are both transformative and practical.
