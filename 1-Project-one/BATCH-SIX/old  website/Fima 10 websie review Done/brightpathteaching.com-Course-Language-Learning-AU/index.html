Frequently Asked Questions

What level of language proficiency do I need to start?

Our courses cater to all levels, from complete beginners to advanced learners. During enrollment, we'll assess your current level to place you in the appropriate class. Don't worry if you're starting from scratch - our beginner courses are designed specifically for those with no prior experience.

How much time should I dedicate to studying each week?

We recommend dedicating at least 5-7 hours per week to your language studies for optimal progress. This includes 2-3 hours of live instruction (depending on your plan) and 2-4 hours of self-study with our provided materials. However, you can adjust this based on your personal goals and schedule.

What teaching methods do you use?

We employ a communicative approach that emphasizes real-world language use from day one. Our methods include task-based learning, immersive techniques, and spaced repetition for vocabulary retention. Lessons combine structured grammar instruction with plenty of conversational practice to build confidence and fluency.

Can I switch courses or cancel my subscription?

Yes, you can switch to a different language course at any time, provided there's availability. For cancellations, we require 14 days' notice before your next billing cycle. There are no long-term contracts - you can cancel anytime, though we'd love to hear how we could improve your experience.

Do you offer certificates upon completion?

Yes, Premium plan students receive a certificate of completion for each course level they finish. Our certificates indicate the CEFR level achieved (A1-C2) and can be used for professional development or academic purposes. Basic and Standard plan students can upgrade to receive certification.
