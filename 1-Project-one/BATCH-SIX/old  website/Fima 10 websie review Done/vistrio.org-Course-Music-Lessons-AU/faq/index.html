Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our music lessons, courses, enrollment process, and more. If you don't find what you're looking for, feel free to contact us.

General Questions

What is Vistrio Music?

Vistrio Music is an online music education platform that provides high-quality music lessons for students of all ages and skill levels. We offer courses in various instruments including piano, guitar, violin, and voice, taught by experienced professional musicians.

Our mission is to make quality music education accessible to everyone, regardless of location or schedule constraints.

Who are the instructors at Vistrio Music?

Our instructors are highly qualified professional musicians with extensive teaching experience. Each instructor goes through a rigorous selection process to ensure they meet our high standards for both musical expertise and teaching ability.

You can view all of our instructors and their credentials on our About page.

Do I need any prior musical experience?

No prior experience is necessary! We offer courses for complete beginners as well as more advanced students. Each course clearly indicates the recommended skill level, and our instructors are skilled at adapting lessons to individual needs.

If you're unsure where to start, we offer a free assessment to help determine the right course for your current skill level.

Course Questions

How do the online courses work?

After enrolling in a course, you'll gain immediate access to our learning platform where you can:

  • Watch pre-recorded video lessons at your own pace
  • Download lesson materials and sheet music
  • Complete practice exercises and assignments
  • Receive feedback from instructors (depending on your plan)
  • Participate in live Q&A sessions (Premium plan)

All courses are self-paced, but we recommend following the suggested weekly schedule for best results.

What equipment do I need for the courses?

The basic requirements for most courses include:

  • The instrument you're learning (piano, guitar, violin, etc.)
  • A computer or tablet with internet access
  • Headphones or speakers
  • A quiet space to practice

Some courses may recommend additional accessories like a metronome, music stand, or specific types of strings/reeds, but these aren't required to begin. Course-specific requirements are listed on each course page.

Can I switch courses after enrolling?

Yes, you can switch to a different course within the first 14 days of enrollment if you find the current course isn't the right fit for you. Simply contact our support team, and we'll help you make the switch.

After 14 days, course switches are evaluated on a case-by-case basis. We want to ensure you're in the right course for your goals and skill level.

Pricing & Plans

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, and Apple Pay. All payments are processed securely through our payment processor.

For annual plans, we also accept bank transfers. Please contact our support team for bank transfer instructions.

Is there a free trial available?

Yes! We offer a 7-day free trial for our Standard and Premium plans. The trial gives you full access to all course materials so you can experience our teaching style before committing.

No credit card is required for the trial. At the end of the trial period, you can choose to continue with a paid subscription or cancel with no obligation.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time with no cancellation fees. When you cancel, you'll retain access to the courses until the end of your current billing period.

To cancel, simply go to your account settings and follow the cancellation instructions. We'd appreciate it if you could share your reason for canceling so we can improve our service.

Technical Support

What are the system requirements?

Our platform works on most modern devices and browsers. Here are the recommended specifications:

  • Computer: Windows 10/macOS 10.14 or later, 4GB RAM
  • Tablet: iOS 13/Android 10 or later
  • Browser: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge (latest versions)
  • Internet: Minimum 5Mbps download speed for HD video

For the best experience, we recommend using a computer with the Chrome browser and a stable internet connection.

How do I access the courses on multiple devices?

You can access your courses on any device by logging into your Vistrio Music account. Your progress is automatically synced across all devices.

For security reasons, you can be logged in on up to 3 devices simultaneously. If you need to log out from a device, you can do so from your account settings.

What should I do if I'm having technical issues?

If you're experiencing technical difficulties, try these troubleshooting steps first:

  1. Refresh the page or restart your device
  2. Clear your browser cache and cookies
  3. Try using a different browser
  4. Check your internet connection

If the issue persists, please contact our support team at support@vistrio.org with details about the problem, including:

  • The device and browser you're using
  • A description of the issue
  • Screenshots if possible
