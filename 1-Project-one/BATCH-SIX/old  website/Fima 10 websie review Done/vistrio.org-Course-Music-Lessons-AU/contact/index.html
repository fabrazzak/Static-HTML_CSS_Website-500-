Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our music lessons and services.

How soon can I expect a response to my inquiry?

We typically respond to all inquiries within 24 hours during business days (Monday-Friday). If you contact us on the weekend, we'll get back to you by the next business day.

Do you offer in-person lessons at your studio?

Yes! While we specialize in online lessons, we do offer in-person instruction at our Fremantle studio for local students. You can indicate your preference when you contact us or enroll in a course.

What's the best way to reach you for urgent matters?

For urgent matters related to scheduled lessons or technical issues, please call us directly at +61 8 6256 5662 during business hours. For after-hours emergencies related to scheduled lessons, please text the same number.

Can I schedule a trial lesson before committing?

Absolutely! We offer a 30-minute trial lesson for new students to meet their instructor and experience our teaching style. Contact us to schedule your trial lesson.
