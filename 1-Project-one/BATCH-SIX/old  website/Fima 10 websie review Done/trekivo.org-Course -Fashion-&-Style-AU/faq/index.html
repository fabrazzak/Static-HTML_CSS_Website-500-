General Questions

What makes Trekivo Fashion courses different?

Trekivo Fashion courses are designed by industry professionals with real-world experience. Our courses focus on practical, actionable knowledge you can apply immediately to improve your personal style. Unlike many fashion resources, we emphasize sustainable practices and building a timeless wardrobe.

Do I need any prior fashion knowledge?

No prior knowledge is required for most of our courses. We've designed our curriculum to be accessible to complete beginners while still providing valuable insights for those with more fashion experience. Our foundational courses will give you all the basics you need to progress to more advanced topics.

Are the courses suitable for all genders?

Absolutely! While some courses may focus on specific styling techniques for different body types or gender expressions, the fundamental principles of style, color theory, and wardrobe building apply universally. We have courses specifically tailored to menswear, womenswear, and gender-neutral styling.

Course Access & Content

How do I access the courses after purchasing?

After completing your purchase, you'll receive an email with login details to our learning platform. All your purchased courses will be available in your account dashboard immediately. You can access them anytime from any device with internet access.

What format are the courses delivered in?

Our courses include a mix of:

  • High-quality video lessons
  • Downloadable workbooks and resources
  • Interactive style exercises
  • Community discussion boards
  • Optional live Q&A sessions (for subscription plans)

You can progress at your own pace and revisit materials anytime.

How long do I have access to each course?

Access duration depends on your purchase type:

  • Subscription plans: Access to all courses for as long as your subscription is active
  • Individual course purchases: Lifetime access to that specific course
  • Free courses: Unlimited access

Subscriptions & Payments

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time through your account settings. Your access will continue until the end of your current billing period. We don't lock you into long-term contracts or charge cancellation fees.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, and Apple Pay. For enterprise or group purchases, we also accept bank transfers (contact us for details).

Do you offer refunds?

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee for all individual course purchases if you're not satisfied. Subscription plans can be canceled before the next billing cycle. To request a refund, please contact our support team with your purchase details.

Certificates & Completion

Do you offer certificates upon completion?

Yes, all our paid courses include a certificate of completion that you can download and share once you've finished all course materials and assignments. Our certificates are digital and can be added to LinkedIn or printed for your records.

Are your certificates accredited?

While our certificates are not formally accredited by educational institutions, they are recognized within the fashion industry. Many of our students have used their certificates to enhance their professional profiles, transition into styling careers, or build credibility for their fashion-related businesses.

What do I need to do to earn a certificate?

To earn your certificate, you'll need to:

  • Complete all video lessons
  • Submit any required assignments (typically style exercises or wardrobe assessments)
  • Pass the course quiz (if applicable) with a score of 80% or higher

Most students earn their certificates within 2-4 weeks of starting a course, depending on the pace they choose.

Technical Support

What devices can I use to access courses?

Our platform is fully responsive and works on:

  • Desktop and laptop computers (Windows, Mac, Linux)
  • Tablets (iPad, Android tablets)
  • Smartphones (iPhone, Android)

We recommend using the latest version of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge for the best experience. Our mobile app is coming soon!

Can I download videos for offline viewing?

Currently, video streaming is online-only for copyright protection reasons. However, all supporting materials (workbooks, checklists, etc.) are downloadable, and we're working on offline access options for future updates.

What if I'm having technical issues?

We're here to help! Please try these troubleshooting steps first:

  • Clear your browser cache and cookies
  • Try a different browser or device
  • Check your internet connection

If you're still experiencing issues, contact our support team at support@trekivo.org with details about the problem and any error messages you're seeing. We typically respond within 24 hours.

Still Have Questions?

Can't find what you're looking for? Our support team is happy to help with any additional questions you may have about our courses or platform.
