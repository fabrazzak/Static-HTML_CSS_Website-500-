Pricing FAQs

Find answers to common questions about our pricing and plans.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, and bank transfers for Australian customers.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time through your account settings. Changes will take effect at your next billing cycle.

Is there a free trial available?

We offer a 7-day free trial for our Standard plan so you can experience our platform before committing. No credit card is required for the trial.

Do you offer discounts for students?

Yes, we offer a 15% student discount with valid student ID. Contact our support team with your student verification to apply this discount.

Are there any hidden fees?

No, our pricing is completely transparent. The price you see is what you'll be charged each billing cycle, with no hidden fees or surprises.

Do you offer annual billing options?

Yes, we offer annual billing with a 10% discount compared to monthly payments. You can select annual billing during checkout.
