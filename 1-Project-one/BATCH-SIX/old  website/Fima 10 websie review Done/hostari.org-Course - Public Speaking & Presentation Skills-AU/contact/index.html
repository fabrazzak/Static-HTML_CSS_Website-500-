Common Questions

How quickly can I expect a response to my inquiry?

We typically respond to all inquiries within 24 hours during our business hours (Monday-Friday, 9am-5pm AWST). If you contact us over the weekend, we'll get back to you first thing Monday morning.

Do you offer corporate training programs?

Yes! We specialize in customized corporate training programs for teams of all sizes. Our corporate programs can be tailored to your specific industry and communication challenges. Contact us to discuss your organization's needs.

Can I schedule a consultation before enrolling in a course?

Absolutely. We offer free 30-minute consultations to help you determine which course is right for you. During the consultation, we'll discuss your goals, assess your current skill level, and recommend the best program for your needs.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, and bank transfers. For corporate clients, we also offer invoice payment options with net-30 terms.
