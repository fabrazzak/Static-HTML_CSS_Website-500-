Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our pricing and plans below. Can't find what you're looking for? Contact us.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, and bank transfers. All payments are processed securely through our payment partner Stripe.

Can I cancel or change my plan anytime?

Yes, you can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your plan at any time. If you cancel, you'll retain access until the end of your current billing period. Annual plans can be canceled with a prorated refund for unused months.

Do you offer discounts for students?

We offer a 20% student discount on all plans. Simply verify your student status during signup using your .edu email address or by uploading a current student ID. The discount applies as long as you remain a student.

Is there a free trial available?

We offer a 7-day free trial for our Pro plan, which gives you full access to all courses and features. No credit card is required to start the trial. After 7 days, you can choose to continue with a paid plan or cancel with no obligation.

How does the 1-on-1 mentoring work?

Our 1-on-1 mentoring sessions are scheduled at your convenience through our platform. You'll meet with an experienced fashion illustrator via video call to discuss your work, get personalized feedback, and receive guidance on your creative development. Pro plan members get one 30-minute session per month, while Annual members get two.
