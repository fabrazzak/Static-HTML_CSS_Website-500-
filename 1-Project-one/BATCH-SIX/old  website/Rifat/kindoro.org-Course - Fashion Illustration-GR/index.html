Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need any prior experience to join these courses?

No prior experience is necessary for our beginner courses. We offer courses at all levels, from complete beginner to advanced. Each course description indicates the required skill level, and we provide recommendations for which courses to take in what order if you're just starting out.

What materials do I need for the courses?

For traditional illustration courses, you'll need basic drawing materials like pencils, markers, and paper. For digital courses, you'll need a tablet and appropriate software (we primarily teach using Procreate and Photoshop). Each course includes a detailed materials list upon enrollment, and we provide recommendations for affordable options.

How long do I have access to the course materials?

Access duration depends on your subscription plan. With the Basic plan, you maintain access to completed courses for 3 months after finishing. Standard plan gives you 6 months access, and Premium offers lifetime access to all completed courses. You can always download certain resources to keep permanently.

Can I get feedback on my work?

Yes! All plans include some level of feedback. Basic includes community feedback, Standard includes monthly instructor feedback, and Premium includes weekly feedback plus portfolio reviews. We believe personalized feedback is crucial for improvement in fashion illustration.
