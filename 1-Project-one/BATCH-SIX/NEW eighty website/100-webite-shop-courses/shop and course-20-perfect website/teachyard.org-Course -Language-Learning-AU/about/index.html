How Teachyard Began

Teachyard was founded in 2018 by language enthusiasts Emma Wilson and Carlos Mendez in Fremantle, Australia. What started as a small local language school has grown into a leading online language learning platform serving students across the globe.

Frustrated by the limitations of traditional language education, our founders set out to create a more accessible, flexible, and effective way to learn languages. They combined their expertise in linguistics and education technology to develop a unique approach that blends structured curriculum with personalized instruction.

Today, Teachyard offers comprehensive courses in six languages, taught by a team of dedicated instructors who share our passion for language and cultural exchange. Our student community spans over 50 countries, united by the common goal of breaking language barriers.
