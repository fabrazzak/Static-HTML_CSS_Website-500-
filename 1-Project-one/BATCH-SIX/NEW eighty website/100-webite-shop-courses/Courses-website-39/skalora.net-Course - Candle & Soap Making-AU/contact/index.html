Common Questions

What's the best way to contact Skalora?

For general inquiries, emailing info@skalora.net is the fastest way to reach us. For urgent matters, please call our studio during business hours. If you're inquiring about a specific course, mentioning the course name in your message will help us respond more efficiently.

Do you offer in-person consultations?

Yes! We offer free 30-minute consultations at our studio by appointment. These are perfect for discussing custom course options, bulk orders, or business opportunities. Please contact us to schedule a time that works for you.

How long does it typically take to get a response?

We strive to respond to all inquiries within 24 hours during business days (Monday-Friday). During peak seasons or holidays, response times may extend to 48 hours. If you haven't heard back within this timeframe, please check your spam folder or call us directly.

Can I visit your studio without an appointment?

Our studio is open to visitors Monday-Friday from 10am to 4pm. However, we recommend contacting us in advance if you'd like a tour or to meet with a specific instructor, as we occasionally have private workshops or events that may limit access to certain areas.
