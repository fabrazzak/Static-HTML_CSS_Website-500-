Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need any prior experience to take these courses?

No prior experience is necessary for our beginner courses. We design our curriculum to accommodate all skill levels, from complete beginners to more experienced writers looking to refine their skills.

How long do I have access to the course materials?

You'll have access to all course materials for as long as your subscription is active. If you cancel, you'll retain access until the end of your current billing period.

Can I get a refund if I'm not satisfied?

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee for all new subscribers. If you're not satisfied with your courses within the first two weeks, we'll give you a full refund.

How much time should I dedicate to the courses each week?

We recommend dedicating 3-5 hours per week to get the most out of your courses. However, you can progress at your own pace and access materials whenever it's convenient for you.

Will I receive a certificate upon completion?

Yes! All our courses include a certificate of completion that you can add to your resume or LinkedIn profile after successfully finishing the course requirements.
