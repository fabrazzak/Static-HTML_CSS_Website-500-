Pricing FAQs

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and bank transfers. All payments are processed securely through our payment partners.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. When you upgrade, you'll immediately gain access to the additional features and your billing will be prorated. Downgrades will take effect at your next billing cycle.

Do you offer discounts for students or families?

We offer a 15% discount for students with valid school ID and for families enrolling multiple members. We also offer group discounts for schools and organizations. Contact our support team for more information.

Is there a free trial available?

Yes! We offer a 7-day free trial for all new students. You'll get full access to one course of your choice during the trial period. No credit card is required to start your trial.

What's your refund policy?

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee. If you're not satisfied with your lessons within the first 30 days, we'll give you a full refund, no questions asked.
