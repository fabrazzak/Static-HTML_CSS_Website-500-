Our Story

Planet Hix was founded in 2020 with a simple mission: to help people transform their living spaces into organized, peaceful environments that support their goals and wellbeing.

What began as a small blog sharing organization tips has grown into a comprehensive platform offering courses, resources, and personalized guidance to thousands of students worldwide.

Our founder, Jennifer Lee, started Planet Hix after helping friends and family declutter their homes. She realized that while many people wanted to be more organized, they lacked the systems and support to make lasting changes.

Today, our team of professional organizers, minimalism experts, and productivity coaches work together to create practical, sustainable solutions for homes of all sizes and styles.

Our Mission & Values

At Planet Hix, we believe that an organized home leads to a more peaceful, productive life. Our mission is to empower people to create living spaces that reflect their priorities and support their wellbeing.

We approach organization holistically, recognizing that decluttering isn't just about physical space—it's about creating room for what truly matters in your life.

Our methods combine practical organizing techniques with psychological insights to help you not only tidy your space but develop habits that prevent clutter from accumulating in the future.
