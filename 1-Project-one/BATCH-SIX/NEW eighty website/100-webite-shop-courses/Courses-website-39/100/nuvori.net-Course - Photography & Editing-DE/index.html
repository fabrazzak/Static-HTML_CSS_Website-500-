Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need expensive equipment to take these courses?

Not at all! While having a DSLR or mirrorless camera can be beneficial, many of our courses are designed to work with whatever equipment you have, including smartphones. We focus on teaching principles that apply across all types of cameras.

How long do I have access to the courses?

With our Standard and Premium plans, you'll have unlimited access to all courses for as long as you maintain your subscription. For individual course purchases, you'll have lifetime access to that specific course.

Are there any prerequisites for the courses?

Most of our courses are designed to accommodate all skill levels. Beginner courses assume no prior knowledge, while advanced courses will clearly state any prerequisites in their descriptions.

Can I download the course materials?

Yes! All our courses include downloadable resources like cheat sheets, presets, and practice files that you can keep forever, even if you cancel your subscription.

What if I'm not satisfied with a course?

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all courses and subscriptions. If you're not completely satisfied, just contact our support team for a full refund.
