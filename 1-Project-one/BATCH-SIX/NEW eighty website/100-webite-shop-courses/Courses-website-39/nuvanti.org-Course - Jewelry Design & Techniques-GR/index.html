Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our jewelry design courses.

Do I need any prior experience to join the courses?

No prior experience is necessary for our beginner courses. We start with the fundamentals and gradually progress to more advanced techniques. Our Basic Jewelry Design course is specifically designed for complete beginners.

What tools and materials will I need?

For beginner courses, we provide a list of basic tools that are affordable and easy to source. As you progress to advanced courses, we'll recommend professional-grade tools. Many basic projects can be completed with simple hand tools.

How are the courses delivered?

All courses are delivered through our online platform with video lessons, downloadable resources, and project assignments. You'll have access to our community forum and regular Q&A sessions with instructors based on your plan.

Can I access the courses on mobile devices?

Yes, our platform is fully responsive and works on smartphones, tablets, and computers. However, some detailed techniques may be easier to follow on a larger screen.

What if I need help during the course?

All plans include access to our community forum where you can ask questions. Standard and Premium plans include direct mentoring sessions with instructors for personalized guidance.
