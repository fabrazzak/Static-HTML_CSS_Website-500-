Frequently Asked Questions

How do I access the courses after purchasing?

After completing your purchase, you'll receive an email with login credentials to our learning platform. All your enrolled courses will be available in your dashboard immediately. You can access them anytime, from any device with internet connection.

Are there any prerequisites for the courses?

Most of our beginner courses require no prior knowledge. For intermediate and advanced courses, we clearly list any prerequisites in the course description. Some editing courses may require you to have the relevant software (like Adobe Lightroom or Photoshop) installed.

Can I download the course materials?

Yes, most course materials (PDF guides, presets, RAW files for practice) are available for download. Video lessons are streamed online but can be accessed anytime during your subscription period.

What's your refund policy?

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee for all courses. If you're not satisfied with your purchase, simply contact our support team within 14 days of enrollment for a full refund, no questions asked.

How often are new courses added?

We release 2-3 new courses every quarter. Premium members get immediate access to all new courses as part of their subscription. We also regularly update existing courses with new content.
