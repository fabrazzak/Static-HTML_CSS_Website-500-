Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our pricing and plans

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, and bank transfers. All payments are processed securely through our payment processor.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. When you switch plans, you'll receive a prorated credit for any unused time on your current plan.

Is there a free trial available?

We don't offer a free trial, but we do have a 30-day money-back guarantee. If you're not satisfied with your purchase within the first 30 days, we'll give you a full refund.

How does the yearly subscription work?

Our yearly subscription offers a 20% discount compared to paying monthly. You'll be billed once per year and have access to all the features of your chosen plan for the entire year.

Do you offer discounts for teams or organizations?

Yes, we offer special pricing for teams of 5 or more. Please contact our sales team at teams@ethicra.org for more information about our group discounts.
