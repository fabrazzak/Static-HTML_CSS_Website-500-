Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our courses and services

What's the best way to get started with your courses?

We recommend starting with our Time Management Fundamentals course, which provides the essential foundation for all our other programs. After completing this, you can progress to more specialized courses based on your specific needs and goals. Our team is also happy to provide personalized recommendations if you contact us with details about your current challenges and objectives.

Do you offer corporate training for teams?

Yes, we offer customized corporate training programs designed to improve team productivity and workflow efficiency. Our corporate solutions include workshops, ongoing coaching, and implementation support to ensure lasting results. We tailor each program to your organization's specific needs, industry, and challenges. Contact our corporate training director Emma Wilson to discuss options.

What's your refund policy for courses?

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee for all our courses. If you're not satisfied with your purchase for any reason, simply contact our support team within 30 days of enrollment for a full refund. No questions asked. We're confident in the quality of our courses and want you to feel completely comfortable investing in your productivity development.

Can I get certified in your productivity methods?

Yes, we offer a Productivity Professional Certification program for individuals who complete our core curriculum and pass the certification exam. This program is ideal for coaches, consultants, and professionals who want to demonstrate their expertise in our methodology. The certification includes ongoing support and resources to help you implement these strategies with clients or within your organization.

How often do you update your course content?

We review and update all course materials quarterly to incorporate the latest productivity research, tools, and best practices. Major revisions are implemented annually. All enrolled students receive free lifetime access to updated versions of the courses they've purchased, so you'll always have access to the most current information.
