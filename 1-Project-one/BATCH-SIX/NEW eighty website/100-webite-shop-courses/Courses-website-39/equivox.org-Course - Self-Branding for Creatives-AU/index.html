Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our courses

Who are these courses for?

Our courses are designed for creative professionals across all disciplines—designers, artists, writers, photographers, illustrators, and more. Whether you're just starting out or looking to elevate your existing brand, our courses provide valuable insights and practical strategies.

How long do I have access to the courses?

You'll have access to the courses for as long as your subscription is active. Our Basic, Standard, and Premium plans are all monthly subscriptions that you can cancel anytime. We also offer annual plans with discounts—contact us for more information.

What's the time commitment for each course?

Most courses include 4-6 hours of video content, plus exercises and assignments. You can complete them at your own pace. We recommend setting aside 2-3 hours per week to work through the material and implement what you're learning.

Do you offer certificates upon completion?

Yes! All our courses include a certificate of completion that you can add to your LinkedIn profile or portfolio. Our Premium plan also includes a personalized letter of recommendation from one of our instructors.

What if I'm not satisfied with the courses?

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee. If you're not completely satisfied with your purchase, simply contact us within 14 days of enrollment for a full refund, no questions asked.
