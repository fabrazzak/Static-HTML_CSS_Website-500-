Frequently Asked Questions

How do I enroll in a course?

You can enroll in any of our courses by visiting the Courses page, selecting your desired program, and clicking the "Enroll Now" button. You'll be guided through the registration process where you can choose your payment plan and create your student account.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, and bank transfers. All payments are processed securely through our payment gateway partners.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes, we offer a 14-day money-back guarantee for all our courses. If you're not satisfied with your purchase within the first two weeks, you can request a full refund with no questions asked.

Can I access courses on mobile devices?

Absolutely! Our platform is fully responsive and works on all devices including smartphones and tablets. You can access your courses anytime, anywhere with an internet connection.

How do I contact my instructor?

Each course has a dedicated discussion area where you can ask questions and interact with your instructor. For private matters, you can message instructors directly through our platform's messaging system.
