Common Questions

How quickly can I expect a response to my inquiry? +

We typically respond to all inquiries within 24-48 hours during our business hours (Monday-Friday, 9am-5pm AEST). If you contact us over the weekend, we'll get back to you first thing Monday morning.

Do you offer in-person consultations? +

Yes! We'd be happy to schedule an in-person consultation at our studio. Please contact us to arrange a suitable time. We also offer virtual consultations via Zoom for students who can't visit in person.

What's the best way to reach you for urgent matters? +

For urgent matters related to ongoing courses or workshops, please call our main office number during business hours. For after-hours emergencies related to scheduled classes, please text our emergency line at +61886759902.

Do you offer corporate or group workshops? +

Absolutely! We love working with groups and can customize workshops for corporate teams, private parties, or special events. Contact us with your group size, preferred dates, and any specific focus areas, and we'll create a tailored proposal for you.
