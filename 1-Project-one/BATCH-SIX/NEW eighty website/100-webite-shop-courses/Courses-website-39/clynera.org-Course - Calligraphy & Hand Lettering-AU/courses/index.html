Modern Calligraphy Fundamentals

6 Weeks Sarah Johnson Beginner

Course Overview

This comprehensive beginner's course will introduce you to the beautiful art of modern calligraphy. Over six weeks, you'll learn all the fundamental techniques needed to create elegant letterforms and develop your own unique calligraphy style.

Perfect for absolute beginners, this course starts with the very basics - how to hold your pen, understanding pressure and release, and mastering basic strokes. From there, we'll build up to forming complete alphabets, connecting letters, and creating balanced compositions.

By the end of the course, you'll have the skills to create beautiful calligraphy for personal projects, gifts, or even professional work.
