Fiction Writing Fundamentals

Master the essential techniques of crafting compelling fiction

Course Description

This comprehensive course covers all the essential elements of fiction writing, from developing compelling characters to crafting engaging plots. You'll learn techniques for creating vivid settings, writing authentic dialogue, and building narrative tension.

Through a series of writing exercises and assignments, you'll apply these techniques to your own work and receive detailed feedback from our experienced instructors. By the end of the course, you'll have a solid foundation in fiction writing and a portfolio of work to build upon.

What You'll Learn

  • How to create complex, believable characters
  • Techniques for building compelling plots
  • Methods for writing authentic dialogue
  • Strategies for creating vivid settings
  • Approaches to point of view and narrative voice
  • Techniques for building and maintaining tension
