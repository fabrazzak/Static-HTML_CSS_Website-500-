Who are these public speaking courses designed for?

Our courses are designed for professionals at all levels who want to improve their public speaking and presentation skills. Whether you're a beginner who gets nervous speaking in front of groups or an experienced speaker looking to refine your technique, we have courses that will help you achieve your goals. Our students range from executives and entrepreneurs to educators and recent graduates.

How are the courses delivered?

All courses are delivered through our online learning platform, which you can access from any device. Each course includes:

  • Video lessons with expert instructors
  • Downloadable workbooks and resources
  • Practical exercises to apply what you learn
  • For Standard and Premium plans: Live coaching sessions
  • Community forum to connect with other students

You can complete the courses at your own pace, with most students spending 3-5 hours per week.

When can I start the courses?

You can start immediately after enrollment! All of our courses are available on-demand, so you can begin learning as soon as you complete the registration process. There are no fixed start dates - you have complete flexibility to learn at your own pace.

For courses that include live components (like our Standard and Premium plans), you'll receive a schedule of upcoming sessions after enrollment, and can join the next available session.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, and bank transfers. All payments are processed securely through our encrypted payment gateway.

For corporate accounts or bulk enrollments, we also accept wire transfers and can issue invoices. Please contact our sales team for these arrangements.

Is there a money-back guarantee?

Yes! We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee for all our courses. If you're not completely satisfied with your purchase, simply contact us within 30 days of enrollment for a full refund, no questions asked.

For our Premium coaching program, we require you to attend at least two coaching sessions before requesting a refund, as the value becomes apparent through the personalized feedback.

What are the technical requirements?

To access our courses, you'll need:

  • A computer, tablet, or smartphone
  • A modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
  • A reliable internet connection (minimum 5Mbps recommended for video)
  • For live sessions: A webcam and microphone (built-in or external)

Our platform is optimized for both desktop and mobile use, so you can learn on whatever device is most convenient for you.

How much time should I dedicate to the courses?

Most students spend 3-5 hours per week on course materials and practice. However, you can progress at your own pace. The courses are designed to be flexible for busy professionals.

Here's a typical weekly breakdown:

  • 1-2 hours watching video lessons
  • 1 hour completing exercises
  • 1 hour practicing techniques
  • For Standard/Premium: 1 hour live session (bi-weekly/weekly)

Of course, the more time you dedicate to practice, the faster you'll see results.

Can I switch between courses or plans?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. When you upgrade, you'll only pay the difference between your current plan and the new one. Downgrades will take effect at your next billing cycle.

If you want to switch to a different course within the same plan level, you can do so at no additional cost. Just contact our support team and we'll help you make the change.

Are there any discounts available?

We occasionally offer seasonal promotions and discounts. Sign up for our newsletter to stay informed about special offers. We also offer:

  • 20% discount for annual payments compared to monthly billing
  • Group discounts for teams of 3+ people
  • Nonprofit and educational discounts (contact us for details)
How long do I have access to the courses?

Access duration depends on your plan:

  • Basic Plan: Access for the duration of your subscription
  • Standard Plan: Access for the duration of your subscription plus 3 months after
  • Premium Plan: Lifetime access to all materials
  • Individual Course Purchases: Lifetime access to that specific course

All plans include all future updates to the courses you've purchased.
