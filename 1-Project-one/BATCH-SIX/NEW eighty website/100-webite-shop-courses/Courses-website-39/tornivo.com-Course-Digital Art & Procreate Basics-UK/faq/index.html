Common Questions

Everything you need to know about learning with Tornivo

Getting Started

Do I need prior art experience to take these courses?

No prior experience is necessary for our beginner courses. We start with the fundamentals and gradually build up your skills. Our Procreate Basics course is specifically designed for complete beginners, while our intermediate and advanced courses do require some foundational knowledge.

What equipment do I need for the courses?

You'll need an iPad (6th generation or newer) and an Apple Pencil (1st or 2nd generation). All our courses use Procreate, which you'll need to purchase from the App Store (it's a one-time purchase). We recommend at least 64GB storage on your iPad for working with multiple art files.

How do I choose the right course for my skill level?

We categorize our courses by skill level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced) and provide detailed course descriptions to help you choose. If you're unsure, you can take our free skill assessment or contact our support team for personalized recommendations.

Enrollment & Access

How do I enroll in a course?

You can enroll directly through our website by selecting your desired course and clicking "Enroll Now." You'll be guided through the payment process and gain immediate access to the course materials upon completion.

How long do I have access to the course materials?

Access duration depends on your subscription plan. With the Basic plan, you maintain access to course materials as long as your subscription is active. Standard and Premium plans include lifetime access to completed courses even if you cancel your subscription.

Can I download course videos for offline viewing?

Yes! All our courses allow you to download video lessons for offline viewing through our mobile app. This feature is perfect for learning on the go or when you don't have internet access.

Learning Experience

How are the courses structured?

Our courses are divided into modules, each containing several video lessons, exercises, and projects. Most courses follow a progressive structure where each lesson builds on the previous one. You'll typically complete 1-2 projects per course to apply what you've learned.

Can I get feedback on my artwork?

Yes! Our Standard and Premium plans include regular feedback opportunities. Premium subscribers receive weekly 1-on-1 feedback sessions with instructors, while Standard plan members get bi-weekly group feedback sessions. Basic plan members can participate in community feedback forums.

Is there a community or forum for students?

Yes, all enrolled students gain access to our private Discord community where you can connect with fellow students, share your work, ask questions, and participate in monthly challenges. Our instructors are also active in the community.

Payments & Plans

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, and Apple Pay. For enterprise or group purchases, we also accept bank transfers (contact us for details).

Can I switch between subscription plans?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your subscription plan at any time through your account settings. Changes will take effect at your next billing cycle, and you'll only be charged the difference if upgrading mid-cycle.

Do you offer refunds?

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee for all courses. If you're not satisfied with your purchase, simply contact our support team within 14 days of enrollment for a full refund. After 14 days, we don't offer refunds but will work with you to resolve any issues.

Still Have Questions?

Can't find what you're looking for? Our support team is happy to help with any questions you have about our courses or learning platform.
