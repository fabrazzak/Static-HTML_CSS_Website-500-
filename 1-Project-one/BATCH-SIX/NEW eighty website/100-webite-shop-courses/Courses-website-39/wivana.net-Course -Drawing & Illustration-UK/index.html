Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our courses and platform.

Do I need any prior experience to join these courses?

Not at all! Our courses are designed to accommodate all skill levels. We offer beginner-friendly courses that start with the fundamentals, as well as more advanced courses for those looking to refine their skills. Each course clearly indicates the recommended skill level.

What materials or software do I need?

For traditional drawing courses, you'll need basic materials like pencils, paper, and erasers. For digital courses, you'll need a computer and drawing tablet. Specific software requirements vary by course, but we always recommend free alternatives when possible. Detailed requirements are listed with each course description.

How does the subscription work?

Our subscription gives you unlimited access to all courses in your plan tier. You can cancel anytime, and your subscription will remain active until the end of the billing period. We offer monthly and annual billing options, with significant savings on the annual plan.

Can I download course materials?

Yes! All course materials, including worksheets, reference images, and video lessons are available for download. This allows you to learn at your own pace and revisit materials whenever you need them.

Is there a certificate upon completion?

Yes, our Premium plan includes certificates of completion for all finished courses. These certificates can be shared on LinkedIn or included in your portfolio to showcase your new skills.
