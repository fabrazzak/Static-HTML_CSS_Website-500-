Frequently Asked Questions

What are your shipping options and delivery times?

We offer standard shipping (3-5 business days) and express shipping (1-2 business days) within Garthside, United Kingdom For international orders, delivery times vary by destination but typically range from 5-10 business days. All orders are processed within 1-2 business days.

What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 30 days of purchase for unused, unburned candles in their original packaging. Customers are responsible for return shipping costs. Once received and inspected, we'll issue a refund to the original payment method. Please contact our customer service team to initiate a return.

Do you offer wholesale or bulk purchasing?

Yes! We work with many retailers and would love to discuss wholesale opportunities. Please select "Wholesale Inquiry" as your subject when contacting us or email wholesale@flexari.org directly for our wholesale catalog and pricing information.

How should I store my candles?

For optimal performance, store your candles in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and heat sources. Extreme temperatures can affect the wax and fragrance. When properly stored, our candles maintain their quality for up to 2 years.

Are your candles vegan and cruelty-free?

Yes! All Flexari candles are 100% vegan and cruelty-free. We use plant-based soy wax and never test on animals. Our fragrances are also free from animal-derived ingredients.
