Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know my size when ordering vintage clothing?

We provide detailed measurements for all our vintage items in the product descriptions. We recommend comparing these measurements to a similar garment you already own that fits well. If you need additional sizing advice, feel free to contact us with your measurements and we'll help you find the perfect fit.

What is your return policy for upcycled items?

We accept returns within 14 days of delivery for store credit. Due to the unique nature of our upcycled pieces, we cannot accept returns for refunds unless the item is damaged or not as described. All items must be returned in their original condition with tags attached. Please contact us before returning any items.

How do you source your vintage fabrics and clothing?

We carefully source our vintage materials from trusted suppliers across Europe, focusing on high-quality natural fibers. Many items come from estate sales, vintage wholesalers, and textile recycling programs. Each piece is thoroughly inspected for quality and condition before being selected for our collection.

Do you offer international shipping?

Yes, we ship worldwide! Shipping costs and delivery times vary by destination. You'll see the exact shipping cost calculated at checkout. Please note that international orders may be subject to customs fees and import taxes, which are the responsibility of the recipient.

Can I visit your physical location?

Our Bad Brambach location is open by appointment only. We'd love to welcome you to our studio - please contact us to schedule a visit. We also participate in local markets and pop-up events throughout the year, which are announced on our Instagram and newsletter.
