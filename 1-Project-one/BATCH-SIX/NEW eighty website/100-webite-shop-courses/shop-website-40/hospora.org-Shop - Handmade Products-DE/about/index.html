Our Story

Hospora began as a small passion project between two friends who shared a love for traditional craftsmanship. During our travels, we discovered incredible artisans creating beautiful pieces using techniques passed down through generations, yet struggling to reach appreciative audiences.

We founded Hospora in 2015 to bridge this gap - connecting skilled makers with customers who value quality, authenticity, and the human stories behind each piece. What started as a pop-up marketplace has grown into a thriving community of over 200 artisans across 15 countries.

Today, every Hospora piece still carries the same care and intention we admired in that first workshop. We're proud to support traditional crafts while helping them evolve for contemporary living.
