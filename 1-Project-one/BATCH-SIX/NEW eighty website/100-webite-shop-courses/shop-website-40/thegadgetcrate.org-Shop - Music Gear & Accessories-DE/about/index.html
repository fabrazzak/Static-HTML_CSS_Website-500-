About The Gadget Crate

Learn about our story, mission, and the team behind your favorite music store

Our Story

The Gadget Crate was founded in 2010 by a group of passionate musicians who were frustrated with the lack of quality music equipment available at reasonable prices. What started as a small shop in Nashville has grown into one of the most respected music retailers in the country.

Over the years, we've expanded our inventory to include a wide range of instruments, audio equipment, and accessories from the top brands in the industry. Through it all, we've maintained our commitment to providing exceptional customer service and expert advice to musicians of all levels.

Today, The Gadget Crate serves thousands of customers nationwide through our online store and flagship location in Nashville. We're proud to be a part of the music community and dedicated to helping artists find the perfect gear to express themselves.

Our Mission & Values

Our Mission

To provide musicians with high-quality instruments and equipment that inspire creativity and help them achieve their musical dreams.

Passion for Music

We're musicians ourselves, and our love for music drives everything we do. We understand the importance of having the right gear.

Quality First

We personally test and select every product we sell to ensure it meets our high standards of quality and performance.
