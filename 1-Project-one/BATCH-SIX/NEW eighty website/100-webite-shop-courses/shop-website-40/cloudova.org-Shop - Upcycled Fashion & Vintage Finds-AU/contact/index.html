Frequently Asked Questions

How can I track my order?

Once your order has shipped, you'll receive a confirmation email with a tracking number and link to track your package. You can also log in to your account on our website to view the status of your order.

What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 14 days of delivery for store credit. Items must be in their original condition with all tags attached. Due to the unique nature of our upcycled and vintage items, we cannot accept returns for change of mind on customized or altered pieces.

Do you offer international shipping?

Yes, we ship worldwide! Shipping costs will be calculated at checkout based on your location. Please note that international orders may be subject to customs fees and import duties, which are the responsibility of the customer.

How do I care for my upcycled clothing?

Each item comes with specific care instructions, but generally we recommend:

  • Hand washing or using the delicate cycle with cold water
  • Air drying flat or hanging to dry
  • Ironing on low heat if needed
  • Storing properly to maintain shape
Can I visit your physical store?

Currently we operate as an online store only, but we occasionally participate in pop-up markets and events. Follow us on social media or subscribe to our newsletter to stay updated about where you can find us in person!
