Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book your services?

We recommend booking our services at least 4-6 weeks in advance for smaller events and 3-6 months for weddings or large corporate events. However, we do accommodate last-minute requests when possible, so don't hesitate to contact us even if your event is soon.

Do you provide setup and takedown services?

Yes, all our full-service packages include professional setup before your event and takedown afterward. For DIY rentals, we provide detailed setup instructions and are available for phone support during your event.

Can you work with my specific theme or color scheme?

Absolutely! We specialize in custom decor solutions tailored to your specific theme, color palette, and vision. During our consultation, we'll discuss all your preferences and create a design that perfectly matches your requirements.

What areas do you serve?

We primarily serve the Bad Brambach area and surrounding regions within a 50-mile radius. For events outside this area, we may charge a travel fee. Contact us with your event location details for specific information.

What is your cancellation policy?

We require a 50% deposit to secure your date, which is refundable up to 30 days before your event (60 days for weddings). Cancellations within 14 days of the event will incur the full service charge as we may have turned away other clients for your date.
