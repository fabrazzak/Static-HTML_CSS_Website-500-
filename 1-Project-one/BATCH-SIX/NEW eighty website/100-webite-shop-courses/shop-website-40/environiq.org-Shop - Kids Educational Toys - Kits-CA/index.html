About EnvironIQ

We're passionate about inspiring the next generation of innovators

Founded in 2025, EnvironIQ is dedicated to providing high-quality educational toys and STEM kits that make learning exciting for children. We believe that play is the most effective way for kids to develop critical thinking, creativity, and problem-solving skills.

Our carefully curated collection includes science kits, engineering toys, coding games, and math puzzles that align with educational standards while being incredibly fun to use.
