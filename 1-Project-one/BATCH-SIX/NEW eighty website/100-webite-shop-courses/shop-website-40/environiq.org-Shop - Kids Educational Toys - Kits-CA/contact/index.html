Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our products and services

What age range are your educational toys designed for?

Our educational toys and STEM kits are designed for children ages 3-12, with products specifically tailored for different developmental stages. Each product page clearly indicates the recommended age range, and many of our kits grow with your child, offering different levels of challenge.

How do I know which educational toy is right for my child?

Consider your child's age, interests, and current skills. Our products are categorized by age group and learning area (science, coding, engineering, math). You can also use our product finder tool or contact our customer service for personalized recommendations based on your child's specific needs and interests.

What safety standards do your products meet?

All EnvironIQ products meet or exceed international safety standards including ASTM F963 (U.S.), EN71 (EU), and ISO 8124 (International). We use non-toxic, child-safe materials and rigorously test all components. Safety certifications for each product are available on request.

Do you offer wholesale or educator discounts?

Yes! We offer special pricing for schools, homeschool groups, and retailers. Please contact our wholesale team at wholesale@environiq.org or fill out our wholesale inquiry form for more information about bulk discounts and educational pricing.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day satisfaction guarantee. If you're not completely happy with your purchase, you can return it in its original condition for a full refund or exchange. Please see our returns page for complete details and instructions.
