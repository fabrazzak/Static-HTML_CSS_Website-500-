Our Story

Discover the passion and craftsmanship behind Thryvia's premium embroidery kits

Who We Are

More than just a company - a community of stitching enthusiasts

Thryvia was born from a simple love of embroidery and a desire to share that passion with others. Our founder, Emma Richardson, grew up surrounded by the beautiful needlework of her grandmother and mother. What began as a childhood hobby blossomed into a lifelong passion.

After years of searching for high-quality embroidery kits with modern designs and finding only limited options, Emma decided to create her own. In 2020, she launched Thryvia with just three designs. Today, we offer dozens of unique patterns and have shipped thousands of kits to stitchers around the world.

At Thryvia, we believe embroidery is more than just a craft - it's a way to slow down, express creativity, and create something beautiful with your own hands. Our mission is to make this rewarding art form accessible to everyone, from complete beginners to experienced stitchers.

Our Journey

Key milestones in Thryvia's story

Spring 2020

Thryvia is Born

Emma launches Thryvia with just three embroidery kit designs, working from her home studio.

Fall 2020

First Team Members

Sarah joins as our first designer, and Thryvia moves to a small studio space.

2021

Expanding Our Line

We introduce our beginner series and cross-stitch kits, growing to 15 designs.

2022

International Shipping

Thryvia kits begin shipping worldwide, reaching stitchers in over 30 countries.

2023

New Studio

We move to a larger studio space and expand our team to meet growing demand.

2024

Sustainability Commitment

We transition to 100% eco-friendly packaging and launch our recycling program for used materials.
