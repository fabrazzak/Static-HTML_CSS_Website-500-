Kindara Shipping Policy

Last Updated: April 22, 2025

We strive to process and ship all orders as quickly as possible. Please review our shipping policy below for detailed information about delivery times, shipping methods, and international orders.

1. Order Processing

Processing Time

All orders are processed within 1-2 business days (Monday through Friday, excluding holidays) after receiving your order confirmation email. You will receive a shipping confirmation email with tracking information once your order has shipped.

Note: Orders placed on weekends or holidays will begin processing the next business day.

Order Changes

If you need to make changes to your order, please contact us immediately at support@kindara.org. We cannot guarantee that changes can be made once an order has entered the shipping process.

2. Shipping Methods & Delivery Times

We offer several shipping options to meet your needs:

Standard Shipping

Delivery Time: 3-5 business days

Cost: £3.99 (Free for orders over £50)

Our most economical option with reliable delivery service.

Express Shipping

Delivery Time: 1-2 business days

Cost: £7.99

Priority handling with expedited delivery service.

Next Day Delivery

Delivery Time: 1 business day (orders must be placed before 12pm)

Cost: £12.99

For urgent deliveries, guaranteed next business day delivery.

Important: Delivery times are estimates only and begin when your order has shipped, not when it is placed. Delivery times may vary depending on your location and carrier delays.

3. UK Delivery Information

Shipping Carriers

We primarily use Royal Mail and DPD for UK deliveries. The carrier used will depend on the shipping method selected at checkout.

Delivery Areas

We ship to all addresses within the United Kingdom, including:

  • England
  • Scotland
  • Wales
  • Northern Ireland
  • Channel Islands
  • Isle of Man

Remote Areas

Some remote locations may experience slightly longer delivery times. These include:

  • Scottish Highlands and Islands
  • Isles of Scilly
  • Other offshore islands

4. International Shipping

Available Countries

We currently ship to the following international destinations:

Region Delivery Time Shipping Cost
European Union 5-7 business days £9.99
United States & Canada 7-10 business days £12.99
Australia & New Zealand 10-14 business days £14.99
Other Countries 10-21 business days £15.99+

Customs & Import Fees

International customers are responsible for any customs duties, taxes, or fees imposed by their country. These charges are not included in the product price or shipping cost and must be paid by the recipient upon delivery.

Important: Kindara is not responsible for delays caused by customs clearance. Please check with your local customs office for more information about potential fees.

5. Order Tracking

Once your order has shipped, you will receive a shipping confirmation email with tracking information. You can track your package using the provided tracking number on the carrier's website.

Tracking Issues

If you experience any issues with tracking your package or if the status hasn't updated for several days, please contact us at support@kindara.org for assistance.

6. Undeliverable Packages

If a package is returned to us as undeliverable due to an incorrect address or failure to pay customs fees, we will contact you to arrange reshipment. Additional shipping fees may apply for reshipment.

Address Accuracy

Please ensure your shipping address is complete and accurate. We cannot be held responsible for packages shipped to incorrect addresses provided by the customer.

7. Missing or Damaged Packages

Missing Packages

If your tracking information shows that your package was delivered but you have not received it, please contact us immediately. We will work with the shipping carrier to resolve the issue.

Damaged Items

If your order arrives damaged, please contact us within 7 days of delivery with photos of the damaged items and packaging. We will arrange for a replacement or refund as appropriate.

Important: Please inspect your package upon delivery. If there is visible damage to the package, note it with the delivery carrier before accepting the package.

8. Holiday Shipping Schedule

During peak holiday seasons, order processing and delivery times may be extended. We will post holiday shipping deadlines on our website to help ensure your gifts arrive on time.

Last Order Dates for Holiday Delivery

To ensure delivery before major holidays, we recommend placing your order by these dates:

  • Christmas: December 15 for standard shipping, December 20 for express shipping
  • Easter: 7 days prior for standard shipping, 3 days prior for express shipping
  • Valentine's Day: February 5 for standard shipping, February 10 for express shipping

9. Contact Information

If you have any questions about our shipping policy, please contact us:

  • By email: shipping@kindara.org
  • By mail: Kindara, Kelso TD5 8PD, United Kingdom
  • By phone: +447831251146 (Monday-Friday, 9am-5pm GMT)

Our customer service team is available to assist with any shipping questions or concerns.
