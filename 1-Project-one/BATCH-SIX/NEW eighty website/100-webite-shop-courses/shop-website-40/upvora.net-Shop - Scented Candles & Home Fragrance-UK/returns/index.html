Return Policy Details

Our Commitment

At UPVORA, we stand behind the quality of our scented candles. If you're not completely satisfied with your purchase, we'll gladly accept returns or exchanges within 30 days of delivery.

Return Conditions

To be eligible for a return or exchange, your item must meet the following criteria:

  • Item must be unused and in its original condition
  • Original packaging must be intact (including any labels or tags)
  • Proof of purchase (order number or receipt) is required
  • Return must be initiated within 30 days of delivery

Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned or exchanged:

  • Used or burned candles
  • Final sale items (clearly marked at time of purchase)
  • Gift cards
  • Custom or personalized orders

How to Return or Exchange

1

Contact Us

Email returns@upvora.net with your order number and reason for return/exchange within 30 days of delivery.

2

Get Approval

We'll email you a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number and return instructions.

3

Package Your Item

Securely pack the unused item in its original packaging with the RMA number clearly visible.

4

Ship It Back

Mail your return package to the address provided. We recommend using a trackable shipping service.

Return Shipping

  • EU Customers: We provide a prepaid return label for defective or incorrect items
  • International Customers: Customer is responsible for return shipping costs
  • Original Shipping: Shipping costs are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error

Refund Process

When to Expect Your Refund

Once your return is received and inspected, we'll send you an email notification. Your refund will be processed within 5 business days and a credit will be applied to your original payment method.

Refund Timeline

  1. Processing Time: 1-3 business days after we receive your return
  2. Credit Card Refunds: 3-5 business days to appear on your statement
  3. PayPal Refunds: 1-3 business days to appear in your account

Late or Missing Refunds

If you haven't received your refund within 10 business days:

  1. Check your bank account or PayPal account again
  2. Contact your credit card company (processing times vary)
  3. If you still haven't received your refund, contact us at returns@upvora.net

Exchanges

How Exchanges Work

We're happy to exchange your item for a different scent or product of equal value. The fastest way to get your exchange is to follow our standard return process and place a new order for the item you want.

Exchange Process

  1. Contact us at returns@upvora.net to initiate the exchange
  2. Return your original item following our return process
  3. Once we receive your return, we'll ship your new item

Exchange Shipping

  • Customer is responsible for return shipping costs
  • We cover standard shipping costs for the replacement item
  • For expedited shipping on exchanges, customer pays the difference

Damaged or Defective Items

Our Quality Guarantee

We take great care in packaging your candles to arrive in perfect condition. If your item arrives damaged or defective, we'll replace it at no cost to you.

What to Do

If you receive a damaged or defective item:

  1. Take photos of the damaged product and packaging
  2. Contact us within 7 days of delivery at support@upvora.net
  3. Include your order number and photos in your email

We'll ship a replacement immediately or issue a full refund - your choice. No need to return the damaged item (please dispose of it safely).

Minor Imperfections

Please note that soy wax candles may develop natural characteristics that don't affect performance:

  • Frosting: A whitish coating that's normal for soy wax
  • Wet Spots: Areas where wax separates slightly from the container
  • Air Bubbles: Small bubbles that form during cooling

These are not considered defects and don't qualify for replacement.
