Our Mission

Why we do what we do

At Puretica, we believe that your environment significantly impacts your productivity, creativity, and overall wellbeing. Our mission is to transform ordinary workspaces into inspiring environments that help you do your best work.

We combine thoughtful design with functional excellence to create office decor and accessories that not only look beautiful but also enhance your daily workflow. Every product we design is created with intention, crafted with care, and delivered with the hope that it will make your work life better.

Our Journey

How Puretica came to be

2018

The Idea Was Born

Our founder, Alex Rivera, struggled with a cluttered, uninspiring home office while working remotely. Frustrated by the lack of functional yet beautiful workspace solutions, the idea for Puretica began to take shape.

2019

First Prototypes

After months of research and design iterations, we created our first product prototypes - a minimalist desk organizer and an adjustable monitor stand that combined form and function.

2020

Official Launch

Puretica officially launched with a small collection of five thoughtfully designed products, just as the world shifted to remote work. Our timing proved fortuitous as people everywhere sought to improve their home offices.

2021

Expanding the Team

As demand grew, we brought on our first full-time employees - a product designer and customer experience specialist - allowing us to expand our product line while maintaining our commitment to quality.

2022

Sustainability Commitment

We implemented our sustainability pledge, transitioning to 100% recycled and eco-friendly materials for all our products and packaging.

2023

International Shipping

We expanded our operations to serve customers worldwide, shipping to over 30 countries while maintaining our commitment to carbon-neutral delivery.
