Minimalist Jewelry with Meaning

At Alumiya, we believe that jewelry should be as unique as the person wearing it. Our handcrafted pieces are designed to complement your personal style while making a positive impact on the world. Each creation tells a story of craftsmanship, sustainability, and timeless beauty.

How It All Began

Founded in 2020 by jewelry designer Mia Johnson, Alumiya was born from a passion for minimalist design and sustainable practices. After years of working in the fashion industry, Mia noticed a gap in the market for high-quality, ethically made jewelry that didn't compromise on style.

What started as a small workshop in her garage has grown into a beloved brand known for its commitment to quality and sustainability. Today, our team of skilled artisans handcrafts each piece in our studio, using ethically sourced materials and traditional techniques.

We're proud to create jewelry that not only looks beautiful but also aligns with our values of environmental responsibility and fair labor practices.

Our Values

Sustainable Materials

We use recycled metals and ethically sourced gemstones to minimize our environmental impact. All our packaging is eco-friendly and biodegradable.

Ethical Production

Every piece is handcrafted by artisans who receive fair wages and work in safe conditions. We're committed to ethical labor practices at every level.

Quality Craftsmanship

Our jewelry is made to last using traditional techniques and the highest quality materials. Each piece undergoes rigorous quality checks.

Timeless Design

We create minimalist pieces that transcend trends. Our jewelry is designed to be worn every day and cherished for years to come.
