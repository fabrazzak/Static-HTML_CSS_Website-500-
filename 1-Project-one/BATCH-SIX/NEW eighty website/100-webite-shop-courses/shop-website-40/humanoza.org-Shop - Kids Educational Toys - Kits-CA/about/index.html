Who We Are

Founded in 2020, Humanoza began with a simple idea: learning should be an adventure. Our founders, a team of educators and parents, noticed a gap in the market for truly engaging educational toys that children would love to play with while developing essential skills.

What started as a small online shop has grown into a trusted resource for parents and educators worldwide. We carefully curate every product in our collection, testing each one with real children to ensure they're not only educational but genuinely fun to play with.

Today, we're proud to serve thousands of families and schools, helping children discover the joy of learning through play. Our team of childhood development experts continues to seek out the best educational toys from around the world.

Our Mission

Inspire Curiosity

We believe every child is born curious. Our products are designed to nurture that natural curiosity and help it flourish into a lifelong love of learning.

Learning Through Play

Play is children's work. We select toys that make learning feel like play, helping children develop essential skills while having fun.

Quality & Safety

Every product in our collection meets the highest standards for educational value, durability, and most importantly, child safety.

Our Values
