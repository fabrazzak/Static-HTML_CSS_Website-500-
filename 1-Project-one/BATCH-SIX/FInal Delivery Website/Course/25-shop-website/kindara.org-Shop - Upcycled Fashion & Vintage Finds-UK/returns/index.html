Kindara Returns & Refunds Policy

Last Updated: April 22, 2025

We want you to be completely satisfied with your Kindara purchase. If you're not happy with your order, we're happy to help with returns, exchanges, or refunds according to the policy outlined below.

1. Returns Eligibility

Timeframe for Returns

You may return most new, unopened items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be in their original packaging with all seals intact.

Non-Returnable Items

Certain items cannot be returned for hygiene and safety reasons:

  • Opened essential oil bottles
  • Used diffusers or other electronic devices
  • Gift cards
  • Personalized or custom-made products
  • Sale or clearance items (unless defective)

Note: We reserve the right to refuse returns of any item that does not meet the above return conditions.

2. How to Return an Item

Follow these simple steps to return your item:

1

Request a Return

Contact our customer service team at returns@kindara.org or through our contact form to initiate your return. Please include your order number and reason for return.

2

Receive Return Authorization

We will email you a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number and return instructions within 2 business days.

3

Package Your Item

Securely package the item in its original packaging with all accessories and documentation. Include the RMA number on the outside of the package.

4

Ship Your Return

Mail your return to the address provided in your return instructions. We recommend using a trackable shipping service and purchasing shipping insurance.

Important: Returns sent without an RMA number may experience significant processing delays or may not be eligible for refund.

3. Refund Process

Refund Timeline

Once your return is received and inspected, we will send you an email to notify you that we have received your returned item. We will also notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund.

If approved, your refund will be processed, and a credit will automatically be applied to your original payment method within the following timeframes:

Payment Method Refund Time
Credit/Debit Card 5-10 business days
PayPal 3-5 business days
Bank Transfer 7-14 business days
Gift Card Immediate (credited to original gift card)

Partial Refunds

Partial refunds may be granted in certain situations, including:

  • Items returned without original packaging
  • Items showing signs of use
  • Missing parts or accessories
  • Items damaged due to customer misuse

4. Exchanges

Faulty or Damaged Items

If you receive a faulty or damaged item, please contact us within 7 days of delivery with photos of the damaged product and packaging. We will arrange for a replacement to be sent at no additional cost to you.

Wrong Item Received

If you receive the wrong item, please contact us immediately. We will provide a prepaid return label and ship the correct item to you as soon as possible.

Product Exchanges

If you would like to exchange an item for a different product, please return the original item for a refund and place a new order for the desired product.

Note: We cannot process direct exchanges at this time due to inventory management requirements.

5. Return Shipping Costs

Customer-Paid Returns

Customers are responsible for return shipping costs unless the return is due to our error (wrong item shipped or defective product).

Prepaid Return Labels

In cases where the return is our fault, we will provide a prepaid return shipping label. This applies to:

  • Wrong items received
  • Defective products
  • Damaged during shipping

Important: We recommend using a trackable shipping service for returns. Kindara is not responsible for return items lost or damaged in transit.

6. International Returns

Return Shipping

International customers are responsible for return shipping costs and any applicable customs fees. We cannot refund original shipping costs for international orders.

Customs Declarations

When returning items internationally, please mark the package as "Returned Goods" to avoid additional customs charges. Any customs fees incurred during the return process will be deducted from your refund.

Refund Amount

Refunds for international returns will be issued for the product cost only, excluding original shipping charges and any import duties or taxes.

7. Late or Missing Refunds

Check Your Account

If you haven't received your refund within the expected timeframe, first check your bank account or payment provider again. Some processing times may vary.

Contact Your Bank

It may take some time before your refund is officially posted to your account. Contact your bank or payment provider for more information about their processing times.

Still Missing Your Refund?

If you've done all of this and you still have not received your refund, please contact us at refunds@kindara.org with your order number and payment details.

8. Sale Items

Only regular-priced items may be refunded. Sale or clearance items cannot be returned unless they are defective.

Gift Returns

If the item was marked as a gift when purchased and shipped directly to you, you'll receive a gift credit for the value of your return. Once the returned item is received, a gift certificate will be emailed to you.

9. Contact Information

For questions about our Returns & Refunds Policy or to initiate a return, please contact us:

  • By email: returns@kindara.org
  • By mail: Kindara Returns, Kelso TD5 8PD, United Kingdom
  • By phone: +447831251146 (Monday-Friday, 9am-5pm GMT)

Please include your order number in all communications regarding returns or refunds.

Need Help With a Return?

Our customer service team is happy to assist with any questions about returns or refunds.
