Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our products and services.

What are your shipping options and delivery times?

We offer standard and express shipping options throughout Canada . Standard shipping typically takes 3-5 business days, while express shipping delivers within 1-2 business days. International shipping is also available with delivery times varying by destination. You'll receive a tracking number as soon as your order ships.

What is your return and exchange policy?

We offer a 30-day return policy for most items. Products must be in their original condition with all packaging intact. To initiate a return or exchange, please contact our customer service team with your order number. Return shipping costs are the responsibility of the customer, unless the item arrived damaged or defective.

Do you offer international shipping?

Yes, we ship to most countries worldwide. International shipping rates and delivery times vary depending on your location. Additional customs fees, taxes, or duties may apply upon delivery, which are the responsibility of the customer. Please contact us before ordering if you have specific questions about shipping to your country.

How can I track my order?

Once your order has shipped, you'll receive an email with your tracking number and a link to track your package. You can also log in to your account on our website to view your order status and tracking information. If you're having trouble tracking your package, our customer service team is happy to assist.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, and Apple Pay. All transactions are securely processed through our encrypted payment gateway. We also offer Afterpay for Canada n customers, allowing you to pay in four interest-free installments.
