Our Story

Discover the passion and purpose behind Plexivo's mission to transform workspaces

Who We Are

Founded in 2023, Plexivo was born from a simple idea: that your workspace should inspire you. As remote work became the new normal, we noticed a gap in the market for home office products that combined functionality with exceptional design.

What started as a small team of design enthusiasts has grown into a thriving business serving customers across Canada . We're proud to offer carefully curated products that help create workspaces people love.

At Plexivo, we believe that your environment significantly impacts your productivity, creativity, and overall well-being. That's why we're committed to providing products that not only look great but also enhance your work experience.
