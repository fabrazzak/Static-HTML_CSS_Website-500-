Frequently Asked Questions

What are your shipping options and costs?

We offer standard shipping (3-5 business days) for $5.99, expedited shipping (2-3 business days) for $12.99, and free shipping on all orders over $50. All orders are processed within 1-2 business days.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day satisfaction guarantee. If you're not completely happy with your purchase, you can return it within 30 days of delivery for a full refund or exchange. Items must be unopened and in their original packaging. For opened products, we offer store credit minus a 15% restocking fee.

Are your products cruelty-free and vegan?

Yes! All Zaptechshop products are 100% cruelty-free and never tested on animals. About 90% of our products are vegan - we clearly label any products that contain animal-derived ingredients like beeswax or honey.

How do I track my order?

Once your order ships, you'll receive a confirmation email with your tracking number and a link to track your package. You can also track your order by logging into your account on our website.

What ingredients do you avoid in your products?

We formulate our products without parabens, phthalates, sulfates, synthetic fragrances, PEGs, petroleum derivatives, or other potentially harmful chemicals. We're committed to using only natural, organic, and safe ingredients that are good for both your skin and the environment.
