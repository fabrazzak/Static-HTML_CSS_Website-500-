Our Story

Discover the passion and craftsmanship behind Relyzo's personalized gifts that help you celebrate life's special moments.

How It All Began

Relyzo was founded in 2025 by Emily Richardson, a passionate artisan who believed that gifts should be as unique as the people receiving them. What started as a small workshop creating custom jewelry for friends and family quickly grew into a thriving business as word spread about the exceptional quality and personal touch of our products.

Emily's vision was simple: to create meaningful, personalized gifts that help people express their love and appreciation in the most special way. Today, our team of skilled craftsmen continues this tradition, combining traditional techniques with modern technology to create truly one-of-a-kind pieces.

From our humble beginnings in Garthside, we've grown to serve customers across the United Kingdom and beyond, but we've never lost sight of our core values - quality craftsmanship, attention to detail, and a personal touch in every piece we create.
