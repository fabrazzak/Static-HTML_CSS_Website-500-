Our Story

Learn about the passion and dedication behind MicroShopGear

Crafting Musical Experiences Since 2010

MicroShopGear began with a simple vision: to provide musicians of all levels with high-quality instruments and gear that inspire creativity and excellence. Founded by a group of passionate musicians in 2010, we understand firsthand the importance of reliable equipment in every musician's journey.

What started as a small shop in downtown Austin has grown into a trusted name in the music industry, serving thousands of musicians across the country. Our commitment to quality, affordability, and exceptional customer service has remained unwavering throughout our growth.

At MicroShopGear, we carefully select each product in our inventory, ensuring that we offer only the best instruments and accessories from renowned brands as well as innovative newcomers in the industry. Our team of experts tests each item to guarantee it meets our high standards before it reaches your hands.

Our Values

Quality

We never compromise on the quality of our products. Each item in our inventory is carefully selected and tested to ensure excellence.
