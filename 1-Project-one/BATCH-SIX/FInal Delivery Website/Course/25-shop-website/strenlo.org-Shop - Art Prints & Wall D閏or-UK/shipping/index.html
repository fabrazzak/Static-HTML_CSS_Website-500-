Shipping Policy

Fast, reliable delivery for your art prints

Order Processing & Shipping Times

We process and ship orders quickly to ensure you receive your art prints as soon as possible. Here's what you can expect:

Processing Time

All orders are processed within 1-2 business days (Monday-Friday, excluding holidays). Custom orders may require additional processing time.

Shipping Times:

  • Standard Shipping: 3-5 business days after processing
  • Express Shipping: 1-2 business days after processing
  • International Shipping: 5-10 business days after processing
  • Framed Prints: Additional 1-2 days processing due to careful packaging

You will receive a shipping confirmation email with tracking information once your order has been shipped.

Free Shipping

We offer free standard shipping on all orders over €100 within United Kingdom. For international orders, free shipping is available on orders over €200.

International Shipping

We ship worldwide to most countries. Please note:

  • International orders may be subject to customs fees, import duties, and taxes which are the responsibility of the recipient
  • Delivery times may vary depending on customs processing
  • We declare the full value of the artwork on customs forms
  • Some restrictions may apply to certain countries - contact us if you have questions

Order Tracking

Once your order has shipped, you'll receive a tracking number via email. You can use this number to track your package through our shipping carrier's website.

Tracking Your Order:

  • Tracking information is typically updated within 24 hours of shipment
  • If your tracking hasn't updated after 2 business days, please contact us
  • For express shipments, tracking updates may be more frequent

Packaging & Handling

We take great care in packaging your art prints to ensure they arrive in perfect condition:

  • Unframed Prints: Carefully rolled in acid-free tissue paper and shipped in sturdy cardboard tubes
  • Framed Prints: Packed in custom boxes with corner protectors and bubble wrap
  • Large Pieces: Additional protective materials and reinforced packaging
  • Fragile Items: Clearly marked with "Fragile" labels

Damaged Shipments

While we take every precaution, if your artwork arrives damaged, please contact us immediately at info@strenlo.org with photos of the damage and packaging. We'll arrange for a replacement or refund.
