Our Story

Discover the passion behind Spinexa and our commitment to creating unforgettable events

Creating Magical Moments Since 2015

Spinexa was founded in 2015 with a simple mission: to transform ordinary events into extraordinary experiences through stunning decor and premium party supplies. What began as a small boutique operation has grown into a trusted name in event decoration across Germany and beyond.

Our founder, Anna Müller, started the company after struggling to find high-quality, unique decorations for her own wedding. Frustrated with the limited options available, she set out to create a company that would offer both exceptional products and expert guidance to help people create their dream events.

Today, we serve thousands of customers each year, from couples planning their weddings to corporate clients organizing large-scale events. Our team of event specialists brings creativity, expertise, and attention to detail to every project we undertake.

Our Mission

At Spinexa, we believe that every event tells a story. Our mission is to provide the perfect decor elements that help bring your unique story to life. We're committed to offering:

  • Premium quality products that exceed expectations
  • Innovative designs that stay ahead of trends
  • Exceptional customer service from planning to execution
  • Sustainable practices in our products and operations
