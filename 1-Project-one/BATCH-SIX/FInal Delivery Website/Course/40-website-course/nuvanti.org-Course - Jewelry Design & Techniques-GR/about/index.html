Our Story & Philosophy

Discover the passion and craftsmanship behind Nuvanti Jewelry Design School

Our Mission

We are dedicated to preserving and advancing the art of jewelry design through comprehensive education

Empowering Creativity Through Education

At Nuvanti, we believe that jewelry design is more than just a craft—it's a form of artistic expression that connects cultures, histories, and personal stories. Our mission is to make this beautiful art form accessible to everyone, regardless of their background or experience level.

We combine traditional techniques with contemporary approaches, ensuring our students receive a well-rounded education that prepares them for both personal fulfillment and professional success in the jewelry industry.

Our approach emphasizes hands-on learning, individual attention, and fostering a supportive community of jewelry enthusiasts and professionals.

Our Journey

From humble beginnings to becoming a leader in jewelry design education

2010

Founded by master goldsmith Sophia Laurent as a small workshop in Athens, offering weekend classes to local enthusiasts.

2013

Expanded to a dedicated studio space and launched our first professional certification program with three instructors.

2016

Introduced online courses, making our jewelry education accessible to students worldwide for the first time.

2019

Opened our current flagship location in Velestino, featuring state-of-the-art equipment and expanded course offerings.

2022

Celebrated our 1,000th graduate and launched advanced masterclasses with visiting international instructors.
