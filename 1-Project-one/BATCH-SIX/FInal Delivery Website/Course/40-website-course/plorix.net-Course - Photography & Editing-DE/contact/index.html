Common Questions

Quick answers to frequently asked questions

How quickly can I expect a response to my inquiry?

We typically respond to all inquiries within 24 hours during our business hours (Monday-Friday, 9am-6pm CET). For urgent matters, please call our support line for immediate assistance.

Do you offer support for current students?

Yes! Current students can access priority support through our dedicated student support portal. Log in to your account and visit the "Help Center" for faster response times and course-specific assistance.

Can I schedule a call with an instructor?

Premium plan members have access to scheduled 1-on-1 sessions with instructors. For other students, we offer monthly Q&A sessions where you can ask questions live. Check your course dashboard for upcoming sessions.

Do you offer in-person workshops or classes?

While most of our courses are online, we occasionally host in-person workshops in major cities. Subscribe to our newsletter or check the "Events" section of our website for upcoming in-person learning opportunities.
