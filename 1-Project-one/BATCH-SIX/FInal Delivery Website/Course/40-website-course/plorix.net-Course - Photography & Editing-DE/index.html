Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our courses

Do I need any prior experience to take these courses?

No prior experience is required for our beginner courses. We offer courses for all skill levels, from complete beginners to advanced photographers. Each course clearly indicates the recommended experience level in its description.

What equipment do I need for the photography courses?

For DSLR courses, you'll need a camera with manual controls. For mobile photography, any modern smartphone will suffice. Our editing courses require a computer with the relevant software (we provide free alternatives for paid software). Specific requirements are listed with each course.

How long do I have access to the courses?

Access duration depends on your subscription plan. Monthly plans provide access while your subscription is active. If you purchase individual courses, you get lifetime access to that specific course content.

Can I get a refund if I'm not satisfied?

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee for all courses. If you're not satisfied with your purchase, simply contact our support team within 30 days for a full refund, no questions asked.

Are the certificates recognized by employers?

Our professional certificates are well-regarded in the creative industry. Many of our students have used them to enhance their portfolios and secure jobs. However, we always recommend building a strong portfolio alongside certification.
