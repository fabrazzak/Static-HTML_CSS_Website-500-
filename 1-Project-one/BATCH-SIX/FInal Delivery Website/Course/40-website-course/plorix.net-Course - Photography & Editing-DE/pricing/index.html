Pricing FAQs

Answers to common questions about our plans

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, and bank transfers for annual plans. All payments are processed securely through our payment partners.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. When you upgrade, you'll only pay the difference for the remaining time in your billing cycle. Downgrades will take effect at your next billing date.

Is there a free trial available?

We offer a 7-day free trial for our Standard plan so you can experience our platform before committing. After the trial, you can choose any plan that fits your needs.

Do you offer discounts for students or teams?

Yes, we offer a 30% discount for students with valid .edu email addresses. For teams of 5 or more, we offer custom enterprise pricing with additional features like progress tracking and centralized billing.

What's your refund policy?

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all plans. If you're not satisfied with your purchase, simply contact our support team within 30 days for a full refund, no questions asked.
