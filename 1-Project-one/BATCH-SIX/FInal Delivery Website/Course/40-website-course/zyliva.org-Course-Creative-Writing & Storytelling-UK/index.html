Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our courses

Do I need any writing experience to join these courses?

No prior experience is necessary! Our courses are designed for writers at all levels, from complete beginners to experienced writers looking to refine their skills. Each course provides foundational knowledge while also offering advanced techniques for more experienced participants.

How much time should I dedicate to the courses each week?

Most students find that dedicating 3-5 hours per week allows them to comfortably complete the coursework. However, our courses are self-paced, so you can spend more or less time depending on your schedule and goals. We recommend setting aside regular writing time to get the most from the experience.

What kind of feedback will I receive on my writing?

You'll receive detailed feedback from both instructors and peers. Instructor feedback focuses on craft elements like structure, character development, voice, and technique. Peer feedback provides diverse perspectives from fellow writers. All feedback is constructive and aimed at helping you improve.

Can I access the course materials after completing the course?

Yes! You'll have lifetime access to all course materials, including videos, worksheets, and resources. This allows you to revisit lessons and continue learning at your own pace even after completing the course.

What if I'm not satisfied with the course?

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee. If you're not completely satisfied with your course within the first two weeks, simply contact us for a full refund, no questions asked.
