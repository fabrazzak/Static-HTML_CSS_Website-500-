Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our pricing and plans.

Can I switch plans later?

Absolutely! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. When you upgrade, you'll only pay the difference for the remaining billing period. Downgrades will take effect at your next billing cycle.

Is there a free trial available?

Yes! We offer a 7-day free trial for our Standard plan so you can experience our platform before committing. No credit card is required to start your trial.

Do you offer annual billing discounts?

Yes, we offer significant discounts for annual billing. Pay for a year upfront and save up to 30% compared to monthly payments. Annual plans include two months free!

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, and in some countries, direct bank transfers. All payments are processed securely through our payment partners.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time. Your access will continue until the end of your current billing period, and you won't be charged again. We don't believe in locking you into long-term contracts.

Do you offer team or group pricing?

Yes! We offer special pricing for groups of 5 or more students. Contact our team at groups@wivana.net for custom pricing based on your group size and needs.
