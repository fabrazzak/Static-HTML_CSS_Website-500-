Our Mission & Vision

Why we do what we do

At Lodgevia Music School, our mission is to make high-quality music education accessible to everyone in our community. We believe that music has the power to transform lives, build confidence, and bring people together.

Founded in 2015 in Fremantle, Western Australia, we've grown from a small studio with two instructors to a thriving music school serving hundreds of students each year. Our vision is to be the leading music education center in Western Australia, known for our exceptional teaching, supportive community, and outstanding student outcomes.

We're committed to:

  • • Providing personalized instruction tailored to each student's goals
  • • Creating a welcoming environment for students of all ages and skill levels
  • • Fostering a lifelong love of music in our students
  • • Supporting our local arts community through performances and events

Our Core Values

The principles that guide everything we do

Passion for Music

We believe music should be joyful. Our instructors are passionate performers who love sharing their knowledge and enthusiasm with students.

Student-Centered

Every student is unique. We tailor our teaching approach to individual learning styles, goals, and musical preferences.

Excellence

We maintain the highest standards in music education. Our instructors are highly qualified and committed to continuous improvement.

Community

Music brings people together. We foster a supportive environment where students can grow and connect with fellow musicians.

Creativity

We encourage creative expression and help students develop their own musical voice, whether through composition or interpretation.

Integrity

We're committed to honest communication, ethical practices, and maintaining the trust of our students and families.

Our History

The Lodgevia story through the years

2015

Founded in Fremantle

Lodgevia Music School was established by David Wilson and Lisa Park in a small studio on High Street. We started with just piano and violin lessons.

2017

Expanded Programs

Added guitar and voice lessons to our offerings. Moved to our current location at 1 Beach St to accommodate growing student numbers.

2019

First Student Recital

Hosted our inaugural student recital at the Fremantle Arts Centre, featuring performances by 35 students.

2021

Online Lessons Launch

Developed our online learning platform to continue serving students during lockdowns and reach regional students.

2023

Community Partnership

Partnered with local schools to provide subsidized lessons for disadvantaged students through our Music for All initiative.

2025

Present Day

Now serving over 200 students weekly with a team of 8 instructors. Recognized as one of WA's leading music schools.
