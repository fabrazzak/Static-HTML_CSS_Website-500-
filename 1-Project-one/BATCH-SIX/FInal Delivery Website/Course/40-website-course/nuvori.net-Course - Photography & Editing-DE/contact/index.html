Common Questions

How quickly can I expect a response to my inquiry?

We typically respond to all inquiries within 24 hours during our business hours. If you contact us on weekends or holidays, we'll get back to you on the next business day.

Do you offer in-person consultations?

Yes! We offer free 30-minute consultations at our office in Bad Brambach. Please contact us to schedule an appointment. We also offer virtual consultations via Zoom if you can't visit in person.

What's the best way to get technical support for a course?

For technical issues with our courses, please email support@nuvori.net with details about your problem. Include screenshots if possible, and we'll help you resolve the issue as quickly as possible.
