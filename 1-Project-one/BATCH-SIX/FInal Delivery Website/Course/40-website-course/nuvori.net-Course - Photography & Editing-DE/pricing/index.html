Pricing Questions

Is there a free trial available?

Yes! We offer a 7-day free trial for our Standard plan so you can experience our courses and teaching style before committing. No credit card is required to start your trial.

Can I switch plans later?

Absolutely. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. When you upgrade, you'll immediately gain access to the additional features, and we'll prorate the difference in cost. Downgrades will take effect at your next billing cycle.

Do you offer annual billing discounts?

Yes! If you choose annual billing, you'll save 15% compared to paying monthly. This works out to $16.15/month for Basic, $41.65/month for Standard, and $84.15/month for Premium.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover), PayPal, and in some countries, Apple Pay and Google Pay. Unfortunately, we don't currently accept cryptocurrency.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time. If you cancel, you'll retain access to the courses until the end of your current billing period. We also offer a 30-day money-back guarantee if you're not satisfied with your purchase.
