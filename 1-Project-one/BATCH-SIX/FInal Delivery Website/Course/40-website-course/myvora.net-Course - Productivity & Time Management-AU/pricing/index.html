Frequently Asked Questions

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, and bank transfers for annual plans. All payments are processed securely through our payment partner Stripe.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan later?

Yes, you can change your plan at any time. When you upgrade, you'll immediately gain access to the additional features. Downgrades will take effect at your next billing cycle.

Is there a free trial available?

We offer a 7-day free trial for our Standard monthly plan. This gives you full access to all features so you can experience the value before committing. No credit card is required to start your trial.

What's your refund policy?

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all plans. If you're not satisfied with your purchase for any reason, simply contact us within 30 days for a full refund, no questions asked.

Do you offer discounts for students or nonprofits?

Yes, we offer a 20% discount for verified students and nonprofit organizations. Please contact our support team with proof of status to receive your discount code.
