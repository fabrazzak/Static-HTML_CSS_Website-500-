Frequently Asked Questions

How do I access the courses after purchasing?

After completing your purchase, you'll receive an email with login credentials and instructions to access our learning platform. All your enrolled courses will be available in your dashboard immediately.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time through your account settings. Your access will continue until the end of your current billing period.

Are there any prerequisites for the courses?

Most of our courses are designed to be accessible to everyone. Some advanced courses may recommend completing our foundational courses first, but this will be clearly indicated on the course page.

How much time should I dedicate to each course?

We recommend dedicating 2-3 hours per week to each course, which includes video lessons, exercises, and implementation. However, you can progress at your own pace as all content remains available.

Do you offer certificates upon completion?

Yes, all our courses include a certificate of completion that you can download and share on LinkedIn or with your employer once you've finished all course requirements.
