Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need any prior writing experience to join?

Not at all! Our courses are designed for writers of all levels, from complete beginners to experienced authors looking to refine their skills. Each course clearly indicates its difficulty level so you can choose the right starting point for you.

How much time should I dedicate to the courses?

Most students find that dedicating 3-5 hours per week allows them to comfortably complete course materials and assignments. However, all our courses are self-paced, so you can move faster or slower depending on your schedule and preferences.

What's included in the Premium plan's personalized feedback?

Premium members can submit up to 2,000 words per month for detailed feedback from our instructors. You'll receive constructive criticism on your writing style, structure, character development, and other key elements, along with specific suggestions for improvement.

Can I switch between plans after signing up?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes will take effect at the start of your next billing cycle, and you'll only be charged the difference if upgrading mid-cycle.

Are there any live components to the courses?

All Standard and Premium plans include live components. Standard members get monthly Q&A sessions, while Premium members enjoy weekly live workshops. Basic members have access to all recorded materials but not live events.
