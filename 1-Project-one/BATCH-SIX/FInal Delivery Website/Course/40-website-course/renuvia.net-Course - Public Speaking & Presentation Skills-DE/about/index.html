About Renuvia

Empowering professionals to communicate with confidence, clarity, and impact through transformative public speaking training.

Our Story

How we became leaders in communication training

Founded in 2020, Renuvia was born from a simple observation: even the most brilliant professionals often struggle to communicate their ideas effectively. Our founder, David Peterson, noticed this gap while coaching executives at Fortune 500 companies and decided to create a solution that would make world-class communication training accessible to everyone.

What began as a small workshop series has grown into a comprehensive training platform serving thousands of professionals across Europe and North America. We've helped startup founders secure funding, executives deliver powerful presentations, and introverts find their voice on stage.

At Renuvia, we believe that great communication isn't about performance—it's about connection. Our approach focuses on helping you develop authentic confidence and clarity, not just polished delivery.

Our Mission

To empower individuals and organizations to communicate with confidence and clarity, transforming ideas into impact. We believe that effective communication is the foundation of professional success and personal growth, and we're committed to making these essential skills accessible to everyone.

Our Values

The principles that guide everything we do

Authenticity

We believe great communication starts with being true to yourself. Our training helps you find and express your authentic voice.

Impact

Our measure of success is the real-world difference our training makes in your career and personal growth.

Community

Learning thrives in supportive environments. We foster a community where students encourage and learn from each other.

Innovation

We continuously refine our methods based on the latest research in communication, psychology, and adult learning.

Empathy

We understand the challenges of public speaking because we've been there. Our approach is supportive, not intimidating.

Excellence

We maintain the highest standards in our curriculum and only work with instructors who are both skilled communicators and great teachers.
