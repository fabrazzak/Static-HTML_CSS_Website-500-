Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need any prior experience to join the courses?

No prior experience is needed! Our beginner courses are designed for complete novices, with step-by-step instructions and video demonstrations to guide you through every process. Many of our most successful students started with absolutely no crafting experience.

What materials and equipment will I need?

Each course includes a detailed list of required materials and equipment. For most beginner courses, you'll need basic items that are either inexpensive or may already be in your kitchen. We provide recommendations for quality suppliers and often suggest budget-friendly alternatives.

How long do I have access to the course materials?

With any of our subscription plans, you'll have access to all course materials for as long as your subscription is active. If you cancel, you'll lose access, but you can re-subscribe at any time to regain access to all your progress and materials.

Can I sell the products I make from your courses?

Absolutely! We encourage our students to turn their new skills into income. Our Premium plan even includes a business starter kit with resources on labeling, pricing, and marketing your handmade products. Just be sure to comply with any local regulations regarding the sale of cosmetic products.
