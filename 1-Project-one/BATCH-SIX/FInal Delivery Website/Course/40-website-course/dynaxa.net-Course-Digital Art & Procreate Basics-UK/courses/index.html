Procreate Basics

Beginner

Course Description

This comprehensive Procreate Basics course will take you from complete beginner to confident digital artist. You'll learn all the essential tools and techniques used by professionals to create stunning artwork on your iPad.

Through step-by-step video tutorials and hands-on projects, you'll master the Procreate interface, brush customization, layer management, selection tools, and essential digital painting techniques.

What You'll Learn

  • Navigate the Procreate interface with confidence
  • Customize and create your own brushes
  • Work effectively with layers and blending modes
  • Use selection tools for precise editing
  • Apply color theory principles to your artwork
  • Create complete digital paintings from start to finish
