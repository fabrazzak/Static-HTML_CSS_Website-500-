Frequently Asked Questions

How do I enroll in a course?

You can enroll in any of our courses by visiting the Courses page, selecting the course you're interested in, and clicking the "Enroll Now" button. You'll be guided through the registration and payment process. If you need any assistance, feel free to contact us using the form on this page.

What equipment do I need for the courses?

For our Procreate courses, you'll need an iPad (6th generation or newer) and Apple Pencil (1st or 2nd generation). The Procreate app (a one-time purchase from the App Store) is also required. For other digital art courses, a computer with appropriate software (which we'll specify in the course requirements) and a drawing tablet are recommended.

Do you offer refunds for courses?

Yes, we offer a 30-day money-back guarantee for all our courses. If you're not satisfied with your purchase, simply contact our support team within 30 days of enrollment for a full refund, no questions asked.

How do I access my course after purchasing?

After completing your purchase, you'll receive an email with instructions to access your course. You can log in to your account on our website at any time to access all your course materials. The courses are self-paced, so you can start and progress whenever it's convenient for you.

Can I get a certificate after completing a course?

Yes! Upon completing all lessons and assignments in a course, you'll be able to download a certificate of completion. This certificate can be added to your portfolio or shared on professional networks like LinkedIn.
