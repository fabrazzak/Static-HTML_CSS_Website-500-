General Questions

What makes Stylory different from other public speaking courses?

Stylory stands out through our:

  • Proven Methodology: Our curriculum is based on 10+ years of research in communication psychology
  • Small Class Sizes: Maximum 8 students per group for personalized attention
  • Practical Focus: 80% of class time is hands-on speaking practice with immediate feedback
  • Expert Instructors: All coaches are professional speakers with real-world experience
  • Ongoing Support: Access to alumni resources and refresher sessions
Do you offer corporate training or group discounts?

Yes! We offer customized corporate training programs and group discounts:

  • Team Training: Tailored programs for organizations (5+ participants)
  • Group Discounts: 10% off for 3-4 people enrolling together
  • Enterprise Solutions: Company-wide communication skills development

Contact our corporate team at corporate@stylory.org for a customized proposal.

Course Information

What's the difference between your beginner and advanced courses?

Our course levels are designed for different experience levels:

Beginner Courses (Fundamentals):

  • For those with little to no public speaking experience
  • Focus on overcoming anxiety and basic techniques
  • 3-5 minute speech practice

Intermediate Courses:

  • For those comfortable speaking but wanting refinement
  • Focus on structure, storytelling, and audience engagement
  • 5-10 minute presentations

Advanced Courses:

  • For experienced speakers wanting professional polish
  • Focus on persuasive techniques, handling tough Q&A, and executive presence
  • 10-20 minute presentations with complex content
How much time should I expect to spend on coursework outside of sessions?

Time commitment varies by course:

  • Group Courses: 2-3 hours per week for preparation and practice
  • Intensive Workshops: 4-5 hours during workshop days
  • Private Coaching: 1-2 hours between sessions

We recommend practicing daily for 15-30 minutes for best results. All courses include optional supplemental materials for those who want to dive deeper.

Do you offer any certification upon completion?

Yes! All our courses include:

  • Certificate of Completion: For participants who attend at least 80% of sessions
  • Performance Evaluation: Detailed assessment from your instructor (upon request)
  • Advanced Certification: For our Professional Speaking Mastery program (requires assessment)

Our certificates are recognized by several professional organizations in Australia including the National Speakers Association.

Enrollment & Payment

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major payment methods:

  • Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express)
  • PayPal
  • Bank Transfers (Australian banks only)
  • Afterpay (for courses over $200)
  • Corporate Invoicing (for business clients)

All payments are processed securely through our PCI-compliant payment gateway.

Can I pay in installments?

Yes, we offer flexible payment options:

  • Payment Plans: Split your payment over 2-3 months (0% interest)
  • Afterpay: Available for courses over $200 (4 interest-free payments)
  • Corporate Billing: Custom payment schedules for organizations

Note: Full payment is required before course completion to receive your certificate.

What's your refund policy?

Our refund policy is designed to be fair while allowing us to maintain small class sizes:

  • Full Refund: Available up to 7 days before course start
  • Partial Refund (50%): Available up to 48 hours before first session
  • Course Credit: After course begins (valid for 12 months)
  • No-Show: No refund for unattended sessions

Exceptions may be made for medical emergencies with documentation.

Technical Requirements

What do I need for online courses?

For optimal online learning, we recommend:

  • Device: Computer/laptop (preferred) or tablet with camera
  • Internet: Stable connection (minimum 5Mbps upload/download)
  • Software: Zoom (latest version), Chrome/Firefox browser
  • Environment: Quiet space where you can speak freely
  • Optional: External microphone, ring light for better video quality

We'll send a tech check guide before your first session to test your setup.

Can I access course materials after completion?

Yes! All students receive:

  • Lifetime Access: To course materials (slides, worksheets, templates)
  • 12 Months Access: To session recordings (where applicable)
  • Alumni Portal: With bonus resources and community forum

Some advanced courses include ongoing access to updated materials.

What if I have technical issues during a session?

We have multiple support options:

  • Live Support: Technical assistant available during all sessions
  • Pre-Session Check: Join 15 minutes early for tech troubleshooting
  • Backup Options: Phone-in available if video fails
  • Session Recording: Provided if you miss part of a session due to tech issues

Our team will work with you to ensure you don't miss important content.
