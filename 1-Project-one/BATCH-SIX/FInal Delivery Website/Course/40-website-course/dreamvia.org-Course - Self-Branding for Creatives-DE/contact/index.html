Common Questions

Quick answers to some of our frequently asked questions about courses and support.

How do I enroll in a course?

You can enroll directly through our Courses page by selecting your desired course and clicking "Enroll Now." You'll be guided through the payment process and gain immediate access to the course materials upon completion.

What's your refund policy?

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee for all our courses. If you're not satisfied with your purchase, simply email us within 30 days of enrollment for a full refund, no questions asked.

Do you offer group discounts?

Yes! We offer special pricing for teams and groups of 3 or more. Contact us at groups@dreamvia.org with details about your team and which courses you're interested in, and we'll provide a customized quote.

Can I get a certificate after completing a course?

Absolutely! All our courses include a certificate of completion that you can download and share on LinkedIn or include in your portfolio. Some courses also offer optional accreditation for an additional fee.
