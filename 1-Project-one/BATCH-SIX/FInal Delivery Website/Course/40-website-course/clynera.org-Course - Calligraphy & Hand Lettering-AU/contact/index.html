Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How quickly can I expect a response to my inquiry?

+

We typically respond to all inquiries within 24-48 hours during business days (Monday-Friday). For urgent matters, please call our office directly during business hours.

Do you offer in-person workshops or only online courses?

+

We offer both! In addition to our comprehensive online courses, we host regular in-person workshops at our studio and occasionally in other cities. Check our events calendar for upcoming workshops.

Can I schedule a one-on-one consultation with an instructor?

+

Yes, we offer private consultations for personalized guidance. Please mention your interest in the message field, and we'll provide available time slots and rates.
