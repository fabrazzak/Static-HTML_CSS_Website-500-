About Clynera

Welcome to Clynera, your premier destination for learning the timeless art of calligraphy and modern hand lettering. Founded in 2018, we've helped thousands of students discover the joy of beautiful writing.

Our mission is to make calligraphy accessible to everyone, regardless of skill level. Whether you're looking to improve your handwriting, create stunning wedding invitations, or start a creative business, our courses provide the perfect foundation.

All our instructors are professional calligraphers with years of teaching experience, passionate about sharing their knowledge and helping you develop your unique style.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our courses

Do I need any prior experience to start?

+

No prior experience is necessary! Our beginner courses are designed for absolute newcomers to calligraphy and hand lettering. We start with the very basics and guide you through each step of the learning process.

What supplies do I need for the courses?

+

For most beginner courses, you'll need basic supplies like calligraphy pens, ink, and paper. Each course provides a detailed supply list before you begin, and we offer recommendations for budget-friendly options as well as professional-grade tools.

How much time should I dedicate to practice?

+

We recommend practicing for at least 20-30 minutes daily for best results. However, our courses are designed to be flexible to fit your schedule. Even practicing a few times a week will show noticeable improvement over time.

Can I access the courses on mobile devices?

+

Yes! Our platform is fully responsive and works on smartphones, tablets, and desktop computers. You can watch lessons and download practice sheets from any device with an internet connection.

What if I need help during the course?

+

We offer multiple support options depending on your plan, including community forums, email support, and live Q&A sessions. Our instructors are committed to helping you succeed and are available to answer your questions.
