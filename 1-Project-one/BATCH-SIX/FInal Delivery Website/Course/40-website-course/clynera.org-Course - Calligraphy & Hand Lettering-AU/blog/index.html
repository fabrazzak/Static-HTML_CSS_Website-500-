About The Author

Meet our lead calligraphy instructor and blog writer

Sarah Johnson

Lead Calligraphy Instructor

With over 10 years of professional calligraphy experience, Sarah has taught thousands of students through workshops, online courses, and private lessons. Her work has been featured in numerous weddings, corporate events, and art exhibitions. Sarah specializes in modern calligraphy but has extensive knowledge of traditional scripts as well.

Through this blog, Sarah shares her expertise to help calligraphers of all levels improve their skills and discover the joy of beautiful writing.
