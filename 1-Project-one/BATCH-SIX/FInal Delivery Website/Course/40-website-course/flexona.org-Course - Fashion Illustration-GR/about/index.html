Our Story

Discover the passion and purpose behind Flexona's mission to revolutionize fashion illustration education.

Who We Are

Founded in 2020, Flexona began as a small collective of fashion illustrators passionate about making high-quality education accessible to everyone. What started as local workshops has grown into a global online platform serving thousands of students across 50+ countries.

Our founders, all industry veterans, recognized a gap in fashion education - while many could access basic tutorials, comprehensive professional training remained out of reach for most. Flexona was created to bridge this gap, offering structured curricula developed by top professionals at a fraction of traditional education costs.

Today, we're proud to be a leader in online fashion illustration education, but our core mission remains the same: to empower creatives with the skills, knowledge, and confidence to bring their artistic visions to life.
