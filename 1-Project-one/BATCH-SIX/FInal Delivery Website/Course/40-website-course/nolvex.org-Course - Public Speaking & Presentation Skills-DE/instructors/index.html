About Dr. Wilson

Dr. James Wilson holds a Ph.D. in Communication Studies from Stanford University and has trained over 5,000 professionals in public speaking and presentation skills. Before joining Nolvex, he served as the Director of Executive Communication at a Fortune 100 company, where he developed their global communication training program.

As a TEDx speaker coach, James has helped craft over 50 TED-style talks that have collectively garnered millions of views. His approach combines neuroscience-based techniques with practical speaking strategies that work in real-world business settings.

James specializes in helping technical professionals communicate complex ideas simply and effectively. His courses on "Speaking with Authority" and "Persuasive Communication" are among our most popular offerings.
