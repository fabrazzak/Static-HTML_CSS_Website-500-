Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our courses

How do I choose the right course for my level?

We offer placement tests for each language to help determine your current proficiency level. You can take these tests for free before enrolling in any course. Based on your results, we'll recommend the most appropriate course level for you.

How much time should I dedicate to studying each week?

For best results, we recommend studying at least 3-5 hours per week. This can be broken into shorter daily sessions. Our courses are designed with flexibility in mind, so you can learn at your own pace while maintaining steady progress.

What makes Marzora different from other language learning platforms?

Our approach combines structured grammar instruction with immersive conversation practice. Unlike many apps that focus only on vocabulary, we provide comprehensive language education that helps you develop all four key skills: reading, writing, listening, and speaking.

Can I switch courses if I find the level too easy or difficult?

Yes, you can switch to a different level within the same language at any time. Just contact our support team, and we'll help you transition to a more suitable course without losing your progress.

Do you offer certificates upon course completion?

Yes, all Premium plan subscribers receive a certificate of completion for each course they finish. These certificates indicate the level achieved and can be shared on LinkedIn or included in professional portfolios.
