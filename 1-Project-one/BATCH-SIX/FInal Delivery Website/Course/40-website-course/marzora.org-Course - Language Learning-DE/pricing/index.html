Pricing FAQs

Find answers to common questions about our pricing and plans

Can I switch plans later?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. When you switch plans, we'll automatically prorate the difference in cost for the remaining days in your billing cycle.

Is there a free trial available?

We offer a 7-day free trial for all new users. You'll have full access to all features of the Standard plan during your trial period. No credit card is required to start your trial.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, and in some countries, direct bank transfers. All payments are processed securely through our payment partners.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Absolutely. You can cancel your subscription at any time through your account settings. After cancellation, you'll retain access until the end of your current billing period.

Do you offer discounts for students or teams?

Yes! We offer a 20% discount for students with valid .edu email addresses. For teams of 5 or more, we provide special enterprise pricing - please contact our sales team for more information.
