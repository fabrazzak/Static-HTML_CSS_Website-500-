Who We Are

A team of creative professionals passionate about personal branding

Founded in 2020, equivox began as a small collective of designers, writers, and artists who saw a common challenge among creative professionals: the struggle to effectively communicate their unique value in a crowded marketplace.

What started as informal workshops among colleagues has grown into a comprehensive platform offering courses, resources, and community support for creatives at all stages of their branding journey.

Today, our team includes brand strategists, social media experts, portfolio specialists, and successful creative professionals who have built thriving careers by mastering their personal brands.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide everything we do

Authenticity First

Our Story

How equivox came to be

2018

The Beginning

Jessica, David, and Maria meet at a creative conference and discover their shared frustration with traditional branding advice that didn't resonate with artistic professionals.

2019

First Workshops

The trio begins hosting informal branding workshops for fellow creatives in their local community, testing approaches that prioritize authenticity over corporate formulas.

Early 2020

Online Transition

As the pandemic hits, they pivot to online courses, discovering a global audience of creatives hungry for personal branding guidance tailored to their needs.

Late 2020

Official Launch

equivox officially launches as a platform with three core courses and a growing community of creative professionals.

2023

Expansion

The team expands to include additional instructors and support staff, offering more specialized courses and one-on-one coaching options.
