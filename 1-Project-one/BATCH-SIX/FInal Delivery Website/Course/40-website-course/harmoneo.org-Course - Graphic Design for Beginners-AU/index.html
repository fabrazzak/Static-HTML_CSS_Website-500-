Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need any prior experience to take these courses?

No prior experience is required! Our beginner courses are designed for absolute newcomers to graphic design. We start with the fundamentals and build up from there.

What software do I need for the courses?

While we demonstrate concepts using popular design tools like Adobe Photoshop and Illustrator, the principles you'll learn apply to any design software. We also provide free alternatives you can use.

How much time should I dedicate to the courses?

We recommend dedicating at least 3-5 hours per week to get the most from the courses. However, you can progress at your own pace—all course materials remain available to you.

Will I receive a certificate upon completion?

Yes! All our courses include a certificate of completion that you can add to your LinkedIn profile or resume.

Can I cancel my subscription at any time?

Absolutely. There are no long-term contracts, and you can cancel your subscription anytime with just a few clicks.
