Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions below

How do I choose the right course for my skill level?

We offer detailed course descriptions and skill level indicators (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced) for each course. If you're unsure, take our free skill assessment quiz or contact our advisors who can recommend the perfect starting point based on your experience and goals.

What equipment do I need for online music lessons?

At minimum, you'll need a reliable internet connection and a device (computer, tablet, or smartphone) with a camera and microphone. For instrument-specific courses, you'll need the instrument (keyboard, guitar, etc.). Some courses may recommend additional accessories like headphones or a microphone for optimal learning.

Can I get a refund if I'm not satisfied with a course?

Yes! We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee for all courses. If you're not completely satisfied with your purchase, simply contact our support team within 30 days of enrollment for a full refund, no questions asked.

How do I access my courses after purchasing?

Immediately after purchase, you'll receive an email with login instructions for your student dashboard. All your enrolled courses will be available there 24/7. You can access them from any device with internet connection.

Do you offer certificates upon course completion?

Yes! All our premium courses include a certificate of completion that you can download and share once you've finished all lessons and assignments. Certificates show the course name, your name, and completion date.
