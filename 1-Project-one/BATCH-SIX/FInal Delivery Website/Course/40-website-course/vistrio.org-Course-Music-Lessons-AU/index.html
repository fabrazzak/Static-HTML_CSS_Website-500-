Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need any prior experience to start?

No prior experience is necessary! Our courses are designed for complete beginners as well as more advanced students. Each course clearly indicates the recommended skill level, and our instructors are skilled at adapting lessons to individual needs.

What equipment do I need?

For most courses, you'll need the instrument you're learning (piano, guitar, violin, etc.), a computer or tablet with internet access, and headphones or speakers. Some courses may recommend additional accessories like a metronome or music stand, but these aren't required to begin.

How does the online learning work?

After enrolling, you'll get immediate access to our learning platform where you can watch video lessons, download materials, and complete exercises at your own pace. Premium plans include live video sessions with instructors, and all students can submit questions and receive feedback.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time with no cancellation fees. When you cancel, you'll retain access until the end of your current billing period.

Is there a free trial available?

We offer a 7-day free trial for our Standard and Premium plans. This gives you full access to all course materials so you can experience our teaching style before committing. No credit card is required for the trial.
