Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our courses

Do I need any prior experience to take these courses?

No prior experience is necessary for our beginner courses. We start with the fundamentals and gradually build up your knowledge and skills. Our courses are designed to be accessible to complete beginners while still being valuable for those with some formulation experience.

What ingredients will I need for the courses?

Each course includes a detailed list of required ingredients and equipment. Most beginner formulations use common natural ingredients that are easy to source locally or online. For advanced courses, we provide recommendations for reputable suppliers of specialized ingredients.

How long do I have access to the course materials?

You'll have access to all course materials for as long as you maintain your subscription. If you cancel, you'll retain access until the end of your current billing period. We also provide downloadable resources that you can keep forever.

Can I sell the products I create from these courses?

Yes! We encourage students to start their own skincare businesses if they wish. Our courses include guidance on small-scale production, labeling requirements, and regulatory considerations for selling homemade skincare products.
