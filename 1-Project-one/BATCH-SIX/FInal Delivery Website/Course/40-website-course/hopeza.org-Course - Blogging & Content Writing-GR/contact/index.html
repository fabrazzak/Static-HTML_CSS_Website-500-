Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can I expect a response to my inquiry?

We typically respond to all inquiries within 24-48 hours during our business hours (Monday-Friday, 9am-5pm EET). For urgent matters, please call our support line.

What's the best way to get technical support for my course?

For technical issues with your course access or platform, please email support@hopeza.org with a detailed description of your issue, including any screenshots if possible. Our technical team monitors this inbox continuously during business hours.

Do you offer in-person consultations or classes?

Currently, all our courses and consultations are conducted online. This allows us to serve students worldwide and provide flexible learning options. We do occasionally host live virtual events and workshops.

Can I schedule a call with an instructor before enrolling?

Yes! We offer free 15-minute consultation calls with our enrollment advisors who can answer your questions about course content, structure, and whether it's the right fit for your goals. through our contact form.

Do you offer discounts for group enrollments?

We do offer group discounts for organizations enrolling 5 or more students. Please contact our partnerships team through the form above, selecting "Partnership" as your subject, and we'll provide customized pricing options.
