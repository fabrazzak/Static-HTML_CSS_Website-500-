About Lazaro

Transforming communication through expert-led public speaking training

Our Story

How we became leaders in public speaking education

Founded on a Passion for Effective Communication

Lazaro was founded in 2015 by a group of professional speakers and communication experts who saw a need for high-quality, accessible public speaking training. What began as small workshops has grown into a comprehensive platform serving thousands of professionals worldwide.

Our founders recognized that while public speaking is one of the most valuable skills for career success, most people never receive proper training. We set out to change that by creating courses that combine proven techniques with practical application.

Today, Lazaro is recognized as a leader in communication education, with our methods being adopted by corporations, universities, and individual professionals across the globe.

Our Mission

Why we do what we do

Empowering Through Communication

At Lazaro, we believe that effective communication is the foundation of personal and professional success. Our mission is to empower individuals to express their ideas with clarity, confidence, and impact.

We're committed to breaking down the barriers that prevent people from becoming effective communicators - whether it's fear, lack of training, or simply not knowing where to start.

Every course we create, every coaching session we deliver, and every resource we develop is designed with one goal in mind: to help you find your voice and share your message with the world.
