Refund Policy

Last Updated: January 01, 2025

At Lazaro, we want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase of our public speaking and presentation skills courses. Please read this Refund Policy carefully before making a purchase.

30-Day Money-Back Guarantee: We offer a full refund within 30 days of your purchase if you're not satisfied with our courses, no questions asked.

1. Eligibility for Refunds

To be eligible for a refund:

  • Your refund request must be made within 30 days of your original purchase date
  • You must not have completed more than 25% of the course content
  • You must not have downloaded excessive course materials

We reserve the right to deny refund requests that don't meet these eligibility requirements.

2. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund:

  1. Email us at support@lazaro.org with the subject line "Refund Request"
  2. Include your full name and the email address used to make the purchase
  3. Provide the order number or receipt
  4. Briefly explain why you're requesting a refund (optional)

We typically process refund requests within 3-5 business days.

3. Refund Processing

Approved refunds will be issued to the original payment method. The time it takes for the refund to appear in your account depends on your payment provider:

  • Credit/debit cards: 5-10 business days
  • PayPal: 3-5 business days
  • Bank transfers: 7-14 business days

We will notify you by email when your refund has been processed.

4. Non-Refundable Items

The following are not eligible for refunds:

  • Purchases made more than 30 days ago
  • Courses where more than 25% of content has been completed
  • Bundles or packages where partial content has been accessed
  • Custom or personalized coaching sessions that have already occurred

5. Subscription Cancellations

For monthly subscription plans:

  • You may cancel your subscription at any time
  • Cancellation will stop future charges but does not qualify for refunds of previous payments
  • You will retain access to the course until the end of your current billing period

To cancel your subscription, log in to your account and follow the cancellation instructions or contact us at support@lazaro.org.

6. Special Circumstances

Duplicate Charges

If you believe you've been charged twice for the same purchase, please contact us immediately at support@lazaro.org with proof of both charges. We will investigate and refund any duplicate charges.

Technical Issues

If you're experiencing technical difficulties accessing our courses, please contact our support team before requesting a refund. We're happy to help resolve any access issues.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Refund Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

Your continued use of our Services after such changes constitutes your acceptance of the new Refund Policy.
