Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our courses

Do I need any prior experience to take these courses?

No prior experience is necessary for our beginner courses. We start with the fundamentals and gradually build your skills. Our courses are designed to be accessible to complete beginners while still providing valuable insights for those with some formulation experience.

What equipment and ingredients will I need?

For most beginner formulations, you'll need basic kitchen equipment (bowls, spoons, measuring tools) and easily accessible ingredients. Each course provides a detailed shopping list of ingredients and equipment needed, with recommendations for affordable starter kits.

How are the courses delivered?

All courses are delivered through our online platform with video lessons, downloadable PDFs, formulation templates, and quizzes. You'll have lifetime access to the course materials so you can learn at your own pace and revisit content whenever you need.

Can I sell the products I create?

While our courses teach you how to create professional-quality products, selling cosmetics commercially involves additional legal and safety considerations. We offer a separate Business of Skincare course that covers regulatory requirements, labeling, and small-scale production for those interested in selling their creations.
