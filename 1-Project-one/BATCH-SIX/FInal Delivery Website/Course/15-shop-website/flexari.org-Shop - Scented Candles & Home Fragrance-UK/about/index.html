Our Story

Crafting Fragrant Moments

Founded in 2025 in the picturesque town of Garthside, United Kingdom, Flexari began as a passion project between two friends who shared a love for fragrance and craftsmanship. What started as small-batch candle making in a home kitchen has grown into a beloved brand known for its premium scented candles and home fragrance products.

Our journey has been guided by a simple philosophy: to create products that transform ordinary spaces into extraordinary experiences. Each Flexari candle is hand-poured with care, using only the finest natural ingredients to ensure a clean, long-lasting burn with exceptional scent throw.

Today, while we've grown beyond our humble beginnings, we remain committed to the artisanal values that started it all - quality, craftsmanship, and the power of scent to evoke emotion and create memories.

Our Values

Sustainability

We're committed to eco-friendly practices, from our natural soy wax base to our recyclable packaging. Our candles are vegan, cruelty-free, and made with responsibly sourced ingredients.

Craftsmanship

Every Flexari candle is hand-poured in small batches to ensure quality and consistency. We take pride in the traditional techniques that go into each product we create.

Quality

From premium fragrance oils to lead-free cotton wicks, we never compromise on materials. Our rigorous testing ensures each candle meets our high standards before it reaches you.

Our Process

1

Fragrance Development

Our perfumers carefully craft each scent profile, blending premium essential oils and fragrance oils to create unique, well-balanced aromas that tell a story.

2

Wax Preparation

We use natural soy wax that's gently melted to the perfect temperature before adding fragrance, ensuring optimal scent throw and a clean, even burn.

3

Hand Pouring

Each candle is carefully hand-poured into its vessel, with attention to detail that only human hands can provide. This allows us to monitor quality at every step.

4

Curing

Our candles cure for several days to allow the fragrance to fully bind with the wax, resulting in a stronger, longer-lasting scent when burned.

5

Quality Control

Every candle undergoes rigorous testing for burn quality, scent throw, and appearance before being approved for sale.
