Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our art prints and services

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping within United Kingdom takes 2-3 business days. For international orders, delivery times vary between 5-10 business days depending on the destination. All orders are processed within 1-2 business days.

What materials are used for the prints?

We use museum-quality archival paper and premium inks to ensure your art print lasts for generations. Our canvas prints are made with heavyweight artist-grade canvas and archival inks that resist fading.

Do you offer framing services?

Yes, we offer premium framing options for all our prints. You can select your frame style during checkout. Our frames are made with high-quality materials and include UV-protective acrylic glass.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day return policy for unused and undamaged prints. Custom and framed prints are final sale. If your print arrives damaged, please contact us within 7 days of delivery for a replacement.

Do you accept custom art requests?

Yes, we work with a network of talented artists who can create custom pieces based on your specifications. Please use the custom art request option in our contact form to discuss your project.
