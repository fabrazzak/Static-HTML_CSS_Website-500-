Our Story

Fynetic was founded in 2020 by a team of cosmetic chemists, herbalists, and skincare educators who saw a need for accessible, science-based education in DIY skincare formulation. What began as a small workshop series has grown into a comprehensive online learning platform serving thousands of students worldwide.

We believe that everyone should have the knowledge and tools to create safe, effective skincare products tailored to their unique needs. Our approach combines rigorous cosmetic science with the wisdom of traditional herbalism, creating a balanced methodology that empowers rather than intimidates.

Today, Fynetic offers a complete curriculum from beginner to advanced formulation, with courses taught by industry experts. Our students range from complete beginners looking to make products for personal use to estheticians and entrepreneurs launching their own skincare lines.

Our Mission & Approach

What drives us and how we're different

Science-Based

We teach the real chemistry behind formulations, not just recipes. Our courses explain why ingredients work together, not just how to combine them.

Accessible

We break down complex concepts into understandable lessons. No PhD required - just curiosity and a willingness to learn.

Natural Focus

While we teach all formulation methods, we emphasize plant-based ingredients and sustainable practices throughout our curriculum.

Safety First

Proper preservation, contamination prevention, and stability testing are core components of all our courses.

Lifelong Learning

Our students get lifetime access to course materials, including updates as formulation science evolves.

Community

Join our vibrant community of formulators to share knowledge, troubleshoot, and celebrate successes.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide everything we do

Scientific Integrity

We base our teachings on peer-reviewed cosmetic science while being transparent about areas where more research is needed.

Education Over Prescription

We empower students with knowledge rather than just providing recipes, enabling true customization and innovation.

Sustainable Practices

From sourcing recommendations to waste reduction strategies, we prioritize environmental responsibility in formulation.

Safety First

Proper preservation, contamination prevention, and stability testing are non-negotiable components of all our teachings.

Community Focus

We foster a supportive learning environment where students at all levels can share knowledge and grow together.

Continuous Innovation

We regularly update our curriculum to reflect the latest advancements in cosmetic science and natural formulation.
