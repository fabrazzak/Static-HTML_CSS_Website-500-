Common Questions

Find quick answers to our most frequently asked questions

How do I know which course is right for me?

Our beginner courses are designed for those with no prior formulation experience, while intermediate and advanced courses build on these fundamentals. If you're unsure, you can take our course selector quiz or contact our advisors who can help recommend the best starting point based on your goals and experience level.

What's your refund policy?

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee for all courses. If you're not satisfied with your purchase for any reason, simply contact us within 14 days of enrollment for a full refund, no questions asked.

Do you offer group discounts?

Yes! We offer discounts for groups of 5 or more students. Contact our team with details about your group and we'll provide a customized quote with special pricing.

Can I get a certificate after completing a course?

All our courses include a certificate of completion that you can download after successfully finishing all course requirements. Our certificates are widely recognized in the natural skincare community.
