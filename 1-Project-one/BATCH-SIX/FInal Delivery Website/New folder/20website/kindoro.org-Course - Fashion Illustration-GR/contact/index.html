Common Questions

How quickly can I expect a response to my inquiry?

We typically respond to all inquiries within 24 hours during our business hours (Monday-Friday, 9AM-6PM GMT+2). For urgent matters, please call our support line for immediate assistance.

Do you offer in-person consultations?

Yes! We offer free 30-minute consultation sessions at our Velestino office by appointment. Please contact us to schedule a time that works for you.

What information should I include in my message?

To help us respond more effectively, please include:

  • Your specific question or concern
  • Any relevant course names or numbers
  • Your preferred contact method
  • Any time constraints you may have

Can I visit your office without an appointment?

While we welcome visitors, we strongly recommend scheduling an appointment to ensure the appropriate team member is available to assist you. Walk-in availability may be limited.
