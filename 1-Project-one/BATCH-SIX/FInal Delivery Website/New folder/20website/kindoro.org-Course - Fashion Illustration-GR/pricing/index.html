Pricing FAQs

Can I switch between plans?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. When you upgrade, you'll only pay the difference for the remaining time in your billing cycle. Downgrades will take effect at your next billing date.

Is there a free trial available?

We offer a 7-day free trial for our Standard plan. This gives you full access to all Standard features so you can experience our platform before committing. No credit card is required to start your trial.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, and in some countries, we also accept bank transfers. All payments are processed securely through our payment partners.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Absolutely. You can cancel your subscription at any time through your account settings. There are no cancellation fees, and you'll continue to have access until the end of your current billing period.

Do you offer discounts for students?

Yes! We offer a 20% discount for students with valid student ID. Contact our support team with proof of student status to get your discount code. This applies to both monthly and annual plans.
