Our Story

Discover the passion and purpose behind Kindoro's mission to revolutionize fashion illustration education.

How Kindoro Began

Founded in 2020, Kindoro started as a small collective of fashion illustrators who saw a gap in accessible, high-quality education for aspiring artists. We believed that talent exists everywhere, but opportunities don't - and we set out to change that.

What began as informal workshops among colleagues has grown into a global platform with students from over 60 countries. Our approach combines traditional fashion illustration techniques with modern digital tools, preparing students for today's industry demands.

At Kindoro, we're more than just an online school - we're a community of passionate creatives supporting each other's growth. Our instructors aren't just teachers; they're working professionals who bring real-world experience into every lesson.
