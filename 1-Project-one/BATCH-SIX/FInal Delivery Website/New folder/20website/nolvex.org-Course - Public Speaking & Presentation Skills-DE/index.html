Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our courses and programs.

What's the best course for complete beginners?

Our "Public Speaking Fundamentals" course is specifically designed for beginners. It starts with overcoming speaking anxiety and builds up to delivering your first complete presentation. Many students then progress to our "Confident Communication" course to further develop their skills.

How are the courses delivered?

All courses are delivered through our online platform with a mix of video lessons, interactive exercises, and live sessions. You'll get lifetime access to course materials so you can learn at your own pace and revisit content whenever you need.

Do you offer any in-person training?

Yes! We offer intensive in-person workshops in major cities throughout the year. Premium plan members get early access to workshop registration and discounted rates. Check our Events page for upcoming dates and locations.

What if I'm not satisfied with a course?

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all courses. If you're not completely satisfied, just contact our support team within 30 days of purchase for a full refund, no questions asked.
