Pricing FAQs

Answers to common questions about our pricing and plans.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, and bank transfers. For corporate accounts, we also accept purchase orders and can invoice you directly.

Is there a free trial available?

Yes! We offer a 7-day free trial for our Basic and Standard plans. You'll get full access to all course materials during this period. No credit card is required to start your free trial.

Can I cancel or change my plan anytime?

Absolutely. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your subscription at any time. If you cancel, you'll retain access until the end of your current billing period.

Do you offer discounts for teams or organizations?

Yes, we offer significant discounts for group enrollments. For teams of 5 or more, please contact our corporate sales team for custom pricing and dedicated coaching options.

What's included in the 30-day guarantee?

If you're not completely satisfied with your purchase within the first 30 days, we'll give you a full refund, no questions asked. This applies to both individual courses and membership plans.
