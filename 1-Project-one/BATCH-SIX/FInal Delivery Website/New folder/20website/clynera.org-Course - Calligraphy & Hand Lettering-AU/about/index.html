Our Story & Passion for Calligraphy

Discover the journey behind Clynera and our commitment to preserving and teaching the beautiful art of calligraphy and hand lettering.

How Clynera Began

Founded in 2018 by a group of passionate calligraphers, Clynera began as a small studio in Melbourne, Canada . What started as weekend workshops for friends quickly grew into a thriving community of lettering enthusiasts.

Our founders recognized a gap in quality calligraphy education - most resources were either overly simplistic or assumed prior knowledge. They set out to create a learning experience that was comprehensive yet accessible, structured yet creative.

Today, Clynera has taught over 15,000 students worldwide through our online platform and in-person workshops. While we've grown, our core mission remains the same: to make the art of beautiful writing accessible to everyone.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide everything we do

Passion for the Craft

We're deeply committed to preserving and evolving the art of calligraphy. Our enthusiasm for beautiful writing is contagious and inspires our students.

Excellence in Teaching

We invest in continuous instructor training and curriculum development to ensure the highest quality learning experience.

Accessibility

Beautiful writing should be available to everyone. We offer scholarships and free resources to make calligraphy education inclusive.

Creativity

While we teach traditional techniques, we encourage students to develop their unique style and artistic voice.

Community

We believe learning happens best in community. Our students support and inspire each other's growth.

Growth Mindset

Every mistake is a learning opportunity. We cultivate an environment where students feel safe to experiment and improve.
