Common Questions

How soon can I expect a response to my inquiry?

We typically respond to all inquiries within 24-48 hours during business days. For urgent matters, please call our office directly during business hours.

Do you offer in-person consultations?

Yes, we welcome prospective students to visit our studio for consultations. Please schedule an appointment in advance to ensure we can dedicate proper time to your visit.

What's the best way to contact you about course enrollment?

For enrollment questions, you can use our contact form, email us directly, or call our office. Be sure to mention which course(s) you're interested in for the fastest response.
