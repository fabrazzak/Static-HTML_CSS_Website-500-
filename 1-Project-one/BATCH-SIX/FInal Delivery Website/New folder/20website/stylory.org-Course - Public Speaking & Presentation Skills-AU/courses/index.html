Public Speaking Essentials

Category: Fundamentals

Level: Beginner

Duration: 6 weeks (2 hours/week)

Format: In-person or Online

Price: $199

This comprehensive course is designed to help you overcome stage fright and master the fundamentals of effective public speaking. Through practical exercises and constructive feedback, you'll develop the skills and confidence to speak clearly and persuasively in any situation.

What You'll Learn:

  • Techniques to manage speaking anxiety and build confidence
  • How to structure your speech for maximum impact
  • Vocal variety and body language techniques
  • Strategies for engaging different types of audiences
  • How to handle Q&A sessions with poise
  • Practical exercises with personalized feedback

Who Should Attend:

  • Professionals who need to present at work
  • Students preparing for class presentations
  • Anyone who wants to overcome fear of public speaking
  • Individuals seeking to improve their communication skills
