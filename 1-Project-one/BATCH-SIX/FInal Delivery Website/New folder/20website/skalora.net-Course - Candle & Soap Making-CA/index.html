About Our Craft

At Skalora, we're passionate about preserving the traditional art of candle and soap making while incorporating modern techniques and designs. Our mission is to empower you with the skills and knowledge to create beautiful, high-quality products right in your own home.

Founded in 2020, we've helped thousands of students discover the joy and satisfaction of crafting their own natural candles and soaps. Whether you're looking for a creative hobby, a way to reduce chemical exposure in your home, or even the foundation for a small business, our courses provide everything you need.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need any prior experience to take these courses?

No prior experience is needed for our beginner courses! We design our curriculum to guide complete novices through each step of the process. Many of our most successful students started with absolutely no crafting experience.

What equipment will I need to get started?

For candle making, you'll need basic items like a double boiler, thermometer, and containers. For soap making, you'll need safety equipment like goggles and gloves. We provide detailed equipment lists for each course, with recommendations for budget-friendly starter kits.

How long do I have access to the course materials?

You'll have unlimited access to all course materials for as long as you maintain your subscription. If you cancel, you'll lose access, but can rejoin at any time to pick up where you left off.

Can I sell the products I make from these courses?

Absolutely! Many of our students start small businesses selling their handmade products. Our Premium plan even includes business development resources to help you get started.

Are the courses suitable for children?

Our courses are designed for adults, but teenagers can participate with adult supervision, especially for soap making which involves working with lye. We recommend our candle making courses for ages 16+ with supervision.
