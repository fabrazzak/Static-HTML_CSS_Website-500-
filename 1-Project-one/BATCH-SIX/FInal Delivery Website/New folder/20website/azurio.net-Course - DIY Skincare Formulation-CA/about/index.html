About Azurio

Home / About

Empowering Your Skincare Journey

Azurio was founded in 2018 with a simple mission: to make professional-grade skincare formulation knowledge accessible to everyone. What began as a small workshop series has grown into a comprehensive online education platform serving thousands of students worldwide.

Our team of cosmetic chemists, herbalists, and skincare experts is passionate about demystifying the science behind skincare products. We believe that understanding what goes into your skincare empowers you to make better choices for your skin and the environment.

Whether you're looking to create safer products for personal use or launch your own skincare line, our courses provide the knowledge, tools, and community support you need to succeed.

Our Mission & Vision

At Azurio, we're committed to revolutionizing the way people think about skincare. We envision a world where everyone has the knowledge and confidence to create personalized, effective skincare products using safe, natural ingredients.

Our approach combines scientific rigor with practical, hands-on learning. We strip away the jargon and marketing hype to focus on what really matters: creating products that work.

Beyond individual empowerment, we're building a community of conscious consumers and ethical formulators who are changing the beauty industry from the ground up.

Natural Ingredients

We prioritize plant-based, sustainably sourced ingredients that are safe for both people and the planet.

Scientific Integrity

Our formulations are based on cosmetic science, not marketing trends or pseudoscience.

Education First

We empower our students with knowledge, not just recipes, so they can formulate with confidence.

Community Focus

We believe in the power of shared knowledge and collaborative learning.

Our Story

The journey of Azurio

2018

Founding of Azurio

Dr. Emily Chen begins offering in-person workshops on natural skincare formulation in Melbourne, Canada .

2019

First Online Course

Launch of our flagship "Natural Skincare Basics" course, making formulation education accessible worldwide.

2020

Team Expansion

James Wilson and Sophia Rodriguez join the team, bringing expertise in small-scale production and herbal skincare.

2021

Course Platform Launch

Introduction of our dedicated learning platform with interactive features and community forums.

2023

10,000 Students

We celebrate reaching over 10,000 students across 45 countries in our mission to democratize skincare knowledge.

2025

New Headquarters

Opening of our new education and research facility in Innamincka, Canada , focused on sustainable skincare innovation.
