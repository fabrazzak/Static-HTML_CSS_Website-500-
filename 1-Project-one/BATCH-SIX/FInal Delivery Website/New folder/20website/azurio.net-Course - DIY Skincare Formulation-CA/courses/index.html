About This Course

Course description will appear here. This comprehensive beginner course will teach you how to create simple, effective skincare products using natural ingredients. You'll learn the science behind skincare formulation while creating practical products you can use every day.

What You'll Learn

  • Fundamentals of skincare formulation
  • How to create basic cleansers, toners, and moisturizers
  • Understanding ingredient properties and interactions
  • Safe formulation practices and preservation
  • Customizing recipes for different skin types

Who This Course Is For

This course is perfect for complete beginners with no prior formulation experience, as well as those with some basic knowledge who want to build a stronger foundation. It's ideal for anyone interested in creating safer, more natural skincare products for personal use.

Course Curriculum

This course includes 4 modules with 20 lessons and 5 hands-on projects.

Module 1: Introduction to Skincare Formulation

  • Understanding skin anatomy and function 45 min
  • Basic skincare ingredients and their properties 60 min
  • Essential equipment and workspace setup 30 min
  • Safety and hygiene practices 45 min
  • Project: Create your first cleanser 90 min

Module 2: Basic Product Formulation

  • Creating water-based products 60 min
  • Oil-based formulations 45 min
  • Introduction to emulsification 60 min
  • Project: Formulate a basic moisturizer 120 min

Module 3: Customization and Preservation

  • Understanding different skin types 45 min
  • Customizing formulations 60 min
  • Introduction to preservation 60 min
  • Project: Create a customized toner 90 min

Module 4: Troubleshooting and Beyond

  • Common formulation problems and solutions 60 min
  • Testing and evaluating your products 45 min
  • Next steps in your formulation journey 30 min
  • Final project: Complete skincare routine 180 min

Dr. Emily Chen

PhD in Cosmetic Science

With over 15 years of experience in cosmetic science, Dr. Chen has formulated products for major skincare brands and now focuses on educating the next generation of formulators. Her passion is making cosmetic science accessible to everyone.
