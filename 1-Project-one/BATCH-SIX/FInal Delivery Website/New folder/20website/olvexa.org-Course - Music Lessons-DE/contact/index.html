Common Questions

What are your customer support hours?

Our customer support team is available Monday through Friday from 9am to 6pm CET. For email inquiries, we typically respond within 24 hours. Live chat is available Monday-Friday from 10am to 4pm CET.

How do I schedule a trial lesson?

You can schedule a trial lesson by contacting us through the form above, by phone, or by email. Please let us know which instrument you're interested in and your availability, and we'll match you with an instructor for your free trial session.

Can I visit your physical location?

While we primarily operate as an online music school, our administrative office in Bad Brambach is open by appointment only. Please contact us in advance if you'd like to visit, and we'll be happy to arrange a meeting.

Do you offer in-person lessons?

Currently, all of our lessons are conducted online through our virtual platform. This allows us to provide high-quality instruction to students worldwide while maintaining affordable pricing. Our online format has proven to be just as effective as in-person lessons for most students.
