Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about our pricing or plans? We've got answers.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, and direct bank transfers for Australian customers. All payments are processed securely through our payment gateway.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. When you upgrade, you'll immediately gain access to the additional features. Downgrades will take effect at your next billing cycle.

Is there a free trial available?

We offer a 7-day free trial for our Standard and Premium plans. This gives you full access to all course materials so you can experience our teaching style before committing. No credit card is required for the trial.

What's your refund policy?

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all plans. If you're not satisfied with your purchase for any reason, simply contact us within 30 days for a full refund, no questions asked.

Do you offer discounts for students or families?

Yes, we offer a 15% student discount with valid student ID. For families enrolling multiple members, we offer family plans with discounted rates. Please contact us for more information about these special pricing options.

How does the yearly billing work?

With yearly billing, you pay for a full year upfront and save 20% compared to monthly payments. Your subscription will automatically renew each year, and you can cancel anytime before the renewal date.
