Our Story

Founded in 2020 in Fremantle, Western Australia, Vistrio Music began with a simple mission: to make high-quality music education accessible to everyone, regardless of location or schedule constraints. What started as a small studio offering local lessons has grown into Australia's premier online music education platform.

Our founder, Michael Richardson, a professional pianist and educator, recognized the need for flexible, personalized music instruction that could adapt to students' busy lives. He assembled a team of like-minded professional musicians who shared his passion for teaching, and Vistrio Music was born.

Today, we've helped over 5,000 students across Australia and beyond discover the joy of music. From complete beginners to advanced players looking to refine their skills, our students come from all walks of life and all age groups.

Our Mission & Values

At Vistrio Music, we're driven by a set of core principles that guide everything we do.

Quality Education

We believe everyone deserves access to exceptional music instruction from professional musicians, regardless of their location or budget.

Passion for Music

Our instructors are not just teachers - they're active performers who bring real-world experience and contagious enthusiasm to every lesson.

Student-Centered

Every student is unique. We tailor our approach to match individual learning styles, goals, and musical preferences.

Continuous Growth

We're committed to constant improvement, regularly updating our curriculum and teaching methods based on the latest pedagogical research.
