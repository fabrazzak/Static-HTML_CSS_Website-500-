Common Questions

How quickly can I expect a response to my inquiry?

We typically respond to all inquiries within 24 hours during our business hours (Monday-Friday, 9am-5pm AEST). If you contact us over the weekend, we'll get back to you first thing Monday morning.

Do you offer in-person consultations or classes?

Currently, all our courses and consultations are conducted online. This allows us to serve students from all over the world and offer flexible scheduling options.

What's the best way to get technical support for course access?

For technical issues, please email support@harmoneo.org with a detailed description of your problem. Include screenshots if possible, and we'll help resolve your issue as quickly as possible.

Can I schedule a call with an instructor before enrolling?

Absolutely! We offer free 15-minute consultation calls with our instructors to help you determine if our courses are right for you. Use the contact form to request a call, and we'll arrange a convenient time.
