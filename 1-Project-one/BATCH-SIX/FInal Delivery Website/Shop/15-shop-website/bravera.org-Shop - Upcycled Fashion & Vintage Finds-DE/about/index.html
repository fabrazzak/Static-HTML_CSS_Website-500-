Our Sustainable Fashion Journey

Discover the story behind Bravera and our commitment to reducing textile waste

Our Story

From Passion Project to Sustainable Brand

Bravera began in 2020 as a small passion project by founders Lena Müller and Marco Schmidt. What started as weekend upcycling sessions in Lena's Berlin apartment quickly grew into a movement as friends and family began requesting custom upcycled pieces.

Seeing the potential to make a real impact on textile waste, we officially launched Bravera in 2021 with a mission to prove that sustainable fashion could be both stylish and accessible. Today, we work with a network of talented artisans across Germany to transform discarded textiles into beautiful, wearable art.

Every piece in our collection carries its own history while being reborn as a contemporary fashion statement. We carefully source vintage garments and high-quality discarded fabrics, ensuring each item meets our standards for quality and sustainability.

Why Upcycling Matters

The fashion industry is one of the largest polluters in the world, with millions of tons of textiles ending up in landfills each year. At Bravera, we believe in giving these materials a second life through creative redesign and careful restoration.

Our upcycling process not only reduces waste but also preserves the craftsmanship and history of vintage textiles. Each upcycled piece saves approximately 1,800 gallons of water that would have been used in producing new fabric.

By choosing Bravera, you're not just buying clothing - you're becoming part of a movement to change how we think about fashion consumption and sustainability.

Our Mission & Values

The principles that guide everything we do at Bravera

Sustainable Practices

We prioritize upcycling and vintage sourcing to minimize our environmental impact, using eco-friendly packaging and carbon-neutral shipping.

Quality Craftsmanship

Each piece is carefully inspected and restored by skilled artisans who take pride in preserving the integrity of vintage textiles.

Ethical Production

We maintain fair wages and safe working conditions for all our artisans and partners, supporting local craftsmanship.

Innovative Design

Our designers creatively reimagine discarded materials, blending vintage aesthetics with contemporary style.

Community Focus

We build relationships with our customers through workshops and events that promote sustainable fashion education.

Local Sourcing

90% of our materials are sourced within Europe to reduce transportation emissions and support regional economies.
