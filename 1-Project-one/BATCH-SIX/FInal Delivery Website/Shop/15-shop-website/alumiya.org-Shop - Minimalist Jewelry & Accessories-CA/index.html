Our Story

Founded in 2020, Alumiya was born from a passion for minimalist design and quality craftsmanship. Our jewelry is created for those who appreciate understated elegance and timeless pieces that can be worn every day.

Each piece in our collection is thoughtfully designed and handcrafted using ethically sourced materials. We believe in creating jewelry that tells a story and becomes a part of yours.
