Our Story

Founded in 2025, Flexari is dedicated to creating premium scented candles and home fragrance products that elevate your living space. Our candles are hand-poured with care using natural soy wax, premium fragrance oils, and lead-free cotton wicks.

Each candle is crafted to provide a clean, long-lasting burn with exceptional scent throw. We believe in the power of scent to transform moods and create memorable experiences.
