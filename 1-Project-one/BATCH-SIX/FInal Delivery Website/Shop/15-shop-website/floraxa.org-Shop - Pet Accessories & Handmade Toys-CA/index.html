About Floraxa

Floraxa was born from our passion for pets and craftsmanship. We believe that our furry friends deserve the best - products that are not only functional but also safe, durable, and beautifully designed.

All our products are handmade using natural, non-toxic materials. We work with local artisans to create unique pieces that stand out in quality and design.
