Processing Time

Order Processing

All orders are processed within 1-2 business days (Monday-Friday, excluding holidays) after receiving your order confirmation. You will receive a notification email when your order has shipped.

Please note:

  • Orders placed after 12 PM EST will be processed the next business day
  • Weekend orders will be processed the following Monday
  • During peak seasons (November-Jan), processing may take 2-3 business days

International Shipping

Shipping Outside United Kingdom

We happily ship our scented candles worldwide. Please note the following for international orders:

  • Customs & Duties: International customers are responsible for any customs fees, import taxes, or duties that may apply to their order. These charges are determined by your local customs office.
  • Shipping Times: International delivery times are estimates only and do not include customs processing time, which can vary significantly by country.
  • Restrictions: Some countries have restrictions on candle imports. Please check your local regulations before ordering.
  • Address Accuracy: Ensure your shipping address is complete and accurate. We cannot redirect packages once shipped.

For more specific information about shipping to your country, please contact us before placing your order.

Order Tracking

How to Track Your Order

Once your order has shipped, you will receive a shipping confirmation email with tracking information. Here's how to track your package:

  1. Click the tracking link in your shipping confirmation email
  2. Visit the carrier's website and enter your tracking number
  3. For EU orders, tracking is usually available via DHL or local postal service

Please allow 24-48 hours for tracking information to update after receiving your shipping confirmation.

Delivery Issues

If you experience any issues with your delivery:

  • Tracking shows delivered but no package: Check with neighbors or your local post office first
  • Package is delayed: Wait a few extra days as delays can occur
  • Package appears lost: Contact us after 15 business days for international or 7 business days for domestic

For any delivery concerns, please email us at shipping@upvora.net with your order number and details.
