Frequently Asked Questions

What are your shipping options and delivery times?

We offer standard shipping (3-5 business days) and express shipping (1-2 business days) within Australia. International shipping typically takes 7-14 business days depending on the destination. All orders are processed within 1-2 business days of purchase.

Can I customize my wellness box?

While our subscription boxes are pre-curated by our wellness experts, we do offer a "Build Your Own Box" option where you can select from our range of products to create a personalized self-care package. This option is available for one-time purchases only.

What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 30 days of purchase for unopened and unused products. Due to hygiene reasons, we cannot accept returns on opened skincare or wellness products. If you received a damaged or incorrect item, please contact us within 7 days of delivery for a replacement or refund.

Do you offer wholesale or corporate gifting options?

Yes! We work with many businesses to create custom wellness packages for employees or clients. Our corporate gifting program includes branded packaging, bulk discounts, and the ability to customize product selections. Please contact our wholesale team for more information.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

You can manage your subscription through your account on our website. To cancel, simply log in, go to "My Subscriptions," and follow the prompts. We require at least 5 business days' notice before your next billing date to process cancellations.
