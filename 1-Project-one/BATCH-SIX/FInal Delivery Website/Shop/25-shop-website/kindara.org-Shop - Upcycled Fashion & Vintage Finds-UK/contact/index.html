Frequently Asked Questions

What are your customer service hours?

Our customer service team is available Monday through Friday from 9am to 5pm GMT. We typically respond to emails within 24-48 hours. For urgent matters, please call us at +447831251146 during business hours.

Do you offer wholesale pricing?

Yes, we offer wholesale pricing for qualified retailers, spas, and practitioners. Please select "Wholesale Inquiry" as your subject when contacting us, and include information about your business. Our wholesale team will get back to you with pricing details and requirements.

How can I track my order?

Once your order has shipped, you'll receive a confirmation email with tracking information. You can also track your order by logging into your account on our website. If you're having trouble tracking your package, please contact us with your order number.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day satisfaction guarantee on all our products. If you're not completely satisfied with your purchase, you may return unopened items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Please contact our customer service team to initiate a return.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we ship to most countries worldwide. Shipping costs and delivery times vary depending on your location. Please note that international customers are responsible for any customs fees or import taxes that may apply to their order.
