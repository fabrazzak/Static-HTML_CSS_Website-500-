Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How long does shipping take?

We typically process and ship orders within 1-2 business days. Delivery times vary by location:

  • Germany: 2-3 business days
  • Europe: 3-7 business days
  • International: 7-14 business days

You'll receive a tracking number once your order ships.

What's included in your embroidery kits?

Each Thryvia embroidery kit includes everything you need to complete your project:

  • Premium quality fabric with printed or stamped design
  • All necessary embroidery threads (DMC or equivalent)
  • Appropriate embroidery needle
  • Detailed instructions with stitch guides
  • Wooden embroidery hoop (for most kits)
Do you offer international shipping?

Yes! We ship worldwide to most countries. Shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on your location and order size.

Please note that international orders may be subject to customs fees or import taxes, which are the responsibility of the recipient.

Can I return or exchange my kit?

We accept returns of unused kits within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. The customer is responsible for return shipping costs.

For exchanges, please contact us first at support@thryvia.org. We'll be happy to help you find the perfect kit!

Do you offer gift wrapping?

Yes! We offer beautiful gift wrapping for an additional €3.50. Select the gift wrapping option at checkout and include a gift message if desired.

All gift-wrapped orders include a handwritten note and are shipped in our signature gift box.
