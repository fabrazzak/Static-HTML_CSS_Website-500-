Our Story

How It All Began

Giventa was founded in 2018 by embroidery enthusiast Clara Schneider in her small apartment in Bad Brambach, Germany. What started as a passion project quickly grew into a thriving business as Clara's beautifully designed kits gained popularity among local crafters.

Frustrated by the lack of high-quality, complete embroidery kits that included everything a stitcher needed, Clara set out to create her own. She sourced the finest materials and designed patterns that were both beautiful and achievable for stitchers of all skill levels.

Today, Giventa ships thousands of kits to happy customers across Europe and beyond, but we still maintain the same commitment to quality and craftsmanship that Clara established in those early days.

Our Craftsmanship

At Giventa, we believe that embroidery is more than just a hobby - it's an art form that connects us to centuries of tradition while allowing for personal creativity and expression.

Each of our kits is carefully curated with premium materials: European linen fabrics, colorfast threads that won't bleed, and needles that glide smoothly through the fabric. Our patterns are all original designs created by our team of talented artists.

We take pride in the fact that our kits are complete - no need to hunt for additional supplies. Everything you need comes in one beautiful package, ready to inspire your next creative journey.
