Our Story

Founded in 2020, Prelixa began as a small passion project between two friends who shared a vision for clean, effective skincare that respected both people and the planet. Frustrated by the lack of truly natural options that delivered real results, we set out to create our own.

What started as handmade batches in a home kitchen has grown into a trusted brand loved by thousands, but our core values remain the same. Every product we create is still formulated with the same care and attention to detail as those first small batches.

Today, we're proud to be a certified B Corporation, meeting the highest standards of social and environmental performance. Our products are carried in boutique stores across Europe, but our heart remains in our small workshop where innovation and quality come first.

Our Values

Purity First

We use only the purest natural ingredients, free from harmful chemicals, synthetic fragrances, and questionable additives.

Cruelty-Free

We never test on animals and are proud to be certified by Leaping Bunny and PETA. All our products are 100% vegan.

Sustainable Sourcing

We carefully select suppliers who share our commitment to ethical and sustainable practices, supporting local communities.

Eco-Conscious

From biodegradable packaging to carbon-neutral shipping, we minimize our environmental impact at every step.

Farm to Face

We prioritize organic, wild-harvested, and fair-trade ingredients that are as close to their natural state as possible.

Community Focus

We donate 5% of profits to environmental causes and partner with organizations promoting clean beauty education.

Our Sustainability Promise

At Prelixa, we believe beauty shouldn't cost the earth. That's why we've built sustainability into every aspect of our business:

  • Carbon Neutral Operations: We offset 100% of our carbon emissions through verified projects
  • Zero Waste Packaging: All our packaging is recyclable, biodegradable, or compostable
  • Ethical Sourcing: We trace every ingredient to its source to ensure fair wages and working conditions
  • Water Conservation: Our production methods use 60% less water than conventional skincare manufacturing

We're constantly innovating to reduce our environmental impact further. Our goal is to be fully circular by 2027, with all packaging returned, refilled, or regenerated.
