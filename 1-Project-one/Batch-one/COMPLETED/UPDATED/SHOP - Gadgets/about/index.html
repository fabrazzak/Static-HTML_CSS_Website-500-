Our Story

Discover the passion behind Digitronix Shop

Who We Are

Founded in 2015, Digitronix Shop began as a small tech repair shop in Berlin with a big vision - to make cutting-edge technology accessible to everyone. What started as a passion project between three university friends has now grown into one of Germany's most trusted tech retailers.

Today, we operate 12 physical stores across Germany and serve customers worldwide through our online store. Despite our growth, we've maintained our commitment to personal service, expert advice, and competitive pricing.
