Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

We offer several shipping options to meet your needs:

  • Standard Shipping: €4.99 (3-5 business days)
  • Express Shipping: €9.99 (1-2 business days)
  • Free Shipping: On all orders over €50

International shipping rates vary by destination. You'll see the exact shipping cost at checkout.

We accept returns within 30 days of purchase for a full refund or exchange. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and with all original tags attached.

To initiate a return:

  1. Log in to your account
  2. Go to "Order History"
  3. Select the item(s) you wish to return
  4. Print the prepaid return label

Refunds are processed within 3-5 business days after we receive your return.

Once your order has shipped, you'll receive a confirmation email with a tracking number and link to track your package.

You can also track your order by:

  1. Logging into your account
  2. Going to "Order History"
  3. Clicking on "Track Package" next to your order

If you're having trouble tracking your order, please contact our customer service team.

Please note:

  • Delivery times vary by destination (typically 7-14 business days)

  • All prices on our site are in Euros (€)

We process orders quickly to ensure fast delivery, so changes or cancellations must be requested within 1 hour of placing your order.

To request a change or cancellation:

  1. Email us immediately at support@chicstack.net
  2. Include your order number and requested changes

If your order has already shipped, you can return it following our standard return policy.
