Orders & Payments

Placing an order with ChicStack is easy:

  1. Browse our collections and select your favorite items
  2. Choose your size and click "Add to Bag"
  3. When ready, click the shopping bag icon to review your items
  4. Proceed to checkout and enter your shipping and payment details
  5. Review your order and click "Place Order"

You'll receive an order confirmation email with all the details.

We accept the following payment methods:

  • All major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express)
  • PayPal
  • Apple Pay
  • Google Pay
  • ChicStack Gift Cards

All payments are processed securely through our encrypted checkout system.

Once your order has shipped, you'll receive a shipping confirmation email with a tracking number and link.

You can also track your order by:

  1. Logging into your account
  2. Going to "Order History"
  3. Clicking on "Track Package" next to your order

If you're having trouble tracking your order, please contact our customer service team.

Shipping & Delivery

We offer several shipping options to meet your needs:

  • Standard Shipping: €4.99 (3-5 business days)
  • Express Shipping: €9.99 (1-2 business days)
  • Free Shipping: On all orders over €50

International shipping rates vary by destination. You'll see the exact shipping cost at checkout.

Yes! We ship to over 100 countries worldwide. International shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on your location and the weight of your order.

Please note:

  • Delivery times vary by destination (typically 7-14 business days)
  • Customs fees may apply depending on your country's regulations
  • All prices on our site are in Euros (€)

Shipping times depend on your location and the shipping method selected:

  • Standard Shipping: 3-5 business days (France), 5-10 business days (Europe), 7-14 business days (International)
  • Express Shipping: 1-2 business days (France), 2-5 business days (Europe), 3-7 business days (International)

Processing time is 1-2 business days for all orders. You'll receive a shipping confirmation email when your order is on its way.

Returns & Exchanges

We accept returns within 30 days of purchase for a full refund or exchange. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and with all original tags attached.

To initiate a return:

  1. Log in to your account
  2. Go to "Order History"
  3. Select the item(s) you wish to return
  4. Print the prepaid return label

Refunds are processed within 3-5 business days after we receive your return.

To exchange an item:

  1. Initiate a return as described above
  2. Once we receive your return, you'll receive store credit for the value of the returned item
  3. Place a new order for the item you'd like instead

Alternatively, you can return the item for a refund and place a completely new order.

Note: We currently don't offer direct exchanges where we ship the new item before receiving the return.

Return processing typically takes 5-7 business days after we receive your package. Here's the timeline:

  • 1-3 days for your return package to reach our warehouse
  • 1-2 days for us to process the return
  • 3-5 business days for the refund to appear in your account

You'll receive email notifications at each step of the process.

Products & Sizing

We provide detailed size guides for all our products to help you find the perfect fit:

  1. On any product page, click the "Size Guide" link below the size options
  2. Compare your measurements with our size chart
  3. If you're between sizes, we recommend sizing up

For specific fit information (like "runs small" or "runs large"), check the product description or customer reviews.

Still unsure? Contact our customer service team for personalized sizing advice.

Most of our products are true to size, but fit can vary by style. Here's what to consider:

  • Dresses & Tops: Generally true to size
  • Pants & Jeans: Some styles may run small, especially skinny fits
  • Outerwear: Designed to fit over other clothing, so may feel roomy
  • Accessories: One-size-fits-most with adjustable features

We always note in the product description if an item runs particularly large or small.

To keep your ChicStack items looking their best:

  • Always check the care label first
  • Wash similar colors together in cold water
  • Use mild detergent and avoid bleach
  • Line dry or tumble dry on low heat
  • Iron on appropriate heat setting if needed
  • Store folded or on padded hangers to maintain shape

Specific care instructions are provided with each product.
