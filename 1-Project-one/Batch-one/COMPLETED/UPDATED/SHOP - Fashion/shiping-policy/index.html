Processing Time

All orders are processed within 1-2 business days (excluding weekends and holidays) after receiving your order confirmation. You will receive a shipping confirmation email with tracking information once your order has shipped.

Please note:

  • Orders placed after 2pm CET will be processed the next business day
  • During peak seasons (November-January), processing may take 2-3 business days
  • Custom or personalized items may require additional processing time (will be noted on product page)

International Shipping

We ship to over 100 countries worldwide. International shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on your location and order weight.

Estimated International Delivery Times:

  • Europe: 5-10 business days
  • North America: 7-14 business days
  • Asia/Pacific: 10-18 business days
  • Middle East/Africa: 12-20 business days

Please note:

  • Customs fees may apply depending on your country's regulations
  • All prices on our site are in Euros (€)
  • Delivery times may vary due to customs processing

Order Tracking

Once your order has shipped, you'll receive a shipping confirmation email with a tracking number and link to track your package.

You can also track your order by:

  1. Logging into your account
  2. Going to "Order History"
  3. Clicking on "Track Package" next to your order

If you're having trouble tracking your order, please contact our customer service team.

Shipping Restrictions

Some items cannot be shipped internationally due to manufacturer restrictions. These items will be marked on the product page.

We currently cannot ship to PO boxes or military addresses.

Undeliverable Packages

If a package is returned to us as undeliverable (due to incorrect address, failed delivery attempts, or unclaimed package), we will contact you to arrange reshipment. Additional shipping fees may apply.

To avoid undeliverable packages:

  • Double-check your shipping address before submitting your order
  • Ensure someone is available to receive the package
  • Track your package and follow up with the carrier if needed
