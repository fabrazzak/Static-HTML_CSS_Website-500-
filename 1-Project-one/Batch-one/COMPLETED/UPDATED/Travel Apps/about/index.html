About VacayHub

Discover the story behind your ultimate travel companion

Our Story

Founded in 2018 by a group of passionate travelers and tech enthusiasts, VacayHub was born out of frustration with the fragmented travel planning experience. We noticed that travelers were spending more time organizing their trips than actually enjoying them, jumping between dozens of apps and websites.

Our mission was clear: create a single platform that seamlessly integrates all aspects of travel planning while leveraging technology to provide personalized, intelligent recommendations that go beyond generic suggestions.

Today, VacayHub serves over 500,000 travelers across Europe, with plans to expand globally. We're proud to have helped our users discover new destinations, save money, and create unforgettable travel memories.

Our Mission & Values

Simplify Travel

We believe travel planning should be enjoyable, not stressful. Our tools remove complexity so you can focus on the excitement of your trip.

Innovate Constantly

We're committed to pushing boundaries in travel tech, developing features that anticipate and solve traveler needs before they arise.

Travel Responsibly

We promote sustainable travel practices and partner with businesses that prioritize environmental and social responsibility.

Community First

Our platform thrives on real traveler insights. We amplify the collective wisdom of our community to benefit all users.
