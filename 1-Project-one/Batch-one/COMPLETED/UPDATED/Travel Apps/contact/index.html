Frequently Asked Questions

How do I download the VacayHub app?

VacayHub is available for both iOS and Android devices. You can download it from the App Store or Google Play Store by searching for "VacayHub". Alternatively, visit our homepage and click the "Download App" button for direct links.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, and in some countries, local payment methods. All transactions are securely processed with bank-level encryption.

Can I modify or cancel my booking?

Modification and cancellation policies vary by service provider. You can check the specific terms for your booking in the "My Trips" section of your account. Many hotel and activity bookings offer free cancellation within a certain period.

How does the price guarantee work?

If you find the same booking (same dates, same conditions) at a lower price within 24 hours of booking with us, we'll match the difference. Simply contact our customer support with proof of the lower price.
