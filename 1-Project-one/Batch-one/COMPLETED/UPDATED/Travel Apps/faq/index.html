Account & Profile

How do I create a VacayHub account?

You can create a VacayHub account either through our website or mobile app. Click on "Sign Up" and follow the prompts to register using your email address or social media accounts. You'll need to verify your email to complete the registration process.

Can I merge multiple accounts?

Currently, we don't support merging multiple accounts. We recommend choosing one account to use and contacting our support team to transfer any bookings or information from other accounts.

How do I update my profile information?

You can update your profile information by going to "Account Settings" in the app or website. From there, you can edit your personal details, profile picture, and preferences. Remember to save your changes before exiting.

Bookings & Reservations

How do I modify or cancel a booking?

To modify or cancel a booking, go to "My Trips" in your account, select the booking you want to change, and follow the prompts. Note that modification and cancellation policies vary by service provider and may incur fees depending on the terms of your booking.

Will I receive a confirmation for my booking?

Yes, you'll receive an email confirmation immediately after completing your booking. You can also find all your booking details in the "My Trips" section of your VacayHub account. We recommend keeping your confirmation email until your trip is completed.

What happens if my flight is cancelled?

If your flight is cancelled by the airline, please contact our customer support immediately. We'll work with the airline to rebook you on the next available flight or process a refund according to the airline's policy and your fare conditions.

Payments & Pricing

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and in some countries, local payment methods. All transactions are securely processed with bank-level encryption.

Why was I charged more than the listed price?

The final price may include taxes, fees, or other mandatory charges that weren't included in the initial displayed price. These can vary by destination and service provider. You'll always see the full breakdown of charges before confirming your booking.

How does your price guarantee work?

If you find the same booking (same dates, same conditions) at a lower price within 24 hours of booking with us, we'll match the difference. Simply contact our customer support with proof of the lower price (screenshot or link) and we'll process the refund.

App Features

Is the VacayHub app available in my country?

The VacayHub app is currently available in most European countries, the United States, Canada, Australia, and select Asian countries. You can check availability in your region by visiting your device's app store or our website.

How do I use the itinerary planner feature?

To use the itinerary planner, go to the "Plan" tab in the app, select your destination and dates, and start adding activities. The app will suggest popular attractions, restaurants, and experiences based on your preferences and can optimize your schedule for efficiency.

Can I access my bookings offline?

Yes, you can save your bookings and itineraries for offline access. In the app, go to "My Trips," select the booking you want to save, and tap the download icon. This will save all relevant details to your device for access without an internet connection.

Travel Planning

How far in advance should I book my trip?

For flights, we recommend booking 2-3 months in advance for domestic trips and 3-5 months for international trips. Hotels can often be booked closer to your travel date, but popular destinations may require 3-6 months advance booking, especially during peak seasons.

Does VacayHub offer travel insurance?

Yes, we offer comprehensive travel insurance options that you can add during the booking process or purchase separately. Our insurance covers trip cancellation, medical emergencies, lost luggage, and other common travel concerns.

Can VacayHub help with visa requirements?

While we don't process visa applications directly, our app provides up-to-date information on visa requirements for your destination based on your nationality. We can also connect you with trusted visa processing partners if you need assistance with your application.

Safety & Security

How does VacayHub protect my personal information?

We use industry-standard encryption for all data transmission and storage. Your payment information is processed through PCI-compliant systems, and we never store your full credit card details on our servers. For more details, please see our Privacy Policy.

What safety features does the app offer for solo travelers?

Our app includes several safety features for solo travelers, including real-time location sharing with trusted contacts, emergency contact integration, safe neighborhood indicators, and 24/7 access to our customer support team for assistance.

What should I do if I suspect fraudulent activity on my account?

If you notice any suspicious activity, immediately change your password and contact our customer support team. We'll investigate the issue and may temporarily freeze your account to prevent further unauthorized access while we resolve the situation.
