About Timeout Ticker

Your trusted source for comprehensive basketball coverage since 2015

Our Story

Founded in 2015 by a group of basketball enthusiasts, Timeout Ticker began as a small blog covering local college basketball games. What started as a passion project quickly grew into one of the most trusted sources for NBA news and analysis.

Today, we serve millions of basketball fans worldwide with up-to-the-minute news, in-depth statistics, and expert commentary on all things NBA, WNBA, and international basketball.

Our Mission

At Timeout Ticker, we're committed to delivering accurate, timely, and insightful basketball coverage that goes beyond the box score. We believe basketball is more than just a game - it's a culture, a community, and a way of life.

  • Deliver advanced statistics in an accessible format
  • Publish original reporting and analysis
  • Foster an engaged basketball community
