Getting Started

Signing up is easy! Just visit our Services page and click on the "Get Started" button for the plan that suits you best. You'll be guided through a simple registration process where you'll create your account and provide basic information about your hockey profile.

For basic features, you just need a smartphone (iOS or Android). For advanced tracking features, we recommend our proprietary Hockey Hype Tracker that attaches to your stick or helmet. Some features also work with popular wearable devices like Apple Watch or Garmin.

Yes! We offer a 14-day free trial for all our premium plans. No credit card is required to start the trial. You'll have full access to all features during this period to evaluate if Hockey Hype App is right for you.

Account

If you've forgotten your password, go to the login page and click "Forgot Password." Enter the email address associated with your account, and we'll send you a link to reset your password. The link will be valid for 24 hours.

Yes, you can change the email address associated with your account. Go to Account Settings > Personal Information and update your email address. You'll need to verify the new email address before it becomes active.

Features

Our performance tracking uses a combination of motion sensors and computer vision technology to analyze your game. The Hockey Hype Tracker captures data on skating speed, shot power, shift length, and more. This data is synced to your account where our algorithms process it into actionable insights.

Yes, the app can record your session data offline when you're on the ice. The next time you have an internet connection, the app will automatically sync your data to the cloud. Some features like video analysis require internet connection to function.

Our analytics have been validated against professional-grade systems and show 95-98% accuracy for most metrics. Skating speed is accurate to within 0.2 km/h, and shot speed is accurate to within 3 km/h. The system becomes more accurate as it learns your specific movement patterns.
