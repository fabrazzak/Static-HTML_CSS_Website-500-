About Hockey Hype App

Revolutionizing hockey performance through technology and innovation

Our Story

Founded in 2018 by former professional hockey player Mikael Koskinen and sports technologist Emma Virtanen, Hockey Hype App began as a passion project to solve the data gap in amateur hockey development.

Frustrated by the lack of accessible performance metrics outside elite professional environments, our founders set out to create affordable, user-friendly tools that would democratize hockey analytics.

What started as a simple stats tracker has grown into a comprehensive platform used by thousands of players, coaches, and teams across Europe and North America.

Our Mission & Values

Mission

To empower hockey players at all levels with professional-grade performance tools that accelerate development, prevent injuries, and deepen their love for the game.

Innovation

We constantly push boundaries to develop new ways to measure, analyze, and improve hockey performance.

Community

We believe hockey thrives when knowledge is shared, which is why we foster connections between players, coaches, and fans.

Integrity

We're committed to ethical data practices and transparent communication with our users.
