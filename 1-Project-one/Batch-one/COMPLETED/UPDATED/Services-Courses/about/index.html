Our Story

How Tech Train Hub began its journey

Founded in 2018, Tech Train Hub started as a small team of passionate educators and industry professionals who saw a gap in quality, affordable tech education. What began as weekend coding workshops has grown into a platform serving over 500,000 learners worldwide.

Our mission is simple: to make cutting-edge tech education accessible to everyone, regardless of background or location. We believe in learning by doing, which is why all our courses emphasize hands-on projects and real-world applications.
