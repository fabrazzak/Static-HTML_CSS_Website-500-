Our Story

How MatchDay Digest became your premier soccer news source

Founded in 2015 by a group of passionate football enthusiasts and professional journalists, MatchDay Digest began as a small blog covering local matches in London. What started as a hobby quickly grew into a respected platform for soccer news and analysis.

Our breakthrough came in 2018 when we were the first to report on a major Premier League transfer, earning recognition from major sports networks. Today, we have a team of 25 dedicated professionals covering all major leagues across Europe and beyond.
