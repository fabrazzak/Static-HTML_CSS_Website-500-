Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about the application process

What are the prerequisites?

No specific prerequisites are required. Our programs are designed for learners at all levels. A passion for learning and commitment to personal growth are the most important factors.

Is financial aid available?

Yes, we offer various payment plans and scholarship opportunities. Contact our admissions team to discuss options that fit your financial situation.

Can I change programs after enrolling?

Yes, you can switch between Intensive and Flexible programs within the first two weeks of the course, subject to availability and schedule compatibility.

What if I miss the interview?

If you can't attend your scheduled interview, simply contact us to reschedule. We offer flexible interview times to accommodate different time zones.
