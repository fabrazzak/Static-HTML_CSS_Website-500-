Cookies Policy

Learn how CloudSkillForge uses cookies and similar technologies to enhance your learning experience and improve our services.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit our website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and provide information to the website owners.

Important: Cookies cannot harm your computer and do not contain viruses. We use cookies to improve your browsing experience and to help us understand how you use our website.

2. How We Use Cookies

CloudSkillForge uses cookies for several purposes, including:

  • Remembering your preferences and settings
  • Keeping you signed in to your account
  • Tracking your learning progress and course completion
  • Understanding how you interact with our courses
  • Improving website performance and user experience
  • Providing personalized content and recommendations
  • Analyzing website traffic and usage patterns

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. They enable core functionality such as security, network management, and accessibility.

  • Session management
  • Security features
  • Load balancing
  • User authentication

Performance Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting and reporting information anonymously.

  • Website analytics
  • Page load times
  • Error tracking
  • User behavior analysis

Functional Cookies

These cookies enable the website to provide enhanced functionality and personalization based on your preferences.

  • Language preferences
  • Course progress tracking
  • Theme selection
  • Customized learning paths

4. Third-Party Cookies

In addition to our own cookies, we may also use various third-party cookies to report usage statistics of the service, deliver advertisements on and through the service, and so on.

4.1 Analytics Partners

We use analytics services to help us understand how users engage with our platform. These services may use cookies to collect information about your use of our services.

4.2 Advertising Partners

We work with advertising partners to show you relevant ads for our courses. These partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to our website.

4.3 Social Media Cookies

These cookies are used when you share information using a social media sharing button or "like" button on our site, or you link your account or engage with our content on social media platforms.

5. Cookie Duration

Cookies can remain on your device for different periods of time:

  • Session Cookies: These are temporary cookies that remain on your device until you close your web browser.
  • Persistent Cookies: These remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them. They help us recognize you when you return to our website.

The specific duration for each cookie varies, but most persistent cookies we use expire within 1-2 years from the date they are set.

7. Do Not Track Signals

Some browsers have a "Do Not Track" feature that lets you tell websites that you do not want to have your online activities tracked. Our website does not currently respond to "Do Not Track" signals as there is no standard for how websites should respond to such signals.

8. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, regulation, or our services. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we use cookies.
