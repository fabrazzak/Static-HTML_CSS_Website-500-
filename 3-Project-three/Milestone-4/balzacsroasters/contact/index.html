Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping take?

We typically process and ship orders within 1-2 business days. Standard shipping within Canada takes 3-7 business days. Express shipping options are available at checkout for faster delivery. During peak seasons, there may be slight delays.

Do you offer wholesale pricing?

Yes! We offer competitive wholesale pricing for cafes, restaurants, and businesses. Please select "Wholesale Partnership" as your subject in the contact form, and our wholesale team will reach out to discuss your needs and provide pricing information.

What is your return policy?

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. You may return unopened coffee bags within 30 days of purchase for a full refund. If you receive a damaged or defective product, please contact us within 7 days of delivery for a replacement or refund.

Do you ship internationally?

Currently, we only ship within Canada. We're working on expanding our shipping options to include international destinations. Please check back with us or sign up for our newsletter to be notified when international shipping becomes available.

How should I store my coffee beans?

For optimal freshness, store your coffee beans in an airtight container in a cool, dark place. Avoid storing coffee in the refrigerator or freezer as moisture and temperature fluctuations can affect the flavor. Whole beans stay fresh for about 2-3 weeks after opening when stored properly.
