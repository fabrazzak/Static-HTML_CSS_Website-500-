Our Story

From a small roastery in Sunnyside to becoming one of Canada's most beloved coffee brands, discover the passion and dedication behind every cup of Balzac's coffee.

Our Journey

Crafting Excellence Since 1993

Balzac's Coffee Roasters began as a dream in a small garage in Sunnyside, Manitoba. Our founder, Jacques Balzac, a third-generation coffee roaster from France, believed that Canadians deserved access to exceptional, ethically sourced coffee.

What started as a passion project quickly grew into a community staple. Jacques' commitment to quality and his innovative roasting techniques soon caught the attention of coffee enthusiasts across the province.

Today, while we've grown beyond that humble garage, we've never lost sight of our roots. Every batch of coffee we roast carries the same dedication to quality, sustainability, and community that Jacques instilled in our company from day one.

Our Commitment

What Drives Us

At the heart of everything we do are three core principles that guide our decisions, our roasting, and our relationships.

Uncompromising Quality

We believe that exceptional coffee starts with exceptional beans. That's why we travel the world to source only the finest, ethically grown coffee beans, then roast them in small batches to ensure peak freshness and flavor.

Sustainable Practices

From farm to cup, we're committed to sustainable and ethical practices. We work directly with farmers who share our values, ensuring fair wages and environmentally responsible farming methods.

Community Connection

Coffee brings people together. We're proud to be part of communities across Canada, creating spaces where people can connect, share stories, and build relationships over great coffee.

Our Craft

The Art of Coffee Roasting

Discover the meticulous process that transforms green coffee beans into the rich, flavorful coffee you love.

Careful Selection

We sample hundreds of coffee lots each year, selecting only the top 2% that meet our strict quality standards for flavor profile and ethical sourcing.

Precision Weighing

Each batch is carefully weighed to ensure consistency and optimal roasting conditions for that specific bean variety and origin.

Artisan Roasting

Our master roasters use their expertise to develop each bean's unique characteristics, carefully monitoring temperature and time.

Rapid Cooling

Immediately after roasting, beans are rapidly cooled to lock in flavors and prevent over-roasting, preserving the delicate aromas and tastes.

Cupping & Quality Control

Every batch undergoes rigorous tasting and quality control to ensure it meets our standards before being approved for packaging.

Fresh Packaging

We package our coffee in specially designed bags with one-way valves to preserve freshness and ensure you receive coffee at its peak.
