Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer international shipping?

Currently, we only ship within Canada. We're working on expanding our shipping options to include international destinations in the near future.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day return policy for unused items in original packaging. Outdoor equipment that has been used may be subject to different return conditions. Please contact us for specific details.

Do you price match?

Yes, we offer price matching on identical items from authorized Canadian retailers. Contact us with the competitor's details and we'll be happy to assist you.

Can I reserve products for in-store pickup?

Absolutely! You can place an order online and select in-store pickup. We'll hold your items for 7 days. You'll receive a notification when your order is ready for pickup.
