Cookies Policy

Learn how we use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your browsing experience

Last Updated: October 05, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to True Outdoors Gear's Cookies Policy. This policy explains how we use cookies and similar tracking technologies when you visit our website trueoutdoorsgear.com (the "Site"). This policy should be read alongside our Privacy Policy, which provides more detailed information about how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

By using our Site, you consent to the use of cookies and similar technologies as described in this policy, unless you disable them through your browser settings or using our cookie management tools. We are committed to being transparent about our use of cookies and providing you with meaningful choices about your privacy.

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device when you visit our website. They help us provide you with a better experience by remembering your preferences, understanding how you use our site, and showing you relevant content.

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and provide information to the website owners. Cookies allow a website to recognize a user's device and remember their actions and preferences over time.

Cookies can be set by the website you are visiting (first-party cookies) or by third parties, such as those who serve content or provide advertising or analytics services on the website (third-party cookies). They can remain on your device for different periods of time:

Cookie Duration Types:

  • Session Cookies: Temporary cookies that remain on your device until you close your web browser
  • Persistent Cookies: Remain on your device for a set period of time or until you delete them
  • First-Party Cookies: Set by the website you are visiting
  • Third-Party Cookies: Set by a domain other than the one you are visiting

In addition to cookies, we may use other similar tracking technologies such as web beacons, pixels, and local storage. These technologies work similarly to cookies and allow us to store information on your device to enhance your browsing experience.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

We use different types of cookies for various purposes, as described below:

Essential Cookies
Purpose: Necessary for the website to function properly
Duration: Session or up to 24 months

These cookies are strictly necessary to provide you with services available through our Site and to use some of its features, such as access to secure areas. Because these cookies are strictly necessary to deliver the Site, you cannot refuse them without impacting how our Site functions.

Performance and Analytics Cookies
Purpose: Help us understand how visitors interact with our website
Duration: Up to 24 months

These cookies allow us to count visits and traffic sources so we can measure and improve the performance of our Site. They help us know which pages are the most and least popular and see how visitors move around the Site. All information these cookies collect is aggregated and therefore anonymous.

Functionality Cookies
Purpose: Enable enhanced functionality and personalization
Duration: Up to 12 months

These cookies are used to recognize you when you return to our Site. They enable us to personalize our content for you, greet you by name, and remember your preferences (for example, your choice of language or region). They may also be used to provide services you have asked for such as watching a video.

Targeting and Advertising Cookies
Purpose: Make advertising more relevant to you and your interests
Duration: Up to 12 months

These cookies may be set through our Site by our advertising partners. They may be used by those companies to build a profile of your interests and show you relevant advertisements on other sites. They do not store directly personal information but are based on uniquely identifying your browser and internet device.

Social Media Cookies
Purpose: Enable you to share content with social networks
Duration: Up to 12 months

These cookies are set by a range of social media services that we have added to the Site to enable you to share our content with your friends and networks. They are capable of tracking your browser across other sites and building up a profile of your interests.

4. Why We Use Cookies

We use cookies for several important reasons that enhance your experience on our Site:

Primary Purposes:

  • Site Functionality: To enable basic functions like page navigation and access to secure areas of the website
  • User Preferences: To remember your preferences and settings, such as language, currency, and login information
  • Shopping Cart: To maintain your shopping cart contents and remember your selections during the checkout process
  • Site Performance: To understand how visitors interact with our Site and identify areas for improvement
  • Personalization: To provide you with personalized content and recommendations based on your browsing behavior
  • Security: To protect your account and prevent fraudulent activities
  • Marketing: To deliver relevant advertisements and measure the effectiveness of our marketing campaigns

Without these cookies, the services that you have asked for cannot be provided, and we only use these cookies to provide you with those services. Some cookies also help us understand how our Site is performing and where we can make improvements to enhance your experience.

We use both session cookies (which expire once you close your web browser) and persistent cookies (which stay on your device until you delete them or they expire) to provide you with a more personal and interactive experience on our Site.

5. Third-Party Cookies

In addition to our own cookies, we may also use various third-party cookies to report usage statistics of the Site, deliver advertisements on and through the Site, and so on. These third-party services may place their own cookies on your device when you visit our Site.

Google Analytics: We use Google Analytics to understand how our visitors engage with our Site. Google Analytics collects information anonymously and reports website trends without identifying individual visitors. You can learn more about Google Analytics and opt out of it using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Social Media Platforms: We use social media cookies from platforms like Facebook, Instagram, and Twitter to enable social sharing functionality and to track the effectiveness of our social media marketing campaigns.

Advertising Networks: We work with advertising partners to serve you ads that may be relevant to you and your interests. These advertising partners may use cookies to collect information about your activities on this and other websites to provide you with targeted advertising.

Payment Processors: When you make a purchase on our Site, our payment processors may set cookies to ensure the security and proper functioning of the payment process.

Please note that we do not control these third-party cookies and their use is governed by the privacy policies of the third parties placing them. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these third parties to understand their practices.

6. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You have several options to control and manage cookies on your device. Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings preferences. However, if you limit the ability of websites to set cookies, you may worsen your overall user experience, since it will no longer be personalized to you.

How to Manage Cookies:

  1. Browser Settings: Most browsers allow you to refuse to accept cookies and to delete cookies. The methods for doing so vary from browser to browser, and from version to version. You can however obtain up-to-date information about blocking and deleting cookies via these links:
    • Google Chrome
    • Mozilla Firefox
    • Apple Safari
    • Microsoft Edge
  2. Cookie Consent Tool: When you first visit our Site, you will be presented with a cookie banner that allows you to accept or reject non-essential cookies. You can change your preferences at any time by clicking on the "Cookie Settings" link in the footer of our website.
  3. Opt-Out Tools: For third-party cookies used for advertising purposes, you can opt out through the following services:
    • Digital Advertising Alliance (DAA) opt-out program
    • Network Advertising Initiative (NAI) opt-out program
    • European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance (EDAA) opt-out program
  4. Do Not Track: Some browsers have a "Do Not Track" feature that lets you tell websites that you do not want to have your online activities tracked. At this time, we do not respond to browser "Do Not Track" signals.

Please note that if you choose to disable cookies, some features of our Site may not function properly. For example, you may not be able to add items to your shopping cart, proceed to checkout, or use any of our services that require you to sign in.
