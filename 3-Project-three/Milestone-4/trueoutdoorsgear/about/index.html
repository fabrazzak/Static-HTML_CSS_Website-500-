From Passion to Purpose

How a shared love for the outdoors grew into Mississauga's premier outdoor equipment destination

Founded on Adventure

True Outdoors Gear began in 2010 when founders Sarah and Michael Chen returned from a challenging backcountry camping trip in Algonquin Park. Frustrated with the lack of quality gear available locally, they envisioned creating a space where outdoor enthusiasts could find premium equipment and expert advice.

What started as a small 800-square-foot store in central Mississauga has grown into a 5,000-square-foot outdoor gear haven, serving adventurers from across the Greater Toronto Area and beyond.

Today, we're proud to be the trusted outdoor equipment provider for thousands of camping enthusiasts, hikers, and adventure seekers. Our team of experienced outdoor experts has collectively logged over 500 camping nights and countless kilometers on trails across Ontario.

Our Values

The principles that guide everything we do at True Outdoors Gear

Quality First

We carefully select every product in our inventory, testing gear in real outdoor conditions to ensure it meets our high standards for durability and performance.

Expert Guidance

Our team consists of passionate outdoor enthusiasts who have firsthand experience with the gear we sell. We're here to share our knowledge and help you make the right choices.

Community Focus

We believe in building a strong outdoor community. From hosting camping workshops to supporting local conservation efforts, we're committed to giving back.

Sustainability

We're committed to protecting the natural spaces we love. We partner with eco-conscious brands and implement sustainable practices in our operations.

Exceptional Service

Your satisfaction is our priority. We stand behind every product we sell and are dedicated to providing outstanding customer service before, during, and after your purchase.

Adventure Ready

Whether you're planning a weekend camping trip or a month-long expedition, we'll make sure you have the right gear to make your adventure safe and memorable.

Our Mission
