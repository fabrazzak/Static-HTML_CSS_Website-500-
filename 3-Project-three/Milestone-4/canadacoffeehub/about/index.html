Our Coffee Journey

For over five decades, we've been passionate about bringing the finest coffee experiences to Canadians. From bean selection to your cup, excellence is our tradition.

Our Story

Roasting Excellence Since 1970

Canada Coffee Hub began as a small family-owned roastery in Merritt, British Columbia, with a simple mission: to source and roast the world's finest coffee beans for Canadian coffee lovers.

Founded by the Thompson family, our journey started with a single roaster and a commitment to quality that has stood the test of time. Through decades of perfecting our craft, we've grown from a local favorite to a respected name across Canada.

Today, while we've expanded our operations and product range, we remain true to our roots. Every batch of coffee is still roasted with the same care and attention to detail that defined our early years.

Our Passion for Quality

At Canada Coffee Hub, we believe that exceptional coffee begins with exceptional beans. That's why we travel across North America to source only the finest 100% Arabica beans from sustainable farms.

Our master roasters bring over 55 years of combined experience to every batch. They understand that each bean variety has its own unique characteristics that need to be carefully developed through precise roasting techniques.

From light roasts that highlight delicate floral notes to dark roasts with rich, chocolatey undertones, we craft each blend to perfection. Our commitment to quality extends beyond roasting to every aspect of our operation.

Our Journey

1970

Humble Beginnings

Founded as a small roastery in Merritt, BC with a single roaster and a dream to bring quality coffee to Canadian homes.

1985

Expansion

Opened our first retail location and expanded our roasting facility to meet growing demand across British Columbia.

1998

Organic Certification

Introduced our first line of certified organic and fair trade coffees, supporting sustainable farming practices.

2005

National Reach

Launched our e-commerce platform, making our premium coffees available to customers across Canada.

2015

Product Diversification

Expanded our offerings to include syrups, teas, and coffee equipment, becoming a complete coffee destination.

2025

Modern Innovation

Renovated our facility with state-of-the-art roasting technology while preserving our traditional craftsmanship.
