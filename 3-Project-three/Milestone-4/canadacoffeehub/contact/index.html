Frequently Asked Questions

What are your shipping options?

We offer standard shipping (3-7 business days) and express shipping (1-3 business days) across Canada. Shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on your location and order weight.

What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 30 days of purchase for unopened products in original packaging. Return shipping is the customer's responsibility. Refunds are processed to the original payment method.

Do you offer wholesale pricing?

Yes, we offer competitive wholesale pricing for cafes, restaurants, and businesses. Please contact us directly for wholesale inquiries and pricing information.

Are your coffee beans organic?

We offer both conventional and certified organic coffee options. Look for the organic label on our product pages, or contact us for more information about specific beans.
