Our Use of Cookies

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit our website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and provide information to the website owners.

Cookies help us understand how you interact with our website, remember your preferences, and provide you with a personalized experience. They do not contain any personal information that can be used to identify you, but they may contain anonymous unique identifiers.

Specific Cookies We Use

Here are some specific cookies you may encounter when using our website:

  • session_id: Essential for maintaining your shopping cart and login session
  • user_preferences: Stores your language, currency, and other display preferences
  • google_analytics: Helps us understand how you use our website to improve our services
  • facebook_pixel: Allows us to measure the effectiveness of our advertising
  • affiliate_tracking: Tracks referrals from our partner websites

We regularly review the cookies we use and update this list accordingly.

Cookie Controls

You have control over which cookies you accept. Use the options below to manage your cookie preferences:

Your Cookie Preferences

You can also manage cookies through your browser settings. Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings preferences. However, limiting cookies may impact your experience on our website.

Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies on our website are placed by third-party services that appear on our pages. We have no control over these third-party cookies, which may include:

  • Social media platforms (Facebook, Instagram, Twitter)
  • Analytics services (Google Analytics)
  • Advertising networks
  • Payment processors

These third parties have their own privacy policies and cookie policies. We recommend reviewing these policies to understand how they use your information.

Data Security

We take the security of your data seriously. All data collected through cookies is encrypted and stored securely. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized or unlawful processing, accidental loss, destruction, or damage.

Cookie data is anonymized where possible and is never shared with third parties for their own marketing purposes without your explicit consent.

Policy Updates

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legislation, or our operations. When we make significant changes, we will notify you through our website or by other means to give you the opportunity to review the changes before they become effective.

We encourage you to periodically review this page for the latest information on our cookie practices. Your continued use of our website after any changes to this policy will constitute your acceptance of such changes.
