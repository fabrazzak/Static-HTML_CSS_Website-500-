Roasting Excellence Since 1970

Canada Coffee Hub has been serving coffee enthusiasts across British Columbia and beyond for over five decades. Our journey began with a simple mission: to source and roast the finest coffee beans available.

Located in the heart of Merritt, BC, we've built our reputation on quality, consistency, and passion for the perfect cup. From our carefully selected 100% arabica beans to our expert roasting techniques, every step is designed to deliver an exceptional coffee experience.

Today, we offer over thirty varieties of premium coffee, organic options, specialty syrups, and professional equipment - everything you need to enjoy café-quality coffee at home.
