Welcome to Gaia Porto Stays

Our hostel is located in the Douro riverside in Gaia/Porto with a magnificent view of the historic city center. We offer the perfect blend of affordability and comfort in one of Portugal's most beautiful locations.

Just 10 minutes from Porto's historic center and near public transportation, our hostel provides easy access to all the attractions while offering a peaceful retreat with stunning river views.
