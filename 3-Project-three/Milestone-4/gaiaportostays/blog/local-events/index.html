Local Events & Festivals in Porto

Discover the exciting local events and festivals happening in Porto throughout the year

September 10, 2025 Admin 12 min read Local Culture

Porto is a city that knows how to celebrate. Throughout the year, the city comes alive with festivals, cultural events, and traditional celebrations that showcase the vibrant spirit of northern Portugal. From ancient religious processions to contemporary music festivals, there's always something happening in Porto.

As your home base in Gaia, we're perfectly positioned to help you experience the best of Porto's event calendar. Here's our guide to the must-see events and festivals throughout the year.

Festival of São João (St. John)

The Festival of São João on June 23-24 is Porto's biggest and most beloved celebration. What starts as a religious festival honoring St. John the Baptist transforms into an all-night street party that engulfs the entire city.

Festival of São João

An unforgettable night of dancing in the streets, hitting each other with plastic hammers, releasing hot air balloons, and grilling sardines over open fires.

June 23-24 annually
Citywide celebration
All night event
Mass participation

Don't Miss

The midnight fireworks display over the Douro River - one of the most spectacular in Portugal. The best viewing spots are along the river in both Porto and Gaia.

Fantasporto - International Fantasy Film Festival

Since 1981, Fantasporto has established itself as one of Europe's most important fantasy, horror, and science fiction film festivals. For two weeks each February/March, Porto becomes a hub for filmmakers and cinema enthusiasts from around the world.

Fantasporto Film Festival

An internationally recognized festival showcasing the best in fantasy, horror, and science fiction cinema with competitions, retrospectives, and special events.

February-March annually
Various cinemas in Porto
International films
Competitive sections

Serralves em Festa

For 48 continuous hours in early June, the Serralves Museum and its magnificent gardens host one of Europe's largest contemporary arts festivals. With free admission, the festival features hundreds of performances, installations, and activities across all artistic disciplines.

Serralves em Festa

A massive celebration of contemporary art with non-stop performances, exhibitions, workshops, and installations throughout the beautiful Serralves estate.

Early June annually
Serralves Museum & Gardens
Contemporary art focus
Free admission

Local Tip

Visit during both day and night to experience different aspects of the festival. The nighttime installations and performances are particularly magical.

Porto Jazz Festival

Each July, the Porto Jazz Festival brings world-class jazz musicians to various venues across the city. From intimate club performances to open-air concerts, the festival showcases both established stars and emerging talents in the jazz world.

Porto Jazz Festival

A celebration of jazz in all its forms, featuring international artists and local talents across multiple venues throughout Porto.

Throughout July
Various venues
Jazz performances
International artists

Seasonal Guide to Porto Events

By Season

Spring (March-May)

Spring brings cultural festivals and the beginning of outdoor events. The weather is mild, perfect for exploring the city and attending early season festivals.

Summer (June-August)

The peak festival season with São João, Serralves em Festa, and Porto Jazz Festival. Long days and warm weather make for perfect festival conditions.

Autumn (September-November)

Cultural events continue with film festivals, food events, and the beginning of the concert season. The Porto Beer Festival is a September highlight.

Winter (December-February)

Christmas markets and celebrations lead into Fantasporto in February. A quieter time for events but with magical holiday atmosphere.

Tips for Enjoying Porto's Events

Planning Ahead

For major festivals like São João, book accommodation well in advance as the city gets very busy. Check official festival websites for program details and ticket information.

Getting Around

During major events, public transport can be crowded. Consider walking between nearby venues - Porto's compact center is very walkable.

Local Cuisine

Many festivals feature special food offerings. Don't miss the grilled sardines during São João or the craft beers at the Porto Beer Festival.

From our location in Gaia, you have easy access to all of Porto's major events. The Dom Luís I Bridge provides a direct connection to the festival areas, and public transport runs frequently during major events. Our reception team stays updated on the latest event information and can help you plan your festival experiences.

Whether you're visiting for a specific festival or want to experience Porto's vibrant cultural scene, there's always something exciting happening in this dynamic city. The events and festivals not only provide entertainment but also offer unique insights into Portuguese culture and the local way of life.
