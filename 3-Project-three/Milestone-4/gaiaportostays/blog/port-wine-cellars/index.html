Top 5 Port Wine Cellars to Visit in Gaia

Discover the best Port wine cellars in Gaia with our exclusive guide and special discounts for our guests

September 15, 2025 Admin 8 min read Travel Tips

Gaia, the sister city of Porto across the Douro River, is world-renowned for its Port wine cellars. These historic establishments are where the famous fortified wine ages to perfection before being shipped around the globe. As your home base in Gaia, we've curated a list of the top 5 cellars you shouldn't miss during your stay.

From historic family-owned establishments to modern tasting experiences, each cellar offers a unique perspective on Portugal's most famous export. Many offer special discounts to our guests - just mention you're staying at Gaia Porto Stays!

1. Taylor's Port Cellars

Founded in 1692, Taylor's is one of the oldest and most respected Port wine houses. Their cellars in Vila Nova de Gaia offer an immersive experience that takes you through the history of Port wine production.

Taylor's Port Cellars

Experience the legacy of one of Port's most iconic producers with a comprehensive tour that includes tasting of their finest wines.

Founded in 1692
90-minute tours
From €15 per person
Rua do Choupelo 250

Special Offer for Our Guests

Show your Gaia Porto Stays room key and receive a complimentary upgrade to the premium tasting experience at no extra cost.

2. Sandeman Cellars

Instantly recognizable by the iconic Don logo, Sandeman offers one of the most modern and engaging cellar tours in Gaia. Their visitor center combines tradition with innovative multimedia presentations.

Sandeman Cellars

Discover the story behind the famous Don logo and taste a selection of their finest Ports in a contemporary setting with stunning river views.

Founded in 1790
60-minute tours
From €12 per person
Largo Miguel Bombarda 3

3. Graham's Lodge

Perched on the hill with breathtaking views over Porto, Graham's Lodge offers not only exceptional Port wines but also one of the best vantage points in Gaia. Their tour includes a visit to the historic lodge and a tasting in their elegant room.

Graham's Lodge

Enjoy spectacular views of Porto while learning about the Symington family's winemaking heritage and tasting their renowned Ports.

Founded in 1820
75-minute tours
From €20 per person
Rua do Agro 141

Special Offer for Our Guests

Mention Gaia Porto Stays when booking and receive a 15% discount on the Graham's Tawny Port tasting experience.

4. Cálem Cellars

Located right on the Gaia waterfront, Cálem offers one of the most accessible and popular cellar tours. Their experience includes an informative museum, cellar visit, and Fado music performance with tasting.

Cálem Cellars

Combine Port tasting with traditional Portuguese Fado music in this popular waterfront cellar that offers tours in multiple languages.

Founded in 1859
Various tour options
From €10 per person
Avenida Diogo Leite 344

5. Ferreira Cellars

As one of the few Portuguese-owned major Port houses, Ferreira has a special place in the hearts of locals. Their cellars offer a more intimate experience focused on Portuguese heritage and tradition.

Ferreira Cellars

Discover the story of Dona Antónia Adelaide Ferreira, the remarkable woman who built this iconic Portuguese brand, and taste their exceptional Ports.

Founded in 1751
60-minute tours
From €13 per person
Avenida Ramos Pinto 70

Special Offer for Our Guests

Planning Your Port Wine Tour

When visiting the Port wine cellars in Gaia, we recommend planning ahead:

  • Book in advance: Especially during peak season, tours can sell out quickly
  • Pace yourself: Consider visiting no more than 2-3 cellars in one day
  • Eat beforehand: Always have food in your stomach before wine tasting
  • Stay hydrated: Drink plenty of water between tastings
  • Ask questions: The guides are incredibly knowledgeable - make the most of their expertise

Most cellars are within walking distance from Gaia Porto Stays, making it easy to explore at your own pace. Our reception team would be happy to help you book tours and provide directions.

Whether you're a wine connoisseur or simply curious about this famous Portuguese tradition, visiting the Port wine cellars is an essential part of any trip to Porto and Gaia. Each offers a unique perspective on the history, production, and enjoyment of this remarkable fortified wine.
