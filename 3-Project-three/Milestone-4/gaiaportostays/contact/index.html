Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our hostel and services

What are your check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 2:00 PM, and check-out is by 11:00 AM. Early check-in and late check-out may be available upon request, subject to availability and additional charges.

Do you offer airport transfers?

While we don't provide airport transfers directly, we can help arrange reliable taxi or shuttle services from Porto Airport. The journey typically takes about 30 minutes depending on traffic.

Is breakfast included in the room rate?

Yes, a complimentary breakfast is included with all room bookings. We offer a variety of options including local specialties, fresh fruits, pastries, and beverages.

Do you have parking facilities?

While we don't have private parking, there are several public parking options nearby. We recommend the parking garage at General Torres Metro Station, just 200m from our hostel.

Are pets allowed in the hostel?

Unfortunately, we do not allow pets in the hostel with the exception of service animals. Please contact us in advance if you require accommodation for a service animal.
