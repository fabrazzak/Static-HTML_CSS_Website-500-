Frequently Asked Questions

What is your cancellation policy?

We offer free cancellation up to 48 hours before your scheduled arrival date. Cancellations made within 48 hours of arrival will be charged for the first night.

What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 2:00 PM, and check-out is by 11:00 AM. Early check-in and late check-out may be available upon request, subject to availability.

Do you offer airport transfers?

We don't provide airport transfers directly, but we can help arrange reliable taxi or shuttle services from Porto Airport. The journey typically takes about 30 minutes.

Is parking available?

While we don't have private parking, there are several public parking options nearby. We recommend the parking garage at General Torres Metro Station, just 200m from our hostel.

Do you have age restrictions?

Guests must be at least 18 years old to book a stay without parental supervision. Children are welcome when accompanied by adults.
