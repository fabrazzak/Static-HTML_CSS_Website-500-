Book your table for an unforgettable Parisian dining experience at Lou Cantou.

Make a Reservation

If you are more than 10 minutes late, we reserve the right to give away your table. Terrace tables cannot be reserved and are allocated upon arrival at the restaurant, subject to availability.

Reservation Policy

We hold reservations for 10 minutes past the booked time. After this, tables may be released to waiting guests.

Terrace seating is available on a first-come, first-served basis and cannot be reserved in advance.

For groups larger than 8, please contact us directly at 09 88 40 20 95.
