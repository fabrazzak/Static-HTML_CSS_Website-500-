Our Story

Welcome to Lou Cantou

Step into our charming Parisian bistro nestled in the heart of the 9th arrondissement. With its bougnat counter, wooden tables and chairs, benches, and vintage decor, Lou Cantou creates a timeless and friendly atmosphere that transports you to the authentic brasseries of old Paris.

Our menu celebrates home-style cooking that goes back to basics with simple and comforting local dishes, accompanied by a refined selection of wines from passionate winemakers. Located on a quiet street away from the hustle and bustle, we offer a large and pleasant terrace sheltered from traffic.

At Lou Cantou, we believe in the art of French culinary tradition, using fresh, local produce to create dishes that warm the soul and delight the palate.
