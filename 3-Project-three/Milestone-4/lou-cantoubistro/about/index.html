Our Heritage

A Taste of Authentic Paris

Founded in 2008, Lou Cantou emerged from a simple dream: to create a space where the authentic flavors of Paris could be celebrated in their purest form. Our name, "Lou Cantou," draws inspiration from the Provençal word for "hearth" or "fireside," reflecting our commitment to warmth, comfort, and traditional cooking.

Nestled in the charming 9th arrondissement, our bistro captures the essence of old Paris with its bougnat counter, wooden tables, vintage decor, and welcoming atmosphere that transports guests to a bygone era of French culinary excellence.

What began as a small neighborhood eatery has blossomed into a beloved destination for both Parisians and visitors seeking an authentic taste of French tradition, prepared with passion and served with genuine hospitality.

Our Philosophy

Our Journey

2008

Lou Cantou opens its doors in the 9th arrondissement, bringing authentic Parisian bistro cuisine to a quiet street near Rue de Provence.

2012

Expansion of our terrace allows for al fresco dining, creating one of the neighborhood's most charming outdoor spaces.

2015

Introduction of our curated wine program, featuring exclusive selections from small French vineyards and passionate winemakers.

2019

Renovation of our interior space preserves the vintage charm while enhancing comfort and ambiance for our guests.

2023

Celebration of 15 years of serving authentic French cuisine and becoming a beloved neighborhood institution.
