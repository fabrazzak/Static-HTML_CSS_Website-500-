Cookies Policy

Understand how Lou Cantou Bistro uses cookies to enhance your browsing experience and how you can control your cookie preferences.

1. Introduction to Cookies

At Lou Cantou Bistro, we believe in being transparent about how we collect and use data related to our website visitors. This Cookies Policy explains what cookies are, how we use them, the types of cookies we use, and how you can manage your cookie preferences.

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer, smartphone, or other device when you visit our website. They help us provide you with a better experience by remembering your preferences, understanding how you use our site, and showing you relevant content.

Important: By using our website, you consent to the use of cookies in accordance with this policy. You can manage your cookie preferences at any time using the controls provided on this page.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for various purposes to enhance your experience on our website:

Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. They enable basic functions like page navigation, secure areas of the website, and reservation processing. The website cannot function properly without these cookies.

Examples of how we use essential cookies:

  • Remembering your reservation details during the booking process
  • Maintaining your logged-in session if we implement user accounts
  • Ensuring the security of our website and your data
  • Load balancing to distribute website traffic across servers

Analytics Cookies

These cookies allow us to count visits and traffic sources so we can measure and improve the performance of our site. They help us know which pages are the most and least popular and see how visitors move around the site.

Examples of how we use analytics cookies:

  • Understanding how users navigate through our website
  • Identifying which pages and content are most engaging
  • Measuring the effectiveness of our online marketing
  • Improving website speed and performance based on user behavior

Marketing Cookies

These cookies may be set through our site by our advertising partners. They may be used by those companies to build a profile of your interests and show you relevant advertisements on other sites.

Examples of how we use marketing cookies:

  • Showing you personalized content based on your browsing history
  • Measuring the effectiveness of advertising campaigns
  • Limiting the number of times you see the same advertisement
  • Understanding user interests to improve future marketing

Preference Cookies

These cookies enable the website to provide enhanced functionality and personalization. They may be set by us or by third-party providers whose services we have added to our pages.

Examples of how we use preference cookies:

  • Remembering your language preferences
  • Storing your dietary preferences for future visits
  • Maintaining your custom display settings
  • Remembering if you've interacted with certain elements on our site

3. Third-Party Cookies

In addition to our own cookies, we may also use various third-party cookies to report usage statistics of the website, deliver advertisements on and through the website, and so on. These third-party services may place their own cookies on your device.

Examples of third-party services we use:

  • Google Analytics: Helps us understand how visitors engage with our website
  • Social Media Platforms: Allow you to share content from our website on social media
  • Payment Processors: Enable secure online transactions for reservations
  • Mapping Services: Provide interactive maps showing our location

These third-party services have their own privacy policies and cookie practices. We recommend that you review their policies to understand how they handle your information.

4. Cookie Duration

Cookies can remain on your device for different periods of time:

  • Session Cookies: These are temporary cookies that remain on your device until you close your web browser. They are often used to manage your visit to our website during a single browsing session.
  • Persistent Cookies: These remain on your device for a set period of time specified in the cookie (usually between a few days and two years). They are activated each time you visit the website that created that particular cookie.

The specific duration of each cookie depends on its purpose. Essential cookies typically have shorter durations, while analytics and marketing cookies may remain on your device for longer periods to track your preferences over time.

5. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You have several options to control how cookies are used on our website:

Cookie Preference Center

Use the toggles below to customize your cookie preferences:

Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function and cannot be switched off. They are usually only set in response to actions made by you such as setting your privacy preferences, logging in, or filling in forms.

Analytics Cookies

These cookies allow us to count visits and traffic sources so we can measure and improve the performance of our site. All information these cookies collect is aggregated and therefore anonymous.

Marketing Cookies

These cookies may be set through our site by our advertising partners. They may be used by those companies to build a profile of your interests and show you relevant advertisements on other sites.

Preference Cookies

These cookies enable the website to provide enhanced functionality and personalization. They may be set by us or by third-party providers whose services we have added to our pages.

Browser Controls: Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings preferences. However, limiting cookies may impact your experience on our website and others.

Opt-Out Tools: Various third parties offer opt-out tools to help you manage your cookie preferences for advertising and analytics cookies across multiple websites.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, regulation, or our business operations. We will post any changes on this page with an updated revision date.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we use cookies. Your continued use of our website after any changes indicates your acceptance of the updated policy.

If we make significant changes to how we use cookies, we will provide a more prominent notice, such as a banner on our website or an email notification for registered users.
