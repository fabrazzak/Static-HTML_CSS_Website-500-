Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to make a reservation?

While walk-ins are welcome, we highly recommend making a reservation, especially for weekend dining and larger parties. Reservations ensure we can provide you with the best possible experience and seating preference.

What is your cancellation policy?

We kindly request at least 24 hours' notice for cancellations. For parties of 6 or more, 48 hours' notice is required. Late cancellations may be subject to a fee, particularly during peak dining periods.

Do you accommodate dietary restrictions?

Yes, we're happy to accommodate dietary restrictions and allergies when possible. Please inform us of any dietary requirements when making your reservation, and our kitchen team will do their best to accommodate your needs.

Is there a dress code?

We maintain a smart casual dress code. While we want you to feel comfortable, we ask that guests avoid beachwear, athletic wear, or excessively casual clothing to maintain our restaurant's ambiance.

Do you offer private dining or events?

Yes, we offer private dining options for special events, business meetings, and celebrations. Please contact us directly to discuss availability, capacity, and customized menu options for your event.
