Cookies Policy

Learn how we use cookies and manage your privacy preferences

Last Updated: October 1, 2025

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit our website. They help us provide you with a better experience by remembering your preferences, understanding how you use our site, and showing you relevant content.

How Cookies Work

When you visit our website, we may store cookies on your browser. These cookies contain information that helps our website recognize your device and remember your preferences for future visits.

We use both session cookies (which expire when you close your browser) and persistent cookies (which remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them).

Types of Cookies We Use

We use different types of cookies for various purposes. Here's a breakdown of the cookies you might encounter on our website:

Essential Cookies Always Active

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. They enable basic functions like page navigation and access to secure areas of the website.

Examples: Shopping cart functionality, user authentication, security protection

Performance Cookies Optional

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting and reporting information anonymously.

Examples: Google Analytics, page visit tracking, performance monitoring

Functional Cookies Optional

These cookies enable the website to provide enhanced functionality and personalization based on your preferences.

Examples: Language preferences, region settings, customized content

Marketing Cookies Optional

These cookies are used to track visitors across websites to display relevant and engaging advertisements.

Examples: Facebook Pixel, Google Ads, retargeting campaigns

Important Note

Essential cookies cannot be disabled as they are necessary for the basic functionality of our website. You can manage your preferences for other cookie types using the settings below.

Third-Party Cookies

We work with third-party services that may also set cookies on your device when you visit our website. These include:

Analytics Services

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our website. This helps us improve our content and user experience.

Social Media

Social media platforms may set cookies when you interact with social sharing buttons or embedded content from platforms like Facebook, Instagram, and YouTube.

Payment Processors

When you make a purchase, our payment processors (like Stripe and PayPal) may set cookies to ensure secure transaction processing.

Privacy Protection

We carefully select third-party partners who respect your privacy and comply with data protection regulations. However, we recommend reviewing their privacy policies for detailed information about their cookie practices.

Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You have control over the cookies used on our website. Here are the different ways you can manage your preferences:

Browser Settings

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings preferences. However, limiting cookies may affect your experience on our website.

Deleting Cookies

You can delete existing cookies from your browser at any time. The method for doing so varies by browser, but you can usually find this option in your browser's settings under "Privacy" or "History."

Opt-Out Tools

For third-party advertising cookies, you can visit websites like Your Online Choices or NAI Consumer Opt-Out to opt out of many companies' cookies used for online advertising.
