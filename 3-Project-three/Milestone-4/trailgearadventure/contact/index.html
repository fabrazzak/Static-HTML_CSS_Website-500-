Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

What are your shipping times and costs?

Our build times are approximately 10 days. Shipping to Canada starts at $15 CAD, while US shipping costs $7.50-10.00 CAD extra due to brokerage requirements. All our products qualify as 'Originating Goods' under USMCA/CUSMA, avoiding excessive fees.

Do you offer custom orders or modifications?

Yes! We specialize in custom gear solutions. Whether you need specific dimensions, colors, or features, we can work with you to create the perfect product for your adventures. Contact us to discuss your custom requirements.

What is your warranty policy?

All our products come with a 1-year warranty against manufacturing defects. We stand behind our craftsmanship and will repair or replace any item that fails under normal use conditions. We also offer repair services for gear damaged during adventures.

Can I visit your workshop in person?

Absolutely! We welcome visitors to our St. Albert workshop. Please contact us in advance to schedule a visit so we can ensure someone is available to give you a proper tour and discuss your needs.
