Our Story

From Canadian wilderness to your next adventure - discover our journey crafting premium outdoor gear

Born from Adventure

TrailGear Adventure was founded in 2020 by a group of outdoor enthusiasts who were frustrated with the lack of durable, functional, and locally-made gear for their adventures. What began as a passion project in a small garage has grown into a respected Canadian manufacturer of premium outdoor equipment.

Our journey started with a simple mission: create gear that we would want to use ourselves on our most challenging expeditions. Every product is tested in real-world conditions before it ever reaches our customers.

Today, we're proud to serve adventurers across North America while maintaining our commitment to quality craftsmanship, sustainable practices, and supporting local communities.

Our Values

The principles that guide everything we do

Quality Craftsmanship

Every product is meticulously handcrafted in Canada using premium materials and traditional techniques combined with modern innovation.

Sustainability

We prioritize eco-friendly materials, minimize waste in our production process, and support local suppliers to reduce our carbon footprint.

Community Focus

We're committed to supporting Canadian manufacturing jobs and giving back to outdoor conservation initiatives.

Adventure Driven

Our products are designed by adventurers, for adventurers. We test everything in the field to ensure it performs when it matters most.

Our Process

How we create premium adventure gear

1

Design & Prototyping

Every product begins with extensive research and field testing. We create multiple prototypes to refine the design before production.

2

Material Selection

We source premium materials like Cordura Nylon, heavy-duty hardware, and sustainable fabrics that meet our durability and environmental standards.

3

Handcrafted Production

Our skilled artisans carefully craft each product in our Canadian workshop, paying close attention to every stitch and detail.

4

Quality Control

Every finished product undergoes rigorous inspection to ensure it meets our standards before being prepared for shipment.
