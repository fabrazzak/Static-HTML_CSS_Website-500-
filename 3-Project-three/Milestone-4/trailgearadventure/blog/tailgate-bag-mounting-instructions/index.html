Tailgate Bag Mounting Instructions - Strap Set-up

Proper installation of your TrailGear Adventure Tailgate Bag is crucial for secure storage during off-road adventures. Follow these step-by-step instructions to ensure your gear stays safe and your bag remains securely attached, even on the roughest trails.

Before You Begin

Ensure you have all the necessary components from your Tailgate Bag kit:

  • Tailgate Bag (V2 or appropriate model)
  • Mounting straps with buckles
  • Adjustment sliders
  • Protective pads (if included)

Pro Tip

Install your Tailgate Bag when the vehicle is clean and dry. This ensures better strap adhesion and allows you to properly inspect all mounting points.

Step-by-Step Installation Guide

1 Position the Bag

Place the Tailgate Bag on your vehicle's tailgate in the desired position. Ensure the bag is centered and sits flush against the tailgate surface. The main compartment should face outward, with strap channels aligned properly.

  • Check that the bag doesn't interfere with tailgate operation
  • Ensure license plate and lights remain visible
  • Verify the bag clears any rear cameras or sensors

2 Route the Top Straps

Begin with the upper mounting straps. Route these straps through the designated channels on the bag and then over the top of your tailgate.

  • Feed straps through the upper reinforced grommets
  • Ensure straps lay flat without twisting
  • Route straps between the tailgate and vehicle body

3 Secure Side Straps

Attach the side straps to secure the bag against lateral movement. These straps typically route around the sides of the tailgate or through available anchor points.

  • Use existing tie-down points if available
  • Route straps through side channels on the bag
  • Ensure straps don't interfere with tailgate latches

4 Adjust and Tighten

Once all straps are routed, begin the tightening process. Start with the top straps, then move to the sides, and finish with any additional stabilization straps.

  • Pull straps snug but avoid over-tightening
  • Use adjustment sliders to secure excess strap length
  • Ensure buckles are properly engaged and secure

5 Final Inspection

Before loading your gear, perform a thorough inspection of the installation:

  • Check all strap connections and buckles
  • Verify the bag doesn't shift when moderate pressure is applied
  • Ensure straps don't contact any sharp edges
  • Test tailgate operation with the bag installed

Proper Loading Techniques

How you load your Tailgate Bag affects both security and vehicle handling:

Weight Distribution

Distribute weight evenly across the bag to maintain vehicle balance and prevent strap strain.

  • Place heavier items at the bottom and center
  • Keep the load balanced left to right
  • Avoid overloading - respect the bag's capacity limits

Compression Strategy

Use the bag's compression straps to stabilize your load and prevent shifting during travel.

  • Engage compression straps after loading
  • Snug straps to minimize load movement
  • Adjust as needed during your trip

Maintenance and Care

Proper maintenance ensures your Tailgate Bag lasts for many adventures:

Regular Inspection

Before each use, inspect your bag and mounting system:

  • Check straps for wear, fraying, or UV damage
  • Inspect buckles and adjustment hardware
  • Look for any signs of stress on stitching
  • Verify all mounting points remain secure

Cleaning

Keep your bag in top condition with proper cleaning:

  • Use mild soap and water for cleaning
  • Allow to air dry completely before storage
  • Avoid harsh chemicals that could damage materials
  • Store in a cool, dry place when not in use

Safety Notice

Always check your Tailgate Bag and mounting system after driving on rough terrain. Re-tighten straps as needed and inspect for any signs of wear or damage that may have occurred during your adventure.

Troubleshooting Common Issues

Bag Shifting During Travel

If your bag moves during transit:

  • Re-tighten all mounting straps
  • Check that straps are properly routed
  • Ensure load is properly balanced
  • Verify you're not exceeding weight capacity

Strap Slippage

If straps loosen during use:

  • Check buckle engagement
  • Ensure adjustment sliders are properly set
  • Verify straps are not twisted in buckles
  • Consider using strap keepers for excess length

With proper installation and regular maintenance, your TrailGear Adventure Tailgate Bag will provide reliable storage for all your off-road adventures. Remember that secure mounting is essential for both the safety of your gear and other road users.
