Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our programs and enrollment process.

What are the program requirements?

No prior experience is required! Our programs are designed for all skill levels, from complete beginners to those looking to refine their creative skills.

Are classes online or in-person?

We offer both live online classes and in-person workshops at our Val-d'Or studio. You can choose the format that works best for your schedule and learning style.

What materials do I need?

We provide a detailed materials list upon enrollment. Basic supplies are included in your course fee, and we offer guidance on sourcing additional materials.

Can I switch between programs?

Yes! If you start with the Intensive Program and need to switch to Flexible (or vice versa), contact our admissions team within the first week to make arrangements.

Do you offer payment plans?

Absolutely! We offer flexible payment plans to make our programs accessible. Contact our admissions team to discuss options that work for your budget.

What happens after I complete the program?

Graduates receive a certificate of completion, portfolio support, and access to our alumni network. We also offer career guidance and business mentorship.
