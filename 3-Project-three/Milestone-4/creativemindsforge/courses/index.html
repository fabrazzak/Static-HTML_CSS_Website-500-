Program Curriculum

1

Creative Foundations (2 weeks)

Build your foundation with essential crafting techniques, design principles, and creative problem-solving skills.

  • Design Theory & Color Psychology
  • Material Selection & Tools
  • Basic Crafting Techniques
  • Creative Workflow & Planning
2

DIY Mastery (3 weeks)

Master woodworking, upcycling, home repairs, and advanced crafting techniques to create stunning DIY projects.

  • Woodworking & Furniture Making
  • Upcycling & Repurposing
  • Home Improvement Basics
  • Advanced DIY Techniques
  • Project Planning & Budgeting
3

Fashion Design (3 weeks)

Learn pattern making, sewing techniques, fashion illustration, and garment construction to create your own clothing designs.

  • Pattern Making & Draping
  • Sewing Techniques & Tailoring
  • Fashion Illustration & Sketching
  • Fabric Selection & Textiles
  • Sustainable Fashion Practices
4

Home Decoration & Final Project (2 weeks)

Explore interior design, space planning, and DIY home decor. Complete a capstone project showcasing your skills.

  • Interior Design Principles
  • Space Planning & Layout
  • DIY Home Decor Projects
  • Styling & Accessorizing
  • Portfolio Development & Presentation
