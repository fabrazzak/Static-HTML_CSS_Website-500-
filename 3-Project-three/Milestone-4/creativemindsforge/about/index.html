Empowering Creators Since 2018

At Creative Minds Forge, we believe everyone has the potential to create something beautiful. Our mission is to unlock that potential through expert instruction, hands-on learning, and a supportive community of makers, designers, and artisans.

Our Story

Creative Minds Forge was founded in 2018 by a group of passionate artisans who believed that creative education should be accessible, practical, and inspiring. What started as a small workshop in Val-d'Or, Quebec, has grown into a thriving creative community serving students worldwide.

We saw a gap in the market for comprehensive, hands-on creative education that combined traditional craftsmanship with modern techniques. Our founders brought together decades of experience in DIY crafts, fashion design, and interior decoration to create programs that truly transform beginners into confident creators.

Today, we're proud to have helped over 3,500 students discover their creative potential, launch their own businesses, and transform their living spaces into works of art.

Our Mission

To empower individuals to express their creativity through accessible, high-quality education in DIY crafts, fashion design, and home decoration. We strive to build a global community of confident creators who can turn their artistic visions into reality.

Our Vision

To be the world's leading creative education platform, recognized for transforming beginners into skilled artisans and fostering a sustainable, innovative approach to craftsmanship that honors tradition while embracing the future.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide everything we do at Creative Minds Forge.

Accessibility

We believe creative education should be available to everyone, regardless of experience level or background. Our programs are designed to welcome and support all learners.

Excellence

We maintain the highest standards in our curriculum, instruction, and student support. Every course is crafted to deliver exceptional value and transformative results.

Innovation

We continuously evolve our teaching methods, combining traditional techniques with modern tools and technologies to provide the most effective learning experience.

Community

We foster a supportive, collaborative environment where students connect, share ideas, and inspire each other throughout their creative journeys.

Our Journey

Key milestones in the Creative Minds Forge story.

2018

The Beginning

Creative Minds Forge opens its first studio in Val-d'Or, Quebec, with three instructors and 25 students in the inaugural cohort.

2019

Expansion

Launched our Fashion Design program and expanded to online learning, allowing us to reach students across Canada and beyond.

2021

1,000 Students

Celebrated training our 1,000th student and introduced the Home Decoration curriculum, completing our three-pillar educational approach.

2023

International Recognition

Received the Creative Education Excellence Award and launched our Flexible Program to accommodate working professionals worldwide.

2025

Continued Growth

Now serving over 3,500 students with 40+ expert instructors, while maintaining our commitment to personalized, hands-on creative education.
