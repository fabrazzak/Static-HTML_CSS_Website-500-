Get To Know Soak

Everything we make is all about helping you clean and care for the washables you love most.

Our Mission

At Soak, our mission is to quickly, comfortably and sustainably move people from fear to elation and into their favourite clothes. Laundry can be easy, fun and fast!

Help us create a cultural shift via a Soakworthy® movement that helps people be their best selves by caring for and wearing their favorite clothes — whether they're purchased, handmade or handed-down.

Soakworthy

/sohk wur-th ee/

1. Something deserving of special care and attention because of its emotional value
2. A person or thing that merits recognition for manifesting Soak values

Our Journey

From humble beginnings to global recognition

2006

Soak Wash gets its start

Soak launches with 3 fresh fragrances – flora, aquae, and citrus – and is sold via Jacq's Hats. Soak launches in Canada and the US, with tons of retail interest at knitting industry events.

2008

Soakworthy pursuits

Scentless and Celebration hit shelves, Soak set its sights on lingerie, and the Soakworthy philosophy gets its start.

2009

Unleash is…unleashed

In partnership with Ravelry, Soak launches limited-edition fragrance Unleash, which sells out in a single day! Real Simple magazine features Soak Wash, and brand awareness just keeps on growing.

2011

The Soak evolution continues

Soak sales grow and expand across the UK, including being sold at luxury UK lingerie shop Rigby & Peller. Lacey launches, Handmaid debuts, Soak makes Martha Stewart magazine.

2012

Sustainability, Soakboxes and super-cute basins

Soak switches to 100% RPET bottles, Soakboxes launch, and Carrie and Phil basins arrive to round out the Soak collection.

2013

Flat-out fabulous

We introduce Flatter ironing spray, Yuzu debuts as Soak's latest scent, and our bottle packaging gets a makeover. In 2014, the world meets earthy, fruity Fig, which becomes a particular favourite among knitters.

2015

Going global

We add distributors in Belgium, Poland, Sweden, Singapore, New Zealand, and Australia, making Soak a truly global brand. Soak introduces Pineapple Grove, Wirecutter says we're #1 for hand-washing AND we win the TNNA Business Innovation Award.

2019

More innovation

We introduce Eco Wash Bags (made with recycled plastic!), and get $100K from the Women Entrepreneurship Fund.

2020

The great pandemic pivot

Hand sanitizer by Soak helps Soak and our retailers stay afloat, and we lean hard into our self-care and community values.

2022

Getting wild about laundry

We introduce our Wild Mint – and a bottle design that's the result of our work with OCADU on inclusivity and accessibility.

The Journey Continues...

Soak Always. Rinse Never. Keep Forever.

The revolutionary three-step philosophy that's changing how people care for their clothes

Soak Always

Made from gentle, skincare-grade ingredients, a little bit of Soak goes a long way, and can be used for hand-washing, machine-washing or spot cleaning.

Rinse Never

Rinse-free means less work for you and less wear-and-tear on your items. But you can always rinse if you want to.

Keep Forever

The gentle, no-rinse formulation is easier on your clothes, so the items you love most will last longer.
