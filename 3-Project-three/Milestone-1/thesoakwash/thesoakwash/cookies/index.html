What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer, smartphone, or other device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and to provide information to website owners about how users interact with their sites.

Cookies contain information that is transferred to your computer's hard drive. They help us improve our website and deliver a better, more personalized service by enabling us to estimate audience size, measure site traffic patterns, and store information about your preferences.

How We Use Cookies

The Soak Wash uses cookies for several important reasons:

  • To provide you with a personalized browsing experience
  • To remember your preferences and settings
  • To enable certain website functionality and features
  • To analyze how our website is used and improve performance
  • To provide targeted advertising and marketing content
  • To measure the effectiveness of our marketing campaigns
  • To prevent fraud and enhance security
  • To comply with legal and regulatory requirements

Specific Cookies We Use

First-Party Cookies

These are cookies set directly by our website:

  • Session ID: Maintains your session while browsing our site
  • Shopping Cart: Remembers items in your cart
  • User Preferences: Stores your language and region preferences
  • Authentication: Keeps you logged in to your account
  • Cookie Consent: Remembers your cookie preferences

Third-Party Cookies

These cookies are set by external services we use:

  • Google Analytics: Website traffic and user behavior analysis
  • Google Ads: Advertising and remarketing campaigns
  • Facebook Pixel: Social media advertising and tracking
  • PayPal/Stripe: Payment processing and fraud prevention
  • Mailchimp: Email marketing and newsletter management
  • Hotjar: User experience and website optimization

Browser Cookie Controls

Most web browsers allow you to manage cookies through their settings. Here's how to control cookies in popular browsers:

Chrome

Go to Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies and other site data. Here you can block cookies, delete existing cookies, or make exceptions for specific sites.

Firefox

Go to Options > Privacy & Security. Under Cookies and Site Data, you can choose to accept or block cookies and clear existing data.

Safari

Go to Preferences > Privacy. You can choose to block all cookies, allow cookies from websites you visit, or allow cookies from current website only.

Edge

Go to Settings > Cookies and site permissions > Cookies and site data. Here you can control cookie behavior and clear existing cookies.

Mobile Device Controls

On mobile devices, you can control cookies through your browser settings:

  • iOS Safari: Settings > Safari > Block All Cookies
  • Android Chrome: Chrome app > Menu > Settings > Site settings > Cookies
  • Android Firefox: Firefox app > Menu > Settings > Privacy > Cookies

Third-Party Opt-Outs

You can opt out of certain third-party cookies directly:

  • Google Analytics: Google Analytics Opt-out
  • Google Ads: Google Ad Settings
  • Facebook: Facebook Ad Preferences
  • Network Advertising Initiative: NAI Opt-out

Data Retention

Different cookies have different retention periods:

  • Session Cookies: Deleted when you close your browser
  • Persistent Cookies: Remain on your device until they expire or you delete them
  • Analytics Cookies: Typically retained for 1-2 years
  • Marketing Cookies: Usually retained for 30 days to 2 years

Impact of Disabling Cookies

If you choose to disable cookies, some features of our website may not function properly:

  • You may not be able to stay logged in to your account
  • Your shopping cart may not work correctly
  • You may see irrelevant advertisements
  • Website performance may be affected
  • Some personalization features won't work
  • We won't be able to remember your preferences

Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or other factors. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the updated policy on our website and updating the "Last Updated" date.

We encourage you to review this Cookie Policy periodically to stay informed about how we use cookies and similar technologies.
