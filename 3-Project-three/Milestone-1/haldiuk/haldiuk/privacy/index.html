Important Notice

This Privacy Policy explains how Haldi Restaurant ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, discloses, and protects your personal information when you visit our restaurant, use our website, or engage with our services. By using our services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Our Commitment to Privacy

At Haldi Restaurant, we understand that your privacy is important to you. We are committed to being transparent about how we collect, use, and share your personal information. This policy applies to all information collected through:

  • Our restaurant website (haldiuk.com)
  • In-person dining experiences
  • Online reservation systems
  • Delivery and takeaway orders
  • Marketing communications
  • Customer service interactions

Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other applicable privacy laws. Our legal basis for processing includes contract performance, legitimate interests, legal obligations, and your consent where required.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information You Provide

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you:

  • Make a reservation through our website or by phone
  • Place an order for delivery or collection
  • Create an account on our website
  • Subscribe to our newsletter or marketing communications
  • Participate in surveys, contests, or promotions
  • Contact us for customer support
  • Leave reviews or feedback

Types of Personal Data

Contact Information

  • • Full name
  • • Email address
  • • Phone number
  • • Postal address

Dining Information

  • • Reservation details
  • • Order history
  • • Dietary preferences
  • • Special requests

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, we automatically collect certain information about your device and usage:

  • IP address and location data
  • Browser type and version
  • Device information and operating system
  • Pages visited and time spent on our website
  • Referring website or search terms
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies

2. How We Use Your Information

Primary Purposes

We use your personal information primarily to provide and improve our restaurant services, process your orders, and enhance your dining experience.

Service Delivery

Processing reservations, orders, and providing customer support services.

Payment Processing

Processing payments and managing billing information securely.

Communication

Sending order confirmations, updates, and promotional offers (with consent).

Service Improvement

Analyzing usage patterns to improve our website, menu, and services.

3. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

Service Providers

We may share information with trusted third-party service providers who help us operate our business:

  • Payment processors (Stripe, PayPal)
  • Delivery service partners
  • Website hosting and analytics providers
  • Email marketing platforms
  • Customer support tools

Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, to protect our rights, or to comply with legal processes such as court orders or government investigations.

4. Data Security

Our Security Commitment

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Technical Safeguards

  • • SSL/TLS encryption for data transmission
  • • Secure server infrastructure
  • • Regular security audits and updates
  • • Encrypted data storage
  • • Firewall protection

Administrative Safeguards

  • • Staff training on data protection
  • • Access controls and permissions
  • • Data breach response procedures
  • • Regular privacy impact assessments
  • • Third-party security certifications

5. Your Privacy Rights

Under GDPR and other privacy laws, you

Right to Access

You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.

Right to Rectification

You can request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.

Right to Erasure

You can request deletion of your personal data under certain circumstances.

Right to Object

You can object to processing of your data for marketing purposes.

Right to Portability

You can request transfer of your data to another service provider.

Right to Restrict Processing

You can request limitation on how we process your data.

How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@haldiuk.com or use the contact information below. We will respond to your request within 30 days.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) if you believe we have not handled your personal data properly.

6. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience on our website. For detailed information about our cookie practices, please see our Cookie Policy.

Essential Cookies

Required for website functionality and security.

Analytics Cookies

Help us understand how visitors use our website.

Marketing Cookies

Used to deliver personalized advertisements.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected and to comply with legal obligations.

Retention Periods

  • Customer accounts: Until account deletion or 3 years of inactivity
  • Order history: 7 years for accounting and tax purposes
    • consent is withdrawn
  • Website analytics: 26 months maximum
  • CCTV footage: 30 days maximum

8. International Data Transfers

Your personal information is primarily stored and processed within the European Economic Area (EEA). When we transfer data outside the EEA, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.

Transfer Safeguards

  • Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission
  • Adequacy decisions for countries with equivalent protection
  • Certification schemes and codes of conduct

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 16 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 16, we will take steps to delete such information.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.
