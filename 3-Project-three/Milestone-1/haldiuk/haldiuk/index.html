Our Story

Haldi is an authentic Indian restaurant dedicated to serving the finest Asian cuisine. Since our inception, we have delighted countless customers with meals crafted to perfection by our experienced, award-winning chefs.

Using traditional cooking techniques and the finest quality ingredients, we create culinary experiences that transport you to the heart of India. Our extensive menu caters to both vegetarian and non-vegetarian preferences.
