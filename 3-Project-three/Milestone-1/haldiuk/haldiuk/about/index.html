Welcome to Haldi

Since our establishment in 2018, Haldi has been more than just a restaurant – we are custodians of India's rich culinary heritage. Located in the heart of London's prestigious Mayfair district, we have become synonymous with authentic Indian dining experiences.

Our name "Haldi," meaning turmeric in Hindi, represents the golden essence that runs through all our dishes. Just as turmeric is essential to Indian cooking, we believe authenticity is essential to great dining.

Every dish tells a story, every spice has a purpose, and every meal creates a memory. This is our promise to you – an unforgettable journey through India's diverse flavors.

Our Timeline

Key milestones in our culinary journey

2018 - The Beginning

Haldi Restaurant opened its doors in London's prestigious Mayfair district with a vision to bring authentic Indian flavors to discerning diners. Our founding chef, with over 20 years of experience, set the foundation for culinary excellence.

9 Conduit Street, London

2019 - Recognition Begins

Within our first year, we received the "Best New Indian Restaurant" award from London Food Critics Association. Our signature dishes started gaining recognition among food enthusiasts and critics alike.

First Major Award

2020 - Adaptation & Growth

Despite global challenges, we adapted with innovative takeaway services and maintained our quality standards. We introduced our famous Sunday Buffet and Wednesday Banquet offers during this period.

Innovation & Resilience

2021 - Culinary Excellence

Our head chef was recognized as "Indian Chef of the Year" by the British Curry Awards. We expanded our menu to include more regional specialties and introduced our premium tasting menu.

Chef Recognition

2022-2025 - Continued Success

With over 25,000 satisfied customers and numerous awards, we continue to set new standards in authentic Indian dining. Our commitment to quality and tradition remains unwavering as we look toward the future.

Ongoing Excellence

Our Core Values

The principles that guide everything we do

Authenticity

We stay true to traditional recipes and cooking methods passed down through generations, ensuring every dish reflects genuine Indian flavors.

Quality

From sourcing the finest spices directly from India to selecting premium ingredients, we never compromise on quality.

Hospitality

Indian hospitality is legendary, and we extend this warmth to every guest, making you feel like part of our family.

Sustainability

We are committed to sustainable practices, from eco-friendly packaging to supporting local suppliers and reducing waste.

Innovation

While honoring tradition, we embrace innovation in presentation and service to enhance your dining experience.

Community

We believe in giving back to our community and supporting local charities and cultural events throughout London.
