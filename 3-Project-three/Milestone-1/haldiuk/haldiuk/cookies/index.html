1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They help websites remember information about your visit, like your preferred language and other settings.

First-Party Cookies

Set directly by our website and can only be read by us. These help us provide essential website functionality.

Third-Party Cookies

Set by external services we use, like Google Analytics or social media platforms, to provide additional features.

5. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

Your Control

You have full control over your cookie preferences. You can change your settings at any time or disable cookies entirely through your browser settings.

Browser Settings

  • Chrome: Settings > Privacy > Cookies
  • Firefox: Options > Privacy > Cookies
  • Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Cookies
  • Edge: Settings > Privacy > Cookies

Cookie Banner

Update your preferences anytime using our cookie banner.

Important Note

Disabling certain cookies may limit website functionality. Essential cookies cannot be disabled as they are required for basic site operations.
