Cookie Policy

Learn how we use cookies to enhance your browsing experience and how you can manage your preferences.

Last updated: September 1, 2025

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) when you visit our website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and to provide information to website owners about how users interact with their sites.

At The Grounds Roastery, we use cookies to enhance your browsing experience, remember your preferences, and provide you with personalized content and relevant advertisements.

Did you know? The term "cookie" comes from the computing term "magic cookie," which refers to a packet of data a program receives and sends back unchanged.

Why We Use Cookies

We use cookies for several important reasons:

Essential Functionality

Enable core website features like shopping cart, user login, and secure checkout processes.

Personalization

Remember your preferences, language settings, and customize your experience.

Analytics & Performance

Understand how you use our site to improve performance and user experience.

Marketing & Advertising

Show you relevant content and advertisements based on your interests.

Types of Cookies We Use

1. Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for our website to function properly. They enable basic functions like page navigation, access to secure areas, and shopping cart functionality. Our website cannot function properly without these cookies.

PHPSESSID

Purpose: Maintains user session

Duration: Session only

cart_token

Purpose: Preserves shopping cart contents

Duration: 30 days

security_token

Purpose: CSRF protection for secure forms

Duration: Session only

cookie_consent

Purpose: Remembers your cookie preferences

Duration: 1 year

2. Performance Cookies

These cookies collect information about how you use our website, such as which pages you visit most often and if you get error messages. This helps us improve how our website works.

_ga (Google Analytics)

Purpose: Tracks user interactions and website usage

Duration: 2 years

_gid (Google Analytics)

Purpose: Distinguishes unique users for analytics

Duration: 24 hours

_gat (Google Analytics)

Purpose: Throttles request rate to prevent overloading

Duration: 1 minute

3. Functionality Cookies

These cookies allow our website to remember choices you make (like your username, language preferences, or region) and provide enhanced, more personal features.

user_preferences

Purpose: Stores user preferences and personalized settings

Duration: 1 year

language

Purpose: Remembers your language preference

Duration: 1 year

recently_viewed

Purpose: Tracks recently viewed products for recommendations

Duration: 30 days

4. Targeting/Advertising Cookies

These cookies are used to deliver relevant advertisements and track the effectiveness of advertising campaigns. They remember that you have visited our website and this information is shared with other organizations such as advertisers.

_fbp (Facebook Pixel)

Purpose: Tracks conversions and user interactions for Facebook ads

Duration: 90 days

ads_preferences

Purpose: Personalizes advertising content based on interests

Duration: 1 year

retargeting_id

Purpose: Enables retargeting campaigns across websites

Duration: 30 days

Third-Party Cookies

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our website. This helps us improve our content and user experience. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect data anonymously.

We work with advertising networks that use cookies to show you relevant ads across the web based on your interests.

Note: Third-party cookies are governed by the respective privacy policies of these service providers.

Browser Cookie Controls

You can also manage cookies directly through your browser settings. Here's how to do it in popular browsers:

Google Chrome

Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and other site data

Mozilla Firefox

Preferences → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data

Safari

Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data

Microsoft Edge

Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Cookies and site data

Important: Disabling cookies may affect the functionality of our website and limit your user experience.

Cookie Lifespan

Cookies have different lifespans depending on their purpose:

Session Cookies

These are temporary cookies that are deleted when you close your browser. They help maintain your session while browsing our site.

Persistent Cookies

These remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them. They remember your preferences between visits.

Automatic Cleanup: We regularly review and remove outdated cookies to protect your privacy.

Cookies on Mobile Devices

Mobile browsers also support cookies, but the management options may be different:

Mobile Safari (iOS)

Settings → Safari → Privacy & Security → Block All Cookies

Chrome Mobile (Android)

Chrome menu → Settings → Privacy → Clear browsing data

Mobile App

If you're using our mobile app, similar tracking technologies may be used. You can manage these in your device's privacy settings.
