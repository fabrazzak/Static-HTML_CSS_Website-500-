Our Story

From a forgotten industrial space to Sydney's most beloved coffee destination. Discover the passion, community, and creativity that makes The Grounds special.

The Grounds Group

We are a hospitality and event organisation that specialises in creating the best possible experiences in beautiful spaces for our guests. Our talented team of passionate and hard-working individuals make The Grounds what it is – a home where creativity thrives.

What We Do

It all started with a simple idea: to turn a forgotten industrial space into a welcoming sanctuary. Back in 2012, we opened the doors to The Grounds of Alexandria, transforming a former pie factory and its concrete car park into a thriving urban oasis of lush gardens, specialty coffee, and wholesome food.

We wanted to create more than just a café; we wanted to build a community.

The Secret Garden themed display at The Grounds of Alexandria

Our Journey

Follow the evolution of The Grounds from a single venue to a beloved hospitality group.

2012

The Beginning

The Grounds of Alexandria opens its doors, transforming a former pie factory into a unique café experience with lush gardens and specialty coffee.

2014

Recognition

Gains recognition as one of the world's most Instagrammable destinations, attracting visitors from around the globe to our themed installations.

2016

Expansion

Launch of The Grounds Roastery, bringing our passion for coffee bean-to-cup with premium roasting and wholesale services.

2018

City Presence

Opening of additional venues across Sydney, each with its own distinct character while maintaining our commitment to quality and community.

2020

Events & Catering

Launch of The Grounds Events and Catering services, bringing our expertise to weddings, corporate events, and special occasions.

2025

Today

A thriving hospitality group continuing to create beautiful, memorable experiences while staying true to our community roots and passion for quality.
