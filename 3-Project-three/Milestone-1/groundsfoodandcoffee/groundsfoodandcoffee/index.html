Our Story

Established in 2012 out of pure passion for coffee, The Grounds Roastery embraces the philosophy that goes beyond perfecting each roast. We celebrate the everyday occasions, special moments, and daily rituals that coffee enhances.

From sparking friendships to inspiring art and building empires - every conversation over our coffee has the power to change the world, one cup at a time.
