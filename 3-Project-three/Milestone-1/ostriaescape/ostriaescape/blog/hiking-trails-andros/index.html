Exploring Andros on Foot: Top Hiking Trails for Nature Lovers

Alex M.

Outdoor Guide • November 18, 2025

Table of Contents

Andros stands apart from other Cycladic islands with its remarkable network of hiking trails that crisscross lush valleys, climb rugged mountains, and trace dramatic coastlines. With over 100km of well-marked paths that form part of the Andros Route network, this island is a paradise for hikers and nature enthusiasts.

As an outdoor guide who has led hiking tours on Andros for the past eight years, I've had the privilege of exploring virtually every path on this beautiful island. In this comprehensive guide, I'll share my insider knowledge of the best hiking trails Andros has to offer, from gentle coastal walks to challenging mountain ascents that reward with breathtaking views.

Northern Trails: Coastal Views & Historical Sites

The northern part of Andros offers some of the most accessible and scenic hiking routes, perfect for those who want to combine nature with cultural exploration.

Menites to Chora (Andros Town) Trail

This moderately challenging 8km trail connects the picturesque village of Menites, known for its natural springs and lush vegetation, with the island's capital, Chora. The path follows ancient stone-paved routes through olive groves and offers spectacular views of the northeast coast.

Distance: 8km Duration: 3-4 hours Difficulty: Moderate Elevation Gain: 350m

Trail Map: Menites to Chora

Start: Menites Village | End: Chora

Highlights:

  • Natural springs of Menites with lion-head fountains
  • Panachrantos Monastery detour option
  • Stunning views of Chora and the sea
  • Well-preserved Venetian-era stone path sections

Guide Tip:

Start early in the morning to avoid the midday heat and enjoy the springs at Menites at their most refreshing. The cafe near the springs makes excellent traditional Greek coffee.

Apikia to Pithara Waterfalls Trail

A shorter but incredibly rewarding 4km round trip that takes you to one of Andros' most beautiful natural attractions—the Pithara Waterfalls. The trail starts in the village of Apikia and follows a lush stream valley.

Distance: 4km round trip Duration: 2 hours Difficulty: Easy Waterfall

Central Mountain Routes: Summit Challenges & Panoramic Views

For those seeking more challenging hikes with spectacular rewards, the central mountain ranges of Andros offer some of the best routes in the Cyclades.

Mount Kouvara Summit Trail

At 997 meters, Mount Kouvara is the highest peak on Andros, and the ascent offers some of the most breathtaking panoramic views in the Cyclades. The 12km round trip trail begins near the village of Fallika and takes you through diverse landscapes including pine forests, high meadows, and rocky outcrops.

Distance: 12km round trip Duration: 5-6 hours Difficulty: Challenging Elevation Gain: 750m

Trail Map: Mount Kouvara Summit

Start: Fallika Village | Summit: 997m

Highlights:

  • 360-degree views of Andros and neighboring islands
  • Chance to see native wildlife including hawks and falcons
  • Ancient shepherd huts along the route
  • Spring wildflowers (April-May)

Essential Gear:

  • Sturdy hiking boots with ankle support
  • Layered clothing for changing conditions
  • At least 2 liters of water per person
  • High-energy snacks and lunch
  • Windproof jacket (summit can be very windy)

Safety Note:

This trail should not be attempted in high winds or poor visibility. Check weather conditions before starting and turn back if weather deteriorates. The best time for this hike is from April to June and September to October.

Dipotamata Gorge Trail

This spectacular 10km point-to-point trail follows the Dipotamata (Two Rivers) Gorge through some of the most dramatic scenery on Andros. The trail connects the villages of Kochylos and Strapouries, passing through a landscape of towering rock formations, ancient watermills, and lush vegetation.

Distance: 10km Duration: 4-5 hours Difficulty: Moderate River Crossings

Southern Coastal Paths: Seascapes & Secluded Beaches

The southern part of Andros offers stunning coastal hiking with opportunities to discover secluded beaches and dramatic sea cliffs.

Zorkos Beach to Vitali Beach Coastal Trail

This beautiful 7km coastal trail connects two of Andros' most stunning beaches, offering incredible sea views and opportunities for swimming along the way. The path follows ancient donkey trails that cling to the cliffs above the Aegean.

Distance: 7km Duration: 3 hours Difficulty: Moderate Beach Access

Trail Map: Coastal Path

Zorkos Beach to Vitali Beach

Highlights:

  • Stunning views of the Aegean Sea
  • Access to secluded swimming coves
  • Spring wildflowers including rare orchids
  • Excellent birdwatching opportunities

Swimming Tip:

Pack your swimsuit and a lightweight towel. There are several perfect swimming spots along this route, particularly around the halfway point where a fresh water spring meets the sea.

Waterfall & Gorge Trails: Andros' Liquid Landscapes

Andros is surprisingly rich in water features for a Cycladic island, with several beautiful waterfalls and gorges that make for rewarding hiking destinations.

Pithara Waterfalls Circuit

While the main trail to Pithara Waterfalls is relatively short, a longer 6km circuit can be created by combining it with paths through the surrounding valley. This route takes you to multiple waterfalls and swimming holes, with the main falls being particularly impressive in spring when water flow is at its peak.

Distance: 6km circuit Duration: 3 hours Difficulty: Moderate Swimming Holes

Ahla Gorge Trail

This challenging but spectacular 8km trail follows the Ahla Gorge from the inland village of Vourkoti to the beautiful Ahla Beach. The trail involves some rock scrambling and river crossings, but rewards with incredible scenery and a stunning beach destination.

Distance: 8km Duration: 4-5 hours Difficulty: Challenging Rock Scrambling

Hiking Preparation: Essential Tips for Andros Trails

Proper preparation is key to enjoying Andros' hiking trails safely and comfortably. Here's what you need to know before you hit the trails.

When to Hike

The best seasons for hiking in Andros are spring (April to June) and autumn (September to October). During these periods, temperatures are pleasant, wildflowers are blooming (in spring), and the landscapes are at their greenest. Summer hiking is possible but requires early starts to avoid the intense midday heat.

Essential Gear

While trail difficulty varies, certain items should always be in your daypack:

  • Sturdy footwear: Trail runners or hiking boots with good grip
  • Sun protection: Hat, sunglasses, and high-SPF sunscreen
  • Hydration: At least 2 liters of water per person
  • Navigation: Detailed map or GPS device (mobile service can be spotty)
  • Nutrition: High-energy snacks and lunch
  • First aid kit: Including blister treatment
  • Layered clothing: Weather can change quickly in the mountains

Trail Etiquette

Please follow these guidelines to help preserve Andros' beautiful trails:

  • Stay on marked paths to avoid erosion and protect fragile ecosystems
  • Carry out all trash, including food waste
  • Respect private property and livestock
  • Keep dogs on leash where required
  • Be considerate of other hikers

Navigation Tip:

The Andros Routes are generally well-marked with colored signs and paint markers. However, it's still wise to carry a detailed map or download the official Andros Routes app for offline navigation.

Guided Hiking Tours: Enhancing Your Experience

While many trails can be hiked independently, joining a guided tour can greatly enhance your experience, especially for less experienced hikers or those interested in learning more about the island's natural and cultural history.

Benefits of Guided Hikes

Local guides offer several advantages:

  • Expert knowledge of trail conditions and difficulty
  • Insights into local flora, fauna, and history
  • Safety support and first aid training
  • Transportation to trailheads
  • Access to areas that might be difficult to find independently

Conclusion: Discovering Andros Step by Step

Hiking is undoubtedly one of the best ways to experience the authentic beauty of Andros. Beyond the beaches and villages that most visitors see, the island's trail network reveals a landscape of surprising diversity—from lush river valleys and dramatic gorges to mountain summits with panoramic views.

Whether you're an experienced hiker seeking challenging ascents or a casual walker looking for gentle coastal paths, Andros offers trails to suit every ability and interest. The well-marked Andros Routes make navigation straightforward, while the island's small size means you're never far from a welcoming village taverna or a refreshing swimming spot.

"We live in a wonderful world that is full of beauty, charm, and adventure. There is no end to the adventures we can have if only we seek them with our eyes open." - Jawaharlal Nehru

As someone who has hiked these trails for years, I can honestly say that each season brings new discoveries and perspectives. The spring wildflowers, the summer scents of herbs warmed by the sun, the autumn colors, and even the dramatic winter landscapes—all offer unique experiences that will leave you with lasting memories of this special island.

So lace up your boots, pack your water and snacks, and set out to discover the authentic Andros that lies beyond the roads and villages. The island's trails are waiting to share their secrets with you.
