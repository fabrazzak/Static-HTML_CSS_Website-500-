Discover the Best Beaches in Andros: A Local's Guide

Maria K.

Hotel Manager • November 15, 2025

Table of Contents

Andros, the northernmost island of the Cyclades, boasts a diverse coastline that rivals any of its Aegean neighbors. With over 30 named beaches ranging from long stretches of golden sand to intimate pebble coves, the island offers coastal experiences for every type of traveler. What sets Andros apart is its natural, unspoiled beauty—you won't find the overwhelming crowds of Mykonos or Santorini here, just pristine waters and authentic Greek island charm.

Having managed Ostria Hotel for over a decade, I've had the pleasure of exploring every corner of this beautiful island and helping our guests discover their perfect beach experience. In this comprehensive guide, I'll share my insider knowledge of Andros' best beaches, from the popular organized beaches with full amenities to hidden gems accessible only by foot or boat.

Northern Beaches: Accessibility & Amenities

The northern part of Andros, where Gavrio (the main port) is located, offers several well-organized beaches with easy access and full amenities. These are perfect for travelers who want convenience without sacrificing natural beauty.

Agios Petros Beach

Just a 5-minute drive from Gavrio port, Agios Petros is one of the most popular beaches on the island. This long, sandy beach boasts crystal-clear waters and is fully organized with sunbeds, umbrellas, beach bars, and tavernas.

Local Tip:

Visit in the late afternoon to enjoy spectacular sunset views over the Aegean. The beach bars often host live music events during summer weekends.

Sandy Organized Tavernas Easy Access Family-Friendly

Batsi Beach

Located in the charming village of Batsi, this crescent-shaped beach is surrounded by whitewashed houses and offers a picturesque setting. The beach is sandy with some pebbles, and the waters are calm and shallow, making it ideal for families with children.

The beachfront is lined with cafes, restaurants, and shops, so you'll have everything you need within walking distance. During July and August, it can get busy, so I recommend arriving before 11 AM to secure a good spot.

Sandy/Pebbles Organized Shops Nearby Safe for Children

Central & Western Beaches: Golden Sands & Dramatic Landscapes

The central and western parts of Andros feature some of the island's most spectacular beaches, with golden sands, turquoise waters, and dramatic backdrops of mountains meeting the sea.

Golden Beach (Chryssi Ammos)

True to its name, Golden Beach boasts an expansive stretch of golden sand that is considered one of the finest in the Cyclades. At approximately 1 kilometer long, it never feels crowded even at the height of summer.

The beach is organized with sunbeds and umbrellas, and there's a beach bar serving refreshments and light meals. The northern part of the beach is popular with windsurfers due to the reliable winds, while the southern end is more sheltered and calmer for swimming.

Local Tip:

For the best experience, walk to the southern end of the beach where you'll find slightly fewer people and stunning views of the nearby islet. The sunset from here is absolutely magical.

Golden Sand Partially Organized Windsurfing Scenic

Achla Beach

For those willing to make the journey, Achla Beach offers one of Andros' most breathtaking natural settings. Located at the mouth of a verdant valley where a freshwater river meets the sea, Achla feels like a hidden paradise.

Access requires a 4x4 vehicle or a boat trip, but the effort is rewarded with fine white sand, crystal-clear emerald waters, and complete seclusion. There are no facilities here, so bring everything you need for the day.

White Sand Natural Setting River Meeting Sea 4x4 Access

Southern Beaches: Seclusion & Natural Beauty

The southern part of Andros is less developed and offers some of the island's most secluded and pristine beach experiences. These beaches require more effort to reach but offer unparalleled natural beauty and tranquility.

Vori Beach

Vori is a long, unorganized sandy beach surrounded by dramatic cliffs and lush vegetation. The waters here are exceptionally clear and ideal for snorkeling. Due to its exposed position, it can get windy, which makes it popular with kite surfers on windier days.

There are no facilities at Vori, so bring your own supplies. The beach is accessed via a somewhat rough road, but regular cars can manage it with careful driving.

Sandy Windy Good Snorkeling Natural Surroundings

Pisolimionas Beach

This beautiful beach gets its name from the small natural pool ("pisolimionas" means small pool) that forms in the rocks at the western end. The beach features golden sand and turquoise waters, with impressive rock formations framing the cove.

There's a small cantina that operates during peak season, but it's best to come prepared with your own supplies. The road down is steep but manageable with a regular car.

Golden Sand Rock Formations Natural Pool Photogenic

Hidden Gems & Secluded Coves

Beyond the main beaches, Andros is dotted with countless smaller coves and hidden spots that offer complete privacy and untouched natural beauty. These require more effort to reach—often involving hiking or boat access—but reward intrepid explorers with unforgettable experiences.

Zorkos Beach

Zorkos is a stunning secluded beach accessible via a 30-minute hike from the village of Kochylos or by boat. The beach consists of white sand and pebbles, with unbelievably clear turquoise waters that rival any Caribbean destination.

There are absolutely no facilities here, so bring water, food, and sun protection. The hike down is moderately challenging but well worth the effort. Many visitors combine a trip to Zorkos with a visit to the nearby Pithara waterfall during spring and early summer.

Local Tip:

The best way to experience Zorkos is by joining one of the boat tours that depart from Gavrio or Batsi. This allows you to enjoy the beach without the challenging hike and often includes stops at other secluded spots along the coast.

Vlychada Beach

Tucked away on the southwest coast, Vlychada is a small, picturesque cove with fine white sand and deep blue waters. Surrounded by high cliffs, it feels completely isolated from the rest of the world.

Access is via a rough dirt road followed by a short but steep path down to the beach. The effort is rewarded with pristine natural beauty and likely having the beach entirely to yourself, even in peak season.

Beach-going Tips for Andros

To make the most of your beach experiences in Andros, keep these practical tips in mind:

Timing Your Visit

The best time to visit Andros beaches is from May to October, with July and August being the warmest but also the busiest months. For fewer crowds, consider visiting in June or September when the weather is still excellent but the beaches are quieter.

What to Bring

For organized beaches, you mainly need your swimwear, towel, and sunscreen. However, for the more remote beaches, come prepared with:

  • Plenty of drinking water
  • Snacks or a picnic lunch
  • Sun protection (hat, sunscreen, cover-up)
  • Comfortable shoes for walking
  • A waterproof bag for electronics
  • Beach shoes for pebbly beaches
  • Snorkeling gear to explore underwater life

Getting Around

While some beaches are accessible by public bus from Gavrio and Batsi, the most efficient way to explore Andros' diverse beaches is by renting a car. For the more remote beaches, consider a 4x4 vehicle, especially if you're not comfortable driving on rough dirt roads.

Boat tours are another excellent option for accessing secluded beaches and seeing the coastline from a different perspective. Several operators offer daily trips from Gavrio and Batsi during the summer months.

Conclusion: Finding Your Perfect Andros Beach

Andros offers an incredible diversity of beach experiences, from the convenience of organized beaches near main towns to the complete seclusion of hidden coves accessible only by foot or boat. What unites all these beaches is their natural beauty, clean waters, and the authentic Greek island atmosphere that makes Andros special.

Whether you're seeking family-friendly amenities, water sports opportunities, or complete isolation, you'll find your perfect beach experience on this beautiful island. The best approach is to mix and match—enjoy the convenience of organized beaches some days, and venture off the beaten path on others.
