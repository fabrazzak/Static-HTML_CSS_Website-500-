About XCraftLab

Discover the passion, dedication, and craftsmanship that drives our mission to empower creators across Saskatchewan with premium DIY supplies and sustainable crafting solutions.

Our Beginning

A Vision Born from Creativity

Founded in 2018 in the vibrant community of Estevan, Saskatchewan, XCraftLab emerged from a simple yet powerful vision: to create a space where creativity knows no bounds and sustainability meets artistry.

Our founders, passionate crafters themselves, recognized a gap in the market for high-quality, eco-friendly crafting supplies that didn't compromise on performance or environmental responsibility. What started as a small workshop has evolved into Saskatchewan's premier destination for premium DIY supplies.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide everything we do at XCraftLab

Sustainability First

We prioritize eco-friendly materials and sustainable practices in every aspect of our business, from sourcing to packaging.

  • Recycled and biodegradable materials
  • Minimal packaging waste
  • Local supplier partnerships

Quality Excellence

Every product we carry meets our rigorous standards for durability, performance, and craftsmanship.

  • Premium material selection
  • Thorough quality testing
  • Professional-grade standards

Community Support

We believe in fostering creativity and supporting our local crafting community through education and collaboration.

  • Local workshop events
  • Expert guidance and tutorials
  • Community project sponsorship

Our Journey

Milestones that shaped XCraftLab into the premier crafting destination it is today

2018

The Foundation

XCraftLab opens its doors in Estevan with a small but carefully curated selection of premium crafting supplies and a commitment to sustainability.

2019

Community Growth

Launch of our first workshop series and establishment of partnerships with local schools and community centers to promote creative education.

2020

Digital Expansion

Launched our online platform to serve customers across Saskatchewan, adapting to changing needs while maintaining our personal touch.

2022

Eco-Innovation Award

Received recognition from the Saskatchewan Environmental Association for our sustainable business practices and eco-friendly product initiatives.

2024

Excellence Recognition

Named "Best Craft Supply Store in Saskatchewan" by Prairie Craft Magazine and expanded our team to better serve our growing community.
