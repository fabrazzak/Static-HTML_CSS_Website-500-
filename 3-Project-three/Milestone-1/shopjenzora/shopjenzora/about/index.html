Redefining Vienna's Fashion Landscape

Founded in 2020, Jenzora emerged from a passion for exceptional craftsmanship and timeless style. Our journey began on the iconic Kärntner Straße, where we set out to create a destination for those who appreciate quality, elegance, and attention to detail.

Today, we continue to curate collections that blend contemporary trends with classic sophistication, offering our customers in Vienna and beyond a truly premium shopping experience.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Jenzora

Where is Jenzora located?

Our flagship store is located at Kärntner Straße 26, in the heart of Vienna, Austria. We're easily accessible by public transportation, with the Stephansplatz station just a short walk away.

What are your opening hours?

Our store is open Monday to Saturday from 10:00 to 19:00, and on Sundays from 11:00 to 17:00. During holiday seasons, we may have extended hours which will be announced on our website and social media channels.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we ship to most countries in Europe. Shipping costs and delivery times vary depending on the destination. You can view all shipping options at checkout before completing your purchase.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day return policy for all unworn items with original tags attached. Returns are free for customers in Austria. For international returns, shipping costs are the responsibility of the customer. Please see our full return policy for more details.

Do you offer personal styling services?

Yes, we offer complimentary personal styling consultations in our Vienna store. Our stylists will work with you to create looks that suit your style, body type, and occasion. Appointments are recommended but walk-ins are welcome based on availability.
