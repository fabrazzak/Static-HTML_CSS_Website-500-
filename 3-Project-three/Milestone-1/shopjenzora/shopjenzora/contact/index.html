Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about shopping at Jenzora

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day return policy for all unworn items with original tags attached. Returns are free for customers in Austria.

Do you offer international shipping?

Yes, we ship to most countries in Europe. Shipping costs and delivery times vary depending on the destination.

How can I track my order?

Once your order ships, you'll receive a confirmation email with a tracking number and link to track your package.

Do you offer gift wrapping?

Yes, we offer complimentary gift wrapping for all items purchased in-store. For online orders, select the gift wrapping option at checkout.

Can I reserve items to try in store?

Absolutely! Call us or email with the items you're interested in, and we'll have them ready for you to try when you visit.

Do you offer alterations?

Yes, we work with expert tailors and can arrange alterations for most items purchased in our store.
