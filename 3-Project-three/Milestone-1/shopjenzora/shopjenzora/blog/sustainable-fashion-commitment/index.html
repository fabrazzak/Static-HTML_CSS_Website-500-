Sustainable Fashion: Our Commitment to a Better Future

Discover how Jenzora is working towards a more sustainable future in fashion through ethical practices, eco-friendly materials, and transparent supply chains that respect both people and planet.

September 5, 2025
12 min read
Sustainability

The fashion industry stands at a critical crossroads. As one of the world's largest polluters and consumers of resources, it has an undeniable responsibility to evolve toward more sustainable practices. At Jenzora, we believe that beautiful fashion shouldn't come at the expense of our planet or its people. This conviction drives our comprehensive sustainability strategy, which touches every aspect of our business—from design to delivery.

Our journey toward sustainability isn't a destination but an ongoing process of learning, improving, and innovating. We're committed to transparency about both our progress and our challenges, because we believe that honest dialogue is essential for meaningful change in our industry. In this article, we'll share our current initiatives, future goals, and how you can join us in building a more sustainable fashion future.

Our Sustainable Materials Initiative

The foundation of sustainable fashion lies in the materials we use. Traditional textile production is notoriously resource-intensive, with conventional cotton farming alone accounting for 16% of all insecticides used globally and requiring enormous amounts of water. That's why we've made a commitment to transition to more sustainable materials across our collections.

Currently, 68% of our collection incorporates sustainable materials, and we're on track to reach 90% by the end of 2026. This includes organic cotton, recycled polyester, Tencel™ lyocell, linen, hemp, and innovative new materials like Piñatex (made from pineapple leaf fibers) and Mylo™ (a mushroom-based leather alternative).

Our Material Transition Progress:

Organic Cotton 85%
Recycled Polyester 75%
Tencel™ Lyocell 90%
Innovative Materials 40%

Ethical Production & Fair Labor Practices

True sustainability encompasses not just environmental responsibility but social equity as well. We believe that garments made with respect for the planet should also be made with respect for the people who create them. Our ethical production commitment ensures fair wages, safe working conditions, and humane treatment for everyone in our supply chain.

We maintain long-term relationships with our manufacturing partners and conduct regular audits to ensure compliance with our strict Code of Conduct. This includes prohibitions on forced labor, child labor, discrimination, and excessive working hours. We also invest in community development programs in the regions where our products are made, supporting education, healthcare, and women's empowerment initiatives.

"Sustainability isn't a marketing strategy for us—it's a moral imperative. We're building a business that our children can be proud of, one thoughtful decision at a time."

Reducing Our Carbon Footprint

The fashion industry contributes an estimated 10% of global carbon emissions—more than all international flights and maritime shipping combined. At Jenzora, we're taking concrete steps to reduce our carbon footprint through energy efficiency, renewable energy, and smarter logistics.

Our Vienna flagship store runs on 100% renewable energy, and we've implemented energy-saving measures throughout our operations. We've optimized our shipping routes to minimize transportation emissions, and we're gradually transitioning to electric delivery vehicles for local orders. For e-commerce shipments, we use minimal, recyclable packaging and carbon-neutral shipping options.

Circular Fashion Initiatives

The linear "take-make-waste" model of fashion is unsustainable. That's why we're embracing circularity—designing waste out of the system and keeping materials in use for as long as possible. Our circular initiatives include:

Garment Recycling Program

Bring back any Jenzora item (from any season) and receive credit toward your next purchase when we recycle it responsibly.

Repair Services

Our in-house tailors repair damaged garments, extending their lifespan and reducing waste.

Design for Longevity

We create timeless pieces with quality construction meant to last for years, not just a season.

Rental & Resale

We're developing programs for renting special occasion wear and reselling pre-loved Jenzora items.

Transparency & Traceability

We believe that transparency is the foundation of accountability. That's why we're working to provide full traceability for our products, from raw material to finished garment. Through our partnership with blockchain technology providers, we're developing a system that will allow customers to scan a QR code and see the journey of their garment—where the materials came from, where it was made, and its environmental impact.

We're also transparent about our challenges and shortcomings. Sustainability is a complex journey with no perfect solutions. When we make mistakes or encounter obstacles, we're committed to sharing these openly and using them as learning opportunities to improve.

How You Can Participate in Sustainable Fashion

Building a more sustainable fashion industry requires all of us—brands and consumers alike. Here are meaningful ways you can participate in the movement:

Consumer Action Guide:

  • Buy Less, Choose Well: Invest in quality pieces that will last for years rather than following fleeting trends
  • Care for Your Clothes: Proper washing, storing, and repairing can significantly extend a garment's life
  • Embrace Secondhand: Explore vintage and consignment options before buying new
  • Learn About Materials: Choose natural, organic, and recycled fibers when possible
  • Support Transparent Brands: Reward companies that are honest about their practices and progress
  • Advocate for Change: Use your voice to ask all brands to prioritize people and planet

Our Future Sustainability Goals

While we're proud of the progress we've made, we know there's much more work to be done. Our ambitious 2030 sustainability roadmap includes:

  • 100% sustainable materials across all collections
  • Carbon-neutral operations and shipping
  • Zero waste packaging solutions
  • Water reduction targets across our supply chain
  • Expanded circularity programs including rental, repair, and resale

The journey toward a truly sustainable fashion industry is long and complex, but we're committed to walking this path with integrity, transparency, and relentless innovation. We invite you to join us—as customers, critics, and collaborators—in reimagining what fashion can be when people and planet come first.

Together, we can build a fashion industry that doesn't just look beautiful but does beautiful things for the world.
