Quick answers to common questions

We welcome news tips and story ideas from our readers. Please email your suggestions to tips@radaroftech.com with as much detail as possible. Our editorial team reviews all submissions and will follow up if we're interested in pursuing your story.

For advertising inquiries, please contact our partnerships team at advertising@radaroftech.com. We offer various advertising options including display ads, sponsored content, newsletter sponsorships, and custom partnerships tailored to your marketing goals.
