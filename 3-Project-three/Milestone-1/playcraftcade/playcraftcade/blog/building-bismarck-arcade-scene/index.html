Building Bismarck's Arcade Scene: The PlayCraftCade Story

👤 The PlayCraftCade Team
📅 December 15, 2024
⏱️ 7 min read
👁️ 1,742 views

Every great arcade has a story, and PlayCraftCade's journey from dream to reality is one of passion, perseverance, and the unwavering belief that Bismarck, North Dakota deserved something truly special. This is our story – the challenges we faced, the victories we celebrated, and how we built more than just an arcade.

🌟 The Dream Begins

It all started with a simple observation: Bismarck had amazing restaurants, great nightlife, and a thriving community, but it was missing something that could bring people together across all ages and backgrounds. We envisioned a place where a grandfather could teach his grandson the art of pinball, where friends could bond over Street Fighter matches, and where families could create lasting memories over great food and classic games.

The idea wasn't just to open another entertainment venue – it was to create a cultural hub that celebrated the golden age of arcade gaming while serving up authentic experiences that couldn't be replicated at home. We wanted to build something that honored the past while embracing the future.

🎯 The Vision Takes Shape

From the beginning, we knew PlayCraftCade had to be more than just a collection of old games. We wanted to create a complete experience that combined three passions: authentic arcade gaming, exceptional food, and innovative craft beer service.

🎮 Our Core Vision

🕹️ Authentic Gaming

Source and restore original arcade cabinets to museum-quality standards, ensuring every game plays exactly as it did during the golden age.

🍕 Exceptional Food

Serve authentic New York-style pizza that rivals anything found in the Big Apple, using traditional techniques and premium ingredients.

🍺 Innovative Beer Service

Pioneer self-pour beer wall technology in North Dakota, giving customers unprecedented control over their craft beer experience.

🏠 Community Hub

Create a welcoming space where gaming culture can flourish and where people of all ages can connect over shared experiences.

🎨 Retro Atmosphere

Design an environment that transports visitors back to the golden age of arcade gaming with authentic sights, sounds, and energy.

📈 Quality Over Quantity

Focus on doing fewer things exceptionally well rather than spreading resources thin across too many concepts.

🔍 The Hunt for Perfect Machines

Building an authentic arcade meant finding authentic machines – and that was easier said than done. Original arcade cabinets from the golden age are becoming increasingly rare, and those that remain are often in various states of disrepair.

Our journey took us across the country, from abandoned warehouses in California to collector basements in New York. We learned to distinguish between original cabinets and reproductions, to identify signs of proper restoration versus quick fixes, and to negotiate with collectors who were as passionate about preserving gaming history as we were.

🔎

The Search Process

Every machine had to meet our strict criteria: original hardware when possible, authentic artwork, proper CRT monitors, and working condition. We traveled thousands of miles to inspect potential acquisitions.

🔧

Restoration Challenges

Many machines required extensive restoration. We partnered with skilled technicians who specialized in vintage arcade hardware, learning the intricate details of each system's quirks and requirements.

🎨

Artwork Preservation

Original cabinet artwork was often faded or damaged. We worked with artists who specialized in arcade restoration to recreate missing elements while preserving as much original material as possible.

📱

Modern Upgrades

While maintaining authenticity, we selectively added modern conveniences like LED coin door lighting and improved wiring, always ensuring changes were reversible and didn't compromise the original experience.

🏗️ Creating the Perfect Space

Finding the right location in Bismarck was crucial to our success. We needed a space large enough to accommodate 80+ arcade machines, a full commercial kitchen, and our innovative beer wall, while still maintaining the intimate atmosphere that makes arcade gaming special.

After months of searching, we found our home – a space with the perfect combination of size, accessibility, and character. But finding the space was just the beginning. Transforming it into PlayCraftCade required months of careful planning and construction.

🏢 Space Planning & Design

Every square foot of PlayCraftCade was designed with purpose. We studied the flow patterns of successful arcades, analyzed sight lines for optimal game placement, and considered acoustics to ensure that the cacophony of games created atmosphere rather than noise.

Design Phase: 85% Historical Research, 15% Innovation

Key Design Elements:

  • Lighting Design: Carefully balanced ambient lighting that captures the feel of classic arcades while ensuring comfortable gameplay
  • Sound Management: Acoustic treatments that allow each game's audio to shine without creating overwhelming noise
  • Traffic Flow: Strategic placement of high-traffic games and clear pathways to prevent bottlenecks
  • Social Spaces: Designated areas for spectating, socializing, and enjoying food and drinks
  • Authentic Atmosphere: Period-appropriate décor and memorabilia that transport visitors to the golden age

👥 Building the Dream Team

PlayCraftCade's success isn't just about games and food – it's about the passionate team of individuals who bring the experience to life every day. Building this team was one of our most important challenges and greatest achievements.

"We didn't just hire employees; we found fellow enthusiasts who shared our vision and passion for creating something special in Bismarck." - PlayCraftCade Management Team

🍕 Kitchen Team

Led by experienced pizza artisans who trained in authentic NY techniques. Every team member understands that great pizza requires precision, patience, and passion.

🔧 Tech Team

Skilled technicians who maintain our arcade machines and beer wall technology. They combine old-school repair knowledge with modern technical skills.

🍺 Beer Specialists

Certified beer servers who understand craft beer styles and can guide customers through our rotating selection of 20 taps.

🎮 Gaming Ambassadors

Passionate gamers who can teach newcomers, facilitate tournaments, and share the history and strategies of classic arcade games.

🚧 Overcoming Challenges

Building PlayCraftCade wasn't without its obstacles. From supply chain issues during the pandemic to the complexities of implementing cutting-edge beer technology in North Dakota, every challenge taught us valuable lessons and made us stronger.

Major Challenges We Faced:

  • Equipment Shortages: The pandemic created delays in arcade machine deliveries and restaurant equipment installation
  • Regulatory Navigation: Working with local authorities to approve our innovative self-pour beer system
  • Skilled Labor Shortage: Finding technicians experienced in both vintage arcade repair and modern food service equipment
  • Supply Chain Disruptions: Securing consistent supplies of specialty ingredients and craft beer during uncertain times
  • Technology Integration: Seamlessly blending retro gaming with modern payment and management systems

🎉 Opening Day and Beyond

After months of planning, construction, and preparation, opening day finally arrived. The response from the Bismarck community exceeded our wildest expectations. Lines formed before we opened, and the energy in the room was electric as people experienced something truly new in their city.

But opening was just the beginning. We've continued to evolve and improve based on customer feedback and our own observations. We've added new games, refined our food offerings, expanded our beer selection, and most importantly, built relationships with our customers that go far beyond simple transactions.

Milestones Since Opening:

  • Month 1: Served our 1,000th pizza slice
  • Month 3: Hosted our first arcade tournament with 50+ participants
  • Month 6: Expanded to 80+ arcade machines
  • Month 9: Launched our loyalty program and mobile app
  • Month 12: Celebrated our first anniversary with record-breaking attendance

🌟 Impact on Bismarck's Culture

What we're most proud of isn't just the business we've built – it's the impact PlayCraftCade has had on Bismarck's cultural landscape. We've watched shy kids become confident gamers, seen friendships form over fighting games, and witnessed multiple generations bonding over shared gaming experiences.

We've become a gathering place for the local gaming community, hosting tournaments, leagues, and special events that bring people together. Our space has become a venue for birthday parties, corporate events, and casual hangouts that create lasting memories.

🔮 Looking to the Future

PlayCraftCade's story is far from over. We continue to dream big and plan for the future. We're constantly seeking new games to add to our collection, exploring new food and beverage offerings, and finding innovative ways to enhance the customer experience.

We're also committed to preserving gaming history for future generations. Every machine we restore, every game we maintain, and every story we share contributes to keeping the golden age of arcade gaming alive and accessible.

Upcoming Projects and Goals:

  • Expansion Plans: Adding more rare and unique arcade machines to our collection
  • Community Programs: Educational workshops on gaming history and arcade machine restoration
  • Tournament Series: Organized competitive gaming events with prizes and recognition
  • Preservation Efforts: Documenting and sharing the history of each machine in our collection
  • Partnerships: Collaborating with other gaming venues and preservation organizations

🤝 Join Our Story

The story of PlayCraftCade isn't just our story – it's Bismarck's story, and it's the story of everyone who walks through our doors. Every high score achieved, every pizza shared, every beer tasted, and every friendship formed adds another chapter to our ongoing narrative.

We invite you to become part of our story. Whether you're a gaming veteran looking to relive the glory days, a newcomer curious about arcade culture, or a family seeking a unique entertainment experience, PlayCraftCade has something special waiting for you.

Building PlayCraftCade was never just about creating a business – it was about creating a legacy. We've built something that celebrates the past while embracing the present, something that brings people together and creates lasting memories.

Every day, we see the impact of what we've created. Children experience the joy of classic games for the first time, adults rediscover childhood favorites, and families create new traditions around shared gaming experiences. This is the true measure of our success.
