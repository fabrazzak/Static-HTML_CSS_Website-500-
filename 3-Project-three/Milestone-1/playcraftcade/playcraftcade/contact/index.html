❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Do you take reservations?

We don't take regular reservations, but we recommend calling ahead for large groups (8+ people). For private events and parties, advance booking is required.

Are kids allowed?

We're a 21+ establishment most days, but every Sunday is Family Funday when ALL AGES are welcome! Perfect for bringing the whole family.

Do you offer catering?

Yes! We offer catering services for events and parties. Contact us to discuss your catering needs and we'll create a custom package for you.

How does the self-pour beer wall work?

Our self-pour system lets you sample and pour exactly what you want! You pay by the ounce, so you can try multiple beers or stick with your favorite. Staff will show you how it works!

Are the arcade games free to play?

Most of our classic arcade games are free play! Some newer games may require tokens or quarters. Come enjoy unlimited retro gaming with your food and drinks!
