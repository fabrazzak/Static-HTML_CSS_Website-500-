🍪 Cookies Policy

Learn how we use cookies and tracking technologies to enhance your PlayCraftCade experience.

Last Updated: September 20 , 2025

This Cookies Policy explains how PlayCraftCade ("we," "our," or "us") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our website and digital services. By using our website, you consent to our use of cookies as described in this policy.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) when you visit a website. They contain information that helps websites remember your preferences, login status, and other details to improve your browsing experience.

Types of Cookies

  • Session Cookies: Temporary cookies that are deleted when you close your browser
  • Persistent Cookies: Cookies that remain on your device for a set period or until manually deleted
  • First-Party Cookies: Set directly by our website
  • Third-Party Cookies: Set by external services we use (analytics, advertising, etc.)

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for various purposes to enhance your experience on our website and improve our services:

Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for our website to function properly and cannot be disabled:

  • Remember your shopping cart contents
  • Keep you logged in to your account
  • Remember your privacy and cookie preferences
  • Enable secure payment processing
  • Prevent spam and security threats

Performance and Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website:

  • Track website traffic and user behavior
  • Identify popular content and features
  • Monitor website performance and loading speeds
  • Help us improve our website design and functionality
  • Generate reports on website usage patterns

Functional Cookies

These cookies enhance your website experience by remembering your preferences:

  • Remember your language and region settings
  • Save your arcade game preferences
  • Remember items you've viewed or added to favorites
  • Customize content based on your interests
  • Enable social media sharing features

Marketing and Advertising Cookies

These cookies help us deliver relevant advertisements and marketing messages:

  • Show you relevant ads on other websites
  • Measure the effectiveness of our advertising campaigns
  • Prevent you from seeing the same ads repeatedly
  • Track conversions from our marketing efforts
  • Enable targeted promotions based on your interests

3. Specific Cookies We Use

PlayCraftCade Essential Cookies

  • playcraftcade-session: Maintains your session while browsing
  • playcraftcade-cart: Remembers items in your shopping cart
  • playcraftcade-auth: Keeps you logged in to your account
  • playcraftcade-preferences: Stores your site preferences

Third-Party Service Cookies

  • Google Analytics: _ga, _ga_*, _gid - Website traffic analysis
  • Google Ads: _gcl_* - Ad performance tracking
  • Facebook Pixel: _fbp, _fbc - Social media advertising
  • Stripe: __stripe_* - Secure payment processing

4. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

Browser Settings

You can control cookies through your web browser settings. Here's how to manage cookies in popular browsers:

  • Chrome: Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Preferences > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions > Cookies and site data

Cookie Consent Manager

When you first visit our website, you'll see a cookie consent banner that allows you to:

  • Accept all cookies for the full website experience
  • Reject non-essential cookies while keeping essential ones
  • Customize your preferences by cookie category
  • Change your preferences at any time

Note: Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality, including your ability to place orders, save preferences, or access certain features.

5. Other Tracking Technologies

Web Beacons

Small transparent images embedded in web pages or emails that help us track user behavior and email engagement.

Local Storage

Browser storage that allows us to save data locally on your device for improved performance and user experience.

Session Storage

Temporary storage that maintains information during your browsing session and is cleared when you close your browser.
