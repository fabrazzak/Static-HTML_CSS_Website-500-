Our Story

Founded in 2020, MakerMind Academy began as a small creative studio in Val-d'Or, Quebec. What started as a passion project to share DIY skills with the local community has evolved into a premier online academy serving thousands of creative minds worldwide.

Our founders, Sarah Chen and Michael Rodriguez, noticed a gap in quality creative education that combined traditional craftsmanship with modern techniques. They envisioned a place where aspiring makers could learn from industry experts in an encouraging, supportive environment.

Today, we're proud to offer comprehensive courses in DIY crafts, fashion design, and home decoration, helping students transform their creative passions into valuable skills and even thriving businesses.

Our Journey

From humble beginnings to becoming a leading creative education platform.

The Beginning

Sarah and Michael opened their first studio in Val-d'Or, offering weekend workshops to the local community. Word spread quickly about their engaging teaching style.

2020

Going Digital

The pandemic pushed us online, where we discovered we could reach students worldwide. Our first online course launched with 50 eager students.

2021

Major Expansion

We expanded our team and curriculum, adding fashion design and home decor courses. Our student community grew to over 1,000 members.

2022

Recognition

MakerMind Academy received the "Excellence in Creative Education" award and was featured in major lifestyle magazines.

2023

Global Impact

We now serve over 5,000 students across 40 countries, with plans to launch mobile learning and AI-powered personalized instruction.
