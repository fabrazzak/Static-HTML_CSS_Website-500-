Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers to help you make the right choice.

When do courses start?

New courses start every month. Once you enroll, you can begin immediately with our self-paced modules, and join the next live session cohort within 2-4 weeks of enrollment.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?

We offer full refunds within 14 days of enrollment if you haven't accessed more than 25% of the course content. After that, you can transfer to a future cohort for a small fee.

Do I need any prior experience?

Our courses are designed for all skill levels. Beginner courses assume no prior knowledge, while intermediate and advanced courses will specify prerequisites in the course description.

What materials do I need?

Each course comes with a detailed materials list. We also offer starter kits for purchase, and many materials can be found at local craft stores. Digital resources are provided for all courses.

Is there ongoing support after the course?

Yes! You'll have access to our alumni community, monthly Q&A sessions with instructors, and course materials for lifetime access. We're here to support your creative journey long after you graduate.
