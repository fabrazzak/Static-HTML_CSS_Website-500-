Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about contacting us and our services.

How quickly will you respond to my message?

We typically respond to all inquiries within 24 hours during business days. For urgent matters, please call us directly at +1 (819) 824-3146.

Can I visit your campus for a tour?

Yes! We offer campus tours by appointment. Contact us to schedule a visit and see our facilities, meet our instructors, and learn about our programs firsthand.

Do you offer phone consultations?

Absolutely! Our enrollment advisors are available for free phone consultations to help you choose the right course and answer any questions about our programs.

What languages do you support?

We provide support in English and French. Our bilingual team can assist you in your preferred language.

How can I provide feedback about a course?

We welcome all feedback! You can submit course reviews through our contact form, email us directly, or speak with our student services team. Your input helps us improve our programs.
