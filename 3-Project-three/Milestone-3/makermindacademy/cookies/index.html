1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit websites. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and provide a better, more personalized user experience. At MakerMind Academy, we use cookies to enhance your learning journey and improve our educational platform.

These small files contain information that websites can read when you visit them again. Cookies help websites remember your preferences, login status, shopping cart contents, and other useful information that makes your browsing experience smoother and more convenient.

Why Do We Use Cookies?

We use cookies to provide you with the best possible learning experience, remember your course progress, personalize content recommendations, and analyze how our platform is being used to make continuous improvements.

Cookie Functionality

Cookies serve various purposes on our platform. They can remember your login credentials so you don't have to enter them every time, keep track of items in your course wishlist, remember your language preferences, and help us understand which courses are most popular among our students.

Without cookies, websites would treat every page visit as the first time you've ever visited, which would make for a frustrating and inefficient user experience. Cookies are essential for the modern web to function properly.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

MakerMind Academy uses different types of cookies to serve various purposes on our platform. Understanding these different types helps you make informed decisions about your cookie preferences.

Session vs. Persistent Cookies

Session Cookies: These temporary cookies are deleted when you close your browser. They help maintain your session while you navigate through our courses and platform features.

Persistent Cookies: These cookies remain on your device for a specified period or until you delete them manually. They remember your preferences and settings for future visits.

First-Party vs. Third-Party Cookies

First-Party Cookies: Set directly by MakerMind Academy, these cookies are essential for our platform's core functionality and your personalized learning experience.

Third-Party Cookies: Set by external services we use, such as analytics providers, payment processors, and social media platforms to enhance our services.

10. Browser Cookie Settings

Most web browsers provide built-in tools for managing cookies. You can use these browser settings to control how cookies are handled across all websites you visit, not just MakerMind Academy.

Common Browser Settings

Google Chrome: Go to Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies and other site data. Here you can block all cookies, block third-party cookies, or manage cookies for specific sites.

Mozilla Firefox: Visit Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data. Firefox offers options to block all cookies, block third-party cookies, or use strict privacy protection.

Safari: Navigate to Preferences > Privacy > Manage Website Data. Safari provides intelligent tracking prevention and options to block all cookies or cross-site tracking.

Microsoft Edge: Go to Settings > Cookies and site permissions > Cookies and site data. Edge offers balanced, strict, or custom cookie blocking options.

Mobile Device Settings

Mobile browsers and apps also provide cookie management options. On iOS devices, you can manage cookies through Safari settings in the main Settings app. Android users can control cookies through their browser settings or Chrome app preferences.

Impact of Blocking Cookies

Blocking certain types of cookies may impact your experience on MakerMind Academy. Essential cookies are required for basic functionality, while blocking performance or functional cookies may reduce personalization and platform optimization capabilities.
