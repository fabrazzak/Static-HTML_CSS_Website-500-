About Motor Matrix Auto

Over two decades of automotive excellence, serving Winnipeg with premium pre-owned luxury vehicles and unmatched customer service.

Our Story

From humble beginnings to becoming Winnipeg's premier destination for luxury pre-owned vehicles

Excellence Through Experience

Founded in 2003, Motor Matrix Auto began as a small family-owned dealership with a simple vision: to provide Winnipeg with access to the finest pre-owned luxury vehicles while delivering an exceptional customer experience that goes beyond the sale.

What started as a passion project has evolved into one of Manitoba's most trusted automotive destinations. Our founder's commitment to quality, transparency, and customer satisfaction has been the driving force behind our growth and success.

Today, we proudly serve customers from across Manitoba and beyond, maintaining the same family values and personal touch that have defined us from the beginning.

Commitment to Quality

Every vehicle that enters our showroom undergoes our rigorous 120-point inspection process. We believe that our customers deserve nothing less than perfection, which is why we invest heavily in ensuring each vehicle meets our exacting standards.

Our state-of-the-art service facility is equipped with the latest diagnostic equipment and staffed by certified technicians who specialize in luxury automotive brands. This allows us to provide comprehensive pre-purchase inspections and ongoing maintenance services.

We're not just selling cars – we're building lasting relationships with our customers based on trust, quality, and exceptional service.
