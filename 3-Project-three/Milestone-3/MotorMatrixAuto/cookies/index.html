Cookie Policy

Understanding how we use cookies to enhance your browsing experience and website functionality

Last Updated: September 1, 2025

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) when you visit our website. They help us provide you with a better browsing experience by remembering your preferences, analyzing website traffic, and delivering personalized content.

Key Points About Cookies:

  • Cookies do not contain personal information that can identify you directly
  • They cannot access other files on your device or install software
  • Most cookies are automatically deleted when you close your browser
  • You have full control over cookie settings in your browser

Types of Cookies We Use

We use different types of cookies for various purposes on our website:

Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for our website to function properly. They enable basic features like page navigation, secure areas access, and form submissions. The website cannot function properly without these cookies.

Examples: Session management, security tokens, load balancing

Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting anonymous information about pages visited, time spent, and user behavior patterns.

Examples: Google Analytics, visitor counting, page performance

Marketing Cookies

Used to deliver relevant advertisements and track advertising campaign effectiveness. They may also be used to limit the number of times you see an advertisement.

Examples: Retargeting ads, social media integration, conversion tracking

Preference Cookies

These cookies remember your preferences and choices to provide a personalized experience, such as language settings, remembered login details, and customized content.

Examples: Language preferences, theme settings, saved searches

Data Retention

Different types of cookies are stored for different periods:

  • Session Cookies: Deleted when you close your browser
  • Persistent Cookies: Remain on your device for a set period or until manually deleted
  • Analytics Cookies: Typically stored for 2 years
  • Marketing Cookies: Usually stored for 30-90 days

You can delete cookies at any time through your browser settings or by using the cookie management tools provided above.

Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legal requirements, or our business practices. When we make changes, we will:

  • Update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy
  • Notify users through website banners or email notifications
  • Request renewed consent for significant changes
  • Maintain previous versions for reference

Your Rights

Under applicable privacy laws, you have the right to:

  • Consent: Choose which cookies to accept
  • Access: Request information about cookies stored on your device
  • Withdrawal: Change your cookie preferences at any time
  • Deletion: Request deletion of non-essential cookies
  • Portability: Receive information about your cookie preferences in a portable format

To exercise these rights or for questions about our cookie practices, please contact us using the information provided below.
