Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our vehicles and services

How do I schedule a test drive?

You can schedule a test drive by calling us at (204) 488-2052, filling out our contact form, or visiting our showroom. We recommend booking in advance to ensure the vehicle you're interested in is available.

Do you offer financing options?

Yes, we work with multiple lending institutions to provide competitive financing options. Our finance team can help you find the best rates and terms that fit your budget and credit situation.

What's included with your vehicle inspection?

Every vehicle undergoes our comprehensive multi-point inspection covering engine, transmission, brakes, suspension, electrical systems, and more. We provide detailed inspection reports and address any issues before the vehicle reaches our showroom.

Do you accept trade-ins?

Absolutely! We accept trade-ins and offer competitive valuations. Our team will assess your current vehicle and provide a fair market value that can be applied toward your next purchase.

What warranty options are available?

We offer various warranty options including manufacturer warranties, extended warranties, and certified pre-owned programs. Our service team can explain the coverage options available for your specific vehicle.

Can I get a vehicle history report?

Yes, we provide detailed vehicle history reports for all our pre-owned vehicles. These reports include accident history, service records, ownership details, and other important information to help you make an informed decision.
