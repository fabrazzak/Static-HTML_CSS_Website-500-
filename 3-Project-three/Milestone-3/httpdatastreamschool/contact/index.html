Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about DataStream School and our programs.

What are the admission requirements for your programs?

Our programs are designed to be accessible to learners from diverse backgrounds. Basic requirements include:

  • High school diploma or equivalent
  • Proficiency in English (for our English-language programs)
  • Basic computer literacy
  • Strong motivation to learn and grow

No prior experience in communication or learning methodologies is required.

Do you offer financial aid or payment plans?

Yes, we offer several options to make our programs more accessible:

  • Flexible payment plans that allow you to pay in installments
  • Scholarships for exceptional candidates
  • Corporate sponsorship options
  • Early enrollment discounts

Contact our admissions team to discuss which option might work best for you.

Can I take your programs while working full-time?

Absolutely! Our Flexible Program is specifically designed for working professionals:

  • Evening and weekend classes
  • Self-paced learning components
  • 24-week duration to allow for balanced study
  • Recorded sessions for when you miss a class

Many of our students successfully complete the program while maintaining full-time employment.

What kind of support do you offer students?

We provide comprehensive support throughout your learning journey:

  • Dedicated student success advisors
  • 24/7 access to learning materials
  • Regular one-on-one check-ins with instructors
  • Peer learning communities
  • Career guidance and portfolio development
  • Technical support for our learning platform
Do you offer job placement assistance?

Yes, we have a robust career services program that includes:

  • Resume and portfolio review
  • Interview preparation
  • Access to our employer network
  • Career coaching sessions
  • Alumni networking events

While we can't guarantee employment, our graduates have a 95% success rate in achieving their career goals within 6 months of completion.
