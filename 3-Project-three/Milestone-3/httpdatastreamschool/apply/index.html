Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our application process and programs.

What are the admission requirements?

We welcome applicants from diverse backgrounds. While there are no strict prerequisites, we look for individuals with a strong motivation to improve their communication and learning skills. Basic proficiency in English is required for our English-language programs.

How long does the application process take?

The initial application takes about 15-20 minutes to complete. After submission, our admissions team will review your application and contact you within 3-5 business days to schedule an interview. The entire process from application to decision typically takes 1-2 weeks.

Is financial aid available?

Yes, we offer several financing options including payment plans and limited scholarships for qualified applicants. Please contact our admissions team for more information about financial assistance options.

Can I apply if I'm not available for the live sessions?

While live sessions are an important part of our programs, we understand that schedules can be challenging. Our flexible program includes recorded sessions and alternative participation options. Please discuss your situation with our admissions team during your interview.

What happens after I submit my application?

After submitting your application, our admissions team will review it and contact you to schedule a brief interview. This interview helps us understand your goals and ensures our program is the right fit for you. You'll receive an admission decision within 5-7 business days after your interview.
