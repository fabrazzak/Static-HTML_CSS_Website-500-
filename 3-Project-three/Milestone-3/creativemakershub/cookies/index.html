Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how Creative Makers Hub uses cookies and similar technologies to recognize you when you visit our website.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit our website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and provide information to the website owners.

Cookies allow a website to recognize a user's device and remember their preferences, settings, or login information. They can also be used to track user behavior for analytics and marketing purposes.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

We use different types of cookies on our website for various purposes:

Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. They enable core functionality such as security, network management, and accessibility.

  • Session management
  • Security features
  • Load balancing
  • User authentication

Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting and reporting information anonymously.

  • Visitor counts
  • Traffic sources
  • User behavior
  • Page performance

Preference Cookies

These cookies enable the website to provide enhanced functionality and personalization based on your choices.

  • Language settings
  • Region selection
  • Layout preferences
  • Course progress

Marketing Cookies

These cookies are used to track visitors across websites to display relevant and engaging advertisements.

  • Ad performance
  • Retargeting
  • Conversion tracking
  • Audience insights

4. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for several important purposes:

  • Authentication: To identify you when you visit our platform and keep you logged in during your session
  • Security: To protect your account and our platform from malicious activities
  • Preferences: To remember your settings and preferences, such as language and region
  • Analytics: To understand how our platform is used and how we can improve it
  • Personalization: To provide you with content and course recommendations based on your interests
  • Marketing: To deliver relevant advertisements and measure their effectiveness

5. Third-Party Cookies

In addition to our own cookies, we may also use various third-party cookies to report usage statistics of the platform, deliver advertisements on and through the platform, and so on.

These third-party services may include:

  • Google Analytics: For understanding how users interact with our platform
  • Facebook Pixel: For measuring the effectiveness of our advertising
  • Hotjar: For analyzing user behavior and improving user experience
  • Stripe: For processing payments securely
  • Mailchimp: For managing email communications

These third-party services have their own privacy policies and may use cookies and tracking technologies independently of our control.

6. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can exercise your cookie preferences by:

Cookie Settings

Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function and cannot be switched off. They are usually only set in response to actions made by you.

Analytics Cookies

These cookies allow us to count visits and traffic sources so we can measure and improve the performance of our site.

Preference Cookies

These cookies enable the website to provide enhanced functionality and personalization.

Marketing Cookies

These cookies may be set through our site by our advertising partners to build a profile of your interests.

You can also manage cookies through your browser settings. Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings preferences. However, limiting cookies may affect your experience on our website.

Browser-Specific Instructions:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and other site data
  • Mozilla Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies and website data
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Privacy, search, and services → Cookies

We encourage you to periodically review this Cookie Policy to stay informed about how we are using cookies.
