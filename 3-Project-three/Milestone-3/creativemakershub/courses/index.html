DIY Program Curriculum

1

Foundation Skills (2.5 weeks)

Master essential DIY fundamentals including tool usage, safety protocols, material selection, and basic techniques across multiple crafting disciplines.

  • Tool Mastery & Workshop Safety
  • Material Science & Selection
  • Measurement & Planning Techniques
  • Basic Joinery & Assembly Methods
2

Woodworking Mastery (3 weeks)

Develop professional woodworking skills from furniture making to decorative pieces. Learn advanced joinery, finishing techniques, and furniture restoration.

  • Advanced Joinery Techniques
  • Furniture Design & Construction
  • Wood Finishing & Staining
  • Restoration & Upcycling Methods
  • Custom Storage Solutions
3

Mixed Media Crafting (2.5 weeks)

Explore pottery, jewelry making, textile arts, and mixed media projects. Create unique decorative pieces using various materials and techniques.

  • Pottery & Ceramics Basics
  • Jewelry Design & Metalworking
  • Textile Arts & Embroidery
  • Mixed Media Art Projects
  • Sustainable Upcycling Techniques
4

Master Project & Portfolio (2 weeks)

Apply all your skills in a comprehensive master project. Build a professional portfolio and learn to market your DIY creations effectively.

  • Master Project Planning & Execution
  • Portfolio Development
  • Photography for Makers
  • Business Basics for Crafters
  • Online Marketplace Setup
