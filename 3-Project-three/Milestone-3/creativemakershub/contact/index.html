Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our programs and enrollment process.

How do I enroll in a course?

You can enroll directly through our website by visiting the enrollment page, or contact us for personalized assistance with the registration process.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes, we offer flexible payment plans for all our programs. Contact our enrollment advisors to discuss options that work for your budget.

Can I visit the studio before enrolling?

Absolutely! We encourage prospective students to visit our studio. Contact us to schedule a tour and meet our instructors.

What if I need to cancel my enrollment?

We offer a 14-day cancellation policy with a full refund if you cancel before the program start date. After the program begins, we offer partial refunds based on sessions attended.
