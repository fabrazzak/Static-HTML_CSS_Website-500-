Our Mission & Vision

Founded in 2018, Creative Makers Hub emerged from a simple observation: creative skills were becoming increasingly specialized and inaccessible to everyday people. We set out to change that.

Our vision is a world where creativity is democratized - where anyone, regardless of background or experience, can access high-quality creative education and build skills that bring joy, purpose, and even new career opportunities.

We combine expert instruction with hands-on learning, creating an environment where students don't just learn techniques - they develop creative confidence that transforms how they see themselves and their capabilities.

Our Story

From a small workshop to a thriving creative education hub - our journey of growth and impact.

2018

The Beginning

Creative Makers Hub started as a small weekend workshop in Val-d'Or, Quebec, founded by three friends passionate about sharing their creative skills. Our first class had just 12 students learning basic woodworking techniques.

2019

Expanding Our Reach

We expanded our curriculum to include fashion design and home decor programs. Moved to our current location at 490 Chem. de Vassan and established our first dedicated studio spaces for different creative disciplines.

2020

Digital Transformation

Pivoted to online learning during the pandemic, developing our signature live online format with materials kits. This allowed us to reach students across Canada and beyond, tripling our enrollment.

2022

Community Recognition

Received the Quebec Creative Education Award and established partnerships with local arts organizations. Launched our scholarship program to increase accessibility for underrepresented communities.

2024

Looking Forward

Celebrated our 2,500th student and expanded our team to 15 expert instructors. Currently developing advanced masterclass programs and planning our first international creative retreats.

Our Creative Studio

Located in the heart of Val-d'Or, our 5,000 square foot studio is designed specifically for creative learning and making. Each space is optimized for different disciplines while maintaining a cohesive, inspiring environment.

Our facility includes dedicated woodworking shops, fashion design studios with industrial equipment, flexible home decor workshop spaces, and a gallery area to showcase student work.

We believe that the environment shapes the creative experience, which is why we've invested in quality tools, natural lighting, and comfortable workspaces that encourage experimentation and collaboration.
