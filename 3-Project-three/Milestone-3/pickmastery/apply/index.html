Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about our coaching process

How long does it take to get matched with a coach?
Most applicants are matched within 24-48 hours after submitting their application. During peak times, it may take up to 72 hours.
Can I request a specific coach?
Yes! If you have a specific coach in mind, you can mention them in your application. We'll do our best to accommodate your request based on their availability.
What if I'm not satisfied with my coach?
We offer a coach replacement guarantee. If you're not satisfied after your first session, we'll match you with a different coach at no additional cost.
Do you offer refunds?
We offer a full refund if you cancel before your first session. After your first session, we offer prorated refunds for unused sessions in package deals.
What equipment do I need for coaching sessions?
You'll need a computer capable of running your game, a stable internet connection, Discord for communication, and screen sharing capabilities for VOD reviews.
Can I change my coaching package later?
Absolutely! You can upgrade or modify your coaching package at any time. Any unused sessions from your current package will be credited toward your new package.
