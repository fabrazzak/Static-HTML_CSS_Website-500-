Cookies Policy

Learn how we use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your gaming coaching experience

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer, smartphone, or other device when you visit our website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and provide information to the website owners.

Cookies help us recognize your device and remember information about your visit, such as your preferred language, login details, and other settings. This allows us to provide you with a better, more personalized gaming coaching experience.

Cookies cannot be used to run programs or deliver viruses to your computer. They are uniquely assigned to you and can only be read by the web server in the domain that issued the cookie.

2. How We Use Cookies

At PickMastery, we use cookies and similar tracking technologies for various purposes to enhance your experience on our gaming coaching platform:

Essential Cookies

These cookies are strictly necessary for the operation of our website. They enable core functionality such as security, network management, and account access. You can set your browser to block these cookies, but some parts of the site may not function properly.

  • Session management and user authentication
  • Security and fraud prevention
  • Load balancing and website performance
  • Remembering your consent preferences

Performance and Analytics Cookies

These cookies allow us to count visits and traffic sources so we can measure and improve the performance of our site. They help us know which pages are the most and least popular and see how visitors move around the site.

  • Tracking page views and user journeys
  • Monitoring website performance and speed
  • Understanding user behavior and preferences
  • Identifying technical issues and areas for improvement

Functionality Cookies

These cookies enable the website to provide enhanced functionality and personalization. They may be set by us or by third-party providers whose services we have added to our pages.

  • Remembering your login details and preferences
  • Storing your coaching session progress and settings
  • Personalizing content based on your gaming interests
  • Remembering your chat preferences and notification settings

Advertising and Marketing Cookies

These cookies may be set through our site by our advertising partners. They may be used by those companies to build a profile of your interests and show you relevant advertisements on other sites.

  • Delivering targeted coaching recommendations
  • Showing relevant gaming content and offers
  • Measuring the effectiveness of advertising campaigns
  • Limiting the number of times you see an advertisement

3. Types of Cookies We Use

We use both session cookies and persistent cookies on our website:

Session Cookies

Session cookies are temporary cookies that remain on your device until you close your web browser. They are essential for the proper functioning of our website and enable you to navigate through the pages and use essential features.

Persistent Cookies

Persistent cookies remain on your device for a set period specified in the cookie. They are activated each time you visit the website that created that particular cookie. We use persistent cookies to remember your preferences and settings.

First-Party Cookies

First-party cookies are set by our website and can only be read by our site. They are commonly used for the functionality of the site, such as remembering what coaching courses you've viewed or items in your shopping cart.

Third-Party Cookies

Third-party cookies are set by a different domain than the one you are visiting. We use third-party cookies for analytics, advertising, and social media features. These cookies may track your use of our website and other websites.

We work with trusted third-party providers who set cookies on our behalf to help us analyze how our website is used, measure the number of visitors, and improve your overall experience.

4. Specific Cookies We Use

Below are examples of specific cookies we use on the PickMastery platform:

Authentication Cookies

These cookies help us verify your account and keep you logged in during your browsing session. They contain encrypted information about your login status and user ID to maintain your session security.

Preference Cookies

These cookies remember your settings and preferences, such as language preference, dark/light mode selection, volume settings for video content, and notification preferences for coaching sessions.

Analytics Cookies

We use analytics cookies to understand how users interact with our website. These cookies collect information about which coaching courses are most popular, how long users spend on different pages, and what features they use most frequently.

Security Cookies

Security cookies help us detect malicious activities and protect both our users and our platform from potential security threats. They help prevent cross-site request forgery and other security vulnerabilities.

Social Media Cookies

These cookies are set by social media services we have added to our site to enable you to share our content with your friends and networks. They are capable of tracking your browser across other sites.

6. Other Tracking Technologies

In addition to cookies, we may use other similar technologies on our platform:

Web Beacons

Web beacons (also known as pixel tags or clear GIFs) are tiny graphics with a unique identifier that are used to track user activity. We may use web beacons to monitor traffic patterns and measure the effectiveness of our coaching content.

Local Storage

Local storage allows websites to store information on your device. We use local storage to remember your preferences and improve performance by storing certain data locally rather than repeatedly downloading it.

Session Storage

Session storage is similar to local storage but is cleared when the browsing session ends. We use session storage to temporarily store information during your visit to our website.

These technologies work alongside cookies to enhance your experience on our platform and help us understand how our services are being used.
