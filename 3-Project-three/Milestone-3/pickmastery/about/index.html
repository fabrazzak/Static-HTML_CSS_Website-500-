About PickMastery

Discover our mission to transform gaming skills through elite coaching and our commitment to building the world's best gaming education platform.

Our Story

PickMastery was born from a simple observation: talented gamers everywhere were hitting skill ceilings they couldn't break through on their own. Traditional gaming guides and YouTube tutorials could only take players so far.

Founded in 2022 by former professional esports players and gaming educators, we set out to create a platform that would connect aspiring gamers with elite coaches who could provide personalized, actionable feedback.

What started as a small community of 10 coaches has grown into a global platform with hundreds of professional instructors, helping thousands of players achieve their gaming dreams.
