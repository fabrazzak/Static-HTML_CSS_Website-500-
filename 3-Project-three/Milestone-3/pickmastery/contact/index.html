Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our services

How quickly will I hear back after submitting a contact form?
We typically respond to all contact form submissions within 24 hours during business days (Monday-Friday). For urgent matters, we recommend calling us directly.
What's the best way to get technical support?
For technical issues, emailing support@pickmastery.org is the fastest way to get help. Include screenshots and detailed descriptions of the problem for quicker resolution.
Do you offer refunds for coaching sessions?
Yes, we offer a satisfaction guarantee. If you're not happy with your first coaching session, we provide a full refund. See our refund policy for complete details.
Can I schedule a call to discuss coaching options?
Absolutely! After you submit a contact form, our team will reach out to schedule a consultation call to discuss your goals and match you with the perfect coach.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and select cryptocurrency payments. All payments are processed securely through our encrypted payment system.
Do you offer coaching for team esports organizations?
Yes, we provide specialized coaching programs for esports teams and organizations. Contact our partnerships team for custom team training solutions.
