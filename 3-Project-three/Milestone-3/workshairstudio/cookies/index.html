Cookies Policy

Learn how Work Hair Studio uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience and provide personalized services.

Last Updated: September 1, 2025

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, smartphone, tablet) when you visit our website. They help us recognize your device and remember information about your visit, such as your preferred language, location, and other settings.

Cookies play an important role in enhancing your browsing experience by making our website more efficient, personalized, and user-friendly. They allow us to understand how visitors use our site and help us improve our services.

Quick Facts About Cookies:

• Cookies cannot access or read other files on your device

• They don't contain viruses or malware

• You have full control over which cookies you accept

• Most cookies are automatically deleted when you close your browser

• Cookies help us provide you with a better experience

Types of Information Stored in Cookies

Cookies may store various types of information, including:

  • Your preferences and settings (language, location, display options)
  • Login information to keep you signed in
  • Items in your shopping cart or appointment booking
  • Information about how you use our website
  • Your device type and browser information

3. First-Party vs. Third-Party Cookies

3.1 First-Party Cookies

First-party cookies are set directly by Work Hair Studio. These cookies are used to improve your experience on our website and remember your preferences.

Our First-Party Cookies Include:

  • Session management cookies
  • Authentication and security cookies
  • User preference cookies
  • Appointment booking cookies

3.2 Third-Party Cookies

Third-party cookies are set by external services that we use on our website. These may include analytics providers, social media platforms, and advertising networks.

Third-Party Services We Use:

  • Google Analytics: Website traffic and usage analysis
  • Facebook Pixel: Social media advertising and analytics
  • Payment Processors: Secure payment transaction handling
  • Booking Systems: Appointment scheduling functionality
  • Social Media Plugins: Share buttons and embedded content

We carefully select our third-party partners and require them to handle your data responsibly and in accordance with applicable privacy laws.

4. Session vs. Persistent Cookies

4.1 Session Cookies

Session cookies are temporary cookies that expire when you close your browser. They allow you to navigate through our website efficiently and maintain your session as you move from page to page.

Common Uses of Session Cookies:

• Keeping you logged in while browsing

• Maintaining items in your booking cart

• Remembering form inputs during a session

• Providing security during your visit

4.2 Persistent Cookies

Persistent cookies remain on your device for a set period (ranging from days to years) or until you manually delete them. They help us recognize you when you return to our website and remember your preferences.

Benefits of Persistent Cookies:

• Remember your login information

• Save your language and display preferences

• Provide personalized content recommendations

• Streamline the booking process on return visits

5. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can exercise your cookie rights by setting your preferences through various methods:

5.1 Cookie Consent Tool

When you first visit our website, you'll see a cookie consent banner that allows you to:

  • Accept all cookies
  • Reject non-essential cookies
  • Customize your cookie preferences by category
  • Learn more about each cookie type before deciding

You can change your preferences at any time by clicking the "Cookie Settings" link in the footer of our website.

5.2 Browser Settings

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can set your browser to:

  • Block all cookies
  • Accept only first-party cookies
  • Notify you when a cookie is set
  • Delete cookies automatically when you close your browser

How to Manage Cookies in Popular Browsers:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies
  • Opera: Settings → Privacy & security → Cookies

5.3 Opting Out of Third-Party Cookies

You can opt out of third-party advertising cookies through:

  • Google Analytics: Install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on
  • Digital Advertising Alliance: Visit www.youradchoices.com
  • Network Advertising Initiative: Visit www.networkadvertising.org/choices
  • Social Media Settings: Adjust privacy settings on Facebook, Instagram, etc.

Important Considerations:

Blocking or deleting cookies may impact your experience on our website. Some features and services may not work properly, including:

• Online appointment booking

• Personalized service recommendations

• Saved preferences and settings

• Website performance optimizations

6. Other Tracking Technologies

In addition to cookies, we may use other similar technologies to collect information and enhance your experience:

6.1 Web Beacons (Pixels)

Web beacons are tiny graphics with unique identifiers embedded in our website and emails. They help us understand user behavior, track email open rates, and measure the effectiveness of our marketing campaigns.

6.2 Local Storage

Local storage allows websites to store larger amounts of data on your device than traditional cookies. We use local storage to:

  • Cache website resources for faster loading
  • Store user preferences locally
  • Maintain application state between sessions
  • Provide offline functionality when available

6.3 Session Storage

Similar to local storage but temporary, session storage is deleted when you close your browser tab. We use it for temporary data during your browsing session.

6.4 Device Fingerprinting

We may collect information about your device (such as browser type, operating system, screen resolution) to enhance security and prevent fraud. This information is used only for legitimate business purposes.
