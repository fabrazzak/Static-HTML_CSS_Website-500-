Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about our salon, services, and booking process.

How do I book an appointment?

You can book an appointment by calling us at (403) 471-8102, filling out our online appointment form above, or visiting us in person. We recommend booking in advance, especially for weekends and evenings, to ensure your preferred time slot.

Do you accept walk-ins?

Yes, we welcome walk-ins! However, availability cannot be guaranteed during busy times. For the best experience and to avoid waiting, we recommend booking an appointment in advance.

What is your cancellation policy?

We require 24 hours notice for cancellations or rescheduling. This allows us to offer the time slot to other clients. Late cancellations or no-shows may result in a cancellation fee.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), and debit cards. We also offer gift cards that can be purchased in-salon or over the phone.

How long will my appointment take?

Appointment times vary depending on the service: haircuts typically take 45-60 minutes, color services 2-3 hours, and balayage 3-4 hours. We'll provide an estimated time when you book your appointment.

Do you offer consultations?

Yes! We offer free consultations for all services. During your consultation, we'll discuss your hair goals, assess your hair condition, and recommend the best services and products for you.

Is parking available?

Yes, free street parking is available near our salon. We also have accessible entrance options for clients with mobility needs. Public transit is also convenient with several bus routes nearby.

Do you do bridal and special event styling?

Absolutely! We specialize in bridal and special event styling. We offer trial sessions, on-site services, and can accommodate bridal parties. Contact us well in advance to discuss your special day.
