Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This policy outlines how Work Hair Studio collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

Last Updated: September 1, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to Work Hair Studio's Privacy Policy. This policy describes how Work Hair Studio ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects your personal information when you visit our salon, use our website, or interact with our services.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is handled in a safe and responsible manner. By using our services, you consent to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Key Points:

• We collect only necessary information to provide our services

• Your information is securely stored and protected

• We never sell your personal information to third parties

• You have control over your personal data

2. Information We Collect

We collect various types of information to provide and improve our services to you:

2.1 Personal Information

When you book an appointment, register for our services, or contact us, we may collect:

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, phone number, mailing address
  • Appointment Information: Service preferences, appointment history, stylist preferences
  • Payment Information: Credit card details, billing address (processed securely through payment processors)
  • Hair Profile: Hair type, color history, allergies, sensitivities, and service notes
  • Communication Preferences: Newsletter subscriptions, appointment reminders, promotional materials

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, we automatically collect certain information:

  • Technical Information: IP address, browser type, device information, operating system
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent on pages, links clicked, referring website
  • Cookies and Tracking: We use cookies to enhance your browsing experience and analyze website traffic

2.3 Information from Third Parties

We may receive information from:

  • Social media platforms if you choose to connect your account
  • Online booking platforms and scheduling services
  • Payment processors for transaction verification
  • Marketing partners (only with your explicit consent)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for various purposes to provide, maintain, and improve our services:

3.1 Service Delivery

  • Schedule and manage your appointments
  • Provide personalized hair care services
  • Process payments and maintain billing records
  • Send appointment confirmations and reminders
  • Communicate about service updates or changes
  • Maintain accurate client records and service history

3.2 Customer Service and Communication

  • Respond to your inquiries and requests
  • Provide customer support and assistance
  • Send service-related notifications
  • Gather feedback to improve our services
  • Resolve disputes and troubleshoot problems

3.3 Marketing and Promotions

  • Send newsletters with hair care tips and salon updates (with your consent)
  • Inform you about special offers and promotions
  • Conduct customer satisfaction surveys
  • Personalize your experience based on preferences
  • Send birthday or anniversary greetings and special offers

3.4 Business Operations

  • Analyze service usage and trends
  • Improve our website and service offerings
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical issues
  • Protect against fraud and unauthorized activities
  • Comply with legal obligations and regulations
  • Maintain business records and accounting

Your Control Over Marketing Communications:

You can opt out of marketing communications at any time by clicking the "unsubscribe" link in our emails or contacting us directly. Note that even if you opt out of marketing emails, we'll still send you important service-related communications about your appointments.

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We respect your privacy and do not sell your personal information. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

4.1 Service Providers

We may share information with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating our business:

  • Payment Processors: To process transactions securely
  • Appointment Scheduling Services: To manage bookings and reminders
  • Email Service Providers: To send newsletters and communications
  • Cloud Storage Providers: To securely store client records
  • Analytics Services: To understand how our website is used

These service providers are contractually obligated to protect your information and use it only for the purposes we specify.

4.2 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to:

  • Valid legal processes (court orders, subpoenas, warrants)
  • Requests from law enforcement or government authorities
  • Protection of our legal rights and interests
  • Prevention of fraud or illegal activities
  • Protection of the safety of our staff, clients, or the public

4.3 Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, sale of assets, or bankruptcy, your personal information may be transferred to the acquiring entity. We will notify you of any such change and provide you with choices regarding your information.

4.4 With Your Consent

We may share your information with other parties when we have your explicit consent to do so, such as:

  • Sharing testimonials or before/after photos (only with written permission)
  • Referring you to partner businesses you've requested
  • Participating in promotional collaborations you've opted into

5. Data Security

We take the security of your personal information seriously and implement various measures to protect it:

5.1 Technical Safeguards

  • Encryption: We use SSL/TLS encryption for data transmission
  • Secure Storage: Data is stored on secure servers with restricted access
  • Firewalls: Network security measures protect against unauthorized access
  • Regular Updates: Systems are regularly updated with security patches
  • Access Controls: Limited employee access based on need-to-know basis

5.2 Organizational Safeguards

  • Staff training on privacy and data protection
  • Confidentiality agreements with all employees
  • Regular security audits and assessments
  • Incident response procedures for data breaches
  • Secure disposal of physical and electronic records

Important Security Note:

While we implement strong security measures, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we continuously work to protect your information using industry-standard practices.

5.3 Your Role in Security

You can help protect your information by:

  • Using strong, unique passwords for your accounts
  • Not sharing your login credentials
  • Logging out of your account after each session
  • Keeping your contact information up to date
  • Reporting any suspicious activity immediately

6. Your Rights and Choices

You have various rights regarding your personal information. We are committed to honoring these rights:

6.1 Access and Correction

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Update your contact details and preferences

6.2 Deletion and Restriction

You may request to:

  • Delete your personal information (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Restrict processing of your data under certain circumstances
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Request data portability in machine-readable format

6.3 Marketing Preferences

You can control marketing communications by:

  • Clicking "unsubscribe" in any marketing email
  • Updating your communication preferences in your account
  • Contacting us directly to opt-out
  • Adjusting cookie settings in your browser

6.4 Exercising Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information provided at the end of this policy. We will respond to your request within 30 days. Some requests may require identity verification to protect your privacy.

Please Note:

Certain information may need to be retained for legal, accounting, or business purposes. We will inform you if we cannot fulfill a deletion request and explain the reasons why.

7. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience on our website.

7.1 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit our website. They help us recognize your device and remember your preferences.

7.2 Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Required for website functionality (e.g., maintaining login sessions)
  • Performance Cookies: Help us understand how visitors use our website
  • Functionality Cookies: Remember your preferences and settings
  • Marketing Cookies: Track your browsing to display relevant advertisements (with consent)

7.3 Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through:

  • Your browser settings (most browsers allow you to refuse or accept cookies)
  • Our cookie consent tool when you first visit our website
  • Third-party opt-out tools for advertising cookies

Note that disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality and your user experience.

7.4 Third-Party Analytics

We use third-party analytics services (such as Google Analytics) to analyze website traffic and usage patterns. These services may use cookies and similar technologies. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this privacy policy.

8.1 Retention Periods

  • Client Records: Maintained for the duration of your relationship with us and up to 7 years after your last appointment for business and legal purposes
  • Financial Records: Retained for 7 years as required by tax and accounting laws
  • Marketing Data: Retained until you unsubscribe or request deletion
  • Website Analytics: Typically retained for 26 months
  • Security Logs: Retained for up to 1 year

8.2 Secure Deletion

When information is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymize it using industry-standard methods. Physical records are shredded, and electronic data is permanently deleted from our systems.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent.

For clients between 13 and 18 years of age, we require parental or guardian consent before collecting personal information or providing services.

If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly delete such information.

10. International Data Transfers

Work Hair Studio operates in Calgary, Alberta, Canada. Your personal information is stored and processed in Canada.

Some of our service providers may be located in other countries. When we transfer data internationally, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your information in accordance with applicable privacy laws.

By using our services, you consent to the transfer of your information to Canada and other countries where our service providers operate.

11. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, social media platforms, or services that are not operated by us. This privacy policy applies only to Work Hair Studio.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party sites you visit.

When you click on third-party links or interact with third-party content, those parties may collect information about you according to their own privacy policies.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or other factors.

When we make material changes to this policy, we will:

  • Update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy
  • Notify you via email if you've provided your email address
  • Post a notice on our website homepage
  • Request your consent if required by applicable law

We encourage you to review this privacy policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.

Your continued use of our services after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

13. Your Privacy Rights (Canada)

As a Canadian business, we comply with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and applicable provincial privacy legislation.

13.1 Canadian Privacy Rights

Under Canadian privacy law, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect and how it's used
  • Access your personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Withdraw consent for certain uses of your information
  • File a complaint with the Privacy Commissioner of Canada

13.2 Alberta-Specific Rights

Under Alberta's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA), you have additional rights regarding your personal information. For more information, contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta.
