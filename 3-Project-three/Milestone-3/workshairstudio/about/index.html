Our Journey

The Work Hair Studio Story

Founded in 2015, Work Hair Studio was born from a passion for transforming lives through the art of hair. What started as a dream to create Calgary's most welcoming and professional hair salon has grown into a beloved community destination where style, skill, and genuine care come together.

Our founder envisioned a space where clients wouldn't just receive a haircut or color service, but would experience a transformation that boosted their confidence and made them feel truly special. Today, that vision continues to guide everything we do.

Located in the heart of Calgary, we've built our reputation on technical excellence, ongoing education, and an unwavering commitment to client satisfaction. Every member of our team shares a dedication to staying ahead of trends while honoring the timeless techniques that form the foundation of great hair artistry.

Our Journey

Studio Timeline

Follow our journey from a small startup to Calgary's most trusted hair salon.

2015

The Beginning

Work Hair Studio opened its doors with a vision to provide exceptional hair services in a welcoming environment. Our first location featured 4 styling stations and a passionate team of 5 stylists.

2017

Expansion & Growth

Due to overwhelming client demand, we expanded to 8 stations and doubled our team. We also introduced our signature balayage and color correction services.

2019

Award Recognition

Received "Best Hair Salon in Calgary" award and launched our advanced training program for stylists, cementing our reputation for excellence.

2021

Premium Services

Introduced luxury treatment services and partnered with premium hair care brands to offer the best products to our clients.

2023

Community Impact

Launched community outreach programs and sustainable salon practices, reinforcing our commitment to giving back and environmental responsibility.

2025

Continued Excellence

Today, we serve hundreds of clients monthly, maintain our award-winning status, and continue to set the standard for hair excellence in Calgary.
