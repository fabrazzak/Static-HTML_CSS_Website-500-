Coffee Geeks Helping Coffee Lovers

At Eight Ounce Brew, we're more than just a store—we're your partners in the perfect cup. Every product we sell has been tested, approved, and loved by our team of coffee enthusiasts.

Our Story

Eight Ounce Brew was born from a simple belief: great coffee starts with great equipment. Founded by a group of coffee geeks who were frustrated with the lack of quality coffee gear available in Canada, we set out to change that.

What started as a small operation has grown into Calgary's premier destination for coffee equipment. But our mission remains the same—to help coffee lovers at every level brew better coffee at home.

We don't just sell products; we curate them. Every grinder, every espresso machine, every brewing device in our store has been personally tested by our team. If we wouldn't use it in our own kitchens, we won't sell it to you.
