Cookies Policy

Learn about how we use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your browsing experience and improve our services.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device when you visit our website. They help us provide you with a better browsing experience by remembering your preferences and analyzing how you use our site.

How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for several purposes:

  • Essential Functions: To enable core website functionality like shopping cart and checkout
  • Performance: To analyze website traffic and understand how visitors interact with our site
  • Personalization: To remember your preferences and provide a customized experience
  • Marketing: To show you relevant advertisements and measure campaign effectiveness

Types of Cookies We Use

Cookie Type Purpose Duration
Essential Cookies Required for basic website functionality, shopping cart, and secure areas Session/Persistent
Analytics Cookies Help us understand how visitors use our website (Google Analytics) 2 years
Marketing Cookies Used to show relevant ads and measure campaign performance 30-90 days
Functional Cookies Remember your preferences like language and region settings 1 year

Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View what cookies are stored on your device
  • Delete cookies individually or all at once
  • Block cookies from specific sites
  • Block all cookies (note: this may affect website functionality)

Browser Cookie Settings

Click on your browser below for instructions on managing cookies:

Chrome Settings > Privacy > Cookies
Firefox Options > Privacy & Security
Safari Preferences > Privacy
Edge Settings > Privacy

Third-Party Cookies

We may use third-party services that set cookies on your device:

  • Google Analytics: For website analytics and performance monitoring
  • Payment Processors: For secure payment processing (Stripe, PayPal)
  • Social Media: For social sharing features and advertising
  • Customer Support: For live chat and help desk functionality

Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable laws. We will notify you of any significant changes by posting the updated policy on our website.

Questions About Cookies?

If you have any questions about our use of cookies or this policy, please don't hesitate to contact us.
