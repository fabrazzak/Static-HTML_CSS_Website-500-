The Complete Aftercare Guide for Perfect Permanent Makeup Results

Everything you need to know to ensure beautiful, long-lasting permanent makeup results

Congratulations on your new permanent makeup! The journey to beautiful, long-lasting results doesn't end when you leave our studio. Proper aftercare is absolutely crucial for achieving the best possible outcome and ensuring your investment lasts as long as possible.

In this comprehensive guide, we'll walk you through every step of the healing process, from the first 24 hours to complete healing. Following these instructions carefully will help you avoid complications and achieve the beautiful, natural results you're looking for.

The First 24-48 Hours: Critical Care Period

The first two days after your permanent makeup procedure are the most important for proper healing. During this time, your skin is most vulnerable and requires special attention.

Immediate Aftercare (First 24 Hours)

  • Gently blot the area every hour with a clean tissue to remove lymph fluid
  • Apply the provided aftercare ointment very lightly (less is more)
  • Keep the area completely dry - no water, sweat, or steam
  • Sleep with your head elevated to reduce swelling
  • Avoid touching the area with unwashed hands

Important Warning

Do not let the treated area get wet during the first 7-10 days. This includes avoiding saunas, steam rooms, swimming pools, and excessive sweating from exercise.

Days 3-10: The Healing Phase

This is when you'll notice scabbing and flaking beginning to occur. This is completely normal and part of the healing process.

What to Expect

  • The area may appear darker and more intense than expected
  • Light scabbing and flaking will begin around day 4-5
  • Some itching is normal as the skin heals
  • The color will appear to fade significantly

Care Instructions

  • Continue applying aftercare ointment 2-3 times daily
  • Never pick, scratch, or peel scabs - let them fall naturally
  • Use a clean pillowcase every night
  • Avoid makeup on the treated area
  • Stay out of direct sunlight

Days 11-28: Color Return Phase

After the initial healing, you might think your permanent makeup has disappeared. Don't worry! This is the "ghost phase" where the color appears very light or almost gone. The pigment is still there, settling into the deeper layers of your skin.

During This Phase

  • Color will gradually return over the next 2-3 weeks
  • You can resume normal skincare routine
  • Gentle makeup application is now safe
  • Continue using SPF protection

Touch-Up Appointment

Your touch-up appointment (included in your service) should be scheduled 6-8 weeks after your initial treatment. This is essential for perfecting your results and ensuring longevity.

Long-Term Care for Lasting Results

Once fully healed, maintaining your permanent makeup is relatively simple but important for longevity.

Daily Maintenance

  • Use SPF 30+ sunscreen daily to prevent fading
  • Avoid harsh exfoliating products directly on the area
  • Be gentle when cleansing around the treated area
  • Schedule annual touch-ups as needed

When to Contact Us

While complications are rare when proper aftercare is followed, contact us immediately if you experience:

  • Excessive swelling beyond the first 48 hours
  • Signs of infection (pus, excessive redness, warmth)
  • Unusual pain or discomfort after the first few days
  • Any concerns about your healing process

Remember, we're here to support you throughout your healing journey. Don't hesitate to reach out with any questions or concerns. Following these aftercare instructions will help ensure you achieve the beautiful, natural-looking permanent makeup results you've always wanted.
