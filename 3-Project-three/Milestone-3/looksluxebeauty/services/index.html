Professional Aftercare

Proper aftercare ensures optimal healing and long-lasting beautiful results

Keep Clean & Dry

Gently clean the area with provided aftercare products. Avoid water, sweat, and makeup for the first 7-10 days to ensure proper healing.

Avoid Sun Exposure

Protect your investment by avoiding direct sunlight and tanning beds during healing. Use SPF 30+ once fully healed to maintain color.

Apply Healing Balm

Use the provided healing ointment as directed to keep the area moisturized and promote proper healing without over-application.

Avoid Picking

Let scabs and flakes fall off naturally. Picking can cause scarring, uneven healing, and pigment loss requiring additional touch-ups.

Stay in Touch

We're here to support you throughout healing. Contact us with any questions or concerns, and attend your follow-up appointments.

Schedule Touch-Up

Your included touch-up appointment (4-8 weeks post-treatment) is essential for perfecting your results and ensuring longevity.
