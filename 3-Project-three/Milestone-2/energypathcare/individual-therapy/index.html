Shiatsu Therapy

Often referred to as Acupuncture without the needles as both methods use the Meridian System, or as Western science sees it, the fascia, lymphatic and nervous system.

Shiatsu applies pressure to specific points on the body to stimulate energy flow, release tension, and promote natural healing. This Japanese therapy helps balance your body's energy systems for improved health and vitality.

  • Releases muscle tension and stiffness
  • Improves circulation and energy flow
  • Reduces stress and promotes relaxation
  • Supports overall wellbeing and balance

Bowen Technique

A beautifully elegant, seemingly gentle yet very powerful way to release fascia, lymph and blood allowing the body to create the space for pain and discomfort to move.

The Bowen Technique involves gentle, rolling movements over muscles, tendons, and fascia at specific points of the body. These subtle moves send signals to the nervous system to reset and heal itself, often providing relief from chronic pain.

  • Addresses chronic pain conditions
  • Improves mobility and flexibility
  • Enhances body's self-healing ability
  • Gentle enough for all ages and conditions

One of the most effective release techniques I have come across, it is stunningly effective on all types of scar tissue. No matter how old the scar, or how big or deep, it stimulates the electrical nature of fascia to reform and reconnect.

This technique has been filmed on scans in hospitals, demonstrating its remarkable ability to transform scar tissue and restore proper function to affected areas. MSRT can address both external scars and internal adhesions.
