My Healing Philosophy

To Thrive, not just Survive - That is what I want for you, me, your family and friends. For all of us. In fact, it's my passion to pass these easy tips and techniques on to you.

I show how you tap into your own power source, your own joy, resilience, and hope. Life becomes so much easier – less confusion, more clarity. Less pain both physically and mentally. Yes, it is a realistic goal.

Working with my tutors and my clients over the past 25 years, a toolbox of effective solutions, resources, techniques, and life-changing options have come together. On the one hand I help your body relax, release, remove the aches and pain and on the other, I show you how to stay pain free.

My Healing Journey

A timeline of my path to becoming a holistic healing practitioner

Early Life in Surrey

Raised in Surrey with an early fascination for natural healing and human energy systems.

Move to London

Moved to London in the 1990s, began exploring various healing modalities and energy work.

Shiatsu Training

Studied Shiatsu with Ray and Susanne Ridolfi, discovering the power of meridian-based healing.

Bowen Technique Certification

Trained in Bowen Technique with Paula Esson, adding this gentle yet powerful modality to my practice.

Move to Frome, Somerset

Moved to Frome in 2002, establishing my practice in this extraordinary town.

Energy Medicine & Scar Work

Studied Energy Medicine with Donna Eden and MSRT, expanding my holistic approach to healing.

Today: Energy Path Care

Now with over 25 years of experience, I combine all these modalities to create personalized healing journeys for each client. My practice focuses on helping people tap into their own innate healing abilities to achieve lasting wellness.

Why Treatments Don't Always Hold

Ever wondered why you go to the best practitioners, receive the best treatments your money can buy only to find yourself back with the same issues a week or so later? Well so did I, and I was the practitioner!

So I set out to find why treatments don't seem to hold long term. Through years of practice and study, I discovered that true healing requires addressing not just the physical symptoms but the underlying energy patterns that create them.

My approach combines immediate relief with long-term strategies, empowering you with techniques to maintain your wellness between sessions. This is the key to lasting transformation.
