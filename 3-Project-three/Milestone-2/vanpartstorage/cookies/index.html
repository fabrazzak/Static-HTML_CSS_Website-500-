Cookie Policy

Learn about the cookies and tracking technologies we use to improve your browsing experience.

Last updated: September 20, 2025

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your computer or mobile device when you visit our website. They help us provide you with a better browsing experience by remembering your preferences and analyzing how you use our site.

How We Use Cookies

Van Part Storage uses cookies for several purposes:

  • Essential functionality: To ensure our website works properly
  • User experience: To remember your preferences and settings
  • Analytics: To understand how visitors use our website
  • Marketing: To show you relevant advertisements
  • Security: To protect against fraud and maintain security

Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies

Purpose: These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. They enable core functionality such as security, network management, and accessibility.

Duration: Session cookies (deleted when you close your browser) or up to 1 year

Can be disabled: No - these are required for the website to work

Examples:

  • Session management and user authentication
  • Shopping cart functionality
  • Security tokens
  • Load balancing

Analytics Cookies

Purpose: These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting and reporting information anonymously.

Duration: Up to 2 years

Can be disabled: Yes

Third-party services:

  • Google Analytics - Website traffic analysis
  • Hotjar - User behavior analysis
  • Facebook Pixel - Conversion tracking

Functional Cookies

Purpose: These cookies enable enhanced functionality and personalization, such as remembering your preferences.

Duration: Up to 1 year

Can be disabled: Yes, but may affect website functionality

Examples:

  • Language preferences
  • Currency selection
  • Recently viewed products
  • Login status

Marketing Cookies

Purpose: These cookies are used to track visitors across websites to display relevant advertisements and marketing campaigns.

Duration: Up to 1 year

Can be disabled: Yes

Third-party services:

  • Google Ads - Advertising and remarketing
  • Facebook Ads - Social media advertising
  • LinkedIn Ads - Professional network advertising
  • YouTube - Video advertising

Managing Your Cookie Preferences

Browser Settings

You can control and manage cookies through your browser settings. Here's how to manage cookies in popular browsers:

Google Chrome:

Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies

Mozilla Firefox:

Options > Privacy & Security > Cookies

Safari:

Preferences > Privacy > Cookies

Microsoft Edge:

Settings > Cookies and Site Permissions

Cookie Consent Management

When you first visit our website, you'll see a cookie consent banner that allows you to:

  • Accept all cookies
  • Reject non-essential cookies
  • Customize your preferences by cookie type

Mobile Devices

For mobile devices, cookie management varies by operating system and browser:

  • iOS Safari: Settings > Safari > Block All Cookies
  • Android Chrome: Chrome app > Settings > Privacy > Clear browsing data
  • Mobile Apps: We may use similar technologies in our mobile apps, which can be managed through app settings

Other Tracking Technologies

In addition to cookies, we may use other tracking technologies:

Web Beacons

Small graphic images used to track email opens and website interactions.

Local Storage

Browser storage that persists longer than cookies and stores larger amounts of data.

Pixel Tags

Invisible images used to track user behavior and measure campaign effectiveness.

Data Protection

We take your privacy seriously an

  • Cookies are encrypted where possible
  • We minimize data collection to what's necessary
  • Third-party cookies are governed by their respective privacy policies
  • We regularly review and update our cookie practices

Cookie Retention

Different types of cookies are retained for different periods:

  • Session cookies: Deleted when you close your browser
  • Persistent cookies: Remain for a specified period (days to years)
  • First-party cookies: Set by our website, typically 1-2 years
  • Third-party cookies: Retention periods set by the third party
