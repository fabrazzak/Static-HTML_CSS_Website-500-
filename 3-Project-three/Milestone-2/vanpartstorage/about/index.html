Our Story

For over 45 years, Van Part Storage has been Canada's trusted partner in professional van equipment and upfitting services, delivering excellence one vehicle at a time.

Van Part Storage Headquarters

Oak Bluff, Manitoba, Canada

Building Excellence Since 1978

Van Part Storage began as a small family business with a simple mission: to provide commercial vehicle owners with the highest quality equipment and unmatched service. What started in a modest workshop in Oak Bluff has grown into Canada's premier destination for van upfitting solutions.

Our commitment to quality craftsmanship, innovative solutions, and customer satisfaction has made us the trusted choice for thousands of professionals across North America. From electricians and plumbers to contractors and fleet managers, we understand the unique challenges of commercial vehicle operations.

Today, we continue to lead the industry with cutting-edge products, expert installation services, and a team of professionals who share our passion for helping businesses succeed on the road.
