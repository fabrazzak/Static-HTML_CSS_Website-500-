This Cookie Policy explains how GearUp Truck uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our website. By using our website, you consent to the use of cookies as described in this policy.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, tablet, or smartphone) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and to provide information to website owners.

How Cookies Work

  • Storage: Cookies are stored locally on your device's browser
  • Information: They contain small amounts of data specific to you and our website
  • Access: They can be accessed by the website that created them when you return
  • Expiration: They have expiration dates and are automatically deleted when they expire

Similar Technologies

We also use similar technologies including:

  • Web Beacons: Small graphic images that track website usage
  • Local Storage: Browser storage that persists longer than cookies
  • Pixel Tags: Invisible images that track email opens and clicks
  • Session Storage: Temporary storage that expires when you close your browser

6. Browser Settings for Cookies

You can also control cookies through your browser settings. Here's how to manage cookies in popular browsers:

Google Chrome

  1. Click on the three dots menu → Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies
  2. Choose your preferred cookie settings
  3. Manage exceptions for specific websites

Mozilla Firefox

  1. Click on the menu button → Options → Privacy & Security
  2. Under "Cookies and Site Data," choose your settings
  3. Click "Manage Data" to view and delete specific cookies

Safari

  1. Safari menu → Preferences → Privacy
  2. Choose your cookie and website data preferences
  3. Click "Manage Website Data" for detailed control

Microsoft Edge

  1. Click on three dots menu → Settings → Cookies and site permissions
  2. Click "Cookies and site data"
  3. Configure your cookie preferences

7. Impact of Disabling Cookies

If you choose to disable certain types of cookies, it may impact your experience on our website:

Essential Cookies (Cannot be disabled)

  • Website may not function properly
  • Security features may be compromised
  • Unable to maintain session state

Performance Cookies

  • We cannot analyze website usage
  • Unable to identify and fix technical issues
  • Cannot improve website performance

Functional Cookies

  • Preferences and settings not remembered
  • Personalized content unavailable
  • Enhanced features may not work

Marketing Cookies

  • Less relevant advertisements
  • Cannot measure marketing effectiveness
  • Social media integration limited
