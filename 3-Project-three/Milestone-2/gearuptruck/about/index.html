Our Story

Over 25 Years of Excellence

GearUp Truck has been Regina's premier destination for truck accessories and professional installation services since 1997. What started as a small shop with a passion for trucks has grown into Saskatchewan's most trusted name in truck customization.

Our experienced team understands that your truck is more than just transportation—it's an extension of your lifestyle, work needs, and personal style. That's why we've dedicated ourselves to offering only the highest quality products and expert installation services.

From tonneau covers and truck caps to complete customization packages, we've helped thousands of customers transform their vehicles into exactly what they need them to be.
