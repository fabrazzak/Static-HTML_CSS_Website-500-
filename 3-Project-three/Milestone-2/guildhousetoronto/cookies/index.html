Cookie Policy

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and to provide information to website owners about how users interact with their sites.

How Cookies Work: When you visit our website, we may place cookies on your device to remember information about your visit, such as your preferred language, login status, and items in your shopping cart. This helps us provide you with a more personalized and efficient browsing experience.

Cookie Consent

We respect your privacy and provide you with control over cookie settings. You can accept or decline cookies, and modify your preferences at any time through your browser settings or our cookie management tools.

Legal Basis: Our use of cookies is based on your consent for non-essential cookies, and on legitimate interests for essential cookies that are necessary for the basic functioning of our website.

Types of Cookies We Use

We use different types of cookies to provide you with the best possible experience on our website. Each type serves a specific purpose in enhancing your interaction with our gaming store.

Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for our website to function properly. They enable basic functions like page navigation, access to secure areas, and shopping cart functionality. These cookies do not store personally identifiable information and cannot be disabled.

Performance Cookies

These cookies collect anonymous information about how visitors use our website, including which pages are most popular and if users encounter error messages. This data helps us improve our website's performance and user experience.

Functional Cookies

These cookies remember your preferences and choices to provide enhanced features and personalization. They may remember your language preference, region, or items you've added to your wish list.

Marketing Cookies

These cookies track your browsing habits to enable us to show advertising which is more likely to be of interest to you. They may be used to build a profile of your interests and show you relevant ads on other websites.

Cookie Retention and Expiration

Different types of cookies have different lifespans, depending on their purpose and the information they store. We use both session cookies and persistent cookies to serve different functions.

Session Cookies: These temporary cookies are deleted when you close your web browser. They're used for essential functions like maintaining your shopping cart contents during a single browsing session.

Persistent Cookies: These cookies remain on your device for a specified period or until you manually delete them. They're used for functions like remembering your login status and preferences between visits.

Cookie Expiration Periods:

  • Essential cookies: Session-based or up to 1 year
  • Performance cookies: Up to 2 years for analytics data
  • Functional cookies: Up to 1 year for user preferences
  • Marketing cookies: Up to 90 days for advertising purposes
  • Shopping cart cookies: 30 days or until checkout completion
  • User preference cookies: 1 year from last visit

Automatic Cleanup: We regularly review and clean up expired cookies to minimize data storage and ensure optimal website performance. You can also manually clear cookies through your browser settings at any time.
