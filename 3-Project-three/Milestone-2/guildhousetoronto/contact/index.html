Frequently Asked Questions

What are your store hours?
We're open Monday through Friday from 10:00 AM to 9:00 PM, and Saturday through Sunday from 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Holiday hours may vary.
Do you offer gaming events and classes?
Yes! We host regular D&D sessions, board game nights, miniature painting workshops, and tournaments. Check our events calendar or contact us for current schedules.
Can I reserve products online?
Absolutely! You can browse our online catalog and reserve items for pickup. We also offer delivery for orders over $100 in the GTA area.
Do you buy or trade used games?
We occasionally accept trade-ins for store credit, depending on the condition and demand for specific games. Please contact us or visit the store to discuss your collection.
Is parking available?
Yes, we have ample free parking available right in front of our store. The parking lot is well-lit and monitored for your safety.
