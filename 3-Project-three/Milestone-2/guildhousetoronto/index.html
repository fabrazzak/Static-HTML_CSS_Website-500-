About Guildhouse Toronto

Your Gaming Sanctuary Since Day One

Located in the heart of Mississauga, Guildhouse Toronto has been the premier destination for tabletop gaming enthusiasts across the Greater Toronto Area. We're more than just a store – we're a community hub where friendships are forged over epic battles, strategic conquests, and shared adventures.

Our carefully curated selection includes everything from classic board games to the latest releases, comprehensive RPG collections, premium miniatures, and professional painting supplies. Whether you're a seasoned veteran or just beginning your gaming journey, our knowledgeable staff is here to guide you to your next favorite game.
