Our Story

From humble beginnings to becoming the Greater Toronto Area's premier gaming destination

Born from Passion

Guildhouse Toronto was born from a simple yet powerful belief: that tabletop gaming has the unique ability to bring people together, spark creativity, and create lasting memories. What started as a small gathering of friends rolling dice in a basement has evolved into the GTA's most beloved gaming sanctuary.

Our founders, lifelong gaming enthusiasts, recognized a gap in the market for a truly community-focused gaming store. They envisioned a place where both seasoned veterans and curious newcomers could discover the magic of tabletop gaming in a welcoming, inclusive environment.

Today, we're proud to serve thousands of gaming enthusiasts, from families looking for their first board game to competitive players seeking the latest releases. Every product we stock, every event we host, and every interaction we have is guided by our commitment to fostering the love of gaming.

Our Mission

To create an inclusive gaming community where everyone can discover the joy of tabletop gaming. We strive to provide exceptional products, expert guidance, and memorable experiences that bring people together around the table.

Our Vision

To be the heart of the tabletop gaming community in the Greater Toronto Area, fostering connections, creativity, and lifelong friendships through the power of play. We envision a world where gaming brings people together.

Our Values

The core principles that guide everything we do

Community First

We believe in building genuine connections and fostering an inclusive environment where everyone feels welcome, regardless of their gaming experience.

Our Journey

Key milestones in our gaming adventure

2018
The Dream Begins
Founded by passionate gamers who wanted to create a true community hub for tabletop gaming in the GTA. Started with a small collection of carefully chosen games.
2019
Community Growth
Launched our first gaming events and workshops. Introduced D&D sessions that quickly became a community favorite, building our reputation as a welcoming space for all.
2020
Adapting & Thriving
Despite challenges, we adapted by offering online events, curbside pickup, and virtual game sessions, keeping our community connected during difficult times.
2022
Expanding Horizons
Expanded our miniature gaming section and introduced professional painting workshops. Became an official Games Workshop stockist, offering premium miniatures and supplies.
2025
Digital Revolution
Launched our comprehensive e-commerce platform while maintaining our personal touch, making it easier than ever for our community to access their favorite games.
