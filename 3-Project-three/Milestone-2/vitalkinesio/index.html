About Kevin Romeira

After years working in film exhibition, I made a transformative decision to pursue my lifelong passion for holistic wellness and healing. Through extensive training and research, I've established deep expertise in connecting emotional experiences, energetic imbalances, and structural issues with their physical and mental manifestations.

As a qualified kinesiologist specializing in Adaptogenesis®, I combine gentle muscle testing with natural energy principles to identify root causes of imbalances. My approach explores the memories and patterns that shape your well-being, offering lasting transformation and profound healing.

My background in French boxing (savate) enriches my practice, allowing me to bridge health and performance through comprehensive, individualized support for daily wellness, recovery optimization, and athletic preparation.
