Cookie Policy

Learn how we use cookies to enhance your browsing experience and understand how you can control your cookie preferences.

Last Updated: September 15, 2025

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and provide a better user experience. Cookies allow websites to recognize your device and remember information about your visit.

Cookie Basics

Cookies contain information such as your language preference, login status, or items in your shopping cart. They cannot access other information on your device, install malware, or harm your computer in any way.

2. How We Use Cookies

At VitalKinesio, we use cookies to enhance your experience on our website and ensure it functions properly. Our cookies help us:

  • Remember your preferences and settings
  • Understand how visitors use our website
  • Improve website performance and functionality
  • Provide personalized content and recommendations
  • Ensure website security and prevent fraud
  • Comply with legal requirements and privacy regulations

3. Types of Cookies We Use

We use different types of cookies for various purposes. Below is a detailed explanation of each type:

Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly and cannot be disabled. They are usually set in response to actions you take, such as setting privacy preferences or filling in forms.

Purpose:
  • Enable core website functionality
  • Remember your cookie consent preferences
  • Maintain session security
  • Process contact form submissions
Retention Period:

Session cookies (deleted when browser closes) to 1 year for persistent preferences

Performance & Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting anonymous information about page visits, time spent on pages, and any error messages.

Purpose:
  • Analyze website traffic and usage patterns
  • Identify popular content and pages
  • Monitor website performance and errors
  • Improve user experience based on data insights
Retention Period:

Up to 2 years, with data anonymized after 14 months

Functional Cookies

These cookies enable enhanced functionality and personalization, such as remembering your preferences and providing personalized content.

Purpose:
  • Remember your language and region preferences
  • Store your contact form information temporarily
  • Provide personalized content recommendations
  • Remember accessibility settings
Retention Period:

Up to 1 year, depending on the specific function

5. Cookie Consent and Your Choices

We respect your right to control how cookies are used on your device. When you first visit our website, you'll see a cookie consent banner that allows you to:

  • Accept All: Allow all cookies for the best experience
  • Decline: Use only essential cookies (some features may be limited)
  • Customize: Choose which types of cookies to allow

6. Managing Cookies in Your Browser

You can also control cookies through your web browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View and delete existing cookies
  • Block cookies from specific websites
  • Block all cookies (not recommended as it may break website functionality)
  • Set preferences for different types of cookies

Browser-Specific Instructions:

Google Chrome

Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies and other site data

Mozilla Firefox

Preferences > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data

Safari

Preferences > Privacy > Manage Website Data

Microsoft Edge

Settings > Cookies and site permissions > Cookies and site data

7. Cookie Retention Periods

Different cookies have different retention periods based on their purpose:

  • Session Cookies: Deleted when you close your browser
  • Persistent Cookies: Remain on your device until they expire or you delete them
  • Essential Cookies: Up to 1 year
  • Performance Cookies: Up to 2 years (anonymized after 14 months)
  • Functional Cookies: Up to 1 year

8. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under 16 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information through cookies from children under 16. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us immediately.

9. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect cookie data and ensure it cannot be accessed by unauthorized parties. This includes:

  • Encryption of sensitive cookie data
  • Secure transmission protocols (HTTPS)
  • Regular security audits of our systems
  • Access controls limiting who can access cookie data

10. International Data Transfers

Some of our third-party cookie providers may transfer data outside the European Union. When this occurs, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as:

  • European Commission adequacy decisions
  • Standard Contractual Clauses
  • Privacy Shield certification (where applicable)
  • Other legally recognized transfer mechanisms

11. Updates to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. When we make significant changes, we will:

  • Post the updated policy on our website
  • Update the "Last Updated" date
  • Notify you through a website banner or email
  • Request renewed consent where required by law

12. Your Rights

Under GDPR and French data protection law, you have specific rights regarding cookies an

  • Right to withdraw consent: Change your cookie preferences at any time
  • Right to access: Request information about how your data is processed
  • Right to rectification: Correct any inaccurate personal data
  • Right to erasure: Request deletion of your personal data
  • Right to data portability: Receive your data in a portable format
