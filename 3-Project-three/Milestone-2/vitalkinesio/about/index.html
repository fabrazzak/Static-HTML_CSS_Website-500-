About Kevin Romeira

Certified kinesiologist passionate about holistic wellness and transformative healing through Adaptogenesis® and personalized care

My Journey to Holistic Wellness

After spending years in the film exhibition industry, I experienced a profound calling to pursue my lifelong passion for holistic wellness and healing. This transformative decision led me to discover the powerful world of kinesiology and energy work.

Through extensive training and dedicated research, I've developed deep expertise in connecting emotional experiences, energetic imbalances, and structural issues with their physical and mental manifestations. My approach goes beyond surface symptoms to address the root causes of imbalance.

As a qualified kinesiologist specializing in Adaptogenesis®, I combine gentle muscle testing with natural energy principles to identify and resolve the underlying patterns that shape your well-being. Every session is a journey of discovery and healing.

My background in French boxing (savate) enriches my practice, allowing me to bridge the worlds of health and performance through comprehensive, individualized support for daily wellness, recovery optimization, and athletic preparation.
