Our Story

Nourishing a community through passion and family authenticity since 1996

Our Humble Beginnings

Bourget Grocery was founded in 1996 by the Pilote family with a simple vision: to provide the Mississauga community with the highest quality groceries while maintaining the personal touch of a family-run business. What started as a small neighborhood store has grown into a beloved local institution, serving generations of families with dedication and care.

"You all have your passions and you are complete with your differences, which means you make a good spaghetti sauce!" - Marc Pilote, Founder

For over 28 years, we've remained committed to our founding principles: quality products, exceptional service, and genuine care for our community. While we've grown and evolved, our family values remain at the heart of everything we do.

Our Mission

To nourish our community through passion and family authenticity, providing the highest quality fresh products while building lasting relationships with our customers. We believe that grocery shopping should be more than a chore - it should be an experience filled with care, quality, and connection.

Our Vision

To be Mississauga's most trusted family grocery destination, where quality meets community. We envision a future where every family has access to fresh, wholesome food and personalized service that makes them feel like part of our family.

Our Journey

1996

Grand Opening

The Pilote family opens the first Bourget Grocery location with a focus on quality meats and fresh produce. The community response is overwhelmingly positive.

2003

Expansion

We expand our store to include a full-service bakery, offering homemade donuts and fresh baked goods that quickly become customer favorites.

2010

Second Generation

The founder's children join the business, bringing fresh ideas while maintaining the family traditions that made us successful.

2018

Modernization

We launch our online ordering system with home delivery, making it easier for our customers to access our quality products.

2025

Community Recognition

Bourget Grocery receives the "Best Local Grocer" award from the Mississauga Business Association, recognizing our commitment to quality and community.
