The Secret Behind Our Famous Homemade Donuts

September 28, 2023 Alex Pilote 3 min read Bakery & Recipes

Discover how we create our beloved donuts using traditional methods and the finest ingredients

For over two decades, Bourget Grocery's homemade donuts have earned a special place in the hearts of our community. Customers often tell us they're "the best donuts they've ever eaten" - fluffy, flavorful, and made with genuine care. Today, we're pulling back the curtain to share what makes our donuts so special.

"The best donut I've ever eaten – fluffy as hell! Absolutely worth every bite." - Julie D.

Our Saint-Ambroise Bakery Heritage

Every donut we sell begins its journey in our Saint-Ambroise bakery, where we've maintained traditional baking methods passed down through generations. Unlike mass-produced donuts, each of ours is carefully prepared by hand, ensuring that personal touch that makes all the difference.

Our bakery team arrives before sunrise each day to begin the process, mixing the first batches while most of the city still sleeps. This commitment to early morning baking ensures that when you visit our store, you're getting donuts that are truly fresh - often still warm from the oven.

More Than 50 Points of Sale

While we're proud of our Mississauga location, our donuts have gained such popularity that we now supply more than 50 points of sale throughout Saguenay-Lac-St-Jean. Despite this growth, we've never compromised on our quality or traditional methods.

Each location receives daily deliveries of fresh donuts, ensuring that whether you're shopping at our main store or one of our partner locations, you're experiencing the same quality that made us famous.

Our Secret Ingredients

Made With Love

It might sound simple, but the care and attention our bakers put into every batch genuinely makes a difference. We believe food tastes better when it's made with passion.

Premium Flour

We use high-quality, unbleached flour that creates the perfect texture - light and airy yet substantial enough to satisfy.

No Preservatives

We proudly use only real ingredients with no artificial preservatives. What you taste is pure, honest baking at its finest.
