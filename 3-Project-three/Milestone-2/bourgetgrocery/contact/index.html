Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order for delivery?

Visit our grocery order page, fill out the form with your desired items (including brand and format), and include your phone number. We'll prepare your order the same day for quick delivery.

What is the minimum order for delivery?

We offer free delivery on orders of $25 or more. Tobacco purchases are excluded from this minimum requirement. Delivery fees vary by address - please refer to our delivery pricing guide.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept credit card payments over the phone for delivery orders. Cash and checks are not accepted for delivery services, but are welcome for in-store purchases.

Do you have fresh donuts available daily?

Yes! Our homemade donuts are made fresh daily in our Saint-Ambroise bakery using only fresh ingredients with no preservatives. Each donut is carefully prepared by hand with love.

Can I order custom cakes for special events?

Absolutely! We create beautiful custom cakes for special occasions. Please contact us in advance to discuss your requirements, design preferences, and pickup/delivery arrangements.

Is my personal information kept confidential?

Yes, absolutely. We take your privacy seriously. No personal information will be stored for promotional purposes or shared with other parties. Confidentiality is paramount to us.
