Current Opportunities

Experienced Butcher

Meat Department • Full-time

Do you have a passion for quality meat and exceptional service? Join our expert butcher team and showcase your skills in our premium meat department that has been serving the community for over 28 years.

What We're Looking For:

  • Minimum 3 years of butcher experience
  • Knowledge of meat cutting techniques and food safety
  • Excellent customer service skills
  • Ability to work in a fast-paced environment
  • Team player with strong communication skills

Store Manager

Management • Full-time

Become the conductor of our flourishing grocery store! We're seeking a passionate leader with exceptional expertise in grocery store management to join our dynamic team.

What We're Looking For:

  • 5+ years of retail management experience
  • Strong leadership and team management skills
  • Experience with inventory management and purchasing
  • Excellent customer service and problem-solving abilities
  • Knowledge of food safety regulations
  • Passion for quality and customer satisfaction

Team Member - Multiple Positions

Customer Service • Part-time & Full-time

We believe in the power of youth and creativity! Join our team and contribute to exciting projects while working alongside seasoned professionals in a supportive environment.

What We're Looking For:

  • Positive attitude and strong work ethic
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills
  • Ability to work flexible hours including weekends
  • Team player with initiative and creativity
  • Previous retail experience preferred but not required
  • Commitment to providing exceptional customer service

Bakery Assistant

Bakery Department • Part-time

Join our bakery team and help create our famous homemade donuts and artisan baked goods. Learn traditional baking techniques and be part of our sweet success!

What We're Looking For:

  • Interest in baking and pastry arts
  • Attention to detail and quality standards
  • Ability to work early morning shifts
  • Food handling certification (or willingness to obtain)
  • Physical ability to lift up to 50 lbs
  • Passion for creating quality baked goods

Application Process

1

Submit Your Application

Choose the position that interests you most and submit your application through our online form. Include your resume, cover letter, and any relevant certifications.

2

Initial Screening

Our HR team will review your application and contact qualified candidates within 48 hours for a brief phone screening to discuss your background and interest.

3

In-Person Interview

Successful candidates will be invited for an in-person interview where you'll meet the team, tour our facilities, and learn more about the role and our company culture.

4

Welcome to the Team

Once selected, we'll guide you through orientation and training to ensure you're fully prepared to succeed in your new role with our family.
