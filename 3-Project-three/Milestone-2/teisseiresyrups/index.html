Crafting Exceptional Flavors Since 1720

Our journey began over 300 years ago in the breathtaking French Alps, where Mathieu Teisseire discovered the art of transforming natural ingredients into extraordinary flavor experiences. What started as a small craft has evolved into a global passion for excellence.

Today, we partner with beverage creators, bartenders, and flavor enthusiasts around the world, helping them craft memorable drinks that reflect their unique style and creativity. Every bottle carries the heritage of our Alpine origins and our commitment to authentic, premium flavors.
