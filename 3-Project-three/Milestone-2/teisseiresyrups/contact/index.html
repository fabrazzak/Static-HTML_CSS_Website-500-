Frequently Asked Questions

What is your minimum order quantity?

Our minimum order quantities vary by product and customer type. For restaurants and bars, we typically require a minimum of 6 bottles per flavor. Wholesale customers have different requirements. Contact us for specific details based on your needs.

Do you offer product samples?

Yes! We provide sample bottles for qualified businesses to help you evaluate our products before placing larger orders. Contact our team to request samples with information about your business and intended use.

What are your shipping options?

We offer various shipping options including standard delivery (3-5 business days), express delivery (1-2 business days), and same-day delivery for local orders. Shipping costs depend on location, order size, and delivery speed selected.

Do you provide training for beverage professionals?

Absolutely! We offer comprehensive training programs for bartenders and beverage managers, including product knowledge, recipe development, and best practices for using premium syrups. Training can be conducted on-site or at our facilities.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept various payment methods including bank transfers, major credit cards, and established net payment terms for qualified business customers. Payment terms can be discussed during the ordering process.

How do you ensure product quality?

Quality is our top priority. We maintain strict quality control throughout our production process, from ingredient sourcing to final packaging. Each batch is tested for consistency, flavor profile, and shelf stability before distribution.
