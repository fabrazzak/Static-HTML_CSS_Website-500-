Cookies Policy

Understanding how we use cookies to enhance your browsing experience

Last updated: September 2025

At Teisseire Syrups, we use cookies to improve your experience on our website, analyze traffic, and provide personalized content. This Cookies Policy explains what cookies are, how we use them, and how you can control your preferences.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember information about your visit, such as your language preference and other settings, which can make your next visit easier and the site more useful to you.

Cookie Basics

Cookies don't contain personal information and can't harm your computer. They're simply data files that help improve your web browsing experience by remembering your preferences and settings.

Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies

Purpose: Required for basic website functionality

Examples: Session management, security features

Duration: Session or up to 1 year

Analytics Cookies

Purpose: Help us understand how visitors use our site

Examples: Google Analytics, page views, user behavior

Duration: Up to 2 years

Marketing Cookies

Purpose: Deliver relevant content and advertisements

Examples: Social media integrations, targeted content

Duration: Up to 1 year

Functional Cookies

Purpose: Remember your preferences and settings

Examples: Language selection, newsletter preferences

Duration: Up to 1 year

How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for several important purposes:

  • Website Functionality: Enable essential features like contact forms and newsletter subscriptions
  • User Experience: Remember your preferences to personalize your visits
  • Analytics: Understand how our website is used to improve content and performance
  • Security: Protect against fraud and maintain website security

Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies on our site are set by third-party services we use, such as:

  • Google Analytics for website statistics
  • Social media platforms for sharing functionality
  • CDN services for faster content delivery

Data Protection

All cookie data is handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy and applicable data protection laws. We do not sell or share personal information collected through cookies with unauthorized third parties.

Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You have control over which cookies you accept. You can manage your preferences through your browser settings or using our cookie preference center.

Cookie Preferences

Browser Settings

You can also control cookies through your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions

Cookie Retention

Different types of cookies are retained for different periods:

  • Session Cookies: Deleted when you close your browser
  • Persistent Cookies: Remain until expiry date or manual deletion
  • Essential Cookies: Kept for up to 1 year maximum
  • Analytics Cookies: Typically retained for 24 months

Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legal requirements, or our practices. We'll notify you of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this policy.

Impact of Disabling Cookies

While you can disable cookies, please note that some website features may not function properly without them. Essential cookies cannot be disabled as they're necessary for basic website operation.
