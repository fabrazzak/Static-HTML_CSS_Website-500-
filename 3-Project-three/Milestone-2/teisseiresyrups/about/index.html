Our Story

In 1720, in the heart of the breathtaking French Alps, Mathieu Teisseire began what would become a legendary journey of flavor creation. Armed with nothing but passion, local Alpine ingredients, and an unwavering commitment to quality, he established the foundations of what we know today as the world's premier syrup craftsmanship.

What started as a small artisanal operation in the mountain villages has evolved into a global symbol of authentic flavor and premium quality. Yet, despite our international presence, we remain true to the principles that guided Mathieu over three centuries ago: respect for natural ingredients, meticulous attention to detail, and an uncompromising dedication to excellence.

Our Journey Through Time

1720

The Beginning

Mathieu Teisseire establishes the first syrup workshop in the French Alps, using traditional methods and local botanicals to create exceptional flavor concentrates for the growing beverage trade.

1850

Innovation Era

Introduction of revolutionary preservation techniques allows Teisseire syrups to reach markets across Europe, establishing our reputation for quality and consistency in international markets.

1920

Expansion & Growth

Celebrating two centuries of craftsmanship, Teisseire expands production capabilities while maintaining artisanal quality standards. New flavor profiles are developed using advanced extraction methods.

1980

Global Recognition

Teisseire becomes the preferred choice for premium bars and restaurants worldwide, with our syrups featured in the most prestigious establishments across five continents.

2025

Modern Excellence

Three centuries later, Teisseire continues to lead flavor innovation while honoring traditional craftsmanship, serving beverage creators in over 80 countries with uncompromising quality.
