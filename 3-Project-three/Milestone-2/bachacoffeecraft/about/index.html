Our Story

Crafting exceptional coffee experiences from the heart of Spain since 2021

Home About Us

From Passion to Perfection

Founded in 2021 in the picturesque town of El Verger, Alicante, Bacha Coffee Craft emerged from a simple yet profound passion: to share the world's finest coffee experiences with discerning palates. Our journey began when founder Carlos Mendez returned from extensive travels through coffee-growing regions with a vision to create a coffee company that honors both tradition and innovation.

What started as a small artisanal roastery has blossomed into a destination for coffee enthusiasts who appreciate the meticulous attention to detail that goes into every batch. At Bacha Coffee, we believe that exceptional coffee is not just a beverage but an experience—a moment of connection, reflection, and pure enjoyment.

"Coffee is more than a drink; it's a journey that begins in distant lands and culminates in the perfect cup. Our mission is to guide you through that journey with expertise, passion, and uncompromising quality." - Carlos Mendez, Founder

Our Philosophy

At the heart of Bacha Coffee Craft lies a commitment to three fundamental principles: quality, sustainability, and education. We source only the finest 100% Arabica beans from ethical growers who share our values of environmental stewardship and fair labor practices.

Our roasting philosophy embraces both science and art. We use state-of-the-art equipment to ensure consistency while maintaining the artisan's touch that allows each bean's unique character to shine through. This balanced approach results in coffees that are both sophisticated and approachable, complex yet harmonious.

The Bacha Difference

While many coffee companies focus on volume, we prioritize quality at every step. From selective sourcing to small-batch roasting and meticulous packaging, every detail is carefully considered to ensure your coffee experience is nothing short of exceptional.

Our Values

The principles that guide everything we do at Bacha Coffee Craft

Quality Excellence

We never compromise on quality, from bean selection to final cup. Every product meets our rigorous standards for flavor, aroma, and freshness.

Sustainable Practices

We partner with farms that practice sustainable agriculture and ensure fair treatment of workers throughout the supply chain.

Coffee Education

We believe in sharing knowledge about coffee origins, brewing techniques, and tasting skills to enhance your coffee experience.
