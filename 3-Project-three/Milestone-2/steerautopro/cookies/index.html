1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile phone) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and provide information to website owners.

Key Characteristics of Cookies

  • Small data files stored locally on your device
  • Contain information about your website interactions
  • Help websites remember your preferences and settings
  • Enable personalized content and functionality
  • Assist in website analytics and performance monitoring
  • Generally harmless and do not contain personal identification

How Cookies Work

When you visit our website, cookies are downloaded to your device. These cookies then communicate with our website servers during your visit and on subsequent visits, providing information that helps us deliver a better user experience.

10. Browser Settings

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can set your browser to accept, reject, or notify you about cookies.

Browser-Specific Instructions

Google Chrome

Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data

Mozilla Firefox

Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data

Apple Safari

Preferences → Privacy → Cookies and website data

Microsoft Edge

Settings → Privacy, search, and services → Cookies

11. Mobile Device Settings

Mobile devices also provide options to control cookies and tracking. Settings vary by device type and browser application.

iOS Devices

  • Settings → Safari → Privacy & Security → Block All Cookies
  • Settings → Privacy → Advertising → Limit Ad Tracking
  • Individual app privacy settings

Android Devices

  • Chrome → Settings → Privacy → Cookies
  • Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization
  • Individual browser settings

12. Data Retention

Different cookies have different retention periods based on their purpose and necessity:

  • Session Cookies: Deleted when you close your browser
  • Essential Cookies: Retained for up to 1 year
  • Analytics Cookies: Typically retained for 26 months
  • Marketing Cookies: Usually retained for 90 days to 2 years
  • Functionality Cookies: Retained based on feature requirements
